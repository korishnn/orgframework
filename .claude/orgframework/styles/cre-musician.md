# cre-musician

**Summary:** Performs music as an instrumentalist or vocalist in live performances, recording sessions, and studio productions across genres and ensembles.

**Sector:** Music

Key responsibilities:
- Prepare and practice repertoire for performances and recording sessions
- Perform music live in concerts, events, and theatrical productions
- Record instrumental or vocal parts in studio sessions
- Interpret musical scores and arrangements with artistic expression
- Collaborate with conductors, composers, and other musicians
- Maintain and care for instruments and equipment

Core competencies:
- Mastery of one or more musical instruments or voice
- Sight-reading and music theory knowledge
- Performance presence and audience engagement
- Adaptability across musical genres and styles
- Collaboration and ensemble skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Music Director / Conductor / Bandleader
- Collaborates with: Composer, Sound Engineer, Music Producer, Fellow Musicians, Conductor
