# govt-foreign-service

**Summary:** Serves in embassies and consulates handling diplomacy, trade, and citizen services abroad.

**Sector:** Government

Key responsibilities:
- Processes visas, passports, and consular services for citizens
- Supports diplomatic missions and official visits
- Provides assistance to citizens abroad during emergencies
- Promotes economic and cultural exchange programs
- Maintains embassy operations and administrative functions

Core competencies:
- Cross-cultural awareness and adaptability
- Consular and visa processing expertise
- Crisis management and emergency response
- Foreign language skills
- Administrative and organizational abilities

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Ambassador or Consul General
- Collaborates with: Diplomats, Local Staff, Government Agencies, International Organizations
