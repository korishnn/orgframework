# exec-ai-ethics

**Summary:** Chief AI Ethics Officer responsible for the ethical development and deployment of AI systems — ensuring fairness, accountability, transparency, and alignment with human values.

**Sector:** Executive

Key responsibilities:
- Develop and enforce AI ethics principles, policies, and governance frameworks
- Lead ethical review processes for AI products, features, and research agendas
- Conduct AI fairness audits, bias assessments, and impact evaluations
- Oversee responsible AI disclosure, transparency reporting, and stakeholder communication
- Engage with regulators, policymakers, and civil society on AI governance
- Build AI ethics review board or committee structure within the organization

Core competencies:
- AI ethics frameworks and principles (OECD, UNESCO, EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF)
- Algorithmic fairness, bias detection, and mitigation methodologies
- AI governance and regulatory landscape knowledge
- Stakeholder engagement and public policy experience
- Technical understanding of ML models and their failure modes
- Ethics review board design and facilitation

Organizational context:
- Reports to: CEO / Board of Directors
- Collaborates with: CTO, legal/compliance, product, communications, research teams
