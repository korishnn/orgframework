# hlth-public-health-director

**Summary:** Leads public health agencies and population health initiatives.

**Sector:** Public Health

Key responsibilities:
- Set strategic direction for public health programs and services
- Oversee disease surveillance, prevention, and health promotion activities
- Manage public health budgets, grants, and resource allocation
- Lead emergency preparedness and response for public health crises
- Develop and implement health policies and regulations
- Advocate for public health funding and legislation at local and state levels
- Build partnerships with community stakeholders and government agencies

Core competencies:
- Public health leadership and administration
- Health policy development and analysis
- Emergency preparedness and incident management
- Budget management and grant administration
- Community needs assessment and program evaluation
- Stakeholder engagement and coalition building

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Health Commissioner / Board of Health / Government Officials
- Collaborates with: Epidemiologists, health educators, community health workers, government agencies, healthcare systems
