# cre-streamer

**Summary:** Broadcasts live content on streaming platforms, entertaining and engaging audiences through interactive real-time video streams.

**Sector:** Digital Content

Key responsibilities:
- Plan and schedule live streaming content across platforms
- Broadcast gameplay, creative work, or talk shows in real time
- Engage with viewers through chat, donations, and interactive features
- Manage stream production including overlays, alerts, and scene transitions
- Grow and maintain an audience through consistent streaming and community building
- Negotiate sponsorship and brand partnership deals

Core competencies:
- Engaging on-camera presence and real-time commentary skills
- Technical knowledge of streaming software (OBS, Streamlabs) and equipment
- Community management and audience engagement abilities
- Understanding of streaming platforms and algorithms (Twitch, YouTube, Kick)
- Brand building and content strategy for live formats

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Network / Talent Manager / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Content Creator, Editor, Graphic Designer, Community Manager, Brand Partners
