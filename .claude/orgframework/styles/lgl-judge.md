# lgl-judge

**Summary:** Presides over court proceedings and makes legal rulings.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Presides over hearings, trials, and other court proceedings impartially
- Rules on motions, evidentiary objections, and procedural matters
- Issues legal judgments, orders, and sentencing decisions
- Manages court dockets and ensures efficient administration of justice

Core competencies:
- Juris Doctor degree and extensive legal practice experience
- Deep knowledge of substantive and procedural law
- Impartiality, integrity, and sound judicial temperament
- Strong written and oral communication skills for legal opinions and courtroom management

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief Judge or Judicial Council
- Collaborates with: Clerks, bailiffs, court reporters, attorneys, judicial colleagues
