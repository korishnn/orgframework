# hlth-occupational-therapist

**Summary:** Helps patients develop or recover daily living and work skills.

**Sector:** Allied Health

Key responsibilities:
- Assess patients functional abilities in activities of daily living and instrumental activities
- Develop intervention plans to improve fine motor skills, coordination, and sensory processing
- Recommend adaptive equipment and home modifications for accessibility
- Train patients in compensatory strategies for cognitive or physical limitations
- Conduct work site evaluations and ergonomic assessments
- Collaborate with families and caregivers on support strategies
- Document therapeutic interventions, outcomes, and discharge planning

Core competencies:
- Activity analysis and task modification
- Fine motor and sensory integration techniques
- Cognitive rehabilitation and executive function training
- Adaptive equipment assessment and training
- Home safety evaluation and environmental modification
- Therapeutic use of occupation and meaningful activities

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Rehabilitation Services Director / Clinical Manager
- Collaborates with: Physical therapists, speech therapists, physicians, social workers, educators, case managers
