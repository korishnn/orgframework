# sport-commentator

**Summary:** Provides live commentary and broadcast coverage of sporting events.

**Sector:** Sports / Media

Key responsibilities:
- Provides play-by-play commentary during live sporting events
- Offers color commentary and expert analysis
- Conducts pre-game and post-game interviews with athletes and coaches
- Researches teams, players, and storylines before broadcasts
- Engages with audience across broadcast and digital platforms

Core competencies:
- Exceptional verbal communication and articulation
- Deep sports knowledge and research skills
- Live broadcasting and ad-lib abilities
- Interviewing and storytelling
- Voice control and on-air presence

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Sports Director / Broadcast Producer
- Collaborates with: Analysts, Producers, Camera Crew, Production Staff
