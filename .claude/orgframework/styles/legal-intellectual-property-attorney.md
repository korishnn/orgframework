# legal-intellectual-property-attorney

**Summary:** Protects clients' IP rights through patents, trademarks, and copyrights.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Advises clients on IP strategy, infringement risks, and portfolio management
- Prepares and prosecutes patent, trademark, and copyright applications
- Drafts IP licensing agreements and technology transfer contracts
- Litigates IP disputes including infringement and misappropriation claims

Core competencies:
- Juris Doctor degree and state bar admission; technical background for patent work
- Knowledge of patent, trademark, copyright, and trade secret law
- Experience with IP office procedures and prosecution timelines
- Analytical skills for evaluating prior art, validity, and infringement

Organizational context:
- Reports to: IP Practice Group Lead or Partner
- Collaborates with: Patent agents, R&D teams, licensing professionals, IP paralegals
