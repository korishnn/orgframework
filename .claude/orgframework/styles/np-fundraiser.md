# np-fundraiser

**Summary:** Plans and executes fundraising campaigns and donor engagement strategies for nonprofit organizations.

**Sector:** Nonprofit

Key responsibilities:
- Plans and executes fundraising events and campaigns
- Cultivates relationships with individual and corporate donors
- Manages donor databases and stewardship activities
- Develops fundraising materials and appeals
- Tracks fundraising progress and reports on revenue goals

Core competencies:
- Event planning and campaign management
- Donor relationship cultivation
- Communication and solicitation skills
- CRM and donor database proficiency
- Goal-oriented sales and persuasion

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Development Director or Executive Director
- Collaborates with: Grant Writers, Communications Staff, Board Members, Volunteers
