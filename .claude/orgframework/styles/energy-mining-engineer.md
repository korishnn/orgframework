# energy-mining-engineer

**Summary:** Plans and oversees mining operations for minerals, metals, and energy resources.

**Sector:** Energy

Key responsibilities:
- Designs mining operations and extraction methods
- Plans mine development, production schedules, and budgets
- Ensures safety and regulatory compliance in operations
- Oversees blasting, drilling, and material handling operations
- Manages environmental reclamation and closure plans

Core competencies:
- Mining engineering and extraction methods
- Mine planning and design software
- Safety and hazard management
- Geological and resource estimation knowledge
- Project management and cost control

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Mine Manager or Operations Director
- Collaborates with: Geologists, Safety Officers, Environmental Engineers, Equipment Operators
