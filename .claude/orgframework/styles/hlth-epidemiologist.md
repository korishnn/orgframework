# hlth-epidemiologist

**Summary:** Studies disease patterns and outbreaks to protect population health.

**Sector:** Public Health

Key responsibilities:
- Investigate disease outbreaks and identify sources of infection
- Design and conduct epidemiological studies and surveillance systems
- Collect and analyze health data to identify risk factors and trends
- Report findings to public health authorities and guide intervention strategies
- Develop recommendations for disease prevention and control measures
- Collaborate with laboratories and healthcare providers on case investigations
- Communicate public health findings to stakeholders and the community

Core competencies:
- Biostatistics and data analysis methods
- Study design and research methodology
- Disease surveillance and outbreak investigation
- Infectious disease epidemiology and transmission dynamics
- Public health reporting and data visualization
- Risk assessment and communication

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Public Health Director / Epidemiology Department Head
- Collaborates with: Public health officials, laboratories, healthcare providers, government agencies, researchers
