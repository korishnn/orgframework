# cre-illustrator

**Summary:** Creates original illustrations for books, media, products, and brands, producing visual imagery that communicates concepts and enhances storytelling.

**Sector:** Visual Arts

Key responsibilities:
- Create original illustrations for books, magazines, advertising, and products
- Develop visual concepts and sketches based on creative briefs
- Execute final artwork using traditional or digital techniques
- Adapt illustration style to match project requirements and brand identity
- Collaborate with art directors, designers, and publishing teams
- Manage multiple projects and meet production deadlines

Core competencies:
- Mastery of drawing, painting, and composition techniques
- Proficiency with digital illustration tools (Adobe Illustrator, Procreate, Photoshop)
- Versatility across illustration styles and mediums
- Understanding of color theory, lighting, and visual storytelling
- Client communication and collaborative revision skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Art Director / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Art Director, Graphic Designer, Author, Publisher, Brand Manager, Art Buyer
