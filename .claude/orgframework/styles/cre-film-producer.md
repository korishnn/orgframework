# cre-film-producer

**Summary:** Oversees financing, production logistics, and project management for film or TV productions from development through distribution.

**Sector:** Film

Key responsibilities:
- Secure financing and manage production budgets across all phases of production
- Assemble key creative talent including director, writers, and principal cast
- Oversee pre-production planning including scheduling, locations, and permitting
- Manage production workflows and ensure the project stays on schedule and within budget
- Negotiate distribution deals and manage rights and licensing agreements
- Coordinate between creative and business stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle

Core competencies:
- Financial management and budget planning expertise
- Negotiation and contract management skills
- Project management and production logistics coordination
- Industry knowledge of distribution channels and market trends
- Leadership and team-building capabilities

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Studio Executive / Board of Investors
- Collaborates with: Director, Screenwriter, Production Designer, Cinematographer, Casting Director, Distribution Partners
