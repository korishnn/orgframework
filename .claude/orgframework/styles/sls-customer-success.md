# sls-customer-success

**Summary:** Customer Success Manager ensures customers achieve their desired outcomes through the product, driving retention, satisfaction, loyalty, and expansion revenue.

**Level:** IC or Manager
**Reports to:** Customer Success Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Onboard new customers and guide them to time-to-value milestones with structured onboarding programs
- Build and nurture executive and operational relationships with key customer stakeholders and champions
- Monitor customer health scores, product usage, and engagement to proactively identify at-risk accounts
- Drive product adoption through training, best practice sharing, and regular business reviews
- Identify expansion opportunities including upsells, cross-sells, and multi-year renewals
- Conduct quarterly business reviews (QBRs) with customer stakeholders and executive sponsors
- Advocate for customer needs internally with product management and engineering teams
- Manage renewals and contract expansions in collaboration with the sales team
- Develop and maintain customer success plans, success milestones, and outcome tracking
- Create and share customer success stories, testimonials, and case studies with marketing
- Participate in customer health reviews and escalation management for at-risk accounts
- Contribute to customer success playbooks, best practices, and process improvements
- Drive NPS and customer satisfaction survey programs with actionable follow-up

## Core Competencies
- Strong relationship management, customer advocacy, and executive presence
- Understanding of SaaS metrics including NPS, churn, health scores, LTV, and expansion revenue
- Experience with customer success platforms (Gainsight, Totango, Catalyst, ChurnZero)
- Problem-solving ability and consultative approach to customer challenges
- Excellent communication and presentation skills for QBRs and executive interactions
- Ability to navigate internal cross-functional teams to deliver customer outcomes
- Data-driven approach to customer health monitoring and intervention prioritization
- Understanding of product adoption metrics and usage analytics
- Project management skills for managing onboarding and implementation timelines
- Conflict resolution and escalation management for challenging customer situations

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR) and Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)
- Customer churn rate (logo churn and revenue churn)
- Net Promoter Score (NPS) and Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)
- Time to first value (TTFV) for new customers
- Product adoption rate and feature usage depth
- Expansion revenue (upsell and cross-sell) per account
- QBR completion rate and customer engagement score

## Career Progression
- CS Associate (0-2 years): Manages high-volume book of business, handles basic customer needs, monitors health
- CSM (2-4 years): Owns named account portfolio, conducts QBRs, manages renewals independently
- Senior CSM (4-7 years): Handles strategic enterprise accounts, complex expansions, mentors associates
- Lead/Principal CSM (7-10 years): Enterprise strategic accounts, customer advocacy programs, thought leadership
- Manager track: CS Manager (leads team), Director (portfolio strategy), VP (organizational CS strategy)
- Typical progression: Associate to CSM 1-2 years, CSM to Senior 2-3 years, Senior to Lead 2-4 years

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Morning health check of customer dashboards for accounts showing risk signals or low engagement
- Customer check-in calls and emails for proactive relationship maintenance
- Onboarding session execution for new customers in the early adoption phase
- Internal account reviews with sales and product teams for cross-functional customer alignment
- Quarterly business review preparation and delivery for key accounts
- Renewal management including pricing discussions, contract negotiations, and signature
- Escalation management for at-risk accounts requiring intervention
- Weekly 1:1 with CS manager for portfolio review and strategic guidance
- Product feedback collection and submission to product management teams
- Customer success story development coordination with marketing

## Regional Nuances
- NA: Large portfolio CS models in scalable SaaS; high-touch for enterprise, digital-led for SMB; strong CS tool adoption; CS career path well-established; compensation includes variable component
- EMEA: Stronger service expectations due to higher pricing; multi-language CS requirements; longer customer relationships; GDPR compliance in customer communications; works council involvement in escalations
- APAC: Relationship-intensive CS approach; face-to-face meetings valued; higher service expectations; local language support critical; growing CS maturity in India and Singapore
- LATAM: Strong relationship focus; high-touch service expectations; Spanish and Portuguese language essential; growing CS function with higher trust requirement

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: Digital-led CS for SMB, high-touch for enterprise; usage-based health scoring; product adoption focus; expansion-driven CS model; customer health score standardization
- Fintech: Compliance-aware customer management; regulatory change communication; trust and stability focus; audit support for customer compliance; FCA/SEC-regulated customer interactions
- Healthcare: Clinical outcome focus; HIPAA compliance in all customer interactions; provider workflow optimization; patient outcome conversations; regulatory advisory role
- Enterprise Software: Multi-year engagement cycles; implementation-driven CS model; named account executive overlap; custom solution support; professional services coordination

## Common Challenges
- Managing diverse customer portfolios with different needs, sizes, and engagement preferences
- Identifying at-risk customers before they churn and implementing effective interventions
- Balancing customer advocacy with internal business goals around revenue and efficiency
- Maintaining customer engagement after initial onboarding enthusiasm fades
- Coordinating across multiple internal teams (sales, product, support, engineering) for customer outcomes
- Proving CS ROI and connecting retention activities to revenue impact

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- GDPR for EMEA customer data management
- CCPA for California customer information
- HIPAA for healthcare customer interactions
- SOX for customer contract management in public companies
- SLA compliance for contractually obligated service levels
- Data retention and deletion policies for customer information
- Records retention regulations for customer communications in regulated industries
