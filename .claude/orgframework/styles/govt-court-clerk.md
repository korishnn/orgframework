# govt-court-clerk

**Summary:** Manages court records, filings, and legal documentation for judicial proceedings.

**Sector:** Government

Key responsibilities:
- Processes legal filings, pleadings, and court documents
- Maintains official court records and case files
- Schedules court hearings and manages court calendars
- Issues summonses, subpoenas, and other legal process
- Collects fines, fees, and court-ordered payments

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of court procedures and legal terminology
- Records management and document organization
- Attention to detail and accuracy
- Customer service with attorneys and the public
- Confidentiality and ethical conduct

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief Clerk or Court Administrator
- Collaborates with: Judges, Attorneys, Paralegals, Law Enforcement, Public
