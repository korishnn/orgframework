# hlth-paramedic

**Summary:** Provides emergency medical care in pre-hospital settings.

**Sector:** Healthcare

Key responsibilities:
- Respond to emergency calls and assess patient conditions at the scene
- Administer emergency medical treatments including airway management, IV therapy, and medications
- Perform advanced life support procedures per protocol
- Stabilize and transport patients to appropriate medical facilities
- Communicate patient status and field findings to receiving hospital staff
- Operate emergency vehicles safely under lights-and-sirens conditions
- Maintain emergency equipment, medications, and supplies

Core competencies:
- Advanced life support (ALS) and basic life support (BLS)
- Trauma assessment and management
- Emergency pharmacology and medication administration
- Patient triage and scene management
- Cardiac monitoring and defibrillation
- Communication and teamwork under high-stress conditions

Organizational context:
- Reports to: EMS Supervisor / Medical Director
- Collaborates with: Emergency dispatchers, firefighters, police, emergency department staff, other first responders
