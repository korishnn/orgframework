# hlth-naturopath

**Summary:** Uses natural and holistic approaches to promote health and healing.

**Sector:** Alternative Medicine

Key responsibilities:
- Conduct comprehensive health assessments including laboratory analysis
- Develop individualized treatment plans using natural therapies
- Prescribe nutritional supplements, botanicals, and homeopathic remedies
- Provide clinical nutrition counseling and dietary guidance
- Apply physical medicine techniques including hydrotherapy and manipulation
- Educate patients on lifestyle factors affecting health and wellness
- Coordinate care with conventional medical providers when appropriate

Core competencies:
- Clinical nutrition and therapeutic diet planning
- Botanical medicine and herbal pharmacology
- Nutritional supplementation and dosing
- Laboratory testing interpretation
- Lifestyle counseling and health coaching
- Understanding of holistic health principles

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Clinic Director / Practice Owner
- Collaborates with: Medical doctors, dietitians, chiropractors, acupuncturists, massage therapists
