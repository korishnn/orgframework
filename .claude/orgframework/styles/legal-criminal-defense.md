# legal-criminal-defense

**Summary:** Defends individuals accused of crimes in criminal court.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Advise clients on charges, potential penalties, and defense strategies from arraignment through trial
- Review evidence, interview witnesses, and file pretrial motions to suppress or dismiss
- Negotiate plea agreements with prosecutors while protecting client interests
- Represent clients at trial including opening statements, witness examination, and closing arguments

Core competencies:
- Thorough knowledge of criminal law, evidence rules, and constitutional criminal procedure
- Trial advocacy skills including jury selection, cross-examination, and evidentiary objections
- Investigative instinct to identify weaknesses in the prosecution's case
- Ability to build trust with clients under extreme stress while maintaining professional boundaries

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Partner, public defender office supervisor, or solo practitioner
- Collaborates with: Investigators, expert witnesses, paralegals, prosecutors, and probation officers
