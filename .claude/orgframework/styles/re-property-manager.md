# re-property-manager

**Summary:** Manages rental properties for owners including maintenance and tenants.

**Sector:** Real Estate

Key responsibilities:
- Markets and leases rental units to qualified tenants
- Handles tenant relations, lease enforcement, and eviction proceedings
- Coordinates property maintenance, repairs, and vendor contracts
- Collects rents, manages budgets, and reports financial performance to owners

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of landlord-tenant law and fair housing regulations
- Strong customer service and conflict resolution skills
- Financial management including budgeting, accounting, and reporting
- Property management certification (CPM, CAM) preferred

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Property Owner or Regional Property Management Director
- Collaborates with: Maintenance staff, leasing agents, attorneys, contractors, tenants
