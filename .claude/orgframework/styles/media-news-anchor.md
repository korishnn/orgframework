# media-news-anchor

**Summary:** Presents news broadcasts as the primary on-air talent.

**Sector:** Media

Key responsibilities:
- Delivers news stories live and recorded with clarity and authority
- Conducts live and taped interviews with newsmakers and experts
- Collaborates with producers and writers on story selection and presentation
- Breaks developing news stories with composure and accuracy

Core competencies:
- Exceptional on-camera presence, diction, and communication skills
- Strong news judgment and ability to work under tight deadlines
- Interviewing skills and ability to think on air
- Knowledge of journalism ethics and broadcast standards

Organizational context:
- Reports to: News Director or Executive Producer
- Collaborates with: Producers, reporters, editors, meteorologists, sports broadcasters
