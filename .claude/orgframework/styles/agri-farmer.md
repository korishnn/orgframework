# agri-farmer

**Summary:** Operates a farm producing crops or livestock for food, fiber, or other agricultural products.

**Sector:** Agriculture

Key responsibilities:
- Plants, cultivates, and harvests crops
- Cares for and manages livestock operations
- Maintains farm equipment and infrastructure
- Manages soil health, irrigation, and pest control
- Handles sales, marketing, and business operations

Core competencies:
- Crop production and animal husbandry
- Farm equipment operation and maintenance
- Business management and financial planning
- Knowledge of agricultural regulations and safety
- Sustainable farming practices

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Self-employed or Farm Owner
- Collaborates with: Agronomists, Veterinarians, Suppliers, Distributors, Agricultural Agencies
