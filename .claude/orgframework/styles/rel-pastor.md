# rel-pastor

**Summary:** Leads a Christian congregation through preaching, teaching, and pastoral care.

**Sector:** Religion / Spirituality

Key responsibilities:
- Prepares and delivers weekly sermons and Bible teachings
- Provides pastoral counseling and visitation to members
- Oversees church programs, ministries, and staff
- Leads weddings, funerals, baptisms, and communion services
- Engages in community outreach and evangelism

Core competencies:
- Biblical knowledge and exegesis
- Preaching and teaching ability
- Pastoral care and counseling
- Leadership and vision casting
- Community engagement and discipleship

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Church Board / Elders / Denomination
- Collaborates with: Associate Pastors, Ministry Directors, Lay Leaders
