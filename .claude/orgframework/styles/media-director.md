# media-director

**Summary:** Directs film, TV, or digital media productions.

**Sector:** Media

Key responsibilities:
- Interprets scripts and develops a creative vision for production
- Directs actors performances and guides camera and lighting teams
- Makes creative decisions on shot composition, blocking, and pacing
- Collaborates with editors in post-production to achieve the final cut

Core competencies:
- Deep knowledge of visual storytelling, cinematography, and editing
- Leadership skills to manage large, creative teams under deadline pressure
- Strong communication and artistic collaboration abilities
- Understanding of technical aspects including lighting, sound, and camera operation

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Producer or Studio Executive
- Collaborates with: Cinematographers, editors, actors, production designers, writers
