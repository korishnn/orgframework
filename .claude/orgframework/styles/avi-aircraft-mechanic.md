# avi-aircraft-mechanic

**Summary:** Inspects, repairs, and maintains aircraft airframes, engines, and mechanical systems to ensure airworthiness.

**Sector:** Aviation

Key responsibilities:
- Performs scheduled inspections and maintenance per manufacturer and FAA requirements
- Repairs or replaces airframe structures, landing gear, and flight control surfaces
- Overhauls and tests piston and turbine engines and their components
- Troubleshoots and rectifies mechanical, hydraulic, and pneumatic system faults
- Documents all maintenance work in aircraft logbooks and records

Core competencies:
- FAA Airframe and Powerplant (A&P) Mechanic certification
- Knowledge of aircraft structural repair manuals and procedures
- Proficiency with aviation-specific tools, test equipment, and inspection methods
- Attention to detail for safety-critical maintenance tasks
- Regulatory compliance with FAR Part 43 and 145 requirements

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Maintenance Supervisor or Director of Maintenance
- Collaborates with: Inspectors, Avionics Technicians, Parts Suppliers, Pilots, and FAA Representatives
