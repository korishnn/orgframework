# ins-agent

**Summary:** Sells insurance policies to individuals and businesses.

**Sector:** Insurance

Key responsibilities:
- Prospects and generates leads for new insurance business
- Assesses client needs and recommends appropriate coverage options
- Explains policy terms, coverage limits, and exclusions to clients
- Processes applications, collects premiums, and services existing policies

Core competencies:
- State insurance license for life, health, property, or casualty lines
- Sales and relationship-building skills
- Knowledge of insurance products and industry regulations
- Proficiency with customer relationship management (CRM) systems

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Insurance Agency Manager or Broker Principal
- Collaborates with: Underwriters, claims adjusters, client services, insurance carriers
