# cs-success

**Summary:** Customer Success Manager responsible for driving product adoption, customer health, retention, and revenue expansion across an assigned portfolio of customer accounts.

**Level:** IC or Manager
**Reports to:** Customer Success Director / VP Customer Success
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Own the post-sale relationship for an assigned portfolio of customer accounts, driving product adoption and measurable value realization
- Monitor customer health scores across key indicators including product usage, support ticket trends, payment history, survey sentiment, and engagement patterns
- Develop and execute structured success plans with defined milestones, success criteria, timelines, and stakeholder alignment for strategic accounts
- Conduct regular business reviews and quarterly business reviews (QBRs) demonstrating ROI, reviewing goals, and identifying growth opportunities
- Identify expansion, upsell, and cross-sell opportunities and coordinate with sales account teams on qualified lead handoff and commercial execution
- Advocate for customer needs internally with product management, support, and engineering teams based on structured feedback and usage data analysis
- Manage renewals proactively with structured campaigns that engage early, demonstrate ongoing value, address concerns, and secure commitments
- Drive onboarding and time-to-first-value for new customers, coordinating with implementation, professional services, and training teams
- Develop deep product expertise to serve as a trusted advisor on best practices, feature adoption optimization, and solution expansion
- Cultivate executive relationships within customer accounts, connecting with champions, economic buyers, and executive sponsors at multiple levels
- Collect and share customer success stories, testimonials, quantitative case studies, and reference opportunities with marketing and sales teams
- Serve as the voice of the customer internally, providing structured feedback to product and leadership teams on product gaps and market needs

## Core Competencies
- Customer relationship management and strategic account planning at scale
- Health score management and data-driven customer insights and reporting
- Executive communication and presentation skills for QBRs and business reviews
- Cross-functional collaboration with sales, support, product, and professional services teams
- Negotiation and retention strategies for at-risk customer recovery
- Product adoption methodology and customer onboarding best practices
- Data analysis skills for customer usage analytics, health scoring, and trend identification
- Time management and portfolio prioritization across accounts at different lifecycle stages
- Emotional intelligence and relationship-building for customer trust and advocacy
- Commercial awareness for expansion revenue identification and pipeline development
- Project management for multi-stakeholder customer initiatives and success plans
- Written communication for executive summaries, escalation documentation, and customer correspondence

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR) and Gross Revenue Retention (GRR) for assigned portfolio
- Customer health score distribution and trend across the portfolio
- Product adoption rates — MAU/DAU, feature adoption breadth, and depth of use
- Time-to-first-value for new customers (days from go-live to first meaningful outcome)
- Renewal rate and churn percentage by account segment and reason code
- Expansion revenue generated through upsell and cross-sell identification
- Customer satisfaction (CSAT) and Net Promoter Score (NPS) trends

## Career Progression
- **Associate / Junior CSM (0-2 years):** Manages a portfolio of small accounts with high support. Focuses on onboarding, adoption basics, and renewal support. Learns product and customer success methodology. Often has support or sales background.
- **Customer Success Manager (2-5 years):** Independently manages a portfolio of mid-market accounts. Conducts QBRs, manages health scores, drives renewals. Identifies expansion opportunities. Proficient in CS platforms and data analysis.
- **Senior CSM / Enterprise CSM (5-8 years):** Manages strategic enterprise accounts with complex organizational structures. Develops executive relationships. Handles at-risk account recovery. Drives significant expansion revenue. Mentors junior CSMs.
- **Lead / Principal CSM (8-12 years):** Leads the CS practice for a customer segment. Develops CS processes and playbooks. Handles the most strategic and highest-value accounts. Partners with sales leadership on account strategy.
- **Director of Customer Success (12+ years):** Manages a CS team. Sets CS strategy, targets, and methodology. Owns NRR and churn targets for the segment. Develops CS talent and career paths. Partners with VP CS on org strategy.
- **VP Customer Success (15+ years):** Executive ownership of the CS organization. Sets CS vision and strategy. Reports to CEO/COO. Owns retention and expansion P&L. Board reporting on customer health and revenue retention.

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Start with health score dashboard review — check portfolio for accounts that have changed status, especially those at risk
- Outreach and engagement — proactive check-in emails, calls, or messages to key accounts based on health triggers and lifecycle stage
- Customer meetings — check-in calls, strategy sessions, executive business reviews, or onboarding kickoffs depending on the day
- Renewal management — track upcoming renewals, prepare value documentation, engage decision-makers, negotiate terms
- Internal coordination — sync with sales on account strategy, with support on open tickets, with product on feature requests
- Health score updates — review recent usage data, support interactions, and survey responses to update customer health assessments
- Escalation handling — address at-risk customer situations with internal stakeholders, develop recovery plans
- QBR preparation — compile usage data, ROI analysis, and strategic recommendations for upcoming quarterly reviews
- Expansion identification — monitor usage patterns for adoption gaps, identify feature expansion opportunities, and qualify leads for sales
- Internal team meetings — weekly CS team sync, account strategy reviews, product feedback sessions
- Quarterly planning — portfolio strategy, target setting, expansion goals, and at-risk account remediation plans
- Communication rhythm is relationship-driven and proactive with structured check-in cadences for each account segment

## Cross-Functional Relationships
- **Sales (sls-ae, sls-customer-success):] Account planning partnership, expansion opportunity handoff, renewal coordination, prospect-to-customer transition, joint QBRs for strategic accounts
- **Support (cs-tier1, cs-tier2, cs-tier3):** Customer health signal sharing, escalation coordination, support ticket trend analysis for health scoring, joined escalation management for at-risk accounts
- **Product (prd-manager, prd-owner):] Customer feedback aggregation and prioritization, feature request advocacy, product roadmap input from customer needs, beta program recruitment from customer base
- **Professional Services / Implementation:** Onboarding coordination, project timeline alignment, customer training handoff, implementation quality feedback
- **Marketing (mkt-growth, mkt-content):] Customer reference recruiting, case study development, customer event participation, customer communication and campaign alignment
- **Finance (fin-fpa, fin-accounting):] Renewal pricing approvals, billing issue resolution, contract compliance for renewals, customer financial health monitoring
- **Legal (lgl-contracts):** Contract renewal terms, amendment processing, MSAs and order form updates, contractual compliance for customer commitments
- **Training / Enablement (hr-lnd, sls-enablement):] Customer training program input, adoption methodology alignment, customer education content development
- **Data / Analytics (prd-analytics, da-analyst):** Health score model development, customer analytics support, churn prediction model collaboration
- **Executive Sponsors:** Executive engagement for strategic accounts, relationship depth at the C-suite level, escalation resolution for critical issues
- **Revenue Operations (sls-revenue-ops):] Renewal forecasting, CRM data quality, CS platform administration, reporting and metrics infrastructure

## Regional Nuances
- **NA:** Mature CS profession with clear role definition and career path. High expectations for data-driven customer engagement. QBRs are standard practice. CS platforms are sophisticated with health scoring and automation. Compensation $800K-$1500K plus variable compensation tied to retention and expansion.
- **EMEA:** Growing CS profession with increasing adoption of North American CS practices. Multi-language capability is essential for regional portfolios. Stronger emphasis on relationship and trust-building with less aggressive expansion focus. GDPR compliance integrated into customer engagement approach. Lower compensation but stronger benefits and work-life balance.
- **APAC:** Expanding CS function in India, Singapore, Australia, and Japan. High-touch relationship culture in Japan and Korea requires frequent in-person visits. Indian CS teams often serve global accounts remotely. Longer decision cycles in enterprise accounts. Mobile-first customer engagement for SaaS products. Cultural emphasis on long-term relationship over short-term expansion.
- **LATAM:** Growing CS profession with strong relationship-based culture. Personal connections are critical — frequent video calls and in-person meetings valued. Less emphasis on formal QBRs and more on ongoing relationship management. Local language support is essential. Economic volatility affects renewal conversations and pricing discussions. Lower compensation but high growth opportunity.

## Industry Variations
- **SaaS:** Standard CS model with health scores, QBRs, and adoption metrics. PLG companies have different CS motion — digital CS at scale for self-serve customers and high-touch for enterprise. Usage-based pricing adds complexity to value demonstration. Feature adoption and depth of use are primary focus areas.
- **Fintech:** Compliance and trust are central to the customer relationship. Regulatory change management is a CS service. Financial health metrics alongside product usage. Longer customer lifecycle with complex implementation. Security and fraud-related health indicators. Sensitive data handling in all customer interactions.
- **Healthcare:** Clinical outcomes and workflow integration are success metrics alongside product usage. Provider and patient success may be separate functions. Regulatory changes create value demonstration opportunities. Longer sales and implementation cycles affect onboarding approach. HIPAA compliance in all customer communications.
- **E-commerce:** Merchant success focus on GMV, conversion rates, and average order value. Buyer success focuses on satisfaction and platform trust. Seasonal usage patterns require flexible health scoring. Marketplace dynamics require managing both seller and buyer success. Logistics and fulfillment performance indicators.
- **Manufacturing/IoT:** On-site and field service integration for customer success. Equipment uptime and performance metrics alongside software adoption. Connected product lifecycle management. Multi-stakeholder relationships within customer organizations (operations, IT, engineering). Longer-term success cycles with hardware replacement timelines.

## Common Challenges
- Demonstrating clear ROI and value to customers when product impact is difficult to isolate from other factors
- Managing a portfolio with accounts at different lifecycle stages that all need attention simultaneously
- Balancing advocacy for the customer with the commercial interests of the organization during renewals and expansions
- Recovering at-risk accounts where the root cause is outside the CSM's control (product gaps, pricing, competitor capabilities)
- Scaling high-touch engagement model across a growing customer base without burning out the CS team
- Getting internal product and engineering teams to prioritize customer-requested features and improvements
- Maintaining consistent engagement across accounts when many are healthy and don't demand attention but still need proactive management to stay that way

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- GDPR — customer data handling in CS platforms, consent management for customer communications
- CCPA/CPRA — customer information handling and data subject rights in CS operations
- HIPAA (if applicable) — PHI handling in customer communications and health records
- PCI-DSS — customer payment information handling during renewal and billing conversations
- SOX compliance for renewal revenue forecasting and reporting accuracy
- TCPA and CAN-SPAM for customer outreach communications
- Anti-bribery (FCPA, UK Bribery Act) — restrictions on gifts, entertainment, and incentives for customer contacts
- Accessibility compliance (ADA, WCAG) for customer-facing content and communications
- Industry-specific regulations (FINRA, FDA, FCC) affecting customer advisory and communication
- Contract law — renewal terms, contractual commitments, service level commitments
- Records retention for customer correspondence, QBR documentation, and renewal records
- Professional liability — customer advisory and recommendations must not exceed product capabilities
