# hosp-resort-manager

**Summary:** Oversees resort operations, amenities, and guest experience.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Manages all resort departments including accommodations, dining, and recreation
- Ensures exceptional guest experience across all touchpoints
- Oversees revenue management, budgeting, and financial targets
- Maintains property standards, safety, and regulatory compliance
- Develops seasonal programming and guest amenities

Core competencies:
- Multi-department hospitality management
- Financial and revenue management
- Guest experience and service excellence
- Strategic planning and execution
- Team leadership and development

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Regional Director / Corporate Leadership / Owner
- Collaborates with: Department Heads, Sales & Marketing, Maintenance
