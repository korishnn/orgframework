# edu-dean

**Summary:** Oversees academic programs, faculty, and student affairs in higher education.

**Sector:** Academia

Key responsibilities:
- Manage academic programs, curriculum quality, and accreditation
- Recruit, evaluate, and support faculty development
- Oversee student disciplinary matters and academic policies
- Allocate departmental resources and manage budgets
- Represent the college or school within the university and externally

Core competencies:
- Academic leadership and faculty management
- Strategic planning and resource allocation
- Curriculum oversight and accreditation knowledge
- Student affairs and conflict resolution

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Provost / Vice President of Academic Affairs
- Collaborates with: Department Chairs, Faculty, Registrar, Student Affairs, Other Deans
