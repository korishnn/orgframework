# cre-photojournalist

**Summary:** Captures newsworthy images and tells stories through photography, documenting events, people, and issues for editorial publication.

**Sector:** Journalism

Key responsibilities:
- Photograph news events, features, and documentary stories in the field
- Edit and caption images for editorial publication
- Research story assignments and plan visual coverage
- Maintain ethical standards in documentary and news photography
- Manage digital asset libraries and photo archives
- Collaborate with editors on visual story selection and layout

Core competencies:
- Advanced photography and lighting technique expertise
- Strong visual storytelling and compositional skills
- Knowledge of photo editing software (Adobe Lightroom, Photoshop)
- Understanding of journalistic ethics and documentary standards
- Ability to work under pressure in dynamic field conditions

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Photo Editor / Assignment Editor
- Collaborates with: Journalist, Editor, Photo Editor, Designer, Picture Researcher
