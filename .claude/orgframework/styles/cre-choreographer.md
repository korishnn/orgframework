# cre-choreographer

**Summary:** Creates and teaches dance routines and movement sequences for theater, film, concerts, and performance productions.

**Sector:** Theater

Key responsibilities:
- Create original dance routines and movement sequences for productions
- Teach and rehearse choreography with performers of varying skill levels
- Adapt movement styles to match production themes, periods, and musical scores
- Collaborate with directors and music directors on integrated performance vision
- Stage musical numbers with attention to spacing, transitions, and audience sightlines
- Ensure performer safety during physically demanding sequences

Core competencies:
- Extensive knowledge of dance techniques and movement styles
- Strong teaching and communication skills for working with performers
- Musicality and ability to synchronize movement with music
- Creative composition and spatial awareness for staging
- Physical demonstration ability and injury prevention awareness

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Director
- Collaborates with: Director, Music Director, Costume Designer, Performers, Stage Manager, Dance Captain
