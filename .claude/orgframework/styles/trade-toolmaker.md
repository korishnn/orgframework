# trade-toolmaker

**Summary:** Creates precision tools, dies, jigs, and fixtures used in manufacturing processes across industries.

**Sector:** Skilled Labor

Key responsibilities:
- Designs and fabricates dies, molds, jigs, and fixtures to specification
- Operates precision machine tools including surface grinders and EDM equipment
- Heat-treats and finishes tool steel to required hardness and tolerance
- Assembles and tests tools to verify function and dimensional accuracy
- Repairs and modifies existing tooling to extend service life

Core competencies:
- Advanced machining skills with tight-tolerance capability
- Knowledge of tool steel properties, heat treating, and surface finishing
- Blueprint reading and CAD/CAM proficiency
- Problem-solving for complex geometric and tolerance challenges
- Patience and precision for intricate, hands-on work

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Tool Room Supervisor or Engineering Manager
- Collaborates with: Machinists, Design Engineers, Production Planners, and Quality Inspectors
