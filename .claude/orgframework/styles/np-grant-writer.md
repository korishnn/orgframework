# np-grant-writer

**Summary:** Writes grant proposals to secure funding from foundations, corporations, and government agencies.

**Sector:** Nonprofit

Key responsibilities:
- Researches grant opportunities from foundations and agencies
- Writes compelling grant proposals and letters of inquiry
- Develops program budgets and supporting documentation
- Manages grant submission calendars and deadlines
- Prepares grant reports for funders on program outcomes

Core competencies:
- Persuasive writing and storytelling
- Research and prospect identification
- Budget development and financial narrative
- Understanding of funder priorities and guidelines
- Project management and deadline tracking

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Development Director or Executive Director
- Collaborates with: Program Managers, Finance Staff, Executive Leadership
