# cre-curator

**Summary:** Selects and organizes exhibitions and collections, researching artworks and developing interpretive content for public engagement.

**Sector:** Museums & Galleries

Key responsibilities:
- Research and select artworks and objects for exhibitions
- Develop exhibition concepts, themes, and narratives
- Write exhibition catalogs, wall text, and interpretive materials
- Coordinate loan agreements with other institutions and collectors
- Oversee installation and display of artworks and artifacts
- Collaborate with educators and public programs teams

Core competencies:
- Deep art historical knowledge and research expertise
- Strong writing skills for catalogs and interpretive content
- Curation and exhibition design sensibility
- Collection management and provenance research abilities
- Collaboration with artists, lenders, and museum departments

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Museum Director / Head of Curatorial
- Collaborates with: Conservator, Exhibition Designer, Educator, Registrar, Art Critic, Artist
