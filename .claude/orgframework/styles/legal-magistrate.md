# legal-magistrate

**Summary:** Presides over lower court proceedings, issuing warrants, hearing preliminary matters, and delivering rulings within a defined jurisdiction.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Preside over arraignments, bail hearings, and preliminary hearings
- Issue search warrants, arrest warrants, and summons
- Adjudicate minor criminal offenses and civil disputes
- Ensure courtroom procedures comply with legal standards

Core competencies:
- Deep knowledge of criminal and civil procedure
- Judicial temperament and impartial decision-making
- Legal research and statutory interpretation
- Effective courtroom management and communication

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief Magistrate or Presiding Judge
- Collaborates with: Court clerks, bailiffs, prosecutors, defense attorneys, law enforcement
