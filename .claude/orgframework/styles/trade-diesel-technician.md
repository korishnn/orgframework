# trade-diesel-technician

**Summary:** Services and repairs diesel engines in trucks, buses, heavy equipment, and industrial machinery.

**Sector:** Skilled Labor

Key responsibilities:
- Diagnoses and repairs diesel engines, fuel systems, and turbochargers
- Services heavy-duty transmissions, drivelines, and hydraulic systems
- Performs preventive maintenance on fleet vehicles and equipment
- Reads technical manuals and wiring schematics for diagnostics
- Tests and adjusts engine performance using diagnostic software

Core competencies:
- Deep knowledge of diesel engine operation and fuel injection systems
- Proficiency with heavy-duty tools, diagnostic software, and shop equipment
- Understanding of hydraulic, pneumatic, and electrical systems
- Physical strength for lifting heavy components and working in awkward positions
- CDL or knowledge of DOT inspection requirements

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Fleet Maintenance Supervisor or Shop Manager
- Collaborates with: Parts Coordinators, Fleet Managers, Service Writers, and Drivers
