# prd-growth-engineer

**Summary:** Growth Engineer responsible for building the technical infrastructure for growth — experimentation platforms, tracking systems, and product-led growth mechanics.

**Sector:** Product

Key responsibilities:
- Build and maintain growth experimentation infrastructure (feature flags, A/B testing frameworks, stats engine)
- Implement product analytics tracking, event schemas, and data pipelines for growth metrics
- Develop self-serve conversion flows, onboarding mechanics, and viral loop features
- Build growth tooling for marketing automation, user segmentation, and personalization
- Optimize landing pages, signup flows, and conversion funnels for speed and conversion
- Integrate with CRM, email, push, and messaging platforms for lifecycle automation

Core competencies:
- Full-stack engineering (frontend + backend) with growth-specific architecture patterns
- Experimentation platforms (Statsig, LaunchDarkly, GrowthBook, custom solutions)
- Product analytics integration (Amplitude, Mixpanel, PostHog, Segment)
- Feature flag and A/B testing infrastructure
- Conversion optimization engineering (landing page perf, form optimization, checkout flows)
- Data engineering for growth (event pipelines, funnel analysis, cohort computation)

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Head of Growth / VP Product
- Collaborates with: Growth PMs, data science, marketing, engineering teams
