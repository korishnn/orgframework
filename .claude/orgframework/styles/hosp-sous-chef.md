# hosp-sous-chef

**Summary:** Second-in-command in the kitchen, manages line cooks and prep.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Supervises line cooks and prep staff during service
- Assists the chef with menu planning and recipe standardization
- Manages kitchen inventory and ingredient ordering
- Ensures consistent plating and food quality
- Trains new kitchen staff on techniques and procedures

Core competencies:
- Advanced culinary skills and techniques
- Team supervision and shift management
- Inventory and supply management
- Communication and coordination
- Speed and efficiency under pressure

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Executive Chef / Chef de Cuisine
- Collaborates with: Line Cooks, Pastry Chef, Dishwasher, Purchasing
