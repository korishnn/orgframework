# media-documentarian

**Summary:** Creates documentary films that explore real-world subjects, issues, and stories through research, filming, and narrative production.

**Sector:** Media

Key responsibilities:
- Research and develop documentary topics, themes, and subjects
- Conduct interviews and capture verite footage in the field
- Write narrative treatments and structure story arcs
- Oversee post-production editing, sound design, and final delivery

Core competencies:
- Storytelling and narrative construction
- Camera operation, lighting, and location sound recording
- Research and fact-checking for accuracy and fairness
- Editing proficiency with non-linear editing software

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Executive Producer or Director of Programming
- Collaborates with: Producers, editors, cinematographers, sound designers, archival researchers
