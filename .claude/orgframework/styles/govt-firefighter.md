# govt-firefighter

**Summary:** Responds to fires, emergencies, and rescue situations to protect life and property.

**Sector:** Government

Key responsibilities:
- Responds to structural and wildland fires
- Performs medical emergency response and first aid
- Conducts vehicle extrication and technical rescues
- Inspects buildings for fire code compliance
- Maintains fire apparatus, equipment, and station readiness

Core competencies:
- Fire suppression techniques and strategies
- Emergency medical skills and certification
- Physical fitness and stamina
- Team coordination under high-stress conditions
- Hazardous materials awareness and response

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Fire Captain or Fire Chief
- Collaborates with: EMS Personnel, Police Officers, Hazard Response Teams, Dispatch
