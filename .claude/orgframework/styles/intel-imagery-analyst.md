# intel-imagery-analyst

**Summary:** Analyzes satellite and aerial imagery to extract intelligence information.

**Sector:** Intelligence

Key responsibilities:
- Analyzes satellite and aerial imagery for intelligence purposes
- Identifies and measures objects, facilities, and activities
- Produces imagery intelligence reports and products
- Conducts change detection and trend analysis over time
- Provides geospatial context for operational planning

Core competencies:
- Imagery analysis and interpretation techniques
- Geospatial analysis and remote sensing
- Photogrammetry and measurement science
- Attention to visual detail and pattern recognition
- Intelligence reporting and briefing

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Imagery Branch Chief or Intelligence Division Lead
- Collaborates with: Intelligence Analysts, Geospatial Analysts, Collection Managers
