# tran-logistics-coordinator

**Summary:** Plans and coordinates freight movement, supply chain activities, and transportation logistics for an organization.

**Sector:** Transportation

Key responsibilities:
- Coordinates inbound and outbound shipments across multiple carriers
- Tracks shipments and provides status updates to stakeholders
- Selects carriers, negotiates rates, and books freight movements
- Resolves shipping delays, damages, and documentation discrepancies
- Maintains logistics records and prepares shipping reports

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of transportation modes (truck, rail, air, ocean)
- Supply chain management and routing principles
- Carrier relationship management and rate negotiation
- Logistics software (TMS) and tracking system proficiency
- Problem-solving for supply chain disruptions

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Logistics Manager or Supply Chain Director
- Collaborates with: Warehouse Staff, Dispatchers, Carriers, Procurement, and Customers
