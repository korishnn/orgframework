# sci-materials-scientist

**Summary:** Develops new materials and analyzes material properties.

**Sector:** Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Research and develop new materials with specific properties
- Characterize material structure, composition, and performance
- Design and conduct experiments to test material behavior
- Collaborate with engineers on material applications and product development
- Document findings and contribute to materials science publications

Core competencies:
- Materials characterization techniques (microscopy, spectroscopy, etc.)
- Knowledge of material properties and processing methods
- Experimental design and failure analysis
- Interdisciplinary collaboration with engineering teams

Organizational context:
- Reports to: R&D Director / Lab Manager
- Collaborates with: Engineers, Product developers, Quality assurance, Manufacturing teams
