# hosp-bartender

**Summary:** Prepares and serves alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Mixes and serves alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages
- Manages bar inventory including spirits, mixers, and garnishes
- Provides attentive service to bar guests
- Processes payments and manages a cash drawer
- Maintains bar cleanliness and compliance with liquor laws

Core competencies:
- Mixology and drink preparation
- Product and cocktail menu knowledge
- Speed and multitasking during busy periods
- Customer engagement and upselling
- Responsible alcohol service and ID verification

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Bar Manager / Restaurant Manager
- Collaborates with: Servers, Bar Back, Manager on Duty
