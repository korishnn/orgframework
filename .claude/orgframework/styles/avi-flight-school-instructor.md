# avi-flight-school-instructor

**Summary:** Trains student pilots for private and commercial certification.

**Sector:** Aviation

Key responsibilities:
- Conduct pre-flight briefings, in-flight instruction, and post-flight debriefings
- Teach aeronautical knowledge including regulations, weather, navigation, and aircraft systems
- Demonstrate and evaluate maneuvers, emergency procedures, and instrument flying
- Track student progress and endorse logbooks for certification milestones

Core competencies:
- Certified Flight Instructor (CFI) credential with strong instrument and multi-engine proficiency
- Ability to break down complex procedures into teachable steps for varied learning styles
- Situational awareness and decision-making to manage risk during student flights
- Patience and clear communication under high-workload cockpit conditions

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief flight instructor or school director of operations
- Collaborates with: Other flight instructors, maintenance personnel, FAA or CASA examiners, and dispatchers
