# sls-bdr

**Summary:** Business Development Representative prospects, qualifies, and converts target accounts into qualified meetings and sales pipeline through outbound outreach and strategic lead development.

**Level:** IC
**Reports to:** Sales Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Research and identify target accounts, decision-makers, and key contacts within ideal customer profile segments
- Execute multi-channel outbound prospecting sequences including cold calls, personalized emails, LinkedIn outreach, and social selling
- Qualify leads using BANT, MEDDIC, or CHAMP frameworks before scheduling meetings with account executives
- Maintain accurate and timely lead activity records in CRM including calls, emails, and meeting outcomes
- Achieve monthly and quarterly targets for qualified meetings, pipeline creation, and sourced revenue
- Collaborate with marketing on lead generation campaigns, feedback loops, and lead quality improvement
- Continuously refine outreach messaging, sequences, and targeting based on conversion data and response rates
- Stay informed about product updates, competitive positioning, and industry trends for effective prospecting conversations
- Develop deep understanding of target personas, their challenges, and how the product addresses their needs
- Participate in sales team meetings and training sessions for skill development and pipeline alignment
- Support account executives with account research and initial discovery for passed opportunities
- Manage lead handoff process ensuring smooth transition from BDR qualification to AE pipeline stage

## Core Competencies
- Strong communication and active listening skills for effective phone and written outreach
- Resilience and persistence in high-volume prospecting with ability to handle rejection
- Familiarity with sales engagement platforms (SalesLoft, Outreach, Gong, Groove, Mixmax)
- CRM proficiency (Salesforce, HubSpot) for activity logging and pipeline management
- Product and market knowledge for credible prospect conversations
- Time management and pipeline organization skills for managing multiple prospect lists
- Social selling skills for LinkedIn prospecting and professional networking
- Research skills for account intelligence and personalized outreach
- Coachability and willingness to iterate on messaging and approach

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Qualified meeting set rate per week and per month
- Pipeline sourced from prospecting activities (influence on sourced pipeline)
- Lead response time and meeting show rate
- Activity metrics (calls made, emails sent, LinkedIn touches per day)
- Conversion rate at each prospecting stage (connect to meeting, meeting to accepted opportunity)
- Booked revenue influence (sourced pipeline that converts to closed-won)
- Quota attainment percentage (meetings set vs. target)

## Career Progression
- BDR (0-1 years): Learns the role, develops prospecting skills, builds pipeline, meets activity targets
- Senior BDR (1-2 years): Consistently exceeds targets, handles complex accounts, develops specialization
- Lead BDR (2-3 years): Top performer, mentors junior BDRs, contributes to process improvements
- Promotion path: BDR to Account Executive or BDR Manager or Sales Development Manager
- AE transition: Strong BDRs promoted to AE after 1-3 years depending on performance and readiness
- Manager track: BDR Manager leads teams of 5-15 BDRs, manages coaching and pipeline generation
- Typical progression: BDR role is 1-3 years before promotion to closing role or management

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Morning prospect research and account prioritization for the day's outreach
- High-volume outbound calling blocks (typically 40-60 calls per day)
- Personalized email and LinkedIn outreach following sequence cadences
- CRM activity logging and meeting scheduling with qualified prospects
- Mid-day team huddle for pipeline review, messaging refinement, and coaching
- Afternoon follow-up on inbound leads and marketing-generated inquiries
- Weekly 1:1 with manager for pipeline review, skill coaching, and development
- Prospect list management and territory planning aligned with AEs
- Regular training sessions on product, positioning, objection handling

## Regional Nuances
- NA: High-volume prospecting culture; cold calling still effective in many industries; strong LinkedIn Sales Navigator adoption; competitive BDR compensation with significant variable component; rapid promotion cycles
- EMEA: GDPR restricts cold email and phone outreach; consent-based prospecting essential; multi-language BDR requirement in some regions; longer sales cycles affect BDR-to-meeting conversion
- APAC: Relationship-first selling; phone and in-person prospecting valued; WhatsApp and WeChat prospecting in some markets; BDR role less established in traditional markets; India has large BDR talent pools
- LATAM: Growing BDR function; Spanish and Portuguese language requirement; relationship-driven prospecting; mobile-first outreach approach; cost-effective BDR talent pool

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: Product knowledge critical for credible outreach; trial-based prospecting angles; competitive displacement prospecting; usage data as prospecting hook
- Fintech: Compliance-aware prospecting; regulated industry knowledge required; trust-building in initial outreach; longer qualification cycles
- Healthcare: Provider and payer prospecting complexity; regulatory awareness needed; relationship-based healthcare selling; conference-driven prospecting
- Enterprise Software: Multi-stakeholder account prospecting; longer sales cycles; executive-level outreach skills; complex organization navigation

## Common Challenges
- Managing high call and email volume while maintaining personalization quality
- Overcoming rejection fatigue and maintaining motivation in a results-driven role
- Getting past gatekeepers and reaching decision-makers in target accounts
- Coordinating territory coverage with multiple account executives effectively
- Adapting to product and market changes that affect prospecting messaging
- Balancing lead quality with meeting volume targets

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- CAN-SPAM Act for commercial email prospecting
- GDPR for EMEA prospecting and consent requirements
- CCPA for California prospect data handling
- TCPA for phone prospecting compliance in the US
- Do Not Call (DNC) registry compliance for cold calling
- LinkedIn terms of service for social selling
- Industry-specific compliance for prospecting in regulated sectors
