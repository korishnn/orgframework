# art-gallery-owner

**Summary:** Operates an art gallery, representing artists and selling art while managing business operations, exhibitions, and client relationships.

**Sector:** Museums & Galleries

Key responsibilities:
- Identify and represent a roster of artists aligned with gallery vision
- Plan and execute exhibition schedules and opening receptions
- Manage art sales, pricing, and client relationships
- Oversee gallery finances including budgets, rent, and payroll
- Build network of collectors, curators, and art world professionals
- Represent the gallery at art fairs and industry events

Core competencies:
- Deep knowledge of contemporary art market and collector base
- Business management and entrepreneurial skills
- Client relationship development and sales expertise
- Curation and exhibition planning capabilities
- Networking and promotion within the art community

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Artist, Curator, Collector, Art Dealer, Art Critic, Gallery Director, Art Fair Organizer
