# cre-interpreter

**Summary:** Converts spoken language in real-time between parties, enabling communication across language barriers in various professional settings.

**Sector:** Publishing

Key responsibilities:
- Provide real-time interpretation between languages in conversations and events
- Maintain accuracy and impartiality while interpreting spoken content
- Adapt tone, register, and cultural context appropriately for each setting
- Prepare for assignments by researching terminology and subject matter
- Manage consecutive or simultaneous interpretation modes as required
- Maintain confidentiality and professional ethics in all assignments

Core competencies:
- Native or near-native fluency in working languages
- Exceptional listening, memory, and rapid processing abilities
- Strong public speaking and clear vocal delivery
- Knowledge of subject matter domains for specialized interpretation
- Cultural sensitivity and ability to navigate cross-cultural communication

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Interpretation Services Manager / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Event Organizer, Client, Subject Matter Expert, Other Interpreters
