# hlth-radiologist

**Summary:** Interprets medical images to diagnose conditions.

**Sector:** Allied Health

Key responsibilities:
- Interpret X-rays, CT scans, MRI, ultrasound, and nuclear medicine studies
- Provide written diagnostic reports to referring physicians
- Perform image-guided procedures such as biopsies and drain placements
- Consult with referring clinicians on appropriate imaging protocols
- Ensure image quality and adherence to radiation safety standards
- Participate in multidisciplinary case conferences and tumor boards
- Maintain expertise in evolving imaging technologies and protocols

Core competencies:
- Diagnostic image interpretation across modalities
- Cross-sectional anatomy and pathology recognition
- Image-guided interventional procedure skills
- Radiation safety and dose optimization
- PACS and radiology information system proficiency
- Communication of findings to referring clinicians

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief of Radiology / Medical Director
- Collaborates with: Referring physicians, radiologic technologists, pathologists, surgeons, oncologists
