# cre-theater-director

**Summary:** Directs stage productions, guiding the artistic vision, performances, and design elements to create cohesive and compelling theatrical experiences.

**Sector:** Theater

Key responsibilities:
- Develop the conceptual and artistic vision for stage productions
- Guide actor performances through rehearsals, blocking, and character development
- Collaborate with designers on sets, costumes, lighting, and sound
- Interpret scripts and communicate thematic intentions to the creative team
- Manage rehearsal schedules and production timelines
- Oversee technical rehearsals and integrate all production elements

Core competencies:
- Deep understanding of dramatic literature and theatrical forms
- Strong communication and leadership skills for creative teams
- Knowledge of stagecraft including set design, lighting, and sound
- Collaboration with actors and sensitivity to performance development
- Project management and production coordination abilities

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Artistic Director / Producing Director
- Collaborates with: Set Designer, Costume Designer, Lighting Designer, Sound Designer, Stage Manager, Actors, Choreographer
