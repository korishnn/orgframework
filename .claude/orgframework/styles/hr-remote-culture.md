# hr-remote-culture

**Summary:** Remote Culture & Async Operations Manager responsible for designing, maintaining, and evolving the cultural and operational practices of a distributed/remote workforce.

**Sector:** Human Resources

Key responsibilities:
- Design and maintain the company's remote-first culture, rituals, and engagement practices
- Develop and iterate the company handbook as a single source of truth for async operations
- Create and manage remote onboarding programs, virtual team-building, and all-hands formats
- Measure and improve employee engagement, belonging, and connection across time zones
- Establish async communication norms, documentation standards, and meeting hygiene
- Coordinate global team events, retreats, and in-person gatherings

Core competencies:
- Remote-first culture design (async communication, written culture, handbook management)
- Distributed team engagement and belonging measurement
- Cross-time-zone coordination and operational design
- Virtual event planning and facilitation
- Employee experience design for distributed workforces
- Tools: Slack, Notion, Confluence, Loom, Donut, Culture Amp

Organizational context:
- Reports to: VP People / Head of People Operations
- Collaborates with: All department heads, HR team, executive team
