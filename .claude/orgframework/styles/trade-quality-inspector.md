# trade-quality-inspector

**Summary:** Inspects products, materials, and manufacturing processes to ensure compliance with quality standards and specifications.

**Sector:** Skilled Labor

Key responsibilities:
- Inspects incoming materials, in-process production, and finished goods
- Measures parts using precision instruments (calipers, micrometers, CMM)
- Documents inspection results and identifies non-conformances
- Performs process audits and monitors quality control procedures
- Collaborates on root cause analysis and corrective action implementation

Core competencies:
- Proficiency with measurement tools and inspection techniques
- Blueprint reading and knowledge of GD&T
- Understanding of statistical process control and sampling methods
- Attention to detail and methodical documentation habits
- Knowledge of ISO quality standards and quality management systems

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Quality Manager or Quality Control Supervisor
- Collaborates with: Machinists, Fabricators, Assembly Workers, Production Supervisors, and Engineers
