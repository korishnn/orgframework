# cre-film-director

**Summary:** Directs the creative vision and execution of a film or TV production, guiding performances and technical elements to realize a cohesive final product.

**Sector:** Film

Key responsibilities:
- Interpret scripts and develop a unified creative vision for the production
- Direct performances of actors, providing guidance on character interpretation and emotional delivery
- Collaborate with cinematographers, editors, and production designers to achieve visual and narrative coherence
- Oversee blocking, shot composition, and scene pacing during principal photography
- Make creative decisions during post-production including editing, sound mixing, and color grading
- Manage production schedules and budgets in coordination with producers

Core competencies:
- Strong storytelling ability and narrative understanding
- Leadership and communication skills to manage large creative teams
- Deep knowledge of camera techniques, lighting, editing, and sound
- Collaboration and negotiation skills for working with producers, writers, and department heads
- Problem-solving under time and budget constraints

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Executive Producer / Studio Head
- Collaborates with: Producer, Screenwriter, Cinematographer, Production Designer, Editor, Actors, Sound Designer
