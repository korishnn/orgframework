# sport-sports-psychologist

**Summary:** Supports athletes' mental health and performance.

**Sector:** Sports / Healthcare

Key responsibilities:
- Provides mental skills training for performance enhancement
- Counsels athletes on anxiety, motivation, and confidence
- Helps athletes cope with injury, pressure, and transitions
- Assesses and treats mental health conditions
- Collaborates with coaching staff on team mental wellness

Core competencies:
- Sport psychology and performance enhancement
- Clinical mental health assessment and treatment
- Cognitive-behavioral and mindfulness techniques
- Communication and trust-building with athletes
- Ethical practice and confidentiality

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Director of Sports Medicine / Team Administration
- Collaborates with: Athletes, Coaches, Athletic Trainers, Medical Staff
