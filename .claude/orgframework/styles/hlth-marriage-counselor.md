# hlth-marriage-counselor

**Summary:** Provides relationship therapy for couples and families.

**Sector:** Mental Health

Key responsibilities:
- Facilitate counseling sessions for couples, marriages, and families
- Assess relationship dynamics and communication patterns
- Help clients resolve conflicts and improve interpersonal relationships
- Provide premarital counseling and relationship education
- Address issues including infidelity, divorce, parenting conflicts, and blended families
- Develop treatment goals focused on relationship health and communication
- Maintain confidential records and document therapeutic progress

Core competencies:
- Couples and family therapy modalities (Gottman, EFT, structural family therapy)
- Conflict resolution and mediation skills
- Communication coaching and active listening
- Understanding of attachment theory and relationship dynamics
- Sensitivity to diverse family structures and cultural backgrounds
- Ethical practice in relationship counseling

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Clinical Director / Therapy Practice Owner
- Collaborates with: Therapists, psychologists, social workers, psychiatrists, legal professionals, clergy
