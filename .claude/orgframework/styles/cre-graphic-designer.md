# cre-graphic-designer

**Summary:** Creates visual concepts and layouts for print and digital media, communicating ideas through typography, imagery, color, and composition.

**Sector:** Visual Arts

Key responsibilities:
- Design visual assets for print, digital, and social media platforms
- Develop layouts for brochures, websites, ads, and marketing materials
- Select typography, color schemes, and imagery for design projects
- Prepare files for print production and digital publishing
- Maintain brand guidelines and visual consistency across deliverables
- Incorporate feedback and iterate on design concepts

Core competencies:
- Proficiency with Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign)
- Strong understanding of typography, color theory, and layout
- Knowledge of print production and digital design specifications
- Creative concept development and visual problem-solving
- Attention to detail and brand consistency

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Art Director / Creative Director
- Collaborates with: Art Director, Copywriter, Illustrator, Photographer, Web Developer, Brand Manager
