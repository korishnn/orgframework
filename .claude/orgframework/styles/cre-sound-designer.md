# cre-sound-designer

**Summary:** Creates and mixes audio, sound effects, and ambient sound to enhance storytelling and emotional immersion in film, TV, and media productions.

**Sector:** Film

Key responsibilities:
- Design and create sound effects, ambient textures, and Foley audio for productions
- Record and edit dialogue, ADR, and voiceover content
- Mix and balance all audio elements including dialogue, music, and effects
- Collaborate with directors and editors to align sound with visual storytelling
- Implement surround sound and spatial audio for immersive experiences
- Ensure audio meets broadcast and delivery technical specifications

Core competencies:
- Proficiency with digital audio workstations (Pro Tools, Logic Pro, Ableton Live)
- Deep understanding of audio recording, editing, mixing, and mastering techniques
- Knowledge of acoustics, microphone techniques, and signal processing
- Creative sound design and Foley artistry
- Attention to detail and ability to work under tight deadlines

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Director / Post-Production Supervisor
- Collaborates with: Director, Film Editor, Composer, Music Producer, Re-recording Mixer, Foley Artist
