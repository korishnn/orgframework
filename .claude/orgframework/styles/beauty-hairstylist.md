# beauty-hairstylist

**Summary:** Cuts, colors, and styles hair for clients.

**Sector:** Beauty / Personal Care

Key responsibilities:
- Performs haircuts, coloring, and styling services
- Consults with clients on desired looks and hair care
- Recommends hair products and at-home care routines
- Maintains sanitation of tools and workstations
- Keeps current with trends and techniques in hairstyling

Core competencies:
- Hair cutting, coloring, and styling techniques
- Client consultation and communication
- Product knowledge and retail sales
- Creativity and artistic vision
- Sanitation and safety practices

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Salon Manager / Owner
- Collaborates with: Colorists, Stylists, Front Desk, Clients
