# sport-athlete

**Summary:** Competes professionally in organized sports.

**Sector:** Sports

Key responsibilities:
- Trains consistently to maintain peak physical condition
- Compares in scheduled games, matches, or events
- Studies opponents and game strategies
- Follows team rules, training regimens, and travel schedules
- Engages with media, fans, and community events

Core competencies:
- Sport-specific technical skills and athleticism
- Physical fitness and injury prevention
- Teamwork and collaboration
- Discipline and time management
- Resilience and mental toughness

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Head Coach / Team Management
- Collaborates with: Teammates, Coaches, Athletic Trainers, Strength Coaches
