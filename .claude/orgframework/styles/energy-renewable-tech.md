# energy-renewable-tech

**Summary:** Installs and maintains renewable energy systems such as solar panels and wind turbines.

**Sector:** Energy

Key responsibilities:
- Installs solar photovoltaic panels and wind turbine systems
- Performs maintenance and repairs on renewable equipment
- Conducts site assessments and system performance testing
- Troubleshoots electrical and mechanical system issues
- Ensures compliance with electrical codes and safety standards

Core competencies:
- Electrical and mechanical installation skills
- Knowledge of renewable energy technologies
- Troubleshooting and diagnostic abilities
- Safety practices and fall protection
- Blueprint reading and system design understanding

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Installation Manager or Service Supervisor
- Collaborates with: Project Managers, Engineers, Utility Companies, Customers
