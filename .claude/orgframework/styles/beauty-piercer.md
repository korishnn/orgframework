# beauty-piercer

**Summary:** Performs body piercings in a safe and sanitary manner.

**Sector:** Beauty / Personal Care

Key responsibilities:
- Performs body piercings including ear, facial, and body placements
- Maintains sterile instruments and a sanitary piercing environment
- Educates clients on aftercare, healing, and jewelry care
- Selects appropriate jewelry for anatomy and piercing type
- Advises on placement and piercing suitability

Core competencies:
- Piercing techniques and placement knowledge
- Sterilization and infection control protocols
- Anatomy knowledge
- Client consultation and aftercare education
- Professional ethics and safety compliance

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Shop Manager / Studio Owner / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Tattoo Artists, Front Desk, Clients
