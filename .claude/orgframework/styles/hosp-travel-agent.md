# hosp-travel-agent

**Summary:** Books travel arrangements including flights, hotels, and tours.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Books flights, hotels, car rentals, and travel packages for clients
- Researches and recommends destinations and itineraries
- Manages travel documentation including visas and passports
- Handles changes, cancellations, and travel issues
- Maintains relationships with travel suppliers and vendors

Core competencies:
- Travel booking systems and GDS proficiency
- Destination and product knowledge
- Customer service and consultation
- Attention to detail and accuracy
- Problem-solving during travel disruptions

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Travel Agency Manager / Owner
- Collaborates with: Airlines, Hotels, Tour Operators, Clients
