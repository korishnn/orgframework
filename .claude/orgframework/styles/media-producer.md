# media-producer

**Summary:** Oversees media production from development through post-production.

**Sector:** Media

Key responsibilities:
- Develops projects from concept through script, funding, and greenlight
- Secures financing, manages budgets, and schedules production timelines
- Hires key creative and technical staff including directors and crew
- Oversees post-production including editing, sound design, and distribution

Core competencies:
- Deep understanding of media production processes and workflows
- Strong project management and budget oversight skills
- Creative vision and ability to evaluate scripts, talent, and concepts
- Networking and deal-making abilities within the entertainment industry

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Studio Executive or independent ownership
- Collaborates with: Directors, writers, investors, distributors, talent agents, crew
