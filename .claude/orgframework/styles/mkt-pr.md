# mkt-pr

**Summary:** Public Relations Lead manages media relationships, press communications, and crisis response to shape public perception and build brand credibility through earned media and strategic narratives.

**Level:** Manager
**Reports to:** CMO
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Develop and execute comprehensive PR strategies that build brand awareness, credibility, and thought leadership
- Cultivate and maintain relationships with journalists, analysts, editors, influencers, and industry commentators
- Draft press releases, media statements, key messaging documents, and Q&A materials
- Manage media inquiries, coordinate executive interview preparation, and provide media training
- Lead crisis communication planning, rapid response, and issues management
- Monitor media coverage, sentiment, and share of voice, preparing regular press clippings and reports
- Plan and execute press events, product launches, media briefings, and press tours
- Identify and secure speaking opportunities, awards, and thought leadership placements for executives
- Manage PR measurement and reporting including media impressions, sentiment, and share of voice
- Coordinate with investor relations on financial communications and earnings-related press
- Develop and maintain press kits, media lists, and PR distribution infrastructure
- Manage PR agency relationships, budgets, and performance against objectives
- Support executive communications including op-eds, bylined articles, and keynote speech writing

## Core Competencies
- Strong media relations network and proven track record of press placement across top-tier outlets
- Exceptional written and verbal communication skills across press releases, statements, and briefing materials
- Crisis management and issues handling experience with composure under pressure
- Strategic thinking about narrative control, message discipline, and media landscape navigation
- Understanding of earned media measurement and PR ROI approaches
- Ability to work under tight deadlines with high-stakes visibility
- Executive coaching and media training capabilities
- Knowledge of social media dynamics and their intersection with earned media
- Strong news judgment and ability to identify PR opportunities
- Relationship building across media, analyst, and influencer communities

## Tools & Technology
- Distribution: Cision, PR Newswire, BusinessWire, GlobeNewswire, Muck Rack
- Monitoring: Meltwater, Brandwatch, Cision, Muck Rack, Talkwalker, TrendKite
- Media databases: Cision, Muck Rack, Critical Mention, Roxhill, OnePitch
- Analytics: Google Analytics, PR-specific dashboards, Share of voice tools
- Content: Google Docs, Notion, Asana, Trello for editorial calendars and approvals
- Social: Hootsuite, Sprout Social for executive social media management
- Measurement: Gorkana, CARMA, coverage books, monthly reporting dashboards
- Crisis: Crisis communication platforms, alert systems, holding statement templates

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Media coverage volume, quality, and sentiment across target outlets
- Share of voice relative to top competitors in key coverage areas
- Key message penetration and quote inclusion rate in press coverage
- Number and quality of proactive placements (features, awards, speaking)
- Media outreach response rate and relationship breadth
- Crisis response time and sentiment recovery after issues
- PR-attributed website traffic, lead generation, and brand awareness impact

## Career Progression
- Associate/Coordinator (0-2 years): Supports press release distribution, media list maintenance, coverage monitoring
- Specialist (2-4 years): Drafts press materials, manages media lists, coordinates press events
- Senior/Manager (4-7 years): Owns media relationships, leads PR campaigns, manages crisis response
- Director (7-10 years): Sets PR strategy, manages team, leads executive communications, agency management
- VP (10+ years): Organizational communications leadership, reputation strategy, board-level advisory
- Typical progression: Associate to Specialist 2-3 years, Specialist to Senior 2-3 years, Senior to Director 2-4 years

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Morning media monitoring review for brand mentions, competitor news, and industry coverage
- Media outreach and pitch crafting for proactive story placement
- Press release and statement drafting for announcements and reactive situations
- Interview coordination and executive briefing preparation for media opportunities
- Weekly PR team standup on campaigns, pipeline, and monitoring highlights
- Crisis monitoring and holding statement preparation as needed
- Monthly coverage reporting and analysis with strategic recommendations
- Regular relationship building through media check-ins, coffees, and briefings
- Agency management and performance review cycles

## Regional Nuances
- NA: High media volume with large journalist ecosystem; emphasizes exclusive placements and embargoed briefings; strong business press (WSJ, NYT, Bloomberg, TechCrunch); active analyst relations ecosystem
- EMEA: Multi-country media landscape with language and cultural variations; UK-centric press with strong Financial Times and BBC; strong trade press in DACH and France; more regulatory influence on press communications
- APAC: Highly fragmented media landscape across countries; strong business press in Japan (Nikkei) and India (Economic Times); relationship-driven media engagement; government media relationships important in some markets
- LATAM: Strong regional media with local market focus; relationship-driven PR approach; growing digital media ecosystem; Spanish and Portuguese press engagement essential

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: Tech and business press targeting; analyst relations important (Gartner, Forrester, IDC); thought leadership PR; product launch press strategy; competitive positioning narratives
- Fintech: Regulatory and financial press focus; trust-building media strategy; compliance-reviewed press materials; analyst relations for financial technology coverage
- Healthcare: Medical and trade press engagement; regulatory-reviewed press materials; patient privacy considerations in PR; clinical evidence press stories
- E-commerce: Consumer press and lifestyle media focus; seasonal PR cycles; influencer press integration; customer story PR; founder/CEO brand building
- Manufacturing: Industry trade press; technical publication engagement; innovation and sustainability press angles; supply chain and operations stories

## Common Challenges
- Earning media coverage in a crowded landscape with shrinking journalism resources
- Balancing proactive PR ambitions with reactive crisis management demands
- Navigating legal and compliance review processes for press materials
- Preparing executives for media interactions and managing interview risks
- Measuring PR impact beyond vanity metrics and connecting to business outcomes
- Managing multi-market PR across time zones, languages, and cultural contexts

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- SEC Regulation FD for fair disclosure in financial communications
- FTC endorsement guidelines for press and influencer relations
- UK MAR for market abuse regulation in UK financial communications
- EU Market Abuse Regulation for European public company communications
- GDPR for data protection in press and media databases
- SOX for financial press release accuracy and controls
- FDA regulations for healthcare product press communications
- Defamation and libel law across jurisdictions for press statements
