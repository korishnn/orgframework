# sci-oceanographer

**Summary:** Studies oceans and marine systems.

**Sector:** Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Conduct research on ocean physics, chemistry, biology, and geology
- Collect water, sediment, and biological samples from marine environments
- Deploy and maintain oceanographic instruments and sensors
- Analyze data to understand ocean circulation, ecosystems, and climate
- Model ocean processes and contribute to climate change research

Core competencies:
- Oceanographic field methods and instrumentation
- Marine data analysis and modeling
- Knowledge of marine ecosystems and ocean dynamics
- Seagoing research experience and safety protocols

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Research Director / Principal Investigator
- Collaborates with: Marine biologists, Climate scientists, Government agencies, Academic institutions
