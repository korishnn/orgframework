# avi-flight-engineer

**Summary:** Monitors and manages aircraft systems including engines, hydraulics, electrical, and pressurization during flight.

**Sector:** Aviation

Key responsibilities:
- Monitors engine performance, fuel systems, and aircraft instrumentation
- Manages electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, and pressurization systems
- Computes takeoff and landing performance data and power settings
- Troubleshoots in-flight system malfunctions using technical manuals
- Performs pre-flight and post-flight exterior and cockpit inspections

Core competencies:
- Flight Engineer certificate (FAA or equivalent) with type rating
- Deep knowledge of aircraft systems, schematics, and emergency procedures
- Mathematical ability for performance calculations
- Systematic troubleshooting and diagnostic skills
- Team coordination with pilots during normal and abnormal operations

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Captain (pilot-in-command)
- Collaborates with: Captain, First Officer, Aircraft Maintenance Technicians, and Dispatchers
