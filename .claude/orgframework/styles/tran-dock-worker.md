# tran-dock-worker

**Summary:** Loads and unloads cargo from ships at ports, operating cargo handling equipment and securing cargo for transport.

**Sector:** Maritime

Key responsibilities:
- Operates cranes, forklifts, and other cargo handling equipment
- Secures and releases cargo using lashings, chains, and twist locks
- Guides crane operators to land loads safely in holds and on decks
- Handles ship lines during docking and undocking operations
- Follows safety protocols for hazardous materials and heavy lifts

Core competencies:
- Proficiency with ship-to-shore cranes, reach stackers, and top loaders
- Knowledge of cargo handling, stowage, and lashing techniques
- Safety awareness for working around heavy machinery and ship operations
- Physical fitness for manual handling and climbing on vessels
- Communication with crane operators via hand signals and radio

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Stevedore Supervisor or Marine Terminal Manager
- Collaborates with: Ship Crews, Crane Operators, Checkers, and Terminal Planners
