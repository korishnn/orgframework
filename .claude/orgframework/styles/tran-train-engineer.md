# tran-train-engineer

**Summary:** Operates locomotives and controls train movement, speed, and braking for freight or passenger rail service.

**Sector:** Transportation

Key responsibilities:
- Operates locomotive controls including throttle, brakes, and dynamic braking
- Monitors track signals, speed limits, and track conditions
- Communicates with conductors and dispatchers via radio
- Performs locomotive pre-trip inspections and basic troubleshooting
- Responds to emergency situations and abnormal operating conditions

Core competencies:
- Locomotive handling and train dynamics knowledge
- Railroad signal system and operating rule proficiency
- Alertness and focus for extended periods of operation
- Mechanical aptitude for locomotive systems
- Clear communication under radio protocols

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Trainmaster or Road Foreman of Engines
- Collaborates with: Conductors, Dispatchers, Yard Masters, and Locomotive Mechanics
