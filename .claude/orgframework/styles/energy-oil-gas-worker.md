# energy-oil-gas-worker

**Summary:** Performs extraction, processing, or transport work in oil and gas operations.

**Sector:** Energy

Key responsibilities:
- Operate and monitor drilling, extraction, or processing equipment on rigs or facilities
- Perform routine maintenance, inspections, and repairs on valves, pumps, and piping
- Handle, sample, and transfer produced fluids following safety and environmental procedures
- Complete shift logs, equipment reports, and safety observations

Core competencies:
- Mechanical aptitude for rig systems, pressure vessels, and rotating equipment
- Strict adherence to hazardous-safety protocols including LOTO, H2S, and fire prevention
- Physical fitness and tolerance for remote, shift-based, and extreme-weather work conditions
- Team coordination and clear handover communication in high-noise or high-risk environments

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Rig manager, platform supervisor, or field operations foreman
- Collaborates with: Drilling engineers, maintenance crews, HSE specialists, logistics coordinators, and wellsite geologists
