# legal-judge

**Summary:** Presides over court proceedings, rules on motions, and issues judgments.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Presides over trials, hearings, and other court proceedings
- Rules on evidentiary objections, procedural motions, and legal arguments
- Issues written opinions, orders, and final judgments
- Instructs juries on applicable law and manages courtroom decorum

Core competencies:
- Juris Doctor degree and significant legal practice or judicial experience
- Comprehensive knowledge of constitutional, statutory, and case law
- Impartial decision-making and strong ethical grounding
- Excellent legal writing ability for clear, well-reasoned opinions

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief Judge or Judicial Nominating Commission
- Collaborates with: Judicial clerks, bailiffs, court reporters, attorneys, appellate judges
