# cre-music-director

**Summary:** Leads musical ensembles and directs performances, overseeing repertoire selection, artistic interpretation, and musical quality.

**Sector:** Music

Key responsibilities:
- Select repertoire and program concert seasons or performance sets
- Conduct rehearsals and performances with ensembles of various sizes
- Interpret scores and communicate artistic vision to musicians
- Manage audition processes and ensemble personnel decisions
- Coordinate with producers, venues, and artistic leadership on programming
- Develop educational and community engagement programming

Core competencies:
- Advanced conducting technique and score reading ability
- Deep knowledge of music literature across historical periods and genres
- Leadership and communication skills for directing ensembles
- Rehearsal planning and efficient time management
- Artistic programming and audience development insight

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Artistic Director / Board of Directors
- Collaborates with: Musicians, Composer, Producer, Artistic Staff, Venue Manager, Choral or Orchestral Librarian
