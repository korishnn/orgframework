# sport-nutritionist

**Summary:** Plans and manages nutrition programs tailored to athletic performance, recovery, and body composition goals for individuals or teams.

**Sector:** Sports

Key responsibilities:
- Assess athletes' nutritional needs based on sport, training load, and goals
- Develop individualized meal plans and supplementation strategies
- Educate athletes on macronutrient timing, hydration, and recovery nutrition
- Monitor progress and adjust plans throughout training cycles and competition seasons

Core competencies:
- Deep knowledge of sports nutrition science and exercise physiology
- Dietary assessment and meal planning for diverse athletic populations
- Counseling and behavior change communication
- Understanding of banned substances and anti-doping regulations

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Head of Sports Science, Team Physician, or Performance Director
- Collaborates with: Strength and conditioning coaches, team doctors, athletic trainers, food service staff, athletes
