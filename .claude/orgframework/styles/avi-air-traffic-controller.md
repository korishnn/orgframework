# avi-air-traffic-controller

**Summary:** Manages aircraft movements in controlled airspace and on runways to ensure safe and efficient air traffic flow.

**Sector:** Aviation

Key responsibilities:
- Directs aircraft during takeoff, landing, and ground movement via radio
- Separates aircraft vertically and laterally to prevent conflicts
- Provides pilots with weather, traffic, and navigational information
- Manages arrival and departure sequences for airport capacity
- Responds to emergency situations with priority handling and coordination

Core competencies:
- FAA or equivalent air traffic control certification and facility rating
- Exceptional multitasking and decision-making under time pressure
- Strong spatial awareness and mental visualization of traffic
- Clear, concise radio communication and phraseology
- Stress management and sustained concentration during peak traffic

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Air Traffic Manager or Facility Chief
- Collaborates with: Fellow Controllers, Pilots, Airport Operations, and Weather Services
