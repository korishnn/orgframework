# edu-daycare-provider

**Summary:** Cares for young children in daycare or home settings.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Supervise children's daily activities and ensure their safety
- Provide meals, snacks, and basic care routines
- Plan and lead age-appropriate play and learning activities
- Support social development and positive peer interactions
- Communicate with parents about daily routines and child development

Core competencies:
- Child supervision and safety practices
- Early childhood nutrition and basic caregiving
- Patience, nurturing demeanor, and communication with parents
- Basic knowledge of child development stages

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Daycare Center Director / Licensing agency (or self-employed)
- Collaborates with: Other caregivers, Parents, Early childhood specialists
