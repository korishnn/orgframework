# edu-school-board-member

**Summary:** Governs school district policy and oversight.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Establish district policies and educational goals
- Approve the district budget and resource allocation
- Hire and evaluate the Superintendent
- Ensure accountability for student achievement and fiscal responsibility
- Represent the community's interests in educational decisions

Core competencies:
- Policy governance and strategic oversight
- Financial literacy and budget analysis
- Community engagement and public accountability
- Understanding of educational law and equity issues

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Constituents (public) / State education authorities
- Collaborates with: Superintendent, District administrators, Community members, Other board members
