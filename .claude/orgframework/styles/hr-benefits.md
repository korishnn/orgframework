# hr-benefits

**Summary:** Designs, administers, and continuously optimizes employee benefits programs including health insurance, retirement plans, wellness initiatives, and leave management to support a competitive total rewards strategy.

**Level:** IC
**Reports to:** HR Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Administer all employee benefits programs including medical, dental, vision, life insurance, disability, retirement plans, and voluntary benefits.
- Manage the annual open enrollment cycle including carrier selection, communication campaigns, enrollment system configuration, and employee education sessions.
- Process and track all leave of absence requests including FMLA, ADA accommodations, parental leave, short-term disability, long-term disability, and personal leaves.
- Serve as the primary point of contact for employee benefits inquiries, resolving issues related to claims, eligibility, coverage changes, and billing discrepancies.
- Evaluate and recommend changes to benefits offerings based on market benchmarking, employee feedback, utilization data, and cost analysis.
- Manage vendor relationships with insurance carriers, brokers, retirement plan administrators, wellness providers, and EAP vendors including annual contract renewals.
- Conduct new hire benefits orientation sessions and ensure timely enrollment within eligibility windows.
- Maintain benefits data accuracy in the HRIS including dependent verification, life event changes, and COBRA administration.
- Prepare and distribute required employee communications including Summary Plan Descriptions (SPDs), Summary of Benefits and Coverage (SBCs), and compliance notices.
- Manage wellness program initiatives including biometric screenings, health challenges, mental health resources, and fitness reimbursement programs.
- Reconcile monthly benefits billing against enrollment data and resolve discrepancies with carriers and finance.
- Stay current on benefits legislation changes (state and federal) and ensure plan compliance with ERISA, ACA, HIPAA, COBRA, and other applicable laws.
- Administer retirement plan including 401(k) matching, loan processing, distribution requests, and non-discrimination testing support.

## Core Competencies
- Benefits plan design and administration across health, wealth, and wellness programs
- Vendor management and broker relationship oversight
- Leave administration under FMLA, ADA, state PFML, and company policies
- Regulatory compliance with ERISA, ACA, HIPAA, COBRA, and SECURE Act
- Employee communication and education on complex benefits topics
- Data analysis, reporting, and cost modeling
- HRIS and benefits administration platform proficiency
- Empathy and customer service orientation in employee interactions
- Project management for open enrollment and program launches
- Financial acumen for budget management and cost forecasting
- Attention to detail in eligibility, enrollment, and billing reconciliation
- Problem-solving for claims disputes and coverage exceptions

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Employee benefits satisfaction score: annual survey rating of overall benefits program
- Open enrollment participation rate: percentage of eligible employees actively enrolling
- Benefits cost per employee: total premium cost divided by covered headcount
- Leave processing accuracy rate: percentage of leave requests processed without error
- Benefits inquiry response time: average time to first resolution on employee questions
- Wellness program utilization rate: percentage of employees participating in wellness initiatives
- Benefits compliance audit score: results from internal or external benefits compliance audit

## Career Progression
- Entry level: Benefits Coordinator handling enrollment processing, employee inquiries, billing reconciliation, and open enrollment logistics.
- Mid level: Benefits Specialist managing core programs independently, leading open enrollment workstreams, and resolving complex claims and eligibility issues.
- Senior level: Senior Benefits Analyst conducting market benchmarking, designing plan changes, modeling costs, and managing vendor relationships.
- Lead level: Benefits Program Manager owning the full benefits strategy, budget, compliance, and vendor governance.
- Manager level: Total Rewards Manager overseeing benefits alongside compensation, recognition, and wellness programs.
- Director level: Director of Total Rewards responsible for the entire compensation and benefits strategy across all regions.
- Timeline: 1-2 years as coordinator, 2-4 years as specialist, 2-3 years as senior, then program manager or management track.

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Morning: Respond to employee benefits inquiries (email and ticket system), process new hire enrollments and qualifying life events, review leave requests submitted overnight.
- Midday: Vendor calls on claims issues, billing discrepancies, or plan administration questions. Meet with employees on complex leave or accommodation cases.
- Afternoon: Reconcile monthly carrier invoices against enrollment data, update HRIS benefits records, prepare compliance filings, review plan documents for accuracy.
- Weekly: Leave case review with HR business partners, claims escalation follow-ups, benefits metric updates.
- Bi-weekly: Retirement plan administration tasks (loan processing, contribution corrections, distribution approvals).
- Monthly: Carrier invoice reconciliation and approval, benefits dashboard update, wellness program activity tracking.
- Quarterly: Benefits utilization reporting, market benchmarking review, compliance filing calendar check, wellness program effectiveness review.
- Annual peak: Open enrollment period (typically 4-8 weeks of intensive work including system configuration, communications, employee meetings, and post-enrollment audit).
- Rhythms: Steady inflow of employee questions creates daily reactive work balanced with proactive project timelines. Open enrollment and leave administration create predictable seasonal peaks.

## Regional Nuances
- NA: Health insurance is primarily employer-sponsored. ACA requires 95% of full-time employees receive affordable coverage. State-specific paid family leave (CA, NY, MA, WA, CO, OR, CT, etc.) creates complex multi-state compliance. 401(k) with employer match is the standard retirement vehicle.
- EMEA: Mandatory public health insurance in most countries with employer and employee contributions. Private supplemental insurance is common for executives. Statutory pension systems (state-run) supplemented by employer plans. Generous statutory parental leave in Nordic countries (up to 12+ months). Works council involvement in benefits design in Germany.
- APAC: India mandatory provident fund, employee state insurance, and gratuity. Australia superannuation guarantee at 11.5%. Singapore Central Provident Fund (CPF) with employer and employee contributions. China mandatory social insurance and housing fund with significant employer cost. Japan health insurance and pension enrollment through Shakai Hoken.
- LATAM: Brazil mandatory benefits include meal vouchers (vale-refeicao), transportation vouchers (vale-transporte), and private health insurance for most formal employees. Mexico IMSS and INFONAVIT contributions mandatory. Aguinaldo (13th salary) required. Argentina has mandatory severance fund (ANSES) contributions. Private health insurance is common as a retention tool.

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: Competitive equity and stock option programs are central. Generous remote work stipends and home office benefits. Mental health and wellness benefits are highly valued in recruitment.
- Fintech: Regulatory scrutiny on benefits vendor selection. Higher retirement plan matching to attract senior talent. Executive deferred compensation plans more common.
- Healthcare: Union-negotiated benefit packages in hospital settings. Mandatory benefits for shift differentials and on-call compensation. Student loan repayment assistance for clinical talent.
- Manufacturing: Multi-tier union and non-union benefit structures. Shift differentials built into benefits eligibility. On-site occupational health services. 401(k) with employer contribution typical.
- E-commerce: Part-time and seasonal worker benefit eligibility challenges. Creative benefit offerings for warehouse staff (tuition assistance, transportation). High turnover requires streamlined enrollment processes.

## Common Challenges
- Managing escalating healthcare costs while maintaining competitive benefit offerings.
- Navigating multi-state and multi-country compliance complexity as organizations expand geographically.
- Communicating complex benefits information to a diverse workforce with varying levels of financial literacy.
- Administering inconsistent state-level paid family leave laws across multiple operating locations.
- Balancing employee desire for personalized benefits with the administrative burden of too many options.
- Managing the transition of benefits for employees who work across international borders.

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- US: ERISA (plan documents, SPDs, Form 5500 filings), ACA (employer mandate, Section 6055/6056 reporting), HIPAA Privacy Rule (protected health information), COBRA (continuation coverage), FMLA (job-protected leave), SECURE Act 2.0 (retirement plan provisions), state PFML laws (CA, MA, NY, WA, CO, OR, CT, etc.), ADA reasonable accommodation.
- EU: GDPR (employee health data processing), EU Working Time Directive (paid leave minimums), Posted Workers Directive (cross-border benefits), country-specific statutory social security systems.
- UK: Pensions Act 2008 (auto-enrolment), Employment Rights Act 1996 (statutory maternity/paternity/sick pay), Equality Act 2010 (non-discrimination in benefits).
- Canada: Provincial employer health tax variations, Canada Pension Plan (CPP) and Quebec Pension Plan (QPP), provincial employment standards for leaves, Privacy Act for benefits data.
- Brazil: CLT benefits mandates (meal/transportation vouchers), LGPD for health data, ANSI regulation of health insurance plans.
- Australia: Superannuation Guarantee (11.5% employer contribution), Fair Work Act minimum leave entitlements, state workers compensation schemes.
