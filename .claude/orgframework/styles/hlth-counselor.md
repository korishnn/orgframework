# hlth-counselor

**Summary:** Offers guidance and support for personal, social, and psychological challenges.

**Sector:** Mental Health

Key responsibilities:
- Provide individual and group counseling sessions for clients
- Help clients identify goals and develop coping strategies
- Address issues such as stress, anxiety, grief, relationship problems, and life transitions
- Assess client needs and make appropriate referrals for specialized care
- Develop personalized treatment plans and monitor progress
- Maintain confidential client records in compliance with regulations
- Facilitate support groups and psychoeducational workshops

Core competencies:
- Active listening and empathic communication
- Counseling techniques and therapeutic interventions
- Crisis counseling and suicide prevention
- Knowledge of community resources and referral networks
- Goal setting and motivational interviewing
- Documentation and ethical record-keeping

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Clinical Supervisor / Counseling Center Director
- Collaborates with: Therapists, social workers, psychologists, psychiatrists, school staff, community organizations
