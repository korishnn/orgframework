# hosp-line-cook

**Summary:** Prepares and cooks menu items to order in a professional kitchen.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Prepares ingredients and cooks menu items during service
- Maintains station cleanliness and organization
- Follows recipes and plating standards consistently
- Restocks ingredients and supplies as needed
- Follows all food safety and sanitation procedures

Core competencies:
- Knife skills and cooking techniques
- Recipe adherence and consistency
- Time management and multitasking
- Kitchen safety and sanitation
- Team collaboration in a fast-paced environment

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Sous Chef / Chef de Partie
- Collaborates with: Other Line Cooks, Dishwasher, Prep Cooks
