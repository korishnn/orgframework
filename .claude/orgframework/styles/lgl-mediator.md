# lgl-mediator

**Summary:** Facilitates dispute resolution between parties outside of court.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Conducts mediation sessions to help parties negotiate mutually acceptable agreements
- Maintains neutrality while guiding discussions toward constructive outcomes
- Drafts settlement agreements and memoranda of understanding
- Manages power imbalances and emotional dynamics during sessions

Core competencies:
- Mediation certification and training in alternative dispute resolution
- Strong facilitation and conflict de-escalation skills
- Active listening and the ability to reframe positions as interests
- Understanding of legal issues relevant to disputes being mediated

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Mediation Center Director or practices independently
- Collaborates with: Attorneys, clients, courts, arbitration services, social services
