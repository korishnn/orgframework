# hosp-wedding-planner

**Summary:** Coordinates all aspects of wedding planning and execution.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Consults with couples to plan wedding vision and details
- Coordinates vendors including caterers, florists, and photographers
- Manages wedding budgets and timelines
- Oversees venue setup, ceremony, and reception logistics
- Serves as point of contact on the wedding day

Core competencies:
- Wedding industry knowledge and vendor network
- Event coordination and timeline management
- Client relationship management
- Creative design and vision execution
- Crisis management and problem-solving

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Events Director / Agency Owner
- Collaborates with: Venues, Caterers, Florists, Photographers, Couples
