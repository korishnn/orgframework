# govt-ems-dispatcher

**Summary:** Coordinates emergency response dispatch for police, fire, and medical services.

**Sector:** Government

Key responsibilities:
- Answers emergency and non-emergency calls for service
- Prioritizes incidents and dispatches appropriate responders
- Provides pre-arrival instructions to callers
- Monitors responder locations and status in real time
- Maintains accurate incident logs and communication records

Core competencies:
- Call triage and priority assessment
- Multitasking under high-pressure conditions
- Clear verbal communication and active listening
- Geographic knowledge of service area
- Emergency medical dispatch certification

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Dispatch Supervisor or Communications Center Manager
- Collaborates with: Police Officers, Firefighters, EMS Personnel, Emergency Management
