# edu-music-teacher

**Summary:** Teaches music skills in school or private settings.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Teach music theory, history, and performance skills
- Direct school ensembles such as choir, band, or orchestra
- Plan and coordinate student performances and recitals
- Assess student progress in musicianship and technique
- Select and maintain repertoire and musical instruments

Core competencies:
- Proficiency in one or more instruments or voice
- Knowledge of music pedagogy and curriculum development
- Ensemble direction and rehearsal techniques
- Performance planning and event coordination

Organizational context:
- Reports to: School Principal / Fine Arts Director (or self-employed)
- Collaborates with: Other music faculty, General classroom teachers, Parents, Community arts organizations
