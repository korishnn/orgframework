# sci-postdoc

**Summary:** Conducts advanced research training after PhD completion.

**Sector:** Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Lead independent research projects within the host lab
- Publish findings in high-impact peer-reviewed journals
- Mentor graduate and undergraduate students in the lab
- Present research at national and international conferences
- Develop grant writing skills and seek fellowship funding

Core competencies:
- Advanced research and experimental design skills
- Scientific writing and publication track record
- Independent project management and problem-solving
- Mentoring and collaboration abilities

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Principal Investigator / Lab Director
- Collaborates with: Research scientists, Graduate students, Research assistants, External collaborators
