# media-radio-host

**Summary:** Hosts radio programs, interviews guests, and curates content.

**Sector:** Media

Key responsibilities:
- Hosts live or recorded radio shows including music, talk, and specialty formats
- Selects and sequences music, segments, and content for broadcast
- Conducts interviews with guests, callers, and experts on air
- Engages audience through social media, contests, and community events

Core competencies:
- Strong verbal communication and engaging on-air personality
- Knowledge of music, current events, or talk format subject matter
- Proficiency with broadcast consoles, editing software, and production tools
- Ad-lib skills and ability to handle technical issues gracefully

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Program Director or Station Manager
- Collaborates with: Producers, engineers, music directors, promotions teams, news staff
