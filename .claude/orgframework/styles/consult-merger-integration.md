# consult-merger-integration

**Summary:** Manages post-merger integration of organizations, systems, and cultures.

**Sector:** Consulting

Key responsibilities:
- Develops integration strategies and program plans
- Manages day-one readiness and transition activities
- Coordinates functional workstreams across organizations
- Identifies synergy targets and tracks realization
- Addresses cultural integration and organizational alignment

Core competencies:
- Merger and acquisition lifecycle knowledge
- Integration planning and program management
- Cross-functional coordination and governance
- Financial analysis and synergy tracking
- Cultural assessment and change management

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Integration Program Director or Partner
- Collaborates with: Client Executives, Legal Teams, HR, Finance, IT, Operations Leads
