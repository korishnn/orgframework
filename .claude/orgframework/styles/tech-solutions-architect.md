# tech-solutions-architect

**Summary:** Designs technical solutions and architectures that address client needs, balancing requirements, constraints, and best practices across the technology stack.

**Sector:** Technology

Key responsibilities:
- Gather and analyze client requirements to design end-to-end technical solutions
- Create architecture diagrams, trade-off analyses, and implementation roadmaps
- Lead technical discussions, demonstrations, and proof-of-concept development
- Provide guidance on technology selection, scalability, security, and cost optimization

Core competencies:
- Broad knowledge across infrastructure, application, data, and security domains
- Cloud platform expertise (AWS, Azure, GCP) and multi-architecture design
- Strong written and verbal communication for technical and non-technical audiences
- Estimation, scoping, and proposal development for client engagements

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Director of Solutions Architecture or CTO
- Collaborates with: Sales and account teams, engineering, product management, clients and stakeholders
