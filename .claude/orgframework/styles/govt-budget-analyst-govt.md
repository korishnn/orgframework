# govt-budget-analyst-govt

**Summary:** Analyzes government budgets and develops fiscal recommendations.

**Sector:** Government

Key responsibilities:
- Review agency budget requests and align them with strategic priorities and revenue forecasts
- Prepare budget justifications, appropriations language, and fiscal notes for legislation
- Monitor spending against enacted budgets and identify variances requiring corrective action
- Model the fiscal impact of proposed policies, programs, and revenue changes

Core competencies:
- Advanced financial analysis and public-sector budgeting methodologies
- Knowledge of government appropriations law, fund accounting, and fiscal reporting standards
- Written and verbal communication for presenting complex budget data to decision-makers
- Proficiency with budget systems, spreadsheets, and data visualization tools

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Budget director, chief financial officer, or agency head
- Collaborates with: Program managers, legislative budget staff, auditors, and treasury or finance officials
