# media-weather-reporter

**Summary:** Reports and explains weather forecasts on broadcast media.

**Sector:** Media

Key responsibilities:
- Analyzes meteorological data and models to produce accurate forecasts
- Presents weather segments on television, radio, or digital platforms
- Provides severe weather coverage including storm tracking and safety information
- Creates weather graphics and visualizations for broadcast

Core competencies:
- Degree in meteorology or atmospheric science (AMS certification preferred)
- Strong on-camera presentation and communication skills
- Proficiency with weather modeling software and radar interpretation
- Ability to explain complex weather phenomena to general audiences

Organizational context:
- Reports to: News Director or Chief Meteorologist
- Collaborates with: News anchors, producers, emergency management, National Weather Service
