# agri-rancher

**Summary:** Manages livestock operations on ranch land for meat, dairy, or wool production.

**Sector:** Agriculture

Key responsibilities:
- Manages breeding, feeding, and herd health programs
- Maintains grazing lands and rotational grazing systems
- Oversees calving, branding, and livestock handling
- Maintains ranch infrastructure and fencing
- Markets and sells livestock and animal products

Core competencies:
- Animal husbandry and herd management
- Range and pasture management
- Livestock health and nutrition knowledge
- Equipment operation and maintenance
- Business and financial management

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Self-employed or Ranch Owner
- Collaborates with: Veterinarians, Feed Suppliers, Livestock Brokers, Land Management Agencies
