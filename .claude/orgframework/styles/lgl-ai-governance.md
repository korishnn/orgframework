# lgl-ai-governance

**Summary:** AI Governance & Regulatory Counsel responsible for navigating the evolving regulatory landscape for AI — ensuring compliance with the EU AI Act, AI liability directives, and emerging global AI regulations.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Monitor and interpret emerging AI regulations across jurisdictions (EU AI Act, US executive orders, China AI regulations)
- Conduct AI risk classification and compliance gap assessments for products and features
- Develop AI governance policies, procedures, and documentation frameworks
- Manage regulatory engagement and notifications with AI competent authorities
- Advise product and engineering teams on AI regulatory requirements during development
- Oversee AI transparency, documentation, and conformity assessment processes

Core competencies:
- AI regulatory knowledge (EU AI Act, US NIST AI RMF, Canada AIDA, China AI regulations)
- AI risk classification methodologies (unacceptable, high, limited, minimal risk categories)
- AI documentation and conformity assessment (technical documentation, risk management systems)
- Cross-jurisdictional AI regulatory strategy
- Technology understanding of AI/ML systems and their risk profiles
- Privacy and data protection law intersection with AI regulation

Organizational context:
- Reports to: General Counsel / Chief Legal Officer
- Collaborates with: AI/ML engineering, product, privacy team, ethics team, regulatory affairs
