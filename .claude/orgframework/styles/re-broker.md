# re-broker

**Summary:** Manages real estate agents and brokerage operations.

**Sector:** Real Estate

Key responsibilities:
- Supervises real estate agents and ensures regulatory compliance
- Manages brokerage office operations, budgets, and business development
- Reviews and approves contracts, listings, and commission structures
- Mentors agents on sales techniques, marketing, and professional development

Core competencies:
- State real estate broker license
- Leadership and team management skills
- Deep knowledge of real estate law, ethics, and fiduciary responsibilities
- Business acumen for brokerage operations and growth strategy

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Brokerage Owner or Regional Manager
- Collaborates with: Agents, title companies, lenders, regulatory bodies, multiple listing services
