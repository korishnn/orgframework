# consult-implementation-lead

**Summary:** Leads execution of consulting recommendations and manages organizational change programs.

**Sector:** Consulting

Key responsibilities:
- Manages implementation of consulting recommendations
- Develops project plans, timelines, and deliverables
- Coordinates cross-functional teams and client resources
- Tracks progress, risks, and issue resolution
- Ensures sustainable adoption of changes

Core competencies:
- Project management and execution
- Change management methodologies
- Cross-functional team coordination
- Risk assessment and mitigation
- Stakeholder communication and engagement

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Engagement Manager or Program Director
- Collaborates with: Client Teams, Subject Matter Experts, Change Managers, Vendors
