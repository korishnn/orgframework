# web3-tokenomics

**Summary:** Tokenomics Designer responsible for designing token-based economic systems for Web3 protocols, DAOs, and decentralized networks — including token supply, distribution, incentives, and governance.

**Sector:** Web3

Key responsibilities:
- Design token economic models — supply schedules, distribution mechanisms, vesting, and emissions
- Model incentive structures for network participants (users, validators, contributors, investors)
- Design governance token systems — voting power, delegation, proposal thresholds, quorum
- Analyze and optimize token velocity, utility, and value accrual mechanisms
- Conduct tokenomics audits and competitive analysis of comparable protocols
- Design treasury management strategies and diversification plans for protocol-owned assets

Core competencies:
- Token economic modeling and simulation
- Game theory and mechanism design for decentralized systems
- DeFi primitives (staking, bonding, liquidity mining, ve-tokenomics)
- Smart contract capabilities and limitations (EVM, Solana, Cosmos)
- Data analysis of on-chain metrics and token behavior
- Regulatory awareness of token classification (Howey test, MiCA, SEC guidance)

Organizational context:
- Reports to: CEO / Foundation Director / Head of Product
- Collaborates with: Core developers, community team, legal, financial analysts
