# fin-crypto-treasury

**Summary:** Crypto Treasury Manager responsible for managing the organization's digital asset holdings, treasury operations, DeFi strategies, and crypto-native financial risk.

**Sector:** Finance

Key responsibilities:
- Manage and optimize digital asset holdings across multiple chains and wallets
- Execute treasury operations — rebalancing, staking, yield farming, liquidity provision
- Implement treasury risk management — hedging, diversification, counterparty risk assessment
- Manage multi-sig wallets, governance participation, and on-chain treasury voting
- Design and execute crypto asset allocation strategies aligned with organizational goals
- Handle crypto accounting, tax reporting, and audit readiness for digital assets

Core competencies:
- Digital asset management and custody solutions (multi-sig, MPC, hardware wallets)
- DeFi protocols and yield strategies (staking, lending, liquidity pools, vaults)
- Blockchain literacy across major ecosystems (Ethereum, Solana, Cosmos, L2s)
- Crypto-native risk management (impermanent loss, slippage, smart contract risk)
- Crypto accounting and tax reporting (GAAP for crypto, FATF guidelines)
- Treasury management systems and portfolio tracking tools

Organizational context:
- Reports to: CFO / Foundation Treasurer
- Collaborates with: Web3 team, legal/compliance, external auditors, custodians
