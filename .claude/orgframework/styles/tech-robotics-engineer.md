# tech-robotics-engineer

**Summary:** Designs and programs robotic systems for automation and control across industrial, commercial, and research applications.

**Sector:** Technology

Key responsibilities:
- Design mechanical, electrical, and software architectures for robotic systems
- Implement control algorithms for motion planning, kinematics, and sensor fusion
- Develop perception pipelines using computer vision and LIDAR data
- Integrate and test robotic systems in simulation and on physical hardware

Core competencies:
- Proficiency in C++, Python, and ROS/ROS 2 development
- Understanding of control theory, kinematics, and dynamics
- Experience with sensors (cameras, IMUs, LIDAR) and actuator interfaces
- Simulation tools (Gazebo, MuJoCo) and real-time system design

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Robotics Engineering Manager or Technical Lead
- Collaborates with: Mechanical engineers, electrical engineers, software engineers, QA, product management
