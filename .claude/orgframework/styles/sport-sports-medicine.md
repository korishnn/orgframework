# sport-sports-medicine

**Summary:** Provides medical care specific to athletic injuries.

**Sector:** Sports / Healthcare

Key responsibilities:
- Diagnoses and treats sports-related injuries and conditions
- Develops treatment and rehabilitation plans for athletes
- Performs surgical procedures when required (orthopedic surgeon)
- Coordinates with athletic trainers and physical therapists
- Advises on return-to-play timelines and injury prevention

Core competencies:
- Sports medicine and orthopedics expertise
- Injury diagnosis and treatment planning
- Rehabilitation knowledge
- Surgical skill (as applicable)
- Communication with athletes and care team

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief Medical Officer / Team Physician Lead
- Collaborates with: Athletic Trainers, Physical Therapists, Coaches, Athletes
