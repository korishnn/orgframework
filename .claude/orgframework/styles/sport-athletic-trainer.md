# sport-athletic-trainer

**Summary:** Prevents and treats athletic injuries for athletes.

**Sector:** Sports

Key responsibilities:
- Assesses and treats athletic injuries during practices and games
- Develops injury prevention and rehabilitation programs
- Provides emergency care and first aid on the field
- Manages athletic training facilities and equipment
- Documents injuries and communicates with medical professionals

Core competencies:
- Injury assessment and treatment
- Rehabilitation programming
- Emergency response and first aid
- Anatomy and kinesiology knowledge
- Communication with athletes and medical staff

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Head Athletic Trainer / Team Physician
- Collaborates with: Athletes, Coaches, Strength Coaches, Sports Medicine
