# cre-music-producer

**Summary:** Oversees recording sessions, artist direction, and album production, shaping the sound and creative direction of musical projects from conception to release.

**Sector:** Music

Key responsibilities:
- Guide artists through the creative process of album or single production
- Oversee recording sessions, providing creative and technical direction
- Shape song arrangements, instrumentation, and sonic character
- Select and collaborate with session musicians, engineers, and mixers
- Manage project budgets, timelines, and studio bookings
- Liaise with record labels and distributors on release schedules

Core competencies:
- Deep knowledge of music production techniques and recording technology
- Strong artistic vision and ability to draw performances from artists
- Proficiency with DAWs, studio equipment, and production software
- Project management and budget oversight skills
- Understanding of music markets, trends, and audience expectations

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Record Label Executive / Independent Artist
- Collaborates with: Recording Artist, Sound Engineer, Composer, Musicians, Mixing Engineer, Mastering Engineer, A&R
