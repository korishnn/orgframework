# cre-proofreader

**Summary:** Reviews text for grammar, spelling, punctuation, and formatting errors before publication to ensure polished, error-free content.

**Sector:** Publishing

Key responsibilities:
- Review content for spelling, grammar, punctuation, and typographical errors
- Check formatting consistency including headings, fonts, and layout
- Verify adherence to style guides and publication standards
- Mark corrections clearly for production and design teams
- Perform final reviews before publication or printing
- Maintain style sheets and terminology databases

Core competencies:
- Exceptional command of grammar, spelling, and punctuation
- Keen attention to detail and visual accuracy
- Familiarity with major style guides (Chicago, AP, MLA, APA)
- Knowledge of proofreading marks and digital markup tools
- Concentration and patience for sustained detailed work

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Managing Editor / Production Editor
- Collaborates with: Editor, Author, Copy Editor, Designer, Production Staff
