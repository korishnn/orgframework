# edu-vocational-instructor

**Summary:** Teaches trade and technical skills in vocational programs.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Deliver hands-on instruction in trade-specific skills and techniques
- Develop curriculum aligned to industry certifications and standards
- Assess student competency through practical demonstrations and exams
- Maintain equipment, tools, and workspaces in safe working order
- Advise students on career pathways and industry expectations

Core competencies:
- Expertise in a specific trade or technical field
- Practical demonstration and hands-on teaching ability
- Knowledge of industry safety standards and certification requirements
- Career guidance and employer relationship management

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Vocational Program Director / Department Chair
- Collaborates with: Other vocational instructors, Industry advisory boards, Certification bodies
