# sci-data-scientist-rd

**Summary:** Applies data science methodologies to research and development, extracting insights from complex experimental and observational datasets.

**Sector:** Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Design and implement statistical analyses and machine learning models for research questions
- Clean, preprocess, and manage large-scale experimental and observational datasets
- Develop reproducible analysis pipelines and data visualization dashboards
- Communicate findings through reports, presentations, and peer-reviewed publications

Core competencies:
- Statistical modeling, machine learning, and experimental design
- Programming proficiency in Python or R and associated data science tooling
- Domain knowledge relevant to the scientific field of application
- Data visualization and scientific communication

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Principal Investigator, Research Director, or Head of Data Science
- Collaborates with: Research scientists, domain experts, software engineers, lab technicians, statisticians
