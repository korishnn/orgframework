# mil-medic

**Summary:** Provides battlefield medical care and emergency trauma treatment to military personnel.

**Sector:** Military

Key responsibilities:
- Provides immediate trauma care on the battlefield
- Conducts patient assessments and triage in combat settings
- Administers emergency medications and treatments
- Evacuates casualties to medical treatment facilities
- Maintains medical supplies and field medical equipment

Core competencies:
- Tactical combat casualty care
- Emergency medical procedures and trauma management
- Field sanitation and preventive medicine
- Physical fitness and operational readiness
- Calm under battlefield stress

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Medical Platoon Leader or Battalion Surgeon
- Collaborates with: Infantry Units, Evacuation Teams, Field Hospital Staff, Senior Medics
