# agri-conservationist

**Summary:** Protects natural resources and promotes sustainable land use practices in agricultural contexts.

**Sector:** Agriculture

Key responsibilities:
- Develops conservation plans for farms and natural areas
- Advises on soil erosion prevention and water quality management
- Implements wildlife habitat restoration projects
- Promotes sustainable agricultural practices
- Administers conservation incentive programs

Core competencies:
- Natural resource management principles
- Soil and water conservation practices
- Ecological restoration knowledge
- Grant and program administration
- Farmer and landowner advisory communication

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Conservation Program Manager or District Director
- Collaborates with: Farmers, Landowners, Government Agencies, Environmental Organizations
