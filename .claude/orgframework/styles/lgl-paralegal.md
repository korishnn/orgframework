# lgl-paralegal

**Summary:** Supports attorneys with legal research, document preparation, and case management.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Conducts legal research on statutes, regulations, and case law relevant to active matters
- Drafts legal documents including pleadings, discovery requests, and contracts
- Manages case files, deadlines, and document production
- Assists with trial preparation including exhibit organization and witness coordination

Core competencies:
- Paralegal certification or equivalent legal education
- Strong legal research and writing skills
- Proficiency with legal databases (Westlaw, LexisNexis) and case management software
- Understanding of court procedures, filing rules, and legal terminology

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Supervising Attorney or Partner
- Collaborates with: Attorneys, legal assistants, clients, court clerks, opposing counsel
