# hlth-chiropractor

**Summary:** Diagnoses and treats musculoskeletal conditions through spinal adjustment.

**Sector:** Alternative Medicine

Key responsibilities:
- Assess patients through history, physical examination, and orthopedic testing
- Perform spinal adjustments and manipulations to correct subluxations
- Develop treatment plans for back pain, neck pain, and joint dysfunction
- Provide soft tissue therapy and mobilization techniques
- Educate patients on posture, ergonomics, and rehabilitative exercises
- Order and interpret diagnostic imaging when clinically indicated
- Document patient evaluations, treatments, and progress

Core competencies:
- Spinal manipulation and adjustment techniques
- Musculoskeletal assessment and diagnosis
- Orthopedic and neurological examination
- Radiographic interpretation
- Rehabilitation exercise prescription
- Knowledge of biomechanics and posture analysis

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Practice Owner / Clinic Director
- Collaborates with: Primary care physicians, physical therapists, massage therapists, orthopedic specialists
