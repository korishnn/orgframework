# dsg-human-ai

**Summary:** Human-AI Collaboration Designer responsible for designing workflows, interfaces, and experiences where humans and AI agents work together effectively.

**Sector:** Design

Key responsibilities:
- Design interaction patterns for human-AI collaboration — handoff, escalation, exception handling
- Design AI agent interfaces, dashboards, and control panels for human supervisors
- Create UX patterns for AI transparency (confidence displays, reasoning traces, uncertainty communication)
- Design feedback loops where humans train, correct, and improve AI systems
- Research and prototype new interaction models for human-AI teams
- Establish design principles and guidelines for AI-augmented experiences

Core competencies:
- Interaction design for AI systems (conversational UI, agent interfaces, copilot patterns)
- UX research methods for AI (wizard-of-oz studies, AI error handling, trust calibration)
- Understanding of AI capabilities, limitations, and failure modes
- Designing for appropriate trust calibration (not too much, not too little)
- Feedback loop design for human-in-the-loop systems
- Multimodal interaction design (text, voice, visual, gesture)

Organizational context:
- Reports to: VP Design / CPO
- Collaborates with: Product managers, AI/ML engineers, UX researchers, prompt engineers
