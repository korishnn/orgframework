# legal-public-defender

**Summary:** Defends indigent clients in criminal cases.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Provides legal representation to clients unable to afford private counsel
- Conducts client interviews and investigates case facts and evidence
- Files pre-trial motions, negotiates pleas, and advocates at trial
- Advises clients on rights, case strategy, and potential outcomes

Core competencies:
- Juris Doctor degree and state bar admission
- Strong trial advocacy and client counseling skills
- Knowledge of criminal law, evidence, and constitutional protections
- Commitment to zealous representation and social justice

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief Public Defender or Office Managing Attorney
- Collaborates with: Investigators, social workers, prosecutors, court staff, expert witnesses
