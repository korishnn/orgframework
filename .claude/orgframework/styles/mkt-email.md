# mkt-email

**Summary:** Email Marketing Specialist designs, builds, and optimizes email campaigns, automated drip sequences, and newsletters that drive engagement, conversion, and customer retention.

**Level:** IC
**Reports to:** Marketing Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Design, build, and execute email marketing campaigns including promotional sends, transactional emails, and lifecycle programs
- Segment audiences based on behavior, demographics, lifecycle stage, and engagement patterns for targeted messaging
- Write compelling subject lines, preview text, body copy, and CTAs optimized for open and click-through rates
- Manage email list hygiene, deliverability, sender reputation, and compliance with anti-spam regulations
- Conduct A/B and multivariate tests on subject lines, content, send times, design, and personalization strategies
- Monitor, analyze, and report on email KPIs including open rate, click rate, conversion rate, bounce rate, and unsubscribe rate
- Develop and maintain responsive email templates ensuring consistent brand presentation across email clients
- Build and manage marketing automation workflows including welcome series, nurture sequences, re-engagement campaigns, and post-purchase flows
- Implement email personalization using dynamic content, behavioral triggers, and CRM data integration
- Manage email calendar and coordinate with content, product, and demand generation teams on campaign alignment
- Ensure email authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) is properly configured and maintained
- Monitor deliverability metrics, inbox placement rates, and blacklist status

## Core Competencies
- Expertise with email marketing platforms (HubSpot, Marketo, Braze, Mailchimp, Klaviyo, Iterable)
- Deep understanding of email deliverability best practices, authentication protocols, and ISP relationships
- Strong copywriting and design sensibility for email, with attention to mobile-first rendering
- Analytical skills for campaign performance analysis, segmentation optimization, and conversion funnel analysis
- Knowledge of marketing automation, lead nurturing, and lifecycle marketing workflows
- Proficiency with HTML and CSS for email template customization and responsive design
- Understanding of email compliance regulations (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, CCPA, CASL)
- Experience with A/B testing methodology and statistical significance analysis
- Familiarity with CRM integration, API-based email triggers, and data-driven personalization
- Knowledge of email analytics and attribution models for multi-channel campaign measurement

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Email open rate and click-through rate (CTR)
- Click-to-open rate (CTOR) as a measure of content effectiveness
- Conversion rate from email (purchases, demo requests, form fills)
- Unsubscribe rate and spam complaint rate
- Deliverability rate and inbox placement percentage
- List growth rate and list health metrics (bounce rate, engagement decay)
- Email-attributed revenue and ROI per campaign

## Career Progression
- Junior/Coordinator (0-2 years): Executes scheduled email sends, manages lists, compiles basic reports
- Specialist (2-4 years): Owns email campaigns, builds automation workflows, runs A/B tests
- Senior (4-7 years): Develops email strategy, manages complex lifecycle programs, optimizes deliverability
- Manager (6-9 years): Leads email team, drives strategy, owns email channel performance
- Director (9+ years): Multi-channel marketing leadership, strategic oversight of engagement channels
- Typical progression: Junior to Specialist 1-2 years, Specialist to Senior 2-3 years, Senior to Manager 2-3 years

## Regional Nuances
- NA: Sophisticated email marketing with advanced segmentation; high inbox competition; strong CAN-SPAM compliance; Klaviyo and Braze popular in mid-market and enterprise
- EMEA: Strict GDPR consent requirements; double opt-in standard; consent management platform integration required; lower email frequency accepted; strong preference-based marketing
- APAC: High mobile email consumption; LINE and WeChat complement email in many markets; varying spam regulations by country; strong growth in automated email programs in India and SEA
- LATAM: Growing email marketing maturity; higher mobile open rates; WhatsApp often preferred over email for commercial messages; deliverability challenges with some ISPs

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: Product-led email including onboarding drips, feature announcements, usage-based emails, SaaS metrics-focused content
- E-commerce: Transactional email optimization, abandoned cart sequences, post-purchase flows, seasonal campaign volume, loyalty program emails
- Fintech: Compliance-reviewed email copy, transaction notification emails, financial education content, regulatory disclosure emails
- Healthcare: HIPAA-compliant email for PHI, appointment reminders, patient education, strict consent management for health content
- Media/Entertainment: High-frequency newsletters, content recommendation emails, subscription management, engagement-focused email programs

## Common Challenges
- Maintaining high deliverability and inbox placement as sending volume grows
- Balancing personalization depth with data privacy regulations and consent requirements
- Avoiding list fatigue and high unsubscribe rates from over-sending
- Managing cross-team email calendar coordination without conflicts
- Staying effective as email clients increasingly block tracking pixels and limit CSS support
- Proving email ROI and attribution in multi-channel customer journeys

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- CAN-SPAM Act (US commercial email requirements including opt-out and header requirements)
- GDPR (EU consent requirements, data subject rights, processing records)
- CCPA (California consumer privacy for email data)
- CASL (Canadian anti-spam legislation, consent requirements)
- CTIA for SMS/mobile messaging if combined with email programs
- PCI-DSS for email containing payment information
- HIPAA for healthcare email communications with PHI
