# hlth-health-educator

**Summary:** Teaches communities about health, prevention, and wellness.

**Sector:** Public Health

Key responsibilities:
- Develop health education programs and materials for diverse populations
- Conduct community workshops, classes, and presentations on health topics
- Assess community health needs and identify priority health issues
- Promote preventive health behaviors and healthy lifestyle choices
- Evaluate effectiveness of health education programs and interventions
- Collaborate with community organizations to reach target populations
- Advocate for health equity and access to health resources

Core competencies:
- Health behavior theory and program design
- Public speaking and group facilitation
- Health literacy and culturally competent communication
- Program evaluation and needs assessment
- Community engagement and coalition building
- Knowledge of preventive health and wellness topics

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Public Health Director / Health Education Manager
- Collaborates with: Community organizations, schools, healthcare providers, government agencies, social workers
