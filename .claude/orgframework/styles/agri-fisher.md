# agri-fisher

**Summary:** Catches fish and seafood commercially or operates a fishing business.

**Sector:** Agriculture

Key responsibilities:
- Navigate vessels to fishing grounds and deploy nets, lines, or traps
- Haul, sort, and store catches according to species and size regulations
- Operate and maintain fishing gear, winches, and onboard processing equipment
- Maintain catch logs and comply with quotas, licensing, and reporting requirements

Core competencies:
- Seamanship and vessel handling skills in varying weather and sea conditions
- Knowledge of fish species behavior, migration patterns, and sustainable harvesting techniques
- Mechanical aptitude for maintaining engines, hydraulics, and deck machinery
- Navigational proficiency using GPS, radar, and chart plotters

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Vessel captain, fleet manager, or self as owner-operator
- Collaborates with: Fish processors, seafood wholesalers, harbor masters, and fisheries regulators
