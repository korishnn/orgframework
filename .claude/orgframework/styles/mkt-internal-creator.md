# mkt-internal-creator

**Summary:** Internal Creator / Evangelist responsible for building internal community, creating engaging content for employees, and evangelizing the company's mission and culture from within.

**Sector:** Marketing

Key responsibilities:
- Create and produce internal content — newsletters, videos, podcasts, and written updates for employees
- Manage internal communication channels and ensure information flows across the organization
- Build and nurture internal communities, ERGs, and interest groups
- Evangelize company mission, values, and strategic priorities through compelling storytelling
- Produce internal events — all-hands, AMAs, town halls, culture celebrations
- Gather and amplify employee stories, wins, and recognition across the organization

Core competencies:
- Content creation and production (video, audio, writing, design)
- Internal communications strategy and channel management
- Community building and facilitation
- Storytelling and narrative development
- Event production and coordination
- Employee engagement measurement and feedback loops

Organizational context:
- Reports to: VP People / Head of Communications / CMO
- Collaborates with: All departments, executive team, HR, culture team
