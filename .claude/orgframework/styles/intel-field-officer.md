# intel-field-officer

**Summary:** Conducts human intelligence operations in the field to gather information from human sources.

**Sector:** Intelligence

Key responsibilities:
- Recruits and handles human sources of intelligence
- Plans and conducts clandestine meetings and operations
- Collects information through elicitation and debriefing
- Assesses source reliability and information credibility
- Reports intelligence findings through secure channels

Core competencies:
- Elicitation and source handling techniques
- Operational security and tradecraft
- Cross-cultural communication and foreign languages
- Risk assessment and situational awareness
- Report writing and intelligence documentation

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Base Chief or Operations Officer
- Collaborates with: Intelligence Analysts, Surveillance Teams, Partner Intelligence Services
