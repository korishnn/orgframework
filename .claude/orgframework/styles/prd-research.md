# prd-research

**Summary:** User Researcher responsible for planning and conducting qualitative and quantitative user research to inform product decisions, uncover user needs, and validate product concepts throughout the development lifecycle.

**Level:** IC
**Reports to:** Director of Product / Director of Research / Design
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Plan, design, and conduct user research studies using a mix of qualitative methods (in-depth interviews, contextual inquiry, diary studies, ethnography) and quantitative methods (surveys, unmoderated testing, behavioral analytics)
- Perform usability testing on prototypes and live products — moderated and unmoderated — to identify friction points, validate design decisions, and benchmark user experience
- Conduct competitive analysis and benchmark studies to inform product direction and identify differentiation opportunities
- Synthesize research findings into actionable insights, recommendations, and design implications for product and design teams
- Present research results effectively to product, design, and leadership teams through reports, presentations, and video highlights
- Maintain a structured research repository that catalogs studies, findings, and design recommendations for organizational access
- Build and manage participant recruitment pipelines including screener surveys, panel management, and incentive programs
- Advocate for user needs and user-centered thinking throughout the product development lifecycle
- Partner with product managers and designers on research prioritization — aligning study timing with product decisions
- Develop and maintain research operations including consent processes, research tools, templates, and best practices guides
- Conduct strategic research for long-term opportunity identification — unmet needs discovery, Jobs-to-Be-Done analysis, future-state envisioning
- Mentor team members and cross-functional partners on research methods, user empathy, and customer-centric product development

## Core Competencies
- Deep qualitative research methodology — interviews, contextual inquiry, focus groups, diary studies, field research, and ethnographic methods
- Quantitative research skills — survey design, statistical analysis, behavioral data interpretation, and A/B test result context
- Usability testing expertise across fidelity levels — paper prototyping, interactive mockups, and live product testing
- Research synthesis and insight communication — affinity mapping, journey mapping, thematic analysis, and presentation
- Participant recruitment and research operations management
- Cross-functional collaboration with product management, design, and engineering
- Study design and methodological rigor — internal validity, bias awareness, sample size planning
- Research ethics including informed consent, privacy protection, and vulnerable population considerations
- Storytelling and data visualization for insight communication
- Agile research methods — rapid cycle testing, lean UX research, continuous discovery practices
- Accessibility research skills for inclusive product design
- Business acumen to connect research findings to product strategy and business outcomes

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Research impact score — number of product decisions directly influenced by research findings per quarter
- Research velocity — studies completed per month and time from study start to insight delivery
- Product team satisfaction with research quality, relevance, and timeliness
- Usability benchmark improvement scores across major product flows
- Participant recruitment pipeline health — time to fill studies, no-show rate, diversity of participant pool
- Research repository utilization — searches, views, and reuse of past research findings
- Customer satisfaction/experience scores linked to research-informed product changes
- Strategic research contribution — new product concepts and market opportunities identified through research

## Career Progression
- **Associate / Junior UX Researcher (0-2 years):** Conducts research under supervision. Runs usability tests. Supports participant recruitment. Takes notes and synthesizes findings. Learns research methodologies. Holds or pursues graduate degree in HCI, cognitive psychology, or social sciences.
- **UX Researcher (2-5 years):** Independently designs and conducts studies. Owns participant recruitment for their studies. Synthesizes findings into actionable insights. Presents to product teams. Manages research ops for their projects.
- **Senior UX Researcher (5-8 years):** Leads complex, strategic research initiatives. Owns methodology decisions. Influences product strategy through insights. Mentors junior researchers. Develops research programs and frameworks. Deep specialization in methods or domains.
- **Lead / Principal Researcher (8-12 years):** Drives research vision and strategy across the organization. Builds and scales research operations. Develops research culture and maturity. Partners with director-level stakeholders. Published and recognized in the research community.
- **Director of Research / VP Research (12+ years):** Leads a research organization. Sets research strategy aligned with business goals. Manages research team hiring and development. Represents customer insights at the executive level. Advocates for user-centered culture across the company.

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Morning review of research project queue — prioritize studies by decision timing, stakeholder needs, and strategic importance
- Study design and planning — write discussion guides, design survey instruments, set up test protocols in research tools
- Participant recruitment management — review screener responses, schedule sessions, manage incentives
- Conduct user research sessions — interviews, usability tests, field visits — typically 2-4 sessions per day during active studies
- Synthesis sessions — affinity mapping, thematic coding, journey mapping — converting raw data into findings
- Insight documentation — write research reports, create presentation decks, clip video highlights
- Share findings with stakeholders — present research readouts, share repository links, integrate insights into product backlogs
- Cross-functional alignment meetings — review product designs for research-needs incorporation, prioritize upcoming studies
- Research operations maintenance — update templates, manage tool subscriptions, improve participant pipeline
- Continuous learning — read research papers, attend conferences, advance methodological skills
- Communication rhythm includes focused research sessions alternating with stakeholder syncs and presentation preparation

## Cross-Functional Relationships
- **Product (prd-manager):** Primary partner — defines research questions, aligns study timing with product decisions, incorporates findings into requirements, prioritizes research alongside feature work
- **Design (dsg-ux, dsg-ui, dsg-visual):] Daily collaboration on usability testing, design concept validation, iterative testing throughout the design process
- **Product Analytics (prd-analytics):** Combines behavioral data with qualitative insights for richer understanding. Quantitative research support for survey design and analysis
- **Data Science (da-scientist):** Collaboration on behavioral segmentation, statistical analysis for research studies, machine learning for pattern detection in qualitative data
- **Customer Support (cs-tier1, cs-success):** Feedback channel — support ticket analysis for research hypothesis generation, customer journey pain point identification
- **Marketing (mkt-growth, mkt-brand):** Market research collaboration, brand perception studies, messaging testing, customer persona development
- **Engineering (eng-arch, eng-frontend):** Feasibility input for research concepts, technical constraints communication for user testing, participation in research sessions
- **Content Strategy (cs-docs):] Content usability testing, documentation effectiveness research, help center content gap identification
- **Privacy/Legal (lgl-privacy):** Research participant data handling compliance, consent form management, IRB-equivalent review processes
- **Sales (sls-ae):** Customer feedback access, win/loss analysis for research input, prospect interview facilitation

## Regional Nuances
- **NA:** Mature UX research profession with strong methodological standards. High research maturity in tech companies. Specialization between qual, quant, and mixed-methods researchers is common. Compensation $900K-$1800K. Strong academic pipeline (HCI and cognitive psychology PhDs and masters). Research ops is a growing specialization.
- **EMEA:** Strong research tradition particularly in UK, Germany, and Nordics. Emphasis on international and cross-cultural research design. GDPR compliance deeply embedded in research operations. Multilingual research capability is a common requirement. Compensation lower than NA but competitive within local markets.
- **APAC:** Growing UX research field with high demand and limited senior talent pool. Rapidly expanding in India and Southeast Asia. Cultural considerations in research methods — hierarchy influences interview dynamics, indirect communication norms require adapted protocols. High demand for bilingual researchers (English + local language). China has a unique digital ecosystem requiring specialized research approaches.
- **LATAM:** Emerging research profession with growing awareness of user-centered design. Economic constraints limit research budgets — lean and scrappy research methods are valued. Remote research is dominant given distributed teams. Portuguese and Spanish bilingual research capability is critical. Strong family and community orientation affects research design.

## Industry Variations
- **SaaS:** Research methods include B2B longitudinal studies, annual relationship surveys, persona research for complex buying groups, onboarding flow optimization, feature discovery research, and churn root cause studies. Research directly linked to subscription metrics.
- **Fintech:** Trust and security are central research themes — risk perception, security behavior, trust-building through interfaces. Regulatory constraints affect study design (financial advice disclosures in testing). Sensitive financial data handling in research. Compliance understanding for participant scenarios.
- **Healthcare:** Clinical research methodology and user research have different traditions — bridging the gap is important. Patient privacy requirements (HIPAA, GDPR) affect all research operations. Accessibility research is critical. Provider workflow research. Informed consent complexity for medical studies.
- **E-commerce:** Purchase decision research, conversion funnel optimization, personalization preference studies, mobile commerce behavior. High-velocity testing culture. Large-scale quantitative research is common. Price sensitivity and promotion response research. Logistics experience research.
- **Manufacturing/IoT:** Field research at industrial sites and facilities. Safety-critical interface testing. Complex system usability. Multi-user workflow research for enterprise systems. Environmental factors in research conditions. Translation to multiple languages for global products.

## Common Challenges
- Getting research findings incorporated into product decisions when there are competing sources of input (data, stakeholder opinion, executive preference)
- Balancing rigor and speed — delivering reliable insights on product timelines that rarely accommodate ideal research timelines
- Recruiting representative participants who accurately reflect the target user population, not just convenient samples
- Building research maturity and user-centric culture in organizations where research is new or undervalued
- Avoiding the overclaiming problem — knowing when sample sizes are too small or methods too limited for confident recommendations
- Maintaining participant engagement and preventing research fatigue in customer panels that are frequently used

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- GDPR requirements for research participant data — consent, right to deletion, data minimization
- CCPA and state privacy law compliance for participant personal information
- HIPAA compliance for health-related research and participant health data handling
- COPPA compliance if research involves children or minors
- Informed consent best practices and documentation for all research studies
- IRB (Institutional Review Board) standards for research ethics, even in non-academic settings
- Accessibility standards for inclusive research methods (WCAG, Section 508)
- ADA compliance for research facilities and participant accommodation
- Audio and video recording consent regulations — one-party vs. two-party consent states
- Deception in research — ethical guidelines and debrief requirements
- Participant incentive compliance — tax reporting for payments, gift card regulations
- International data transfer compliance (SCCs, BCRs) for global research studies
- Internal data security policies for storing research recordings and participant data
