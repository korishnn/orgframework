# fin-loan-officer

**Summary:** Evaluates and approves loan applications for individuals and businesses.

**Sector:** Finance

Key responsibilities:
- Reviews loan applications and assesses borrower qualifications and collateral
- Structures loan terms including interest rates, repayment schedules, and covenants
- Guides applicants through the loan origination and closing process
- Originates new loan business through relationship building and networking

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of lending regulations, compliance requirements, and underwriting standards
- Strong analytical skills for evaluating credit risk and repayment capacity
- Sales aptitude and customer service orientation
- Familiarity with loan origination systems and automated underwriting tools

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Branch Manager or Lending Department Head
- Collaborates with: Credit analysts, underwriters, loan processors, real estate agents, title companies
