# govt-policy-analyst

**Summary:** Researches and analyzes policy issues, develops recommendations for government decision-makers.

**Sector:** Government

Key responsibilities:
- Conducts research on policy issues and legislative proposals
- Analyzes data to evaluate policy effectiveness and impact
- Drafts policy briefs, reports, and recommendation documents
- Consults with stakeholders and subject matter experts
- Monitors legislative and regulatory developments

Core competencies:
- Analytical and critical thinking skills
- Research methodology and data analysis
- Written and verbal communication
- Understanding of political and legislative processes
- Policy evaluation and impact assessment

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Policy Director or Agency Head
- Collaborates with: Civil Servants, Legislative Staff, Subject Matter Experts
