# beauty-cosmetologist

**Summary:** Provides comprehensive beauty services including hair, skin, and nails.

**Sector:** Beauty / Personal Care

Key responsibilities:
- Performs hair services including cutting, coloring, and styling
- Provides skincare treatments including facials and waxing
- Offers nail services including manicures and pedicures
- Consults with clients on overall beauty and grooming goals
- Recommends and retails beauty products

Core competencies:
- Broad knowledge of hair, skin, and nail services
- Sanitation and safety compliance
- Client consultation and relationship building
- Creativity and trend awareness
- Retail sales and product knowledge

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Salon Manager / Spa Manager / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Other Stylists, Estheticians, Nail Techs, Clients
