# govt-ethics-officer

**Summary:** Ensures government compliance with ethics laws, conflicts of interest rules, and standards of conduct.

**Sector:** Government

Key responsibilities:
- Advises government officials on ethics laws and disclosure requirements
- Reviews financial disclosures and potential conflicts of interest
- Investigates ethics complaints and alleged violations
- Develops and delivers ethics training programs
- Issues advisory opinions on ethical matters

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of government ethics laws and regulations
- Investigative and analytical skills
- Impartiality and professional integrity
- Advisory communication and training delivery
- Confidentiality and discretion

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Agency Head, Inspector General, or Ethics Commission
- Collaborates with: Agency Counsel, HR, Inspectors General, Oversight Bodies
