# hosp-conference-planner

**Summary:** Organizes conferences, conventions, and trade shows.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Selects venues and manages logistics for conferences
- Coordinates speakers, schedules, and session programming
- Manages registration, attendee communications, and materials
- Oversees audiovisual, catering, and exhibit hall setup
- Manages budgets, contracts, and vendor relationships

Core competencies:
- Large-scale event logistics and planning
- Vendor and contract negotiation
- Budget and financial management
- Stakeholder communication and coordination
- Problem-solving under pressure

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Director of Events / Conference Manager
- Collaborates with: Venue Staff, Speakers, Exhibitors, AV Team
