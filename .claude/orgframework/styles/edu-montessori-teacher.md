# edu-montessori-teacher

**Summary:** Guides children using Montessori educational philosophy.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Prepare and maintain a carefully designed Montessori classroom environment
- Observe children's interests and development to guide individualized learning
- Present Montessori materials and lessons to individuals and small groups
- Foster independence, self-discipline, and intrinsic motivation
- Document each child's progress and developmental observations

Core competencies:
- Montessori philosophy and methodology expertise
- Observation and individualized lesson planning
- Classroom environment design and material curation
- Respect for the child's natural psychological development

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Montessori Program Director / School Head
- Collaborates with: Other Montessori teachers, Classroom assistants, Parents
