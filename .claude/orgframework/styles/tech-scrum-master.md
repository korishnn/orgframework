# tech-scrum-master

**Summary:** Facilitates Agile processes and removes impediments for development teams.

**Sector:** Technology

Key responsibilities:
- Facilitates Scrum ceremonies including daily stand-ups, sprint planning, and retrospectives
- Removes blockers and shields the team from external interruptions
- Coaches the team on Agile principles, self-organization, and continuous improvement
- Tracks sprint progress, metrics, and communicates status to stakeholders

Core competencies:
- Certified Scrum Master (CSM) or equivalent Agile certification
- Deep understanding of Agile frameworks and servant leadership principles
- Strong facilitation, coaching, and conflict resolution skills
- Proficiency with Agile project management tools (Jira, Trello, Azure DevOps)

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Agile Coach, Engineering Manager, or Delivery Lead
- Collaborates with: Product owners, development teams, QA, stakeholders, project managers
