# acad-psychologist-research

**Summary:** Conducts research on mental processes and behavior.

**Sector:** Academia / Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Design and run experiments to study cognition, emotion, and behavior
- Collect and analyze behavioral and physiological data
- Develop and test theories of mental processes
- Write grant proposals and publish in peer-reviewed journals
- Supervise graduate and undergraduate research assistants

Core competencies:
- Experimental design and research methodology
- Statistical analysis and psychometrics
- Knowledge of cognitive, social, or developmental psychology
- Scientific writing and ethical research conduct

Organizational context:
- Reports to: University Department Chair / Lab Director
- Collaborates with: Clinical psychologists, Neuroscientists, Data scientists, Academic colleagues
