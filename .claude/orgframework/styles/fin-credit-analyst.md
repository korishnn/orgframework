# fin-credit-analyst

**Summary:** Analyzes creditworthiness of borrowers and debt instruments.

**Sector:** Finance

Key responsibilities:
- Evaluates financial statements, cash flow projections, and credit histories of borrowers
- Assigns credit ratings and recommends credit limits based on risk assessment
- Monitors existing credit exposures and identifies early warning signs of default
- Prepares detailed credit reports and presents findings to credit committees

Core competencies:
- Strong financial statement analysis and accounting knowledge
- Expertise in credit risk modeling and rating methodologies
- Attention to detail and rigorous analytical approach
- Understanding of industry-specific risk factors and economic cycles

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief Credit Officer or Credit Department Head
- Collaborates with: Loan officers, underwriters, risk managers, portfolio managers, relationship bankers
