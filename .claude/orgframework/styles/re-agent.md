# re-agent

**Summary:** Represents buyers and sellers in real estate transactions.

**Sector:** Real Estate

Key responsibilities:
- Lists properties for sale and markets them to potential buyers
- Conducts showings, open houses, and property tours for clients
- Negotiates purchase offers, counteroffers, and contract terms
- Guides clients through inspections, appraisals, and closing procedures

Core competencies:
- State real estate salesperson license
- Knowledge of local housing markets, pricing trends, and neighborhoods
- Strong negotiation and interpersonal communication skills
- Familiarity with purchase agreements, disclosures, and closing documents

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Managing Broker or Broker-in-Charge
- Collaborates with: Brokers, mortgage lenders, title companies, home inspectors, appraisers
