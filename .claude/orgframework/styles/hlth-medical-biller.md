# hlth-medical-biller

**Summary:** Processes medical billing, coding, and insurance claims.

**Sector:** Healthcare

Key responsibilities:
- Prepare and submit accurate medical claims to insurance companies
- Assign appropriate ICD-10, CPT, and HCPCS codes for services rendered
- Review claim denials, identify root causes, and resubmit corrected claims
- Process patient statements and manage accounts receivable
- Verify patient insurance eligibility and benefits prior to services
- Post payments from insurers and patients to billing system
- Maintain compliance with billing regulations and payer policies

Core competencies:
- Medical coding (ICD-10, CPT, HCPCS) and billing procedures
- Insurance claim submission and denial management
- Revenue cycle management and accounts receivable
- Knowledge of payer policies and reimbursement methodologies
- Healthcare compliance and fraud prevention awareness
- Billing software and practice management systems

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Billing Manager / Revenue Cycle Director
- Collaborates with: Medical records staff, physicians, insurance companies, patients, front office staff
