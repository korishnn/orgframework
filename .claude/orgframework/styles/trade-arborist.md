# trade-arborist

**Summary:** Cares for trees including pruning, removal, health assessment, and disease management in urban and natural settings.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Prunes trees for health, safety, and aesthetics using climbing or aerial lift techniques
- Removes hazardous or dead trees with rigging and cutting methods
- Diagnoses tree diseases, pest infestations, and nutritional deficiencies
- Performs cabling, bracing, and lightning protection installations
- Stumps grinding and site cleanup after tree operations

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of tree biology, species identification, and disease diagnosis
- Proficiency with chainsaws, chippers, rigging, and climbing equipment
- Certification (ISA Certified Arborist) and safety protocols
- Physical fitness for climbing, lifting, and working at heights
- Risk assessment and hazard tree evaluation skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Arborist Crew Lead or Tree Care Company Owner
- Collaborates with: Landscapers, Utility Line Clearance Crews, Municipal Foresters, and Property Owners
