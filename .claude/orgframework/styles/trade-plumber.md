# trade-plumber

**Summary:** Installs and repairs piping, fixtures, and plumbing systems for water supply, drainage, and gas in buildings.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Installs pipes, fittings, and fixtures for water, drainage, and gas systems
- Reads blueprints and building codes to plan plumbing layouts
- Diagnoses and repairs leaks, clogs, and plumbing failures
- Solders, threads, or solvent-welds pipe connections
- Installs and maintains water heaters and sewage systems

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of pipe materials (copper, PVC, PEX, cast iron) and joining methods
- Proficiency with pipe wrenches, cutters, threading tools, and drain snakes
- Understanding of plumbing codes and water pressure principles
- Strong troubleshooting and diagnostic ability
- Physical stamina for confined spaces and heavy lifting

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Plumbing Foreman or Project Manager
- Collaborates with: Electricians, Carpenters, HVAC technicians, and Building Inspectors
