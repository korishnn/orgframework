# hlth-pathologist

**Summary:** Examines tissues and fluids to diagnose disease.

**Sector:** Allied Health

Key responsibilities:
- Examine surgical specimens and biopsy tissues for diagnosis
- Perform autopsies to determine cause of death
- Interpret laboratory results including hematology, chemistry, and microbiology
- Diagnose cancers, infections, and inflammatory conditions through microscopic analysis
- Oversee clinical laboratory operations and quality assurance
- Consult with clinicians on test selection and result interpretation
- Participate in tumor boards and diagnostic case conferences

Core competencies:
- Histopathology and cytopathology interpretation
- Clinical pathology and laboratory medicine
- Microscopic examination and tissue processing knowledge
- Immunohistochemistry and molecular diagnostics
- Laboratory quality control and regulatory compliance
- Diagnostic reasoning and clinicopathologic correlation

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief of Pathology / Laboratory Medical Director
- Collaborates with: Surgeons, oncologists, radiologists, laboratory technicians, primary care physicians
