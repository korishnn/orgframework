# ops-fractional-cxo

**Summary:** Fractional Executive Coordinator responsible for managing and coordinating a network of part-time/fractional C-suite executives (fractional CFO, CTO, CMO, COO, etc.) serving multiple organizations.

**Sector:** Operations

Key responsibilities:
- Coordinate scheduling, priorities, and handoffs across fractional executives serving multiple clients
- Maintain continuity and institutional knowledge across fractional leadership transitions
- Manage onboarding and offboarding of fractional executives for each client engagement
- Ensure fractional executives have access to necessary context, data, and stakeholder relationships
- Track and report on fractional executive utilization, effectiveness, and client satisfaction
- Build knowledge management systems so fractional leaders can ramp quickly on each client

Core competencies:
- Multi-stakeholder coordination and scheduling at executive level
- Knowledge management and context handoff processes
- Executive communication and relationship management
- Fractional executive model operations
- Project management for C-suite workflows
- Confidentiality and discretion in multi-client contexts

Organizational context:
- Reports to: CEO / Founder (client side) or Managing Partner (fractional firm side)
- Collaborates with: All fractional C-suite members, client leadership teams, board members
