# np-foundation-program-officer

**Summary:** Manages grantmaking programs, evaluates funding proposals, and oversees grantee relationships for philanthropic foundations.

**Sector:** Nonprofit

Key responsibilities:
- Review and evaluate grant proposals against foundation priorities and criteria
- Manage a portfolio of active grants including monitoring, reporting, and compliance
- Conduct site visits and due diligence on prospective and current grantees
- Develop requests for proposals and program strategy recommendations

Core competencies:
- Grant evaluation and portfolio management
- Knowledge of philanthropic and nonprofit sectors
- Strong written analysis and recommendation drafting
- Relationship management with diverse stakeholders

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Program Director or Foundation Executive Director
- Collaborates with: Grantee organizations, finance team, evaluation and impact staff, board members
