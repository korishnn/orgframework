# tran-delivery-driver

**Summary:** Delivers packages, food, or goods on local routes for courier services, retailers, and food establishments.

**Sector:** Transportation

Key responsibilities:
- Drives delivery vehicles on assigned local routes
- Loads and organizes deliveries for efficient route completion
- Obtains customer signatures and collects payments as needed
- Communicates with dispatch regarding delivery status and issues
- Maintains vehicle cleanliness and reports mechanical problems

Core competencies:
- Valid driver's license with clean driving record
- Knowledge of local roads, traffic patterns, and delivery zones
- Customer service skills for professional interactions
- Physical stamina for lifting, carrying, and walking
- Time management for completing routes within service windows

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Route Supervisor or Dispatch Manager
- Collaborates with: Dispatchers, Warehouse Staff, and Customers
