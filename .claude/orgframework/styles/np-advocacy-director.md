# np-advocacy-director

**Summary:** Leads advocacy and lobbying efforts to advance nonprofit mission through policy change.

**Sector:** Nonprofit

Key responsibilities:
- Develops advocacy strategy and policy positions
- Lobbies elected officials and government agencies
- Mobilizes supporters for grassroots advocacy campaigns
- Builds coalitions with aligned organizations
- Monitors legislation and regulatory developments

Core competencies:
- Advocacy and lobbying strategy
- Knowledge of legislative and regulatory processes
- Coalition building and stakeholder engagement
- Policy analysis and position development
- Media relations and message development

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Executive Director or CEO
- Collaborates with: Community Organizers, Policy Analysts, Communications Team, Coalition Partners
