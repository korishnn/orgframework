# hlth-dietitian

**Summary:** Provides medical nutrition therapy and dietary counseling.

**Sector:** Alternative Medicine

Key responsibilities:
- Assess patients nutritional status through dietary analysis and clinical evaluation
- Develop individualized medical nutrition therapy plans for disease management
- Counsel patients on therapeutic diets for diabetes, heart disease, renal disease, and other conditions
- Provide nutrition support including enteral and parenteral nutrition management
- Monitor patient progress and adjust nutrition interventions based on outcomes
- Educate patients and groups on evidence-based nutrition principles
- Collaborate with healthcare teams on multidisciplinary patient care

Core competencies:
- Medical nutrition therapy for acute and chronic conditions
- Dietary assessment and nutritional analysis
- Therapeutic diet planning and modification
- Nutrition support and supplementation
- Patient education and behavior change counseling
- Knowledge of biochemistry and nutrient metabolism

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Clinical Nutrition Manager / Food Service Director
- Collaborates with: Physicians, nurses, pharmacists, food service staff, physical therapists, case managers
