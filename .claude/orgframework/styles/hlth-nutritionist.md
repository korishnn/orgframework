# hlth-nutritionist

**Summary:** Advises on nutrition, diet, and healthy eating habits.

**Sector:** Alternative Medicine

Key responsibilities:
- Assess clients dietary habits and nutritional needs
- Provide personalized nutrition and meal planning guidance
- Educate clients on healthy eating, portion control, and food choices
- Support weight management, sports nutrition, and wellness goals
- Recommend dietary supplements when appropriate
- Develop nutrition education materials and programs
- Stay current with nutritional science and dietary guidelines

Core competencies:
- Nutrition science and dietary guidelines knowledge
- Meal planning and dietary analysis
- Client counseling and motivational interviewing
- Weight management strategies
- Sports and fitness nutrition
- Nutrition communication and education

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Wellness Director / Practice Manager
- Collaborates with: Fitness trainers, dietitians, health coaches, physicians, wellness program coordinators
