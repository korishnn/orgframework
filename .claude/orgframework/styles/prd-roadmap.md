# prd-roadmap

**Summary:** Roadmap Manager responsible for maintaining the unified product roadmap, evolving prioritization frameworks, and managing stakeholder communication across the product organization.

**Level:** IC or Manager
**Reports to:** Director of Product / VP Product
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Maintain and communicate the unified product roadmap across all product areas, squads, and time horizons (now/next/later)
- Develop, evolve, and socialize prioritization frameworks including RICE scoring, weighted effort-impact models, and WSJF for consistent decision-making
- Manage the roadmap intake process including feature requests, improvement ideas, and strategic initiatives from multiple sources
- Track roadmap commitments, dependencies across teams, delivery risks, and timeline confidence levels
- Create and maintain roadmap artifacts — visual timelines, theme-based roadmaps, release calendars, and capability heatmaps
- Facilitate cross-team roadmap alignment meetings, dependency resolution sessions, and quarterly planning sessions
- Ensure the roadmap accurately reflects strategic priorities, resource constraints, and capacity allocations
- Provide roadmap visibility and status reporting to internal stakeholders, executive leadership, and sometimes external customers
- Manage the quarterly planning cadence including objective setting, capacity allocation, and commitment definition
- Drive roadmap governance — defining what goes on the roadmap, confidence levels, and change management processes
- Coordinate product release train schedules and inter-team dependency alignment for synchronized deliveries
- Build and maintain roadmap analytics — commitment hit rates, scope change tracking, and delivery predictability reporting

## Core Competencies
- Roadmap planning, dependency management, and multi-horizon visualization
- Prioritization frameworks and quantitative decision-making — RICE, WSJF, Kano, opportunity scoring
- Stakeholder communication and expectation management across diverse audiences
- Cross-team coordination, facilitation, and conflict resolution for competing priorities
- Presentation and data visualization skills for roadmap decks and dashboards
- Project management rigor and planning methodology (SAFe, Scrum of Scrums, LeSS)
- Change management for priorities and scope adjustments
- Strategic thinking — connecting roadmap items to business outcomes and strategic themes
- Analytical skills for capacity modeling, velocity tracking, and what-if scenario planning
- Conflict resolution when teams or stakeholders have competing priorities
- Executive communication and synthesis — complex roadmap status in simple, clear language
- Tooling expertise for roadmap platforms, agile project management, and visualization tools

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Roadmap commitment accuracy — percentage of committed items delivered on time per quarter
- Roadmap change frequency — number of material priority shifts per quarter indicating stability
- Stakeholder satisfaction with roadmap visibility and communication
- Time from feature request intake to roadmap inclusion decision
- Cross-team dependency resolution cycle time
- Prioritization framework adoption rate across product teams
- Delivery predictability — variance between planned and actual delivery quarter

## Career Progression
- **Associate / Junior Roadmap Coordinator (0-2 years):** Supports roadmap maintenance, data entry in roadmapping tools, meeting scheduling, and artifact creation. Learns prioritization frameworks and stakeholder communication.
- **Roadmap Manager / Program Manager (2-5 years):** Independently manages the unified roadmap. Facilitates quarterly planning. Handles stakeholder communication. Develops and evolves prioritization frameworks. Manages intake process.
- **Senior Roadmap / Program Manager (5-8 years):** Owns roadmap governance and processes across a large product organization. Leads dependency management across multiple product lines. Develops roadmap analytics and reporting. Mentors junior team members.
- **Director of Product Operations (8-12 years):** Oversees product operations including roadmap, intake, prioritization frameworks, and product analytics. Partners with product leadership on org-wide planning. Manages a team of roadmap and program managers.
- **VP Product Operations / COO of Product (12+ years):** Executive-level ownership of product operations. Sets product operating model, planning cadence, and governance. Partners with CPO on org design and scale. May transition to general management.

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Start with roadmap health check — review updated statuses, identify items at risk, flag upcoming decisions needed
- Manage the intake queue — triage new feature requests, route to appropriate product teams, log for prioritization discussion
- Produce and distribute roadmap status communications — weekly snapshots, executive summaries, and milestone tracking
- Facilitate cross-team dependency sessions — map dependencies between squads, identify sequencing conflicts, drive resolution
- Maintain roadmap artifacts — update timelines, adjust confidence levels, refresh theme groupings
- Run prioritization sessions with product managers — apply frameworks, score initiatives, determine quarterly focus
- Prepare for quarterly planning — capacity analysis, strategic initiative packaging, resource allocation modeling
- Coordinate with product managers on commitment definitions and confidence level assessments
- Stakeholder communication meetings — executive briefings, sales roadshow presentations, customer-facing roadmap content
- Continuous improvement of roadmap processes — retrospectives on planning accuracy, framework effectiveness, and communication quality
- Communication rhythm includes regular cadenced meetings and consistent async roadmap updates through shared tools

## Cross-Functional Relationships
- **Product Managers (prd-manager):** Primary partnership — defining roadmap content, assessing priorities, managing commitments, and ensuring delivery visibility
- **Product Leadership (prd-strategy, exec-cto):** Strategic alignment, investment decisions, capacity allocation decisions, quarterly priority setting
- **Engineering (eng-arch, eng-platform, eng-manager):** Technical dependency identification, capacity validation, delivery risk assessment, milestone commitment confirmation
- **Sales (sls-ae, sls-solutions):** Customer-facing roadmap content, feature request intake from the field, sales enablement on upcoming capabilities
- **Marketing (mkt-growth, mkt-email):** Launch planning coordination, feature announcement timing, campaign alignment with roadmap releases
- **Customer Success (cs-success):] Internal roadmap training for customer conversations, customer feature request prioritization input, churn risk related to roadmap gaps
- **Executive (exec-ceo, exec-cfo):] Roadmap visibility, strategic milestone tracking, resource investment decisions, board-level roadmap reporting
- **Finance (fin-fpa):** Resource planning alignment with budget cycles, ROI tracking for major initiatives, capacity modeling validation
- **Support (cs-tier1, cs-tier3):] Product issue escalations affecting roadmap priorities, customer feedback from support tickets informing backlog prioritization
- **Legal (lgl-contracts, lgl-compliance):** Roadmap commitment risk assessment, contractual obligations reflected in roadmap, compliance feature delivery timelines

## Regional Nuances
- **NA:** Structured roadmap processes with clear SAFe or similar framework adoption. Strong emphasis on predictability and commitment accuracy. Executive stakeholders expect high-confidence delivery dates. Compensation $110K-$180K. Roadmap tooling is sophisticated.
- **EMEA:** More flexible and adaptive roadmap approaches with less rigid commitment culture. Theme-based roadmaps preferred over date-driven. Emphasis on outcomes over output. Stronger agile and lean influences from European methodology origins. Works Council consultation may affect planning timelines. Compensation $70K-$120K.
- **APAC:** Execution-heavy roadmap culture in India with strong delivery focus. In Singapore and Australia, roadmap practices are more mature. Time zone challenges for global roadmap coordination. Rapidly changing priorities common in high-growth markets. Quarterly planning cycles may be compressed.
- **LATAM:** Less formalized roadmap processes in many organizations. Adaptable and flexible planning approaches. Nearshore coordination with NA teams requires roadmap visibility across time zones. Exchange rate and economic volatility can disrupt resource planning. Growing adoption of structured roadmapping practices.

## Industry Variations
- **SaaS:** Continuous delivery roadmap with theme-based planning. Quarterly commitments with monthly checkpoints. Heavy emphasis on customer-facing roadmap transparency. Competitive intelligence influences roadmap priority shifts. Experimentation results feed roadmap iteration.
- **Fintech:** Compliance and regulatory deadlines are non-negotiable roadmap drivers. Longer planning horizons for regulated features. Audit trail for all roadmap decisions. Regulatory approval dependencies create schedule risk. Seasonal planning around fiscal year regulatory calendars.
- **Healthcare:** FDA clearance timelines are critical roadmap dependencies. Clinical validation milestones. HIPAA compliance feature sequencing. Provider procurement cycles influence feature delivery timing. Slower roadmap iteration due to safety-critical requirements.
- **E-commerce:** Seasonal roadmap planning around Black Friday, holiday peaks. Highly dynamic — competitive moves require rapid roadmap rebalancing. Parallel workstreams for customer-facing and operational/supply chain features. A/B test-driven roadmap adjustments.
- **Manufacturing:** Hardware and software roadmap integration. Longer planning horizons for hardware-dependent features. Supply chain dependencies for connected products. Product version and model year planning cycles. Retrofit and upgrade feature sequencing.

## Common Challenges
- Maintaining roadmap stability while responding to urgent competitive moves, customer requests, and strategic pivots
- Getting accurate capacity and dependency information across multiple teams with competing priorities
- Managing executive expectations that everything is a priority and timelines are always too aggressive
- Communicating roadmap uncertainty and confidence levels without losing stakeholder trust
- Aligning roadmaps across product, engineering, marketing, and sales for coordinated launches
- Preventing roadmap from becoming a wishlist rather than a committed plan with real resource constraints

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- SOX compliance for roadmap commitments that may affect financial reporting and revenue guidance
- Securities regulations for public company — roadmap communications that may be considered forward-looking statements
- Contractual commitments reflected in roadmap items must be tracked and delivered (customer SLAs, partner commitments)
- Regulatory deadlines (GDPR compliance dates, PSD2 implementation, CCPA enforcement) are non-negotiable roadmap drivers
- Accessibility compliance deadlines (WCAG conformance dates) must be scheduled and tracked on the roadmap
- Industry-specific regulatory milestones (FDA approvals, FINRA rules, FCC certifications) drive hard roadmap deadlines
- Data privacy roadmap items (DPIA completion, consent infrastructure) for new product features
- Security compliance roadmap (SOC 2 certification, penetration testing, vulnerability remediation) parallel to product roadmap
