# ins-adjuster

**Summary:** Investigates insurance claims and determines settlement amounts.

**Sector:** Insurance

Key responsibilities:
- Investigates claims by inspecting damage, reviewing police reports, and interviewing claimants
- Evaluates policy coverage and determines liability and settlement values
- Negotiates settlements with claimants, attorneys, and repair vendors
- Documents claim files with detailed reports and supporting evidence

Core competencies:
- State adjuster license or certification
- Strong investigative and analytical skills
- Knowledge of insurance policies, coverage interpretation, and tort law
- Negotiation and conflict resolution abilities

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Claims Manager or Claims Supervisor
- Collaborates with: Agents, underwriters, attorneys, medical professionals, repair shops
