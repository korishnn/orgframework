# hlth-community-health-worker

**Summary:** Connects communities with health services and resources.

**Sector:** Public Health

Key responsibilities:
- Serve as a liaison between community members and healthcare providers
- Provide health education and information to underserved populations
- Assist individuals with navigating health insurance and social services
- Conduct outreach to connect community members with preventive care
- Support patients in managing chronic conditions and treatment adherence
- Advocate for community health needs and cultural competency in care
- Document interactions and track health outcomes for program reporting

Core competencies:
- Community outreach and engagement
- Health system navigation and resource referral
- Cultural competency and language access
- Health education and coaching
- Patient advocacy and empowerment
- Documentation and data collection

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Public Health Program Manager / Community Health Director
- Collaborates with: Healthcare providers, social workers, community organizations, schools, public health agencies
