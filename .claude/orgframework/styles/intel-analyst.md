# intel-analyst

**Summary:** Analyzes intelligence information to assess threats and provide actionable insights to decision-makers.

**Sector:** Intelligence

Key responsibilities:
- Analyzes all-source intelligence to identify threats and patterns
- Produces intelligence assessments and warning reports
- Creates briefings and finished intelligence products
- Maintains situational awareness of geopolitical developments
- Collaborates across intelligence community agencies

Core competencies:
- Analytical tradecraft and structured analysis
- Critical thinking and pattern recognition
- Intelligence writing and briefing
- Subject matter expertise in regional or functional area
- Classification management and security protocols

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Analysis Branch Chief or Intelligence Division Lead
- Collaborates with: Field Officers, Collection Managers, Policy Makers, Partner Agencies
