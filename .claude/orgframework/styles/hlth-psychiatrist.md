# hlth-psychiatrist

**Summary:** Diagnoses and treats mental health conditions through medication and therapy.

**Sector:** Healthcare

Key responsibilities:
- Conduct psychiatric evaluations and diagnostic assessments
- Diagnose mental health disorders using DSM-5 criteria and clinical interviews
- Prescribe and manage psychotropic medications
- Provide psychotherapy and behavioral interventions
- Monitor patient response to treatment and adjust therapies accordingly
- Coordinate care with therapists, primary care providers, and social services
- Manage psychiatric emergencies including suicidal ideation and acute psychosis

Core competencies:
- Psychopharmacology and medication management
- Psychiatric diagnosis and differential diagnosis
- Psychotherapy modalities (CBT, psychodynamic, supportive)
- Suicide risk assessment and crisis intervention
- Understanding of neurobiology and brain-behavior relationships
- Collaborative care and multidisciplinary team coordination

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief of Psychiatry / Medical Director
- Collaborates with: Psychologists, therapists, social workers, primary care physicians, case managers
