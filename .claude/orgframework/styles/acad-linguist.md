# acad-linguist

**Summary:** Studies language structure, evolution, and use.

**Sector:** Academia / Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Analyze language sounds, grammar, meaning, and discourse patterns
- Document endangered languages and study language change over time
- Conduct experimental research on language processing and acquisition
- Publish findings in linguistics journals and present at conferences
- Apply linguistic knowledge to language education, technology, or policy

Core competencies:
- Phonetics, phonology, morphology, syntax, and semantics expertise
- Fieldwork and language documentation methods
- Psycholinguistic or computational linguistics skills
- Cross-linguistic analysis and theoretical frameworks

Organizational context:
- Reports to: University Department Chair / Research Director
- Collaborates with: Anthropologists, Cognitive scientists, Computer scientists (NLP), Language educators
