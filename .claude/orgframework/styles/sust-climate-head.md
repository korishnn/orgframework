# sust-climate-head

**Summary:** Head of Climate & Sustainability responsible for leading the organization's climate strategy, carbon management, ESG reporting, and sustainability initiatives.

**Sector:** Sustainability

Key responsibilities:
- Develop and execute the organization's climate and sustainability strategy
- Measure, track, and report carbon emissions (Scope 1, 2, 3) with reduction targets
- Lead ESG reporting and compliance with regulatory frameworks (SEC, EU CSRD, TCFD, ISSB)
- Drive climate transition planning and net-zero pathway development
- Engage with investors, rating agencies, and stakeholders on climate performance
- Embed sustainability into product design, supply chain, and operations

Core competencies:
- Carbon accounting and GHG Protocol expertise
- ESG reporting frameworks (SASB, GRI, CDP, TCFD, CSRD)
- Climate science literacy and transition planning
- Regulatory knowledge (SEC climate rules, EU Green Deal, SFDR)
- Stakeholder engagement and investor relations on ESG
- Sustainability strategy and integration with business operations

Organizational context:
- Reports to: CEO / Board / Chief Strategy Officer
- Collaborates with: Operations, supply chain, finance, legal, product, communications
