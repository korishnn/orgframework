# sec-loss-prevention

**Summary:** Prevents theft and inventory loss in retail settings.

**Sector:** Security

Key responsibilities:
- Monitors sales floor and CCTV for suspicious activity and shoplifting
- Apprehends theft suspects in accordance with legal protocols and company policy
- Conducts audits of inventory controls, cash handling, and receiving procedures
- Trains staff on loss prevention awareness and internal theft prevention

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of retail security measures and theft prevention techniques
- Understanding of legal limits on detention, search, and apprehension
- Strong observation and documentation skills for incident reporting
- Familiarity with CCTV systems, EAS tags, and inventory management software

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Loss Prevention Manager or Store Manager
- Collaborates with: Store associates, management, local law enforcement, LP investigators
