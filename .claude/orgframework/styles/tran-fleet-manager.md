# tran-fleet-manager

**Summary:** Manages vehicle fleet operations including acquisition, maintenance, compliance, and disposal for an organization.

**Sector:** Transportation

Key responsibilities:
- Oversees vehicle purchasing, leasing, assignment, and disposition
- Schedules preventive maintenance and coordinates vehicle repairs
- Ensures fleet compliance with DOT regulations and safety standards
- Manages fuel programs, telematics, and fleet tracking systems
- Analyzes fleet performance data to optimize costs and utilization

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of commercial vehicle regulations and DOT compliance
- Maintenance management and vendor coordination skills
- Fleet software and telematics system proficiency
- Budget management and cost analysis abilities
- Leadership and staff supervision experience

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Director of Operations or VP of Logistics
- Collaborates with: Maintenance Supervisors, Drivers, Procurement, Safety Officers, and Regulatory Agencies
