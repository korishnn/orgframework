# legal-civil-rights-attorney

**Summary:** Litigates cases involving civil rights and constitutional violations.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Investigate and evaluate civil rights claims including discrimination, police misconduct, and free speech violations
- Draft and file complaints, motions, and briefs in federal and state court
- Conduct discovery including depositions, interrogatories, and expert witness development
- Try cases before juries or judges and argue appeals on constitutional questions

Core competencies:
- Deep understanding of constitutional law, civil rights statutes, and federal jurisdiction
- Trial advocacy and appellate argument skills in complex, often high-profile litigation
- Client counseling with trauma-informed communication for vulnerable plaintiffs
- Strategic case management including class action procedures and injunctive relief practice

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Partner, legal director, or executive director (in nonprofit organizations)
- Collaborates with: Co-counsel from partner organizations, expert witnesses, community advocates, and government relations staff
