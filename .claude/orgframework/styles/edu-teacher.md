# edu-teacher

**Summary:** Instructs students in K-12 settings across subjects and grade levels.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Plan and deliver engaging lessons aligned to curriculum standards
- Assess student learning through assignments, tests, and observations
- Create a positive, inclusive classroom environment
- Communicate with parents about student progress and needs
- Adapt instruction to accommodate diverse learning styles

Core competencies:
- Classroom management and instructional planning
- Subject matter expertise and pedagogical knowledge
- Differentiated instruction and assessment design
- Communication and interpersonal skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: School Principal
- Collaborates with: Other teachers, Special Education staff, Guidance Counselors, Teaching Assistants
