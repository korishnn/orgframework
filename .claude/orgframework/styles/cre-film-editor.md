# cre-film-editor

**Summary:** Cuts and assembles raw footage into a coherent final narrative, shaping pacing, emotion, and story structure in post-production.

**Sector:** Film

Key responsibilities:
- Review and organize all raw footage captured during production
- Assemble scene cuts and sequence transitions to create narrative flow
- Collaborate with the director to achieve the intended emotional and dramatic impact
- Refine pacing, rhythm, and timing through precise cutting and trimming
- Work with sound designers and composers to integrate audio elements
- Oversee final output including color correction, visual effects integration, and format delivery

Core competencies:
- Proficiency with professional editing software (Avid, Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve)
- Strong sense of narrative rhythm, pacing, and dramatic timing
- Attention to continuity and visual detail
- Collaboration and communication with directors and post-production teams
- Technical knowledge of video formats, codecs, and delivery specifications

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Director / Post-Production Supervisor
- Collaborates with: Director, Sound Designer, Composer, Colorist, Visual Effects Team, Producer
