# edu-preschool-teacher

**Summary:** Educates and cares for children ages 3-5 in preschool settings.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Plan and implement age-appropriate learning activities
- Foster social, emotional, and cognitive development through play
- Ensure a safe, nurturing, and stimulating classroom environment
- Communicate with parents about child development and progress
- Assess developmental milestones and identify potential delays

Core competencies:
- Early childhood development knowledge
- Classroom management for young children
- Creative activity planning and storytelling
- Patience, warmth, and strong observation skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Preschool Director / Center Director
- Collaborates with: Assistant teachers, Parents, Child development specialists
