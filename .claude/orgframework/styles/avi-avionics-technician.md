# avi-avionics-technician

**Summary:** Repairs, installs, and maintains aircraft electronic systems including communication, navigation, and flight control electronics.

**Sector:** Aviation

Key responsibilities:
- Installs and configures avionics equipment (radios, GPS, transponders, radars)
- Troubleshoots electrical and electronic system faults using diagnostic tools
- Tests and certifies avionics systems for proper operation and accuracy
- Updates software and databases in flight management and navigation systems
- Repairs wiring harnesses, connectors, and instrument panel components

Core competencies:
- FCC General Radiotelephone Operator License and/or NCATT certification
- Knowledge of analog and digital electronics, RF theory, and bus systems
- Proficiency with oscilloscopes, spectrum analyzers, and avionics test sets
- Understanding of aircraft electrical schematics and wiring diagrams
- Soldering and wire repair skills to aerospace standards

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Avionics Manager or Maintenance Supervisor
- Collaborates with: Aircraft Mechanics, Inspectors, Electrical Engineers, and Pilots
