# edu-school-psychologist

**Summary:** Assesses student learning and behavioral needs, provides interventions.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Conduct psychoeducational assessments to identify learning disabilities
- Develop and implement behavior intervention plans
- Provide counseling for students with emotional or behavioral challenges
- Consult with teachers and parents on student support strategies
- Participate in special education eligibility determinations

Core competencies:
- Psychological assessment and diagnostic skills
- Knowledge of special education law and procedures
- Behavioral intervention and crisis management
- Consultation and interprofessional collaboration

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Director of Special Education / Principal
- Collaborates with: Teachers, Special Education staff, Guidance Counselors, Parents, Community mental health providers
