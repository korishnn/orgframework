# govt-police-officer

**Summary:** Enforces laws, responds to incidents, and protects public safety within a jurisdiction.

**Sector:** Government

Key responsibilities:
- Patrols assigned areas to deter and detect crime
- Responds to emergency and non-emergency calls for service
- Conducts investigations and gathers evidence
- Makes arrests and issues citations for violations
- Builds community relationships through outreach programs

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of criminal law and procedures
- Crisis de-escalation and conflict resolution
- Physical fitness and defensive tactics
- Report writing and evidence documentation
- Community policing and interpersonal skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Sergeant or Police Captain
- Collaborates with: Detectives, Dispatchers, Firefighters, EMS, District Attorney's Office
