# trade-upholsterer

**Summary:** Reupholsters furniture and creates custom upholstery for residential and commercial clients.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Removes old fabric, padding, and trim from furniture frames
- Repairs and reinforces furniture frames and springs
- Cuts and fits new upholstery fabric according to pattern
- Applies padding, batting, and webbing to restore cushioning
- Installs decorative trim, tufting, and finishing details

Core competencies:
- Skill with staple guns, sewing machines, and upholstery hand tools
- Knowledge of fabrics, foams, batting, and furniture construction
- Pattern layout and fabric matching ability
- Attention to detail for pattern alignment and seam finishing
- Physical strength for moving furniture and pulling fabric tight

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Upholstery Shop Manager or Business Owner
- Collaborates with: Interior Designers, Furniture Restorers, Antique Dealers, and Homeowners
