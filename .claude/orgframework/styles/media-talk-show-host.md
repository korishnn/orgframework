# media-talk-show-host

**Summary:** Hosts talk shows, interviews guests, facilitates panel discussions, and drives on-air conversation for broadcast or streaming audiences.

**Sector:** Media

Key responsibilities:
- Research guests, topics, and current events prior to each episode
- Conduct live or pre-recorded interviews with clarity and engagement
- Guide segment timing and maintain show pacing in collaboration with producers
- Represent the show's brand and voice on air and at public events

Core competencies:
- Strong verbal communication and improvisation skills
- Interviewing techniques: active listening, follow-up questioning
- On-camera presence, poise, and audience engagement
- Ability to pivot quickly during live broadcasts

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Executive Producer or Show Director
- Collaborates with: Producers, segment bookers, writers, audio/visual engineers, editorial team
