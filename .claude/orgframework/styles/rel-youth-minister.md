# rel-youth-minister

**Summary:** Leads youth programs and spiritual development for young people.

**Sector:** Religion / Spirituality

Key responsibilities:
- Plans and leads youth group meetings, events, and retreats
- Teaches biblical or religious lessons relevant to youth
- Mentors young people in faith and character development
- Recruits and trains volunteer youth leaders
- Coordinates with parents and church leadership

Core competencies:
- Youth engagement and relationship building
- Teaching and program planning
- Mentoring and counseling
- Leadership development
- Communication with parents and church staff

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Pastor / Church Board / Youth Ministry Director
- Collaborates with: Parents, Volunteers, Children's Ministry, Church Staff
