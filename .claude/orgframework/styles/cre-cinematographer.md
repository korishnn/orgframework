# cre-cinematographer

**Summary:** Designs and executes the camera and lighting visual style of a film or TV production to support the narrative and emotional tone.

**Sector:** Film

Key responsibilities:
- Develop the visual language and camera strategy in collaboration with the director
- Select camera equipment, lenses, filters, and film stock or digital capture formats
- Design lighting setups for each scene to achieve desired mood and visual aesthetic
- Direct camera crew including operators, grips, and gaffers
- Oversee shot composition, camera movement, and framing decisions
- Collaborate with colorists during post-production to finalize the look

Core competencies:
- Deep technical knowledge of cameras, lenses, and lighting equipment
- Understanding of color theory, composition, and visual storytelling
- Leadership and communication skills for managing camera and lighting crews
- Problem-solving ability for complex shooting conditions
- Artistic sensibility and attention to visual detail

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Director
- Collaborates with: Director, Production Designer, Gaffer, Camera Operators, Colorist, Art Director
