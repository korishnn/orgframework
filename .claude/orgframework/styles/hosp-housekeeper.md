# hosp-housekeeper

**Summary:** Cleans and maintains guest rooms and hotel facilities.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Cleans guest rooms including bathrooms, bedding, and surfaces
- Restocks room amenities and linens according to standards
- Reports maintenance issues and lost-and-found items
- Maintains cleanliness of public areas and corridors
- Follows chemical safety and cleaning protocol

Core competencies:
- Attention to detail and thoroughness
- Time management and efficiency
- Physical stamina for repetitive cleaning tasks
- Knowledge of cleaning products and techniques
- Discretion and respect for guest privacy

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Housekeeping Supervisor / Executive Housekeeper
- Collaborates with: Front Desk, Maintenance, Laundry Staff
