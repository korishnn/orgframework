# mkt-analytics

**Summary:** Marketing Analytics professional measures campaign performance, marketing attribution, customer acquisition costs, and ROI across all marketing channels to enable data-driven decision-making.

**Level:** IC
**Reports to:** Marketing Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Build and maintain marketing dashboards and performance reports for all marketing channels and campaigns
- Implement and manage marketing attribution models to understand channel and campaign contribution
- Analyze campaign effectiveness, ROI, and efficiency across paid, owned, and earned channels
- Track and report on key marketing metrics including CAC, LTV, payback period, funnel conversion rates, and channel mix
- Partner with marketing teams on measurement strategy, KPI definition, and goal setting
- Conduct deep-dive analyses to identify optimization opportunities and growth areas
- Ensure data accuracy, consistency, and governance across marketing data sources and systems
- Implement and maintain marketing tracking infrastructure including UTM parameters, event tracking, and conversion pixels
- Build and maintain marketing data models for reporting, forecasting, and analysis
- Collaborate with data engineering on marketing data pipeline requirements and data quality
- Present actionable insights and recommendations to marketing leadership and stakeholders
- Stay current with marketing analytics tools, attribution approaches, and measurement best practices

## Core Competencies
- Strong analytical and quantitative reasoning skills with attention to data accuracy
- Proficiency in analytics platforms (GA4, Amplitude, Mixpanel, Heap) and marketing analytics tools
- Experience with marketing attribution models (first-touch, last-touch, multi-touch, data-driven)
- Advanced data visualization skills (Looker Studio, Tableau, Power BI, Mode, Metabase)
- Strong SQL skills for querying marketing data across databases and warehouses
- Understanding of marketing operations including campaign tracking, lead scoring, and funnel management
- Knowledge of marketing technology ecosystem (CRM, ESP, ad platforms, analytics tools)
- Experience with A/B test analysis and statistical significance testing
- Ability to translate complex data into clear, actionable business recommendations
- Understanding of customer LTV modeling and cohort analysis methodologies

## Tools & Technology
- Analytics: Google Analytics 4, Amplitude, Mixpanel, Heap, Pendo, PostHog
- Visualization: Looker Studio, Tableau, Power BI, Mode, Metabase, Hex
- Data: SQL, Snowflake/BigQuery/Redshift, dbt, Excel, Google Sheets

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Marketing data accuracy (tracking coverage, data quality scores, dashboard freshness)
- Reporting timeliness and stakeholder satisfaction with analytics deliverables
- Marketing ROI and efficiency metrics tracked and reported consistently
- Attribution model clarity and cross-team alignment on measurement approach
- Dashboard adoption rates and self-service analytics usage
- Insights generated that led to measurable marketing optimization
- Forecast accuracy for marketing performance predictions

## Career Progression
- Junior/Analyst (0-2 years): Builds standard reports, maintains dashboards, validates data quality
- Analyst (2-4 years): Owns channel-level analytics, manages attribution, conducts deep-dive analyses
- Senior (4-7 years): Leads marketing measurement strategy, builds advanced analytics, mentors analysts
- Manager/Director (7-10 years): Manages analytics team, shapes measurement strategy, drives data culture
- VP (10+ years): Executive analytics leadership, company-wide measurement strategy, data-driven marketing culture
- Typical progression: Junior to Analyst 1-2 years, Analyst to Senior 2-3 years, Senior to Manager 2-4 years

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Morning data quality check ensuring dashboards and reports are accurate and up to date
- Report building and dashboard maintenance for recurring marketing performance views
- Ad-hoc analysis requests from marketing teams for campaign performance and optimization insights
- Data QA and validation as new campaigns launch with tracking requirements
- Weekly marketing team meetings to present analytical findings and recommendations
- Attribution model review and analysis to understand channel performance contribution
- Funnel analysis deep dives to identify conversion opportunities and bottlenecks
- Monthly and quarterly performance reporting cycles for marketing leadership

## Regional Nuances
- NA: Most advanced marketing analytics maturity; strong emphasis on attribution modeling; sophisticated tool ecosystem; privacy-challenge-driven analytics evolution
- EMEA: GDPR significantly impacts analytics tracking and data collection; privacy-compliant analytics approach required; growing adoption of consent-based analytics; strong focus on data governance
- APAC: Rapidly evolving marketing analytics; mobile-first analytics focus; varying data maturity across markets; cost-sensitive tool selection; growing SQL and data skills in India
- LATAM: Growing marketing analytics function; cost-effective tool choices; tracking infrastructure still developing; strong Excel usage alongside emerging tool adoption

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: SaaS metrics expertise (CAC, LTV, payback, churn); trial-to-paid funnel analytics; product-qualified lead tracking; expansion revenue measurement; usage-based analytics
- E-commerce: Purchase funnel analytics; customer lifetime value modeling; channel-mix optimization; ROAS measurement; cohort analysis for retention; inventory-demand correlation
- Fintech: Compliance-aware analytics; transaction data integration; risk-adjusted CAC; regulatory reporting requirements; financial forecast alignment
- Healthcare: HIPAA-compliant analytics; PHI data handling restrictions; patient acquisition cost analysis; provider funnel measurement; regulatory reporting for marketing data

## Common Challenges
- Maintaining accurate attribution as customer journeys grow more complex and multi-channel
- Adapting to privacy-driven tracking limitations (cookie deprecation, iOS ATT, regulation changes)
- Ensuring data quality and consistency across disconnected marketing tools and data sources
- Balancing comprehensive measurement with practical, actionable reporting
- Getting alignment on marketing KPIs and attribution models across teams and leadership
- Keeping up with evolving analytics tools, platform changes, and measurement methodologies

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- GDPR for EMEA analytics data collection and consent management
- CCPA for California marketing data privacy
- Cookie consent regulations (EU ePrivacy Directive) for analytics tracking
- HIPAA for healthcare marketing analytics data
- PCI-DSS for marketing data containing payment information
- Data retention regulations for marketing analytics data
- Industry-specific data privacy regulations affecting marketing measurement
