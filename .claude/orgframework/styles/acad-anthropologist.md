# acad-anthropologist

**Summary:** Studies human cultures, evolution, and social organization.

**Sector:** Academia / Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Conduct ethnographic fieldwork and participant observation
- Analyze cultural practices, social structures, and human behavior
- Study human biological and cultural evolution
- Write research reports, monographs, and journal articles
- Advise on cultural sensitivity and cross-cultural understanding

Core competencies:
- Ethnographic research and qualitative methods
- Cross-cultural analysis and cultural theory
- Biological or linguistic anthropology expertise
- Academic writing and ethical research practices

Organizational context:
- Reports to: University Department Chair / Research Director
- Collaborates with: Sociologists, Historians, Archaeologists, Indigenous communities, Policy advisors
