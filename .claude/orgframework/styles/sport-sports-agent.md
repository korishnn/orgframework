# sport-sports-agent

**Summary:** Represents athletes in contract negotiations and career management.

**Sector:** Sports

Key responsibilities:
- Negotiates player contracts, endorsements, and sponsorship deals
- Advises athletes on career decisions and financial planning
- Markets clients to teams, brands, and media opportunities
- Manages contract compliance and legal matters
- Builds relationships with team executives and brand partners

Core competencies:
- Contract negotiation and legal knowledge
- Business development and networking
- Understanding of league rules and collective bargaining
- Financial and career advisory
- Communication and client relationship management

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Agency Partner / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Athletes, Team Executives, Brands, Legal Counsel
