# agri-greenhouse-manager

**Summary:** Manages greenhouse operations for year-round crop production.

**Sector:** Agriculture

Key responsibilities:
- Plan planting schedules and manage crop rotation to maximize year-round yield
- Control greenhouse climate systems including heating, cooling, irrigation, and lighting
- Supervise planting, pruning, harvesting, and post-harvest handling crews
- Monitor plant health and manage integrated pest and disease control programs

Core competencies:
- Deep understanding of plant physiology, hydroponics, and controlled-environment agriculture
- Technical proficiency with environmental control systems and irrigation automation
- Leadership and crew management skills for seasonal and permanent staff
- Record-keeping and data analysis for yield optimization and regulatory compliance

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Farm owner, operations director, or agribusiness general manager
- Collaborates with: Plant breeders, agricultural supply vendors, food safety auditors, and distribution partners
