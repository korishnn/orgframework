# hosp-restaurant-manager

**Summary:** Manages restaurant operations, staff, and financial performance.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Oversees daily restaurant operations and service quality
- Manages staff scheduling, hiring, training, and performance
- Controls costs including food, labor, and operating expenses
- Ensures compliance with health, safety, and liquor regulations
- Drives revenue through marketing, events, and customer experience

Core competencies:
- Operations and financial management
- Team leadership and development
- Customer service excellence
- Inventory and cost control
- Problem-solving and decision-making

Organizational context:
- Reports to: General Manager / Owner / Regional Manager
- Collaborates with: Chef, Bar Manager, Marketing, HR
