# hosp-sommelier

**Summary:** Curates wine lists and advises on wine pairings.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Curates and maintains the restaurant wine list and beverage program
- Advises guests on wine selections and food pairings
- Purchases and manages wine inventory and storage
- Educates service staff on wine knowledge and service
- Leads wine tastings and staff training sessions

Core competencies:
- Extensive wine and beverage knowledge
- Palate for pairing with menu items
- Inventory management and purchasing
- Staff training and education
- Upscale hospitality and guest interaction

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Restaurant Manager / General Manager
- Collaborates with: Chef, Servers, Beverage Distributors
