# tran-bus-driver

**Summary:** Drives assigned bus routes transporting passengers safely and efficiently in municipal, school, or charter operations.

**Sector:** Transportation

Key responsibilities:
- Operates buses on fixed routes or charter itineraries
- Collects fares, issues transfers, and assists passengers with boarding
- Inspects bus before trips for safety and mechanical condition
- Announces stops and responds to passenger questions
- Follows traffic laws and safety procedures for passenger protection

Core competencies:
- CDL with Passenger and School Bus endorsements as applicable
- Defensive driving and large vehicle maneuvering skills
- Customer service and conflict de-escalation ability
- Punctuality and adherence to route schedules
- Patience and focus in high-traffic conditions

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Transit Operations Supervisor or Route Manager
- Collaborates with: Dispatchers, Transit Mechanics, Transit Police, and Passengers
