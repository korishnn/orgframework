# edu-curriculum-developer

**Summary:** Creates and aligns curricula to educational standards.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Develop scope and sequence documents for courses and grade levels
- Align curriculum materials to state and national standards
- Create instructional units, lesson plans, and assessment materials
- Review and revise existing curricula based on feedback and outcomes
- Provide professional development on curriculum implementation

Core competencies:
- Deep knowledge of educational standards and frameworks
- Curriculum mapping and backward design methodology
- Assessment development and alignment expertise
- Collaboration with educators and content experts

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Curriculum Director / Assistant Superintendent
- Collaborates with: Teachers, Department heads, Instructional Designers, Assessment specialists
