# sci-lab-director

**Summary:** Leads a research laboratory's strategy, funding, and operations.

**Sector:** Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Define the lab's research agenda and scientific priorities
- Secure funding through grants, contracts, and partnerships
- Supervise research staff, postdocs, and students
- Oversee lab budget, compliance, and resource allocation
- Publish influential research and represent the lab externally

Core competencies:
- Scientific vision and research strategy
- Grant acquisition and financial management
- Team leadership and mentoring
- External relations and scientific communication

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Department Chair / Institute Director
- Collaborates with: Research scientists, Funding agencies, Academic collaborators, Industry partners
