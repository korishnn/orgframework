# art-art-therapist

**Summary:** Uses art-making as therapeutic practice with clients, integrating creative expression with psychological treatment to support mental health and well-being.

**Sector:** Visual Arts

Key responsibilities:
- Facilitate art therapy sessions with individuals and groups
- Assess client needs and develop therapeutic art interventions
- Guide clients in using art materials for self-expression and exploration
- Document and evaluate client progress toward therapeutic goals
- Maintain client confidentiality and professional ethical standards
- Collaborate with healthcare teams on integrated treatment plans

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of art therapy theory, techniques, and clinical applications
- Understanding of psychology, counseling, and therapeutic relationships
- Proficiency with a range of art materials and creative modalities
- Clinical assessment, documentation, and treatment planning skills
- Empathy, active listening, and cultural competence in therapy settings

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Clinical Director / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Psychologist, Social Worker, Occupational Therapist, Educator, Healthcare Team
