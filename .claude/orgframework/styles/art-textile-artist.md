# art-textile-artist

**Summary:** Creates fiber and textile artworks using weaving, embroidery, dyeing, felting, and other fabric-based techniques for artistic expression.

**Sector:** Visual Arts

Key responsibilities:
- Create textile artworks using weaving, embroidery, felting, or fabric manipulation
- Experiment with fibers, dyes, and textile techniques for artistic expression
- Develop designs and patterns for handcrafted textile pieces
- Maintain textile equipment including looms, sewing machines, and dye stations
- Exhibit and sell textile works in galleries, craft fairs, and exhibitions
- Commission custom textile pieces for clients and interior projects

Core competencies:
- Mastery of textile techniques appropriate to chosen medium
- Understanding of fiber properties, dyes, and textile chemistry
- Design sensibility including color, pattern, and texture
- Knowledge of textile history, traditions, and contemporary practice
- Technical skills for equipment operation and maintenance

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Gallery Owner, Interior Designer, Curator, Fashion Designer, Commissioning Client
