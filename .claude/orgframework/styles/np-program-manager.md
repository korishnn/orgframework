# np-program-manager

**Summary:** Manages nonprofit programs, budgets, and outcomes to deliver mission-aligned services.

**Sector:** Nonprofit

Key responsibilities:
- Plans and implements program activities and services
- Manages program budgets and resource allocation
- Supervises program staff and volunteers
- Tracks program outcomes and prepares impact reports
- Ensures compliance with grant requirements and regulations

Core competencies:
- Program planning and project management
- Budget management and financial oversight
- Staff supervision and team leadership
- Monitoring and evaluation methodologies
- Grant compliance and reporting

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Executive Director or Program Director
- Collaborates with: Grant Writers, Finance Staff, Community Partners, Program Evaluators
