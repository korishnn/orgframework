# sport-referee

**Summary:** Officiates sports games ensuring rule compliance and fair play.

**Sector:** Sports

Key responsibilities:
- Enforces game rules and ensures fair competition
- Makes real-time calls on fouls, violations, and scoring
- Communicates with coaches, players, and fellow officials
- Keeps game time and score when applicable
- Prepares game reports and documents rule infractions

Core competencies:
- Comprehensive rulebook knowledge
- Decision-making under pressure
- Positioning and game flow management
- Communication and conflict resolution
- Physical fitness for game coverage

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Officials Supervisor / League Office
- Collaborates with: Other Officials, Game Staff, League Administrators
