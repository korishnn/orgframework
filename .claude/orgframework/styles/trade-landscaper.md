# trade-landscaper

**Summary:** Designs and maintains outdoor landscapes and green spaces for residential and commercial properties.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Mows lawns, trims edges, and maintains turf areas
- Plants trees, shrubs, flowers, and ground cover
- Installs irrigation systems, hardscapes, and landscape features
- Applies fertilizers, pesticides, and soil amendments
- Operates mowers, trimmers, blowers, and other landscape equipment

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of plant species, soil types, and growing conditions
- Proficiency with landscape maintenance and irrigation equipment
- Understanding of landscape design principles and drainage
- Physical endurance for outdoor work in varied weather
- Customer service and client communication skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Landscaping Crew Lead or Landscape Manager
- Collaborates with: Arborists, Irrigation Specialists, Garden Designers, and Property Owners
