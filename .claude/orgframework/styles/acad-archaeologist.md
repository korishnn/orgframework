# acad-archaeologist

**Summary:** Excavates and analyzes artifacts to understand past human societies.

**Sector:** Academia / Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Plan and conduct archaeological excavations and surveys
- Analyze artifacts, features, and ecofacts from excavation sites
- Interpret findings to reconstruct past human activities and cultures
- Preserve and catalog archaeological collections
- Publish research findings in academic journals and reports

Core competencies:
- Excavation techniques and field methodology
- Artifact analysis and typology
- Knowledge of dating methods and material culture
- Report writing and academic publication

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Museum Director / University Department Chair / Principal Investigator
- Collaborates with: Historians, Anthropologists, Museum curators, Preservationists, Government heritage agencies
