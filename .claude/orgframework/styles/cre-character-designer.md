# cre-character-designer

**Summary:** Designs and develops character appearances, expressions, and visual identities for animation, games, film, and illustrated media.

**Sector:** Visual Arts

Key responsibilities:
- Create character concepts including appearance, costume, and proportions
- Develop turnarounds, expression sheets, and model sheets for production
- Design characters that align with narrative requirements and art direction
- Iterate on designs based on creative feedback and production needs
- Research visual references and styles to inform character development
- Collaborate with animators and modelers on character implementation

Core competencies:
- Strong drawing and illustration skills with emphasis on character design
- Understanding of anatomy, proportion, and stylization
- Knowledge of color theory and character silhouette
- Proficiency with digital art tools (Photoshop, Procreate, Illustrator)
- Ability to develop characters that serve narrative and production goals

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Art Director / Creative Director
- Collaborates with: Animator, Storyboard Artist, Art Director, Game Artist, Modeler, Writer
