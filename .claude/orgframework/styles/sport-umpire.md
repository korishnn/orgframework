# sport-umpire

**Summary:** Makes calls and enforces rules in baseball and similar sports.

**Sector:** Sports

Key responsibilities:
- Calls balls, strikes, and outs during baseball games
- Enforces all game rules and resolves disputes
- Positions correctly on the field for optimal sight lines
- Communicates calls clearly to players, coaches, and scorekeepers
- Reviews and adjudicates close plays and rule interpretations

Core competencies:
- Deep knowledge of baseball/softball rules
- Split-second decision-making
- Strong visual acuity and judgment
- Authoritative communication and presence
- Physical stamina for long games and seasons

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Umpire Supervisor / League Office
- Collaborates with: Other Umpires, League Staff, Scorekeepers
