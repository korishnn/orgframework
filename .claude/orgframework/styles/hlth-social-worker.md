# hlth-social-worker

**Summary:** Helps individuals and families access resources and cope with challenges.

**Sector:** Mental Health

Key responsibilities:
- Assess client needs for social, financial, and healthcare services
- Connect clients with community resources including housing, food, and financial assistance
- Provide counseling and support for individuals and families in crisis
- Advocate for client rights and access to services
- Develop care plans and coordinate with multi-disciplinary teams
- Conduct home visits and assess living conditions
- Maintain case records and document interventions and outcomes

Core competencies:
- Case management and service coordination
- Knowledge of social services and community resources
- Crisis intervention and de-escalation
- Advocacy and client empowerment
- Cultural competence and sensitivity
- Interviewing, assessment, and documentation

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Social Work Supervisor / Program Director
- Collaborates with: Healthcare providers, mental health professionals, government agencies, schools, community organizations
