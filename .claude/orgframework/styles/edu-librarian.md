# edu-librarian

**Summary:** Manages library resources, research support, and information literacy.

**Sector:** Education / Academia

Key responsibilities:
- Curate and maintain library collections of books, journals, and digital resources
- Teach information literacy and research skills to students and faculty
- Provide reference services and research guidance
- Manage library budgets, cataloging systems, and technology
- Promote reading and lifelong learning through programs and events

Core competencies:
- Information organization and cataloging expertise
- Research assistance and database navigation
- Digital literacy and emerging technology awareness
- Collection development and budget management

Organizational context:
- Reports to: School Principal / Library Director / Dean
- Collaborates with: Teachers, Faculty, Students, IT staff, Publishers and vendors
