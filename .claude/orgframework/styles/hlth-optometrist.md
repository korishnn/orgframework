# hlth-optometrist

**Summary:** Examines eyes and prescribes corrective lenses.

**Sector:** Alternative Medicine

Key responsibilities:
- Perform comprehensive eye examinations including visual acuity and refraction
- Diagnose refractive errors and prescribe eyeglasses and contact lenses
- Detect eye diseases such as glaucoma, cataracts, and macular degeneration
- Manage binocular vision disorders and provide vision therapy
- Prescribe topical medications for eye conditions where permitted
- Provide pre- and postoperative care for refractive and cataract surgery patients
- Educate patients on eye health, vision care, and preventive measures

Core competencies:
- Refraction and visual assessment
- Contact lens fitting and evaluation
- Ocular disease detection and diagnosis
- Binocular vision assessment and management
- Low vision evaluation and rehabilitation
- Knowledge of ocular anatomy and physiology

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Practice Owner / Clinical Director
- Collaborates with: Ophthalmologists, opticians, primary care physicians, optical laboratory staff
