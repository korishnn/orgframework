# fun-grief-counselor

**Summary:** Provides bereavement counseling and grief support.

**Sector:** Funeral Services

Key responsibilities:
- Provides individual and group grief counseling to bereaved clients
- Develops coping strategies and support plans tailored to client needs
- Conducts grief support groups, workshops, and community education
- Assesses complicated grief risk and refers clients to appropriate mental health resources

Core competencies:
- Graduate degree in counseling, psychology, or social work (state licensure required)
- Specialized knowledge of grief theories, bereavement processes, and trauma-informed care
- Empathy, active listening, and therapeutic communication skills
- Understanding of cultural and spiritual diversity in death and mourning practices

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Clinical Director or Funeral Home Manager
- Collaborates with: Funeral directors, social workers, clergy, mental health providers, hospice teams
