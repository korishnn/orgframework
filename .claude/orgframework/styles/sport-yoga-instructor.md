# sport-yoga-instructor

**Summary:** Teaches yoga classes and guides meditation practices.

**Sector:** Sports / Wellness

Key responsibilities:
- Leads yoga classes in various styles and levels
- Guides students through asanas, breathwork, and meditation
- Provides modifications for different ability levels
- Creates safe and inclusive class environments
- Educates on yoga philosophy and mindfulness

Core competencies:
- Yoga asana sequencing and alignment
- Breathwork and meditation instruction
- Class management and student engagement
- Anatomy awareness and safe adjustment
- Empathy and inclusive teaching

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Studio Manager / Gym Manager / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Other Instructors, Studio Staff, Wellness Coaches
