# cre-influencer

**Summary:** Builds audience and influence on social media platforms, creating content that shapes opinions and driving engagement around personal brand.

**Sector:** Digital Content

Key responsibilities:
- Create authentic content that resonates with target audiences
- Grow and nurture a loyal following across social media platforms
- Partner with brands for sponsored content and endorsements
- Engage with followers through comments, messages, and community features
- Develop a personal brand identity and content niche
- Track and report engagement and performance metrics to partners

Core competencies:
- Strong content creation skills across photo, video, and written formats
- Understanding of social media trends, algorithms, and growth strategies
- Authentic personal branding and audience connection abilities
- Negotiation and brand partnership management skills
- Analytics literacy for measuring content performance

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Talent Manager / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Content Creator, Brand Manager, Social Media Manager, Photographer, Agent
