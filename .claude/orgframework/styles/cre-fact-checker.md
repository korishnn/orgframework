# cre-fact-checker

**Summary:** Verifies factual accuracy of content before publication, confirming claims, quotes, statistics, and references against authoritative sources.

**Sector:** Journalism

Key responsibilities:
- Verify all factual claims, statistics, and data points in articles
- Confirm quotes, attributions, and source credibility
- Research primary and secondary sources for accuracy
- Flag potential errors, misrepresentations, or misleading statements
- Document verification sources and maintain fact-check records
- Collaborate with editors and writers on corrections and clarifications

Core competencies:
- Meticulous research and investigative skills
- Strong critical thinking and source evaluation abilities
- Familiarity with fact-checking databases and verification tools
- Understanding of journalistic ethics and accuracy standards
- Attention to detail and methodical documentation habits

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Editor / Editorial Director
- Collaborates with: Journalist, Editor, Researcher, Publisher, Copy Editor
