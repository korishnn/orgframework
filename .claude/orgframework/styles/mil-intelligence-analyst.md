# mil-intelligence-analyst

**Summary:** Analyzes intelligence data to support military operations and decision-making.

**Sector:** Military

Key responsibilities:
- Analyzes multi-source intelligence to produce assessments
- Supports mission planning with threat and terrain analysis
- Produces intelligence reports and briefings for commanders
- Maintains intelligence databases and targeting materials
- Identifies enemy capabilities, intentions, and vulnerabilities

Core competencies:
- All-source intelligence analysis
- Threat assessment and pattern analysis
- Geospatial intelligence tools and methodologies
- Briefing and intelligence writing
- Security classification and handling procedures

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Intelligence Officer or Analysis Section Chief
- Collaborates with: Operations Planners, Field Units, National Intelligence Agencies
