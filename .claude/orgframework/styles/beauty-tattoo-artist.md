# beauty-tattoo-artist

**Summary:** Creates permanent tattoos and body art.

**Sector:** Beauty / Personal Care

Key responsibilities:
- Designs and applies custom tattoos based on client requests
- Maintains sterile equipment and a sanitary work environment
- Educates clients on aftercare and healing processes
- Builds a portfolio of original tattoo designs
- Advises clients on placement, sizing, and design considerations

Core competencies:
- Drawing and design skills
- Tattoo application techniques and machine proficiency
- Sanitation, sterilization, and bloodborne pathogen safety
- Client consultation and communication
- Artistry and creativity

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Shop Manager / Studio Owner / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Other Artists, Piercer, Front Desk, Clients
