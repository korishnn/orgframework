# prd-analytics

**Summary:** Product Analyst responsible for analyzing product usage data, user behavior, and experimentation results to provide actionable insights that drive product decisions and measurable outcomes.

**Level:** IC
**Reports to:** Director of Product / Director of Analytics
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Analyze product usage data to identify trends, behavioral patterns, feature adoption rates, and growth opportunities across the customer base
- Build, maintain, and evolve dashboards for product KPIs, conversion funnels, retention curves, and feature-level health metrics
- Design, analyze, and report on A/B tests and product experiments with statistical rigor and actionable recommendations
- Develop cohort analyses, behavioral segments, and user lifecycle models to understand how different user groups engage with the product
- Partner with product managers to define success metrics, establish baselines, and set measurable targets for product initiatives
- Deliver data-driven insights and actionable recommendations to product teams through reports, presentations, and direct collaboration
- Ensure data quality and proper event tracking implementation across product surfaces — audit instrumentation, identify gaps, and drive remediation
- Develop self-service analytics capabilities for product teams through curated datasets, documentation, and tool training
- Perform ad hoc analyses to answer strategic questions about product performance, user behavior, and market dynamics
- Build predictive models for user churn, feature adoption, and lifetime value in collaboration with data science
- Define and maintain the product data taxonomy ensuring consistent event naming, property definitions, and tracking standards
- Conduct competitive product benchmarking using available data sources and industry benchmarks

## Core Competencies
- Advanced SQL proficiency — complex queries, window functions, CTEs, and query optimization across large datasets
- Statistical analysis and experimentation design — hypothesis testing, sample size calculation, power analysis, A/B test methodology
- Data visualization and dashboard development using modern BI tools
- Product metric frameworks — AARRR (Pirate Metrics), HEART, North Star metric identification, OKR development
- Critical thinking and hypothesis-driven analysis — framing questions, testing assumptions, drawing defensible conclusions
- Communication of data insights to non-technical audiences with compelling data storytelling
- Understanding of event tracking and instrumentation — SDKs, event schemas, property validation
- Programming skills for data manipulation and analysis (Python, R) for advanced analytics
- Business acumen — understanding how product metrics connect to revenue, retention, and business outcomes
- Experimentation platform expertise — feature flagging, randomization, and experiment analysis
- Segmentation and personalization analytics — behavioral cohorts, RFM analysis, propensity modeling
- Data warehouse architecture understanding — star schemas, dimensional modeling, ETL processes

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Analysis impact score — product decisions influenced or changed based on analytical insights
- Experiment velocity and quality — number of tests analyzed with statistically sound methodology
- Dashboard adoption and usage across product teams
- Data quality metrics — tracking coverage percentage, event naming consistency score, data latency SLAs
- Stakeholder satisfaction with analytical support and insight delivery timeliness
- Time from question to answer for ad hoc analysis requests
- Percentage of product decisions informed by data rather than intuition
- Self-service analytics adoption — number of product team members using dashboards and tools independently

## Career Progression
- **Junior Product Analyst / Associate (0-2 years):** Writes basic SQL queries, builds simple dashboards, supports ad hoc analysis requests, learns product analytics tools and metric frameworks. Develops domain knowledge about the product.
- **Product Analyst (2-5 years):** Independently analyzes product data, designs and analyzes A/B tests, builds complex dashboards, partners with product managers. Proficient in SQL, product analytics tools, and basic statistics. Begins developing domain specialization.
- **Senior Product Analyst (5-8 years):** Leads analytical support for a product area. Designs sophisticated experiments and measurement plans. Mentors junior analysts. Drives data quality initiatives. Deep domain expertise. Advanced statistical methods and modeling.
- **Lead / Principal Analyst (8-12 years):** Drives analytical strategy across multiple product areas. Develops measurement frameworks. Builds analytical tools and self-service infrastructure. Influences product strategy through insights. Publishes analytical best practices.
- **Director of Product Analytics / VP Data (12+ years):** Leads an analytics organization. Sets analytical strategy and culture. Manages analytics team hiring and development. Partners with executive leadership on data-driven strategy. May transition to Chief Data Officer.

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Start with metrics monitoring — review key product dashboards for anomalies, investigate metric movements, and flag significant changes to product teams
- Experiment analysis — review active A/B tests for statistical significance, sample ratio mismatch, and segment-level effects; prepare results reports
- Event tracking review — audit new event implementations for accuracy, identify missing or misconfigured events, work with engineering on fixes
- Dashboard development — build or update product dashboards responding to new analytical needs, changing metrics, or strategic priorities
- Ad hoc analysis requests from product managers — investigate specific questions about user behavior, feature performance, and conversion flows
- Data modeling and metric definition — collaborate with engineering and data engineering on data models, metric definitions, and instrumentation specs
- Cohort analysis — build and refresh retention, engagement, and conversion cohorts to track product health trends
- Deep dive analyses — investigate specific product questions with multi-faceted analysis combining multiple data sources
- Cross-functional syncs — product team meetings, data team stand-ups, experiment review sessions
- Documentation — update data dictionaries, metric definitions, analysis methodology notes, and knowledge base articles
- Communication rhythm combines deep analytical work with regular syncs and ad hoc stakeholder requests

## Cross-Functional Relationships
- **Product (prd-manager, prd-owner):** Primary partnership — defining success metrics, analyzing feature performance, measuring experiment results, informing product decisions with data
- **Engineering (eng-backend, eng-data):** Event tracking implementation, data pipeline quality, instrumentation specification, schema design for product analytics
- **Data Engineering (da-engineer, da-etl):** Data pipeline reliability, data warehouse architecture, ETL logic for product data, data quality monitoring
- **Data Science (da-scientist):] Collaboration on advanced modeling, ML-based product features, causal inference studies, behavioral prediction models
- **Marketing (mkt-analytics, mkt-growth):** Marketing attribution, campaign performance analysis, funnel optimization between marketing and product, customer acquisition analytics
- **Sales (sls-revenue-ops, sls-ae):** Product usage data for customer conversations, adoption metrics for renewals, feature usage insights for expansion selling
- **Customer Success (cs-success):** Health score modeling, churn prediction analytics, adoption trend analysis, expansion opportunity identification
- **Design (dsg-ux, dsg-research):** Behavioral analytics to complement qualitative research, usability metric measurement, design change impact analysis
- **Executive (exec-cfo, exec-cto):** Product health reporting, strategic metric dashboards, board-level product performance summaries
- **Finance (fin-fpa):** Product revenue analytics, unit economics analysis, feature-level P&L estimation, experimentation ROI measurement

## Regional Nuances
- **NA:** Highly sophisticated analytics function with strong tooling and deep specialization. Data-driven culture with high expectations for analytical rigor. Compensation $100K-$200K+. Significant equity component at larger companies. Career path includes Data Science crossover.
- **EMEA:** Growing analytics maturity with strong statistical foundations from European education systems. GDPR compliance affects data collection and analysis — more limited behavioral tracking without consent. Privacy-preserving analytics methods are more common. Compensation lower than NA ($60K-$120K).
- **APAC:** Rapidly expanding analytics capability particularly in India and Singapore. High demand for analytics talent outstripping supply. Strong execution focus with less emphasis on complex statistical methodology. Mobile analytics expertise is especially valued. English-language analytics tool proficiency is important.
- **LATAM:** Emerging analytics function with strong growth. Often requires working across multiple product areas due to lean teams. Nearshore analytics support for NA companies is a common model. Portuguese/Spanish bilingual capability valued. Lower compensation but high growth opportunities.

## Industry Variations
- **SaaS:** Subscription analytics focus — MRR/ARR tracking, expansion/revenue retention analysis, funnel optimization for free-to-paid conversion, account-level analytics for multi-user products, usage-based pricing analytics, churn analysis and prediction.
- **Fintech:** Financial metric analysis — transaction volume analytics, fraud detection metrics, regulatory reporting analytics, balance sheet product optimization, payment flow funnel analysis. Compliance constraints on data analysis — stricter data privacy requirements.
- **Healthcare:** Patient outcome analytics, clinical workflow analysis, provider adoption metrics, regulatory compliance analytics. HIPAA constraints significantly affect data analysis — de-identification requirements. Longer analysis cycles due to data sensitivity.
- **E-commerce:** Conversion funnel analytics, AOV optimization, basket analysis, recommendation engine performance, inventory analytics, pricing analytics, customer lifetime value modeling, return rate analysis, seasonal pattern analysis.
- **Manufacturing/IoT:** Device telemetry analytics, sensor data analysis, predictive maintenance analytics, field service optimization, product quality analytics, connected product usage patterns. Large-scale time series data expertise needed.

## Common Challenges
- Ensuring data quality and event tracking accuracy across rapidly changing product surfaces and multiple platforms
- Getting product teams to act on analytical insights rather than just consuming reports without behavior change
- Balancing deep analytical rigor with the speed demanded by fast product iteration cycles
- Maintaining clean data taxonomies as the product grows and event volume explodes
- Communicating statistical uncertainty to non-technical stakeholders who want definitive answers
- Accessing the right data when data systems are fragmented across multiple warehouses, tools, and platforms

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- GDPR data minimization and purpose limitation — only tracking necessary events for product improvement
- CCPA/CPRA data subject rights — analysis must support user data deletion requests
- Cookie and tracking consent requirements for web analytics (ePrivacy Directive, GDPR, state laws)
- Data retention policies for product analytics data — define and enforce deletion schedules
- HIPAA compliance for analytics involving protected health information (if applicable)
- PCI-DSS compliance if analyzing transaction or payment data
- Data governance policies for PII handling in analytics datasets — aggregation, anonymization, access controls
- Statistical standards for experiment reporting ensuring methodology transparency and reproducibility
- Internal data classification and access controls for product analytics data
- SOX compliance if analytics data feeds into financial reporting or revenue metrics
- AI Act compliance for analytics involving automated decision-making or profiling
