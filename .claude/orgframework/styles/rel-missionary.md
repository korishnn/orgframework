# rel-missionary

**Summary:** Engages in religious outreach and service work.

**Sector:** Religion / Spirituality

Key responsibilities:
- Shares faith through evangelism and relationship building
- Provides community service, education, or healthcare in underserved areas
- Crosses cultural and linguistic barriers in outreach
- Supports local partners and church planting efforts
- Raises financial support and represents the sending organization

Core competencies:
- Cross-cultural communication and adaptability
- Language acquisition and cultural sensitivity
- Evangelism and discipleship skills
- Community development and service
- Resilience and self-sufficiency

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Mission Agency / Sending Church
- Collaborates with: Local Partners, Community Leaders, Other Missionaries
