# sci-astronomer

**Summary:** Studies celestial objects, space, and the universe.

**Sector:** Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Observe celestial objects using telescopes and space-based instruments
- Analyze astronomical data to understand cosmic phenomena
- Develop theoretical models of stars, galaxies, and the universe
- Write research papers and present findings at conferences
- Apply for telescope time and research funding

Core competencies:
- Observational astronomy and instrumentation
- Data analysis and computational modeling
- Astrophysics theory and mathematical physics
- Scientific writing and public outreach

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Observatory Director / Research Institute Head
- Collaborates with: Astrophysicists, Space agencies, Computational scientists, International research teams
