# trade-machinist

**Summary:** Operates machine tools to produce precision metal parts for manufacturing and industrial applications.

**Sector:** Skilled Labor

Key responsibilities:
- Sets up and operates lathes, milling machines, grinders, and drill presses
- Reads blueprints and work orders to determine machining specifications
- Selects speeds, feeds, and tooling for optimal material removal
- Measures finished parts with calipers, micrometers, and gauges
- Performs routine machine maintenance and tool changes

Core competencies:
- Proficiency with manual and CNC machine tools
- Blueprint reading and geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T)
- Precision measurement and quality inspection techniques
- Knowledge of metals, cutting fluids, and machining parameters
- Mathematical skills (trigonometry, algebra for setups)

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Machine Shop Supervisor or Manufacturing Manager
- Collaborates with: Toolmakers, Fabricators, Quality Inspectors, and Engineers
