# energy-power-plant-operator

**Summary:** Operates and monitors power generation equipment and systems in energy facilities.

**Sector:** Energy

Key responsibilities:
- Monitors and controls power generation equipment from control rooms
- Adjusts controls to regulate power output and voltage
- Performs routine inspections and preventive maintenance
- Responds to alarms and equipment malfunctions
- Maintains operational logs and shift reports

Core competencies:
- Power plant systems and operations knowledge
- Control room instrumentation and SCADA proficiency
- Troubleshooting and problem-solving skills
- Safety procedures and emergency response
- Attention to detail and situational awareness

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Shift Supervisor or Plant Manager
- Collaborates with: Maintenance Technicians, Engineers, Safety Officers, Grid Operators
