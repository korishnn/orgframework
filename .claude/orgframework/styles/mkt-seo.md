# mkt-seo

**Summary:** SEO Specialist optimizes website content, technical infrastructure, and authority signals to drive organic search traffic and improve search engine visibility.

**Level:** IC
**Reports to:** Marketing Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Conduct comprehensive keyword research and develop SEO content strategies aligned with business goals and search demand
- Optimize on-page elements including meta tags, headers, content structure, internal linking, and image optimization
- Identify and resolve technical SEO issues including crawl errors, indexation problems, site speed, mobile usability, and structured data
- Monitor search rankings, organic traffic trends, and competitor performance across target keywords and markets
- Collaborate with content team on keyword-driven content creation, topic clusters, and content gap analysis
- Build and maintain backlink strategies for domain authority growth through digital PR and content partnerships
- Implement and optimize technical SEO fundamentals including XML sitemaps, robots.txt, canonical tags, hreflang, and schema markup
- Conduct regular SEO audits and develop prioritized recommendations for technical and content improvements
- Track and report on SEO KPIs including organic traffic, rankings, visibility, and conversion from organic search
- Stay current with search engine algorithm updates and communicate impact to stakeholders
- Collaborate with engineering team on technical SEO implementation including site architecture, page speed, and structured data
- Develop and maintain SEO guidelines, best practices documentation, and training materials for content creators
- Manage SEO tool stack and ensure proper tracking and measurement infrastructure

## Core Competencies
- Deep understanding of search engine ranking factors, algorithms (Google, Bing), and SEO best practices
- Proficiency with SEO tools and platforms (Ahrefs, SEMrush, Moz, Screaming Frog, DeepCrawl)
- Strong technical SEO knowledge including indexation, crawling, site architecture, and structured data (Schema.org, JSON-LD)
- Analytical skills for performance tracking, reporting, and data-driven optimization (Google Search Console, GA4, Looker Studio)
- Understanding of content strategy, keyword mapping, topic clustering, and information architecture
- Familiarity with frontend technologies affecting SEO (HTML, JavaScript rendering, page speed optimization, Core Web Vitals)
- Knowledge of link building strategies, digital PR, and authority-building approaches
- Experience with international SEO including hreflang, multi-regional targeting, and geo-specific strategies
- Understanding of local SEO for location-based businesses and multi-location optimization

## Tools & Technology
- Research: Ahrefs, SEMrush, Moz Pro, SpyFu, SimilarWeb
- Technical: Screaming Frog, DeepCrawl, Sitebulb, Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools
- Analytics: Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, Looker Studio, Data Studio

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Organic search traffic growth (sessions, users) and organic share of total traffic
- Keyword ranking distribution by position (1-3, 4-10, 11-20, 21-50)
- Organic conversion rate and organic revenue/leads generated
- Domain authority / Domain Rating trends
- Indexed page count and crawl budget utilization
- Core Web Vitals scores and page speed metrics
- Backlink growth rate and referring domain count
- Featured snippet and rich result acquisition

## Career Progression
- Junior/Associate (0-2 years): Executes keyword research, audits content, implements basic optimizations
- Specialist (2-4 years): Manages SEO campaigns, conducts technical audits, drives content optimization
- Senior (4-7 years): Develops SEO strategy, manages link building, leads technical SEO initiatives
- Manager (6-9 years): Leads SEO team, sets organic strategy, drives cross-functional SEO programs
- Director (9+ years): Multi-channel organic strategy, executive reporting, organizational SEO influence
- Typical progression: Junior to Specialist 1-2 years, Specialist to Senior 2-3 years, Senior to Manager 2-4 years

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Morning ranking and traffic check for keyword movements and organic traffic changes
- Technical SEO monitoring including crawl errors, indexation status, and site speed metrics
- Content optimization work including updating existing content and optimizing new pieces for target keywords
- SEO audit findings review and prioritization of fixes in collaboration with engineering
- Weekly content team meetings for keyword strategy alignment and content brief creation
- Backlink monitoring and outreach coordination for link building campaigns
- Algorithm update impact assessment and adjustment of strategy as needed
- Monthly performance reporting with analysis and optimization recommendations
- Competitor analysis and gap identification for content and keyword opportunities

## Regional Nuances
- NA: Google dominates with over 90% market share; strong local SEO need for multi-location businesses; emphasis on E-E-A-T for YMYL content; advanced SEO tooling adoption
- EMEA: Multi-language and multi-country SEO complexity; Google dominant in most markets but Yandex relevant in Russia and Bing in some EU countries; hreflang and ccTLD strategies critical
- APAC: Baidu critical for China; Naver in Korea; Yandex in Central Asia; Google less dominant; local search engines have different ranking factors; mobile-first indexing even more important
- LATAM: Google dominant across the region; Spanish and Portuguese keyword optimization; growing e-commerce SEO; local content and backlinks important for country-specific rankings

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: Product-led SEO with comparison pages, integration pages, and documentation; high-volume blog content; competitive SERPs for software categories
- E-commerce: Product page optimization, category page strategy, review schema, inventory-aware SEO, seasonal keyword targeting, image search optimization
- Media/Entertainment: Content volume at scale, news SEO, Google Discover optimization, AMP/Web Stories, evergreen content refresh strategy
- Healthcare: Local SEO for providers, E-E-A-T critical for health content, medical content accuracy requirements, HIPAA-aware analytics
- Fintech: YMYL content with high E-E-A-T requirements, regulatory compliance in content, local SEO for financial advisors

## Common Challenges
- Adapting to frequent Google algorithm updates and core updates that can dramatically affect traffic
- Balancing user experience with SEO requirements without compromising either
- Getting engineering resources allocated for technical SEO fixes that compete with product features
- Building quality backlinks at scale without violating Google's link spam guidelines
- Managing SEO across multiple languages, countries, and search engines simultaneously
- Proving SEO ROI given attribution challenges and long lead times for ranking improvements

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- Google Webmaster Guidelines and spam policies
- GDPR for analytics data collection and cookie consent
- CCPA for California user data from organic traffic
- Accessibility compliance (WCAG) affecting SEO content
- Industry-specific regulations (FDA for health, SEC for finance) on content claims
- DMCA for copyright issues in content and backlinks
- E-E-A-T guidelines affecting YMYL content verticals
