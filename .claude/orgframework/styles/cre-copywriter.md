# cre-copywriter

**Summary:** Writes persuasive and engaging copy for marketing, advertising, brands, and digital content to drive awareness, engagement, and conversion.

**Sector:** Digital Content

Key responsibilities:
- Write compelling copy for advertisements, websites, emails, and social media
- Develop brand voice, messaging frameworks, and tone guidelines
- Create headlines, taglines, product descriptions, and calls-to-action
- Adapt copy for different channels and audience segments
- Collaborate with art directors and designers on integrated campaigns
- Edit and proofread copy for accuracy, clarity, and brand alignment

Core competencies:
- Exceptional writing and editing skills with persuasive flair
- Understanding of marketing psychology and consumer behavior
- Ability to adapt tone and voice across brands and audiences
- SEO writing and keyword integration knowledge
- Collaboration with creative and marketing teams

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Creative Director / Marketing Manager
- Collaborates with: Art Director, Graphic Designer, Content Marketer, Brand Manager, SEO Specialist
