# edu-provost

**Summary:** Chief academic officer responsible for curriculum, faculty, and research.

**Sector:** Academia

Key responsibilities:
- Oversee all academic programs, curriculum, and degree requirements
- Lead faculty hiring, promotion, tenure, and development
- Guide the university's research strategy and resource allocation
- Manage academic budgets and allocate resources across colleges
- Ensure accreditation standards and academic quality

Core competencies:
- Academic leadership and strategic vision
- Faculty relations and governance expertise
- Financial planning and resource management
- Understanding of higher education policy and trends

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chancellor / University President
- Collaborates with: Deans, Vice Presidents, Faculty Senate, Registrar, Research administrators
