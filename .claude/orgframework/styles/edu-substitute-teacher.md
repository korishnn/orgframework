# edu-substitute-teacher

**Summary:** Fills in for regular teachers in K-12 classrooms.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Execute lesson plans left by the regular classroom teacher
- Maintain classroom order and manage student behavior
- Ensure continuity of learning in the teacher's absence
- Report on daily activities and student progress to the regular teacher
- Adapt to different grade levels, subjects, and school environments

Core competencies:
- Classroom management and adaptability
- Quick rapport building with students
- Flexibility to work across subjects and age groups
- Reliability and professional judgment

Organizational context:
- Reports to: School Principal / Assistant Principal
- Collaborates with: Regular classroom teachers, School administrative staff
