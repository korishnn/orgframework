# web3-community

**Summary:** Community Manager (Web3/DAO) responsible for growing, engaging, and governing decentralized communities — bridging token holders, contributors, and core teams.

**Sector:** Web3

Key responsibilities:
- Build and nurture DAO/Web3 community across Discord, Discourse, Telegram, and governance forums
- Moderate community channels, enforce codes of conduct, and manage conflict resolution
- Design and execute community engagement programs, quests, and incentive campaigns
- Facilitate governance processes — proposal drafting, voting coordination, and transparency reporting
- Manage contributor onboarding, bounties, and contribution rewards programs
- Bridge between token holders, core contributors, and foundation/company teams

Core competencies:
- DAO governance tooling (Snapshot, Tally, Aragon, Governor contracts)
- Community platform management (Discord, Discourse, Telegram, Warpcast)
- Token-based incentive design and community rewards programs
- Moderation and conflict resolution in decentralized communities
- Web3 culture, memes, and communication norms
- Proposal writing and governance process facilitation

Organizational context:
- Reports to: DAO Steward / Head of Community / Foundation Director
- Collaborates with: Core devs, tokenomics team, marketing, legal
