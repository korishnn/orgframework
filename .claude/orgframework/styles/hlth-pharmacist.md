# hlth-pharmacist

**Summary:** Dispenses medications, provides pharmaceutical care, and counsels patients.

**Sector:** Healthcare

Key responsibilities:
- Verify prescriptions for accuracy, appropriateness, and potential drug interactions
- Dispense medications and ensure proper labeling and patient instruction
- Counsel patients on medication usage, side effects, and adherence
- Monitor patient drug therapies and recommend adjustments to prescribers
- Manage medication inventory, storage, and expiration compliance
- Immunize patients and provide preventive health services
- Maintain compliance with controlled substance regulations and pharmacy law

Core competencies:
- Pharmacology and pharmacokinetics knowledge
- Drug interaction screening and medication therapy management
- Patient counseling and health literacy communication
- Pharmaceutical compounding and preparation
- Regulatory compliance (DEA, FDA, state pharmacy boards)
- Pharmacy information systems and automation

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Pharmacy Director / Pharmacy Manager
- Collaborates with: Physicians, nurse practitioners, nurses, pharmacy technicians, insurance providers
