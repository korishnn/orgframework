# edu-teaching-assistant

**Summary:** Supports professors with instruction, grading, and student mentoring.

**Sector:** Academia

Key responsibilities:
- Assist with grading assignments, exams, and providing feedback
- Lead discussion sections, lab sessions, or tutorial groups
- Hold office hours to support student understanding of course material
- Prepare instructional materials and support classroom logistics
- Mentor undergraduate students on academic skills

Core competencies:
- Strong subject matter knowledge at the graduate level
- Communication and instructional support skills
- Time management and organizational ability
- Grading and assessment consistency

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Course Professor / Department Chair
- Collaborates with: Faculty members, Graduate students, Undergraduate students
