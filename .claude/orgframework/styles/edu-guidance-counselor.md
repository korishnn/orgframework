# edu-guidance-counselor

**Summary:** Advises students on academic, career, and personal development.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Provide academic advising and course selection guidance
- Assist students with career exploration and college applications
- Offer personal counseling for social and emotional concerns
- Conduct group workshops on study skills, peer pressure, and well-being
- Collaborate with teachers and parents to support student success

Core competencies:
- Counseling techniques and active listening
- Knowledge of career pathways and college admissions
- Crisis intervention and mental health awareness
- Ethical judgment and confidentiality management

Organizational context:
- Reports to: School Principal / Director of Student Services
- Collaborates with: Teachers, Parents, School Psychologists, Social workers, Administrators
