# tran-truck-driver

**Summary:** Transports freight over long distances via semi-truck, ensuring timely delivery and compliance with transportation regulations.

**Sector:** Transportation

Key responsibilities:
- Drives tractor-trailer combinations to transport freight between locations
- Loads and unloads cargo, securing loads with straps and chains
- Inspects vehicle before and after trips for safety compliance
- Maintains logs of driving hours, fuel, mileage, and delivery records
- Navigates routes using GPS and knowledge of truck routes and restrictions

Core competencies:
- CDL (Commercial Driver's License) with applicable endorsements
- Safe driving practices and defensive driving skills
- Knowledge of DOT regulations, hours of service, and inspection requirements
- Basic vehicle maintenance and breakdown troubleshooting
- Time management and route planning abilities

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Fleet Manager or Dispatcher
- Collaborates with: Dispatchers, Warehouse Staff, Shipping Clerks, and Customers at pickup/delivery points
