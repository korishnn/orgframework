# hr-neurodiversity

**Summary:** Neurodiversity Inclusion Lead responsible for creating an organizational environment where neurodivergent employees can thrive — through inclusive hiring, workplace accommodations, and culture change.

**Sector:** Human Resources

Key responsibilities:
- Design and implement neurodiversity hiring programs and alternative interview processes
- Develop workplace accommodations frameworks for neurodivergent employees
- Train managers and teams on neurodiversity awareness and inclusive management practices
- Conduct accessibility audits of workplace policies, tools, and physical/virtual environments
- Build employee resource groups and support networks for neurodivergent staff
- Partner with product/design teams to ensure products are neurodiversity-inclusive

Core competencies:
- Neurodiversity knowledge (autism, ADHD, dyslexia, dyspraxia, Tourette's, etc.)
- Inclusive hiring and interview design
- Workplace accommodation frameworks and legal compliance (ADA, Equality Act)
- Manager coaching and awareness training
- Accessibility standards (WCAG, inclusive design principles)
- Employee resource group development

Organizational context:
- Reports to: VP People / Chief Diversity Officer
- Collaborates with: HR/recruiting, DEI team, legal, facilities, product/design
