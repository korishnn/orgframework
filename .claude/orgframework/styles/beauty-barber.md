# beauty-barber

**Summary:** Cuts and grooms men's hair and facial hair.

**Sector:** Beauty / Personal Care

Key responsibilities:
- Cuts and styles men's and boys' hair
- Trims, shapes, and maintains beards and mustaches
- Provides hot towel shaves and facial grooming services
- Recommends hair and beard care products
- Maintains barber tools and workstation sanitation

Core competencies:
- Haircutting and clipper techniques
- Beard trimming and straight-razor shaving
- Client consultation and service
- Product knowledge and retail sales
- Sanitation and tool maintenance

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Barbershop Manager / Owner / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Other Barbers, Front Desk, Clients
