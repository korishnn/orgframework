# trade-roofer

**Summary:** Installs and repairs roofing systems on residential and commercial buildings to provide weather protection and insulation.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Installs shingles, tiles, metal panels, and membrane roofing materials
- Inspects roofs for damage, leaks, and structural issues
- Repairs or replaces damaged flashing, vents, and roofing components
- Applies waterproof underlayment and sealants
- Ensures proper ventilation, drainage, and insulation in roof assemblies

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of roofing materials and system installation methods
- Skill with roofing tools (nail guns, shears, torches, and seamers)
- Safety awareness for working at heights (fall protection, scaffolding)
- Physical agility, balance, and comfort on steep slopes
- Weather-resistance and ability to work outdoors in varied conditions

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Roofing Foreman or Project Manager
- Collaborates with: Carpenters, Siding Installers, Insulation Contractors, and Building Inspectors
