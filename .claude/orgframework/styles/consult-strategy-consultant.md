# consult-strategy-consultant

**Summary:** Develops corporate strategy, market entry plans, and growth strategies for organizations.

**Sector:** Consulting

Key responsibilities:
- Analyzes markets, competitors, and industry trends
- Develops corporate and business unit strategies
- Identifies growth opportunities and new market entry options
- Conducts financial modeling and valuation analysis
- Presents strategic recommendations to executive leadership

Core competencies:
- Strategic analysis and frameworks
- Market research and competitive intelligence
- Financial modeling and valuation
- Executive communication and persuasion
- Industry and macroeconomic knowledge

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Engagement Manager or Partner
- Collaborates with: Client Executives, Industry Analysts, Functional Consultants, Research Teams
