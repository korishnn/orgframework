# avi-flight-attendant

**Summary:** Ensures passenger safety, comfort, and service during commercial airline flights.

**Sector:** Aviation

Key responsibilities:
- Conducts pre-flight safety checks and cabin security inspections
- Demonstrates safety equipment and briefs passengers on emergency procedures
- Serves meals, beverages, and retail products to passengers
- Responds to medical emergencies, turbulence, and cabin incidents
- Assists with boarding, stowing luggage, and passenger deplaning

Core competencies:
- FAA or equivalent cabin crew certification and recurrent training
- Emergency response including evacuation, firefighting, and first aid
- Customer service excellence and conflict resolution abilities
- Public speaking and intercom announcement skills
- Flexibility for irregular schedules, long days, and time zone changes

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Inflight Base Manager or Purser/Lead Flight Attendant
- Collaborates with: Pilots, Gate Agents, Ground Crew, Ramp Agents, and Passengers
