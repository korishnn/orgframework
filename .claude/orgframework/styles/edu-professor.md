# edu-professor

**Summary:** Teaches college courses, conducts research, and publishes scholarship.

**Sector:** Academia

Key responsibilities:
- Design and teach undergraduate and graduate courses
- Conduct original research and publish findings in scholarly venues
- Mentor graduate students and advise on research projects
- Serve on academic committees and contribute to departmental governance
- Secure external funding through grant proposals

Core competencies:
- Advanced subject matter expertise and research methodology
- Publication record and grant writing ability
- Curriculum design and student mentorship
- Public speaking and academic writing

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Department Chair / Dean
- Collaborates with: Other faculty, Research Assistants, Teaching Assistants, Department staff
