# hosp-catering-manager

**Summary:** Manages catering operations for events and venues.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Plans and coordinates catering for events and functions
- Develops catering menus and pricing strategies
- Manages catering staff including servers and kitchen team
- Oversees event setup, service, and breakdown
- Handles client consultations and customized menu planning

Core competencies:
- Menu planning and food presentation
- Event logistics and operations
- Staff management and scheduling
- Client relations and sales
- Budget and cost management

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Food & Beverage Director / General Manager
- Collaborates with: Chef, Event Coordinator, Sales, Clients
