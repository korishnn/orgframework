# edu-registrar

**Summary:** Manages student records, course registration, and academic policies.

**Sector:** Academia

Key responsibilities:
- Maintain accurate student academic records and transcripts
- Manage course registration, scheduling, and classroom assignments
- Enforce academic policies regarding enrollment, grading, and graduation
- Oversee degree audits and verify graduation eligibility
- Ensure compliance with data privacy regulations (e.g., FERPA)

Core competencies:
- Records management and database administration
- Knowledge of academic policy and regulatory compliance
- Attention to detail and organizational efficiency
- Customer service and student support

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Provost / Vice President of Academic Affairs
- Collaborates with: Faculty, Department administrators, IT, Admissions, Student Affairs
