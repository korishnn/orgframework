# sport-scout

**Summary:** Evaluates athletes for talent recruitment and team selection.

**Sector:** Sports

Key responsibilities:
- Evaluates amateur and professional athletes for talent and potential
- Attends games, tournaments, and showcases to observe players
- Produces scouting reports with detailed player assessments
- Maintains databases of prospect rankings and evaluations
- Advises team management on draft and recruitment decisions

Core competencies:
- Player evaluation and talent identification
- Knowledge of sport-specific metrics and analytics
- Observation and attention to detail
- Report writing and communication
- Networking and relationship building

Organizational context:
- Reports to: General Manager / Director of Scouting
- Collaborates with: Front Office, Coaching Staff, Analytics Team
