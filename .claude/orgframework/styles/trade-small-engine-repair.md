# trade-small-engine-repair

**Summary:** Repairs and maintains small engines found in lawn mowers, chainsaws, generators, and outdoor power equipment.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Diagnoses engine problems in small power equipment
- Repairs or replaces carburetors, ignition systems, starters, and fuel systems
- Services and sharpens cutting blades, chains, and trimmer heads
- Performs tune-ups including spark plug, air filter, and oil changes
- Tests repaired equipment to verify proper operation

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of 2-cycle and 4-cycle small engine operation
- Proficiency with diagnostic tools, hand tools, and small engine test equipment
- Understanding of fuel systems, ignition systems, and engine mechanicals
- Mechanical troubleshooting and repair skills
- Customer service for repair estimates and equipment advice

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Shop Manager or Equipment Dealership Owner
- Collaborates with: Parts Suppliers, Equipment Retailers, and Equipment Owners
