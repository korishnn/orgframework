# govt-postal-worker

**Summary:** Processes and delivers mail through the postal system to businesses and residences.

**Sector:** Government

Key responsibilities:
- Sorts and processes incoming and outgoing mail
- Delivers mail along designated routes
- Collects postage and sells postal products
- Handles registered, certified, and express mail
- Maintains delivery records and forwards mail as needed

Core competencies:
- Route navigation and time management
- Attention to detail in sorting and delivery
- Customer service and public interaction
- Physical stamina for walking and lifting
- Knowledge of postal regulations and procedures

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Postmaster or Mail Processing Supervisor
- Collaborates with: Other Postal Workers, Mail Handlers, Clerical Staff
