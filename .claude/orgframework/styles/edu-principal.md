# edu-principal

**Summary:** Leads K-12 school operations, culture, and instructional quality.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Set the school's vision, academic goals, and improvement plans
- Supervise and evaluate teaching staff and school personnel
- Manage school budget, facilities, and operations
- Foster a positive school culture and safe learning environment
- Engage with parents, community members, and district leadership

Core competencies:
- Instructional leadership and staff development
- Budget management and operational planning
- Conflict resolution and community relations
- Data-driven decision-making and strategic planning

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Superintendent
- Collaborates with: Assistant Principals, Teachers, School Board, District administrators, Parents
