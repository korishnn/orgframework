# legal-tax-attorney

**Summary:** Advises on tax law, tax planning, and represents clients before tax authorities.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Provides tax planning advice for individuals, corporations, and estates
- Prepares and reviews tax filings, opinions, and ruling requests
- Represents clients in tax audits, appeals, and litigation
- Structures transactions for tax efficiency and regulatory compliance

Core competencies:
- Juris Doctor degree and state bar admission; often an LL.M. in Taxation
- Deep knowledge of federal, state, and international tax codes
- Analytical skills for complex tax research and transaction structuring
- Proficiency with tax preparation software and financial modeling

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Tax Practice Group Lead or Partner
- Collaborates with: Accountants, financial advisors, corporate counsel, IRS or tax authority officials
