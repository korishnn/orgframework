# govt-city-planner

**Summary:** Plans land use, zoning, and development for municipalities to create sustainable communities.

**Sector:** Government

Key responsibilities:
- Develops comprehensive land use and zoning plans
- Reviews development proposals for regulatory compliance
- Conducts community needs assessments and transportation studies
- Facilitates public hearings and community engagement sessions
- Coordinates with environmental and infrastructure agencies

Core competencies:
- Urban planning principles and zoning regulations
- Spatial analysis and GIS proficiency
- Community engagement and public presentation
- Environmental and sustainability knowledge
- Project management and interagency coordination

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Planning Director or City Manager
- Collaborates with: Urban Planners, Architects, Developers, Community Boards, Environmental Agencies
