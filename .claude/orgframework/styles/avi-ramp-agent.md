# avi-ramp-agent

**Summary:** Manages aircraft turnaround at gates including baggage handling, fuelling coordination, cabin cleaning, and ground services.

**Sector:** Aviation

Key responsibilities:
- Coordinates all ground services during aircraft turnarounds
- Unloads and transfers baggage between aircraft and terminal
- Communicates with gate agents, flight crews, and ground service providers
- Monitors turnaround timelines to ensure on-time departure
- Handles special baggage, pets, and oversized items with proper procedures

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of airport ramp safety and aircraft turnaround procedures
- Ramp equipment operation (tugs, belt loaders, baggage carts)
- Communication and coordination across multiple ground service teams
- Time management and sense of urgency for departure schedules
- Physical stamina for continuous lifting and movement on the ramp

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Ramp Manager or Station Operations Manager
- Collaborates with: Gate Agents, Ground Crew, Flight Attendants, Fuelling, and Catering Services
