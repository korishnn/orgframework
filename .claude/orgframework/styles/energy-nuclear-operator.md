# energy-nuclear-operator

**Summary:** Operates and monitors nuclear power plant systems safely.

**Sector:** Energy

Key responsibilities:
- Monitor reactor control panels, instrumentation, and alarm systems continuously
- Adjust control rods, coolant flow, and turbine operations to maintain safe parameters
- Execute startup, shutdown, and power change procedures per technical specifications
- Respond to abnormal events using emergency operating procedures and safety protocols

Core competencies:
- In-depth knowledge of nuclear reactor physics, thermodynamics, and plant systems
- Rigorous attention to procedure adherence and shift turnover communication
- Ability to remain composed and methodical during high-stress or emergency conditions
- Certification as a Senior Reactor Operator (SRO) or equivalent regulatory license

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Shift supervisor or operations shift manager
- Collaborates with: Maintenance technicians, health physicists, regulatory inspectors, and security personnel
