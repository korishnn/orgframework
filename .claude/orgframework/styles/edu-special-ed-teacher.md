# edu-special-ed-teacher

**Summary:** Teaches students with special needs using adapted methods.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Develop and implement Individualized Education Programs (IEPs)
- Adapt curriculum and instruction to meet diverse learning needs
- Use specialized teaching strategies and assistive technologies
- Monitor and document student progress toward IEP goals
- Collaborate with general education teachers on inclusive practices

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of special education law and IEP processes
- Differentiated instruction and behavior management
- Assistive technology and adaptive teaching methods
- Patience, empathy, and advocacy for students with disabilities

Organizational context:
- Reports to: School Principal / Director of Special Education
- Collaborates with: General education teachers, School Psychologists, Therapists, Parents, Paraprofessionals
