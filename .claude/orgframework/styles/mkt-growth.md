# mkt-growth

**Summary:** Growth Marketer drives customer acquisition, activation, retention, and revenue expansion through data-informed experiments, cross-channel strategies, and product-marketing integration.

**Level:** IC or Manager
**Reports to:** Marketing Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Design and execute growth experiments across the full customer lifecycle from acquisition through retention and advocacy
- Identify high-impact growth levers through quantitative data analysis and qualitative user research
- Optimize conversion funnels from first touch through activation, monetization, and retention
- Run A/B and multivariate tests on landing pages, onboarding flows, pricing pages, and email campaigns
- Coordinate cross-channel growth campaigns integrating email, paid, content, social, and product channels
- Analyze experiment results with statistical rigor and translate findings into actionable growth strategies
- Build and optimize viral, referral, and community-driven growth programs
- Develop growth models to forecast impact of different growth initiatives and channels
- Collaborate with product teams on in-product growth tactics including feature adoption and onboarding optimization
- Manage experimentation roadmap, prioritization framework, and growth backlog
- Implement growth analytics infrastructure including event tracking, funnel analysis, and cohort analysis
- Present growth experiment findings and strategic recommendations to leadership

## Core Competencies
- Strong analytical mindset with expertise in experimental design, statistical analysis, and hypothesis testing
- Deep understanding of growth frameworks (AARRR pirate metrics, growth loops, flywheel, north star metric)
- Experience with experimentation and A/B testing platforms (Optimizely, VWO, Google Optimize, LaunchDarkly)
- Proficiency in data analysis tools (SQL, Python/R, Excel, Amplitude, Mixpanel, Heap)
- Knowledge of user psychology, behavioral economics, and conversion optimization principles
- Cross-functional collaboration skills across marketing, product, engineering, and data teams
- Understanding of multi-channel marketing and growth channel mechanics
- Product sense for in-product growth tactics and user journey optimization
- Creative problem-solving for identifying unique growth opportunities
- Results-oriented with focus on measurable business impact and growth metrics

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) by channel and cohort
- Activation rate (percentage of new users reaching key milestone)
- Retention and churn rates by cohort (daily, weekly, monthly)
- North Star Metric movement relative to growth initiatives
- Experiment velocity (experiments run per month with statistically significant results)
- Viral coefficient and referral-driven acquisition rate
- Revenue expansion (upsell, cross-sell, and upgrade rates)
- Funnel conversion rate improvements from baseline

## Career Progression
- Associate (0-2 years): Supports experiments, compiles data, manages growth campaign execution
- Growth Marketer (2-4 years): Owns growth experiments, analyzes data, executes cross-channel programs
- Senior (4-7 years): Leads growth verticals, manages experimentation roadmap, mentors team members
- Growth Lead (6-9 years): Defines growth strategy, leads growth team, drives organizational growth culture
- Director/VP (9+ years): Executive growth leadership, multi-product growth strategy, company growth vision
- Typical progression: Associate to Mid 1-2 years, Mid to Senior 2-3 years, Senior to Lead 2-4 years

## Regional Nuances
- NA: Most mature growth marketing practice; strong growth team culture; emphasis on experimentation-driven growth; high adoption of growth tools and infrastructure
- EMEA: Growing growth marketing practices; GDPR impacts experimentation and tracking; strong PLG approach in SaaS; UK and Berlin are growth hubs
- APAC: Mobile-first growth strategies; social and messaging channel growth; emerging growth practices in India and SEA; strong referral-driven growth culture
- LATAM: Early growth marketing adoption; cost-effective growth channels; mobile-social growth strategies; growth mindset emerging in startups

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: Product-led growth dominant; freemium-to-paid conversion focus; self-serve and sales-assist growth motions; expansion revenue from usage-based growth; community-led growth
- E-commerce: Acquisition channel optimization; purchase funnel growth; retention and repeat purchase growth; referral and loyalty programs; mobile app growth
- Fintech: Trust-building required in acquisition; regulatory-aware growth tactics; transaction-driven retention; referral growth with compliance; onboarding optimization for financial products
- Healthcare: Compliance-constrained growth channels; provider network effects; patient activation and adherence focus; professional referral growth in provider networks

## Common Challenges
- Maintaining experiment velocity while ensuring statistical rigor and meaningful results
- Navigating data privacy changes that limit tracking and targeting capabilities
- Balancing short-term growth tactics with long-term brand and customer relationship health
- Getting engineering resources for growth experiment implementation against product roadmap priorities
- Identifying truly incremental growth levers versus optimizing already-performing channels
- Managing growth across different lifecycle stages as the business matures

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- GDPR for EMEA experiment data, tracking consent, and personalization
- CCPA for California consumer data in growth experiments
- CAN-SPAM for email growth campaigns
- Platform-specific terms of service for growth tactics on various channels
- FTC guidelines for referral programs, testimonials, and user-generated content
- Industry-specific regulations for growth tactics (fintech, healthcare)
- Cookie consent regulations for experiment tracking and personalization
