# hosp-concierge

**Summary:** Assists hotel guests with arrangements, recommendations, and services.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Assists guests with restaurant reservations and activity bookings
- Provides recommendations for dining, entertainment, and attractions
- Arranges transportation including taxis, shuttles, and car rentals
- Handles special guest requests and delivers personalized service
- Maintains knowledge of local attractions and events

Core competencies:
- Extensive local knowledge and networking
- Customer service and hospitality excellence
- Communication and interpersonal skills
- Resourcefulness and problem-solving
- Discretion and professionalism

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Front Desk Manager / Hotel Manager
- Collaborates with: Front Desk, Guest Services, Local Vendors
