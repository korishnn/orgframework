# sci-physicist

**Summary:** Studies matter, energy, and the fundamental laws of the universe.

**Sector:** Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Develop theoretical models and mathematical descriptions of physical phenomena
- Design and conduct experiments to test physical theories
- Analyze experimental data using computational and statistical methods
- Publish research findings in peer-reviewed journals
- Collaborate on large-scale research projects and facilities

Core competencies:
- Advanced mathematics and theoretical modeling
- Experimental design and instrumentation
- Computational physics and data analysis
- Scientific writing and peer collaboration

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Principal Investigator / Lab Director
- Collaborates with: Research scientists, Engineers, Computational specialists, International research collaborations
