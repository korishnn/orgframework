# hr-talent

**Summary:** Drives performance management, succession planning, high-potential identification and development, and talent strategy to build a strong leadership pipeline and maximize organizational capability.

**Level:** Manager
**Reports to:** HR Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Manage the full performance management cycle including goal setting, mid-year check-ins, annual reviews, calibration sessions, and performance ratings.
- Design and execute succession planning for all critical and executive-level roles, maintaining up-to-date succession slates with readiness assessments.
- Identify high-potential employees (HiPos) through rigorous assessment and calibration, and manage accelerated development programs for this cohort.
- Partner with executive leadership to build talent pipelines, retention strategies, and engagement initiatives for top talent and key roles.
- Analyze talent metrics including promotion rates, internal mobility, performance distribution, retention of top talent, and diversity representation at each level.
- Design and manage 360-degree feedback processes for leadership development and self-awareness building.
- Facilitate talent review sessions (Talent Review / 9-box grid) with business unit leaders to assess performance, potential, and succession readiness.
- Develop and maintain talent assessment criteria and calibration standards to ensure consistency across the organization.
- Create individual development plans (IDPs) for high-potential employees in partnership with managers and hr-lnd.
- Manage the internal mobility and promotion process, ensuring transparent criteria and equitable consideration of internal candidates.
- Partner with hr-recruiting on internal mobility programs and processes for recruiting from within before external sourcing.
- Design and manage retention programs for critical talent including retention bonuses, special projects, and stretch assignments.
- Conduct stay interviews and exit interview analysis to identify retention drivers and flight risks across the organization.
- Manage talent data and reporting in the HRIS, ensuring accurate tracking of performance ratings, succession status, and HiPo designations.

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Internal promotion rate: percentage of roles filled through internal promotion vs. external hire
- Succession readiness: percentage of critical roles with a ready-now successor identified
- High-potential retention rate: retention of identified HiPo employees vs. general population
- Performance rating distribution: alignment with organizational targets and calibration goals
- Time-to-promotion: average time between level advancements for high performers
- 9-box grid accuracy: correlation between potential ratings and actual promotion within 12 months
- Manager effectiveness score: employee survey scores related to manager quality and feedback
- Top talent voluntary turnover: percentage of top-rated talent leaving voluntarily per year

## Career Progression
- Entry level: Talent Management Coordinator supporting talent review preparation, performance cycle logistics, and data management.
- Mid level: Talent Management Specialist facilitating talent reviews, administering assessments, developing programs, and analyzing talent data.
- Senior level: Senior Talent Management Consultant partnering with business leaders on succession planning, HiPo development, and organizational capability building.
- Lead level: Talent Management Program Manager owning the enterprise talent management framework, 360 feedback process, and performance philosophy.
- Manager level: Talent Management Manager leading a team and developing the talent strategy for a region, function, or business unit.
- Director level: Director of Talent Management or Head of Talent responsible for enterprise talent strategy, executive succession, and organization development.
- VP level: VP of Talent / Chief Talent Officer responsible for all talent systems including recruiting, development, performance, and succession.
- Timeline: 1-2 years as coordinator, 2-3 years as specialist, 2-3 years as senior, then lead/manager track.

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Morning: Review talent data dashboards, respond to talent inquiries from business leaders, prepare for talent review meetings or calibration sessions.
- Midday: Facilitate talent review sessions with business leaders, conduct 360 feedback coaching sessions, meet with HiPo employees on development plans.
- Afternoon: Analyze talent metrics, prepare succession reports for leadership, update employee talent profiles in HRIS, design talent program materials.
- Weekly: HiPo program check-ins, talent review preparation meetings, performance cycle milestone tracking, talent team standup.
- Monthly: Talent metrics reporting to HR leadership, internal mobility tracking, promotion review meetings, succession slate updates.
- Quarterly: Talent review and succession planning sessions with business units, 9-box grid calibration across departments, HiPo cohort program sessions.
- Annual peaks: Performance review cycle (typically 6-8 weeks with goal setting, mid-year, and year-end phases), annual talent review and succession cycle (typically 4-6 weeks).
- Rhythms: Annual performance and talent cycles create predictable heavy periods. Ongoing work includes executive coaching, succession monitoring, and program management. Strong calendar management is required around talent review scheduling.

## Regional Nuances
- NA: Performance management is widely practiced with increasing focus on continuous feedback over annual reviews. 360-degree feedback is common. Pay-for-performance culture predominates. Succession planning disclosure to employees varies by company preference.
- EMEA: Works council involvement in performance management system design in Germany, France. Performance ratings may be subject to collective bargaining. Open feedback culture varies significantly across countries. Dismissal based on performance is harder with stronger employment protection, making performance documentation critical. France has mandatory annual negotiation on career paths.
- APAC: Hierarchy and seniority can conflict with performance-based evaluation in Japan and Korea. India has adopted Western performance management practices with high process orientation. Singapore emphasizes transparent and structured talent processes. China's guanxi culture can influence feedback receptivity. Indonesia and Philippines have more indirect communication styles affecting feedback.
- LATAM: Relationship-based culture affects how feedback is delivered (direct negative feedback can damage working relationships). Brazil has formalized performance processes in most large companies. Informal networks heavily influence promotion decisions. Higher power distance affects subordinate-manager feedback dynamics.

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: Performance management tied heavily to OKR achievement. Fast promotion cycles based on demonstrated impact. Talent management practices evolve rapidly as startups scale. HiPo identification often happens early and informally.
- Fintech: Compliance and regulatory skills heavily influence succession criteria. Performance management includes regulatory compliance adherence as a dimension. Risk management talent development is a specific focus area.
- Healthcare: Clinical competency assessments alongside performance ratings. Succession planning for clinical leadership requires balancing medical and administrative skills. Performance improvement plans must navigate union environments in many settings.
- Manufacturing: Performance criteria often include safety metrics and quality scores. Succession planning for plant management requires deep operational experience. Skilled trades talent pipeline development is a critical focus area.
- E-commerce: Performance management for hourly workforce is more metrics-driven (productivity, accuracy, attendance). High-volume, fast-paced environment requires streamlined talent processes. Internal mobility from warehouse to corporate roles is a retention strategy.

## Common Challenges
- Building credible succession pipelines when senior leaders avoid identifying successor candidates out of competitive concerns.
- Ensuring performance ratings are fair and calibrated across departments with different rating philosophies.
- Keeping high-potential employees engaged and retained when promotion opportunities are constrained by organizational structure.
- Moving from annual performance reviews to continuous feedback culture requires significant change management.
- Integrating talent management across acquired companies with different performance cultures and systems.
- Measuring the effectiveness of talent management programs and demonstrating ROI to leadership.

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- US: Title VII (adverse impact analysis of promotion decisions), ADEA (age discrimination in succession planning), ADA (non-discrimination in performance assessments), state non-compete restrictions (impact on retention strategies), OFCCP requirements for documented and non-discriminatory talent processes (federal contractors).
- UK: Equality Act 2010 (indirect discrimination in promotion criteria), Employment Rights Act 1996 (unfair dismissal in performance terminations), ACAS guidance on performance management, GDPR for talent data processing.
- EU: GDPR (lawful basis for talent profiling and HiPo identification, automated decision-making restrictions), EU Employment Equality Directives, country-specific works council rights on talent management systems (Germany BetrVG Section 92, 99, 102).
- Canada: Provincial Human Rights Codes (non-discrimination in performance and promotion), PIPEDA talent data privacy, Pay Equity Act talent classification requirements (ON, QC, federally regulated).
- Australia: Fair Work Act (unfair dismissal in performance terminations), Privacy Act 1988 (talent data handling), state anti-discrimination acts, modern award promotion and progression provisions.
- Brazil: CLT (performance termination legal requirements), LGPD (talent data protection), Labor Court precedents on performance evaluation documentation, quota compliance (disabled, apprentice) in succession consideration.
