# beauty-massage-therapist

**Summary:** Provides therapeutic massage treatments.

**Sector:** Beauty / Personal Care / Wellness

Key responsibilities:
- Performs therapeutic massage techniques including Swedish, deep tissue, and sports
- Assesses client needs and tailors treatments accordingly
- Maintains a clean, comfortable treatment environment
- Educates clients on benefits of massage and self-care
- Manages appointment scheduling and client records

Core competencies:
- Anatomy knowledge and body mechanics
- Multiple massage modality expertise
- Client assessment and treatment planning
- Professional ethics and boundaries
- Physical stamina and ergonomic body mechanics

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Spa Manager / Clinic Director / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Estheticians, Front Desk, Clients
