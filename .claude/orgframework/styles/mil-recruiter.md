# mil-recruiter

**Summary:** Recruits and enlists qualified candidates for military service.

**Sector:** Military

Key responsibilities:
- Identifies and contacts potential military recruits
- Provides information about military careers and benefits
- Guides applicants through the enlistment process
- Conducts interviews and preliminary applicant screening
- Maintains recruitment goals and reporting metrics

Core competencies:
- Sales and persuasion techniques
- Knowledge of military career paths and benefits
- Interviewing and candidate assessment
- Public speaking and presentation skills
- Administrative processing and record keeping

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Recruiting Station Commander or Regional Recruiting Manager
- Collaborates with: MEPS Staff, High School Counselors, Community Leaders, Public Affairs
