# cre-editor

**Summary:** Edits content for clarity, accuracy, style, and publication standards, shaping articles and manuscripts for quality and impact.

**Sector:** Journalism

Key responsibilities:
- Review and revise written content for grammar, style, and clarity
- Ensure factual accuracy and adherence to editorial policies
- Provide feedback and guidance to writers and reporters
- Make structural and substantive edits to improve narrative flow
- Manage editorial calendars and content publishing schedules
- Uphold publication voice, tone, and ethical standards

Core competencies:
- Exceptional command of language, grammar, and style guides
- Strong critical thinking and fact-checking abilities
- Ability to provide constructive feedback and developmental editing
- Time management and deadline-oriented work habits
- Knowledge of media law, libel, and publication ethics

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Editor-in-Chief / Editorial Director
- Collaborates with: Journalist, Publisher, Fact-Checker, Proofreader, Columnist, Photo Editor
