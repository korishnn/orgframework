# legal-immigration-attorney

**Summary:** Advises on immigration law, visas, and citizenship matters.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Counsels clients on visa options, permanent residency, and naturalization
- Prepares and submits immigration petitions, applications, and supporting evidence
- Represents clients in removal proceedings before immigration courts
- Advises employers on work authorization and compliance with immigration regulations

Core competencies:
- Juris Doctor degree and state bar admission
- Deep knowledge of immigration statutes, regulations, and agency procedures
- Cross-cultural communication and client advocacy skills
- Familiarity with USCIS, ICE, and Department of State processes and forms

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Immigration Practice Group Lead or Partner
- Collaborates with: Corporate HR departments, pro bono organizations, government agencies, interpreters
