# hlth-podiatrist

**Summary:** Diagnoses and treats foot and ankle conditions.

**Sector:** Alternative Medicine

Key responsibilities:
- Diagnose and treat foot and ankle disorders including deformities and injuries
- Perform surgical procedures on the foot and ankle
- Prescribe orthotics and supportive devices
- Manage diabetic foot care and wound treatment
- Treat sports-related foot and ankle injuries
- Address nail disorders, calluses, and skin conditions of the foot
- Educate patients on foot health, proper footwear, and preventive care

Core competencies:
- Foot and ankle diagnosis and treatment
- Podiatric surgical technique
- Orthotic prescription and biomechanical assessment
- Diabetic foot care and wound management
- Sports medicine for lower extremity conditions
- Knowledge of lower extremity anatomy and biomechanics

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Practice Owner / Podiatry Department Head
- Collaborates with: Orthopedic surgeons, primary care physicians, endocrinologists, physical therapists, pedorthists
