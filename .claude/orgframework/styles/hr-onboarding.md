# hr-onboarding

**Summary:** Designs, coordinates, and continuously improves the new hire experience from pre-boarding through full integration to ensure rapid productivity and strong cultural connection.

**Level:** IC
**Reports to:** HR Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Design and maintain a structured onboarding program spanning pre-boarding, day one, first week, first 30 days, and 90-day checkpoints.
- Coordinate with IT, facilities, recruiting, and team leads to ensure all new hire equipment, system access, and workspace are ready before the start date.
- Facilitate new hire orientation sessions covering company history, values, policies, benefits enrollment, organizational structure, and compliance training.
- Create and maintain onboarding documentation including welcome packets, new hire checklists, org charts, role-specific ramp plans, and FAQs.
- Track onboarding task completion across departments using a centralized onboarding platform or project management tool.
- Assign and coordinate onboarding buddies and mentors for each new hire to support cultural and social integration.
- Gather and analyze new hire feedback at 30, 60, and 90 days to identify friction points and drive continuous program improvement.
- Partner with hr-lnd to align onboarding content with broader learning and development curricula and compliance training requirements.
- Manage the offboarding process for departing employees including exit interviews, knowledge transfer, and asset return coordination.
- Administer employee referral bonus processing and new hire documentation (I-9 verification, W-4, direct deposit enrollment).
- Coordinate cohort-based onboarding for high-volume hiring waves, creating social connection across new hires starting in the same period.
- Develop manager onboarding guides so that hiring managers understand their role in new hire integration and know what to prepare before day one.
- Monitor early attrition trends and conduct stay interviews at the 90-day mark to identify retention risks early.

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Time-to-productivity: average time until new hire is meeting role-specific milestones
- First-year attrition rate: percentage of new hires who leave within 12 months
- Onboarding satisfaction score: NPS from 30/60/90-day new hire surveys
- Onboarding task completion rate: percentage of required tasks completed on time
- Manager satisfaction with onboarding: post-onboarding manager survey score
- Time-to-first-feedback: days from start until first structured performance feedback
- Compliance training completion rate within first week

- Senior level: Senior Onboarding Specialist developing program strategy, measuring effectiveness, and implementing improvements based on data.
- Lead level: Onboarding Program Manager owning the full onboarding ecosystem including cross-functional governance, vendor management, and executive reporting.

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Pre-start week: Verify all pre-boarding items are complete (background check, I-9, equipment order, system access requests). Send welcome communications to incoming new hires.
- Day one morning: Greet new hires, conduct orientation session, facilitate benefits enrollment overview, tour office or virtual workspace, introduce to team and buddy.
- Afternoon: Ensure all system access is working, review ramp plan with new hire and manager, confirm first-week schedule is set.
- Ongoing: Monitor onboarding task progress across active new hires, respond to questions from new hires and managers, troubleshoot any access or equipment issues.
- Weekly: Check in with new hires who started in the last 30 days, gather early feedback, update onboarding metrics dashboard.
- Monthly: Analyze feedback data, update onboarding materials based on common questions or friction points, coordinate with IT and facilities on upcoming start cohorts.
- Quarterly: Report onboarding metrics to HR leadership, review program effectiveness, plan improvements for next quarter's cohorts.
- Rhythms: Heavy calendar management around start dates. Communication is primarily via Slack, email, and scheduled check-ins. Peaks around the first of the month when most cohorts start.

## Regional Nuances
- NA: I-9 Section 2 must be completed within 3 business days. E-Verify states require additional verification steps. At-will employment acknowledgment is standard. Remote onboarding has become predominant in tech-heavy regions.
- EMEA: Works council involvement in onboarding process in Germany, France. GDPR data processing consent must be obtained. Probation periods typically 3-6 months with specific termination notice rules. Statutory benefits enrollment (health insurance, pension) is mandatory.
- APAC: India requires labor law compliance certificates during onboarding. Singapore mandates CPF enrollment. China requires social insurance and housing fund setup on day one. Longer probation periods up to 6 months are standard.
- LATAM: Brazil's CLT requires specific labor registration (CTPS) annotation within 48 hours. Mexico requires IMSS registration before the employee starts work. Argentina's labor laws require specific onboarding documentation for union registration in certain industries.

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: Heavy emphasis on product and culture immersion. New hires often attend structured product bootcamps. Remote-first onboarding with async content delivery.
- Fintech: Extensive compliance and regulatory onboarding modules before role-specific training. Background checks can take 2-4 weeks. Security and confidentiality training is mandatory before system access.
- Healthcare: Credentialing and licensing verification before start date. HIPAA training required before patient data access. Clinical onboarding can take 4-8 weeks.
- Manufacturing: Safety training and PPE certification required before floor access. Plant-specific onboarding includes hazard communication. Shift-based onboarding sessions to accommodate 24/7 operations.
- E-commerce: High-volume seasonal onboarding with rapid training turnaround. Temporary worker onboarding with simplified but compliant processes. Warehouse safety and equipment training as first priority.

## Common Challenges
- Coordinating across multiple departments (IT, facilities, recruiting, payroll) when start dates are tight or concurrent.
- Delivering consistent onboarding experiences for remote employees who may feel disconnected from company culture.
- Reducing time-to-productivity when new hires join during busy periods with limited manager bandwidth.
- Managing document collection and compliance for international hires across different jurisdictions.
- Adapting onboarding content for different roles, levels, and departments without creating a fragmented experience.
- Preventing early attrition when onboarding experience fails to meet expectations set during recruiting.

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- US: I-9 and E-Verify compliance, Form W-4 tax withholding, state-specific new hire reporting requirements, OSHA workplace safety posting requirements.
- EU: GDPR data processing consent for employee data, Agency Workers Directive for temporary staff, works council information rights in Germany (BetrVG).
- UK: Right to Work checks (Immigration Act 2016), Pension Auto-Enrolment enrollment within 3 months, Employment Rights Act 1996 written statement of particulars.
- Canada: Provincial employment standards orientation requirements, Workplace Safety and Insurance Board (WSIB) registration in Ontario.
- Brazil: CLT Article 29 requiring CTPS annotation, LGPD consent for data processing, FGTS account setup within 5 days.
- Global: Anti-bribery training (FCPA/UK Bribery Act), sanctions screening for international hires, data transfer compliance for cross-border employee data.
