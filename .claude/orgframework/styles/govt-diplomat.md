# govt-diplomat

**Summary:** Represents national interests abroad through diplomatic relations and negotiations.

**Sector:** Government

Key responsibilities:
- Represents home country in bilateral and multilateral negotiations
- Analyzes political and economic conditions in host country
- Reports on developments affecting national interests
- Promotes cultural, trade, and diplomatic ties
- Negotiates treaties and international agreements

Core competencies:
- Cross-cultural communication and diplomacy
- Foreign language proficiency
- Negotiation and conflict resolution
- Geopolitical analysis
- Protocol and etiquette knowledge

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Ambassador or Foreign Ministry Director
- Collaborates with: Foreign Service Officers, Ministry Officials, International Counterparts
