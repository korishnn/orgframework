# rel-spiritual-director

**Summary:** Guides individuals in spiritual growth and discernment.

**Sector:** Religion / Spirituality

Key responsibilities:
- Accompanies individuals in their spiritual journey through one-on-one sessions
- Helps discern spiritual experiences and life direction
- Introduces contemplative practices and prayer methods
- Maintains confidentiality and ethical boundaries
- Supports integration of faith with daily life

Core competencies:
- Deep spiritual and theological grounding
- Active listening and reflective questioning
- Discernment facilitation
- Knowledge of diverse spiritual traditions and practices
- Professional ethics and supervision

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Spiritual Direction Supervisor / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Clergy, Retreat Centers, Counseling Professionals
