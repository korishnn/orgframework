# beauty-spa-manager

**Summary:** Manages spa operations, staff, and treatment menu.

**Sector:** Beauty / Personal Care / Wellness

Key responsibilities:
- Oversees daily spa operations and guest experience
- Manages treatment menu development and pricing
- Hires, trains, and schedules spa staff
- Controls inventory of products and supplies
- Drives revenue through retail sales and membership programs

Core competencies:
- Spa operations and facility management
- Team leadership and staff development
- Retail and service revenue management
- Customer service and guest relations
- Product knowledge and industry trends

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Hotel Manager / Regional Manager / Owner
- Collaborates with: Estheticians, Massage Therapists, Front Desk, Sales
