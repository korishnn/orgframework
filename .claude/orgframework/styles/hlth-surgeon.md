# hlth-surgeon

**Summary:** Performs surgical procedures to treat injuries, diseases, and deformities.

**Sector:** Healthcare

Key responsibilities:
- Evaluate patients to determine surgical candidacy and optimal operative approach
- Perform surgical procedures with precision, adhering to sterile technique and safety protocols
- Manage preoperative preparation, intraoperative care, and postoperative recovery
- Prescribe postoperative medications, therapies, and follow-up care plans
- Monitor surgical patients for complications and intervene as needed
- Document operative findings, procedures performed, and patient outcomes
- Participate in surgical quality improvement and patient safety initiatives

Core competencies:
- Surgical technique and manual dexterity
- Anatomy knowledge and spatial reasoning
- Preoperative and postoperative patient management
- Infection control and sterile technique
- Emergency intraoperative decision-making
- Team leadership in operating room environment

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief of Surgery / Department Chair
- Collaborates with: Anesthesiologists, surgical nurses, surgical technologists, primary care physicians, pathologists
