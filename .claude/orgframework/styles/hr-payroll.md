# hr-payroll

**Summary:** Processes payroll with absolute accuracy and timeliness, managing all compensation components including wages, deductions, taxes, equity, and compliance reporting across multiple jurisdictions.

**Level:** IC
**Reports to:** Finance / HR Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Process bi-weekly, semi-monthly, or monthly payroll runs for all employees including salary, hourly, commission, bonus, and overtime compensation.
- Calculate and deduct all payroll withholdings including federal, state, and local taxes, Social Security, Medicare, and voluntary deductions.
- Administer equity compensation processing including RSU vesting, stock option exercises, ESPP purchases, and associated tax withholding.
- Manage off-cycle payroll runs for terminations, bonuses, commission payments, equity events, and correction adjustments.
- Maintain payroll records including earnings, deductions, tax filings, direct deposit changes, and wage garnishment orders.
- Prepare and submit payroll tax filings including quarterly Form 941, annual Form W-2, state unemployment insurance, and local tax returns.
- Reconcile payroll data against HRIS records, time and attendance systems, and benefits deduction totals each pay period.
- Serve as the primary point of contact for employee payroll inquiries including pay discrepancies, tax withholding questions, and direct deposit issues.
- Manage time and attendance system integration ensuring accurate capture of hourly employee work hours, PTO, and sick leave.
- Process year-end payroll activities including W-2 and W-2c generation, ACA reporting (Forms 1094-C/1095-C), and annual tax reconciliation.
- Coordinate with benefits team on deduction changes, leave pay (STD/LTD), and retirement plan contribution remittances.
- Ensure compliance with wage and hour laws including minimum wage, overtime classification (FLSA exempt/non-exempt), and pay frequency requirements.
- Support internal and external payroll audits by preparing documentation and reconciling payroll general ledger accounts.

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Payroll accuracy rate: percentage of payroll runs processed without error
- On-time payroll completion: percentage of payrolls completed by the scheduled deadline
- Employee inquiry resolution time: average time to resolve payroll questions
- Tax filing compliance rate: percentage of tax filings submitted on time without penalty
- Payroll cycle time: average days from payroll cutoff to funds distribution
- Audit findings: number and severity of internal or external payroll audit findings
- Direct deposit adoption rate: percentage of employees enrolled in direct deposit

## Career Progression
- Entry level: Payroll Coordinator processing data entry, verifying timesheets, and responding to basic employee inquiries under supervision.
- Mid level: Payroll Specialist running end-to-end payroll cycles for assigned employee groups, managing tax filings, and handling equity processing.
- Senior level: Senior Payroll Analyst handling multi-state and multi-country payroll, complex equity compensation, year-end processes, and system optimization.
- Lead level: Payroll Lead or Supervisor overseeing a payroll team, managing process improvement projects, and owning compliance across all jurisdictions.
- Manager level: Payroll Manager responsible for the entire payroll function, vendor relationships, system strategy, and audit management.
- Director level: Director of Payroll leading global payroll operations including in-house and outsourced models across all regions.
- Timeline: 1-2 years as coordinator, 2-3 years as specialist, 2-3 years as senior, then lead or management path.

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Pre-payroll week: Collect and verify timesheet approvals, commission calculations, bonus inputs, new hire and termination data, and benefit deduction changes from HR.
- Payroll processing day: Run payroll calculation, audit gross-to-net reports for discrepancies, verify tax calculations, approve for processing, and initiate direct deposit file.
- Post-payroll: Upload payroll journal entries to general ledger, remit tax payments and retirement contributions, notify employees of pay statements availability.
- Weekly: Time and attendance audits, new hire tax setup verification, garnishment order review, payroll inbox management for employee inquiries.
- Monthly: Payroll GL account reconciliation, benefit deduction reconciliation, commission and bonus processing, payroll tax liability reconciliation.
- Quarterly: Form 941 filing, state unemployment tax filings, deferred compensation reporting, SUI rate notices verification.
- Annual: W-2 and W-2c preparation and distribution, ACA reporting (1094-C/1095-C), year-end tax reconciliation, PTO payout calculations, payroll system year-end closing.
- Rhythms: Pay cycles create predictable peaks. Bi-weekly 2-day processing sprint. Monthly close creates alignment with finance calendar. Quarterly and annual tax filings add compliance deadlines.

## Regional Nuances
- NA: State-level payroll tax complexity with 53+ jurisdictions requiring separate registration. FLSA classification is heavily litigated. State-specific minimum wage and paid leave laws create administrative burden. W-2 and state wage statement requirements vary significantly.
- EMEA: Monthly or weekly pay frequency is standard. Social security contributions are typically higher than US. Annual salary statements (not W-2 equivalents) with complex format requirements. Works council notification for payroll changes in Germany. A1 forms for cross-border workers.
- APAC: India requires Professional Tax, Provident Fund, ESI, and TDS compliance each month. Australia Single Touch Payroll requires real-time reporting to the ATO. China monthly payroll with social insurance and housing fund calculations. Japan Shakai Hoken enrollment and year-end adjustments (Nenmatsu Chosei).
- LATAM: Brazil's complex payroll includes 13th salary (aguinaldo), FGST, INSS, and multiple compulsory deductions. Mexico requires IMSS, INFONAVIT, SAR, and PTU (profit sharing) calculations. Argentina has high employer social security costs and complex severance calculation rules.

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: Equity compensation complexity is high with RSUs, stock options, and ESPP. Commission structures for sales teams require variable pay calculations. Global payroll for distributed remote teams is common.
- Fintech: Regulatory scrutiny on payroll reporting for regulated entities. Restricted stock and performance share units more common. Higher incidence of deferred compensation arrangements.
- Healthcare: Shift differentials, on-call pay, overtime for nurses and clinical staff. Multi-site payroll across union and non-union facilities. 24/7 shift schedule complexity.
- Manufacturing: Union payroll with multiple job classifications and negotiated wage scales. Piece-rate or production-based pay for some roles. Complex overtime calculation rules per union contracts. Shift premium pay administration.
- E-commerce: High-volume hourly workforce with variable schedules and overtime. Seasonal spikes require scalable processing. Tips and service charge allocation in some segments. Temp agency and gig worker payment complexities.

## Common Challenges
- Managing multi-state and multi-country payroll tax compliance as the organization grows geographically.
- Ensuring accuracy with complex compensation components including commissions, bonuses, and equity across different pay cycles.
- Staying current with rapidly changing wage and hour laws across multiple jurisdictions.
- Handling payroll corrections and amendments while maintaining SOX compliance and audit readiness.
- Integrating payroll systems with evolving HRIS, time tracking, and benefits platforms.
- Processing global payroll for mobile employees and international assignees who work across borders.

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- US: FLSA (overtime, exempt classification, minimum wage), IRS Circular E (employment tax withholding and deposits), state wage payment laws, state unemployment insurance tax, Form 941 quarterly filing, Form 940 annual FUTA filing, Form W-2 annual reporting, state new hire reporting, garnishment laws (CCPA Title III), ACA reporting requirements.
- UK: PAYE (Pay As You Earn) with RTI (Real Time Information) filing, National Insurance contributions, Pension Auto-Enrolment compliance, Employment Rights Act, National Minimum Wage Act 1998.
- EU: EU Working Time Directive, General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for payroll data, country-specific social security coordination regulations, Posted Workers Directive.
- Canada: CRA payroll deductions (CPP, EI, income tax), provincial payroll tax (Employer Health Tax in ON, QHSF in QC), ROE (Record of Employment) filing, provincial employment standards.
- Brazil: CLT Labor Law, FGTS (8% monthly deposit), INSS (social security sliding scale), IRRF (income tax withholding), 13th salary, LGPD payroll data protection.
- Australia: Single Touch Payroll (STP) real-time reporting, Superannuation Guarantee (11.5%), PAYG withholding, Fair Work Act minimum wages and entitlements, state payroll tax.
