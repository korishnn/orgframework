# tran-mariner

**Summary:** Performs deck duties and vessel operations as a crew member on commercial ships, including navigation watch and maintenance.

**Sector:** Maritime

Key responsibilities:
- Stands navigational watches and assists with bridge operations
- Performs deck maintenance including painting, chipping rust, and rigging
- Assists with mooring, anchoring, and cargo operations
- Conducts safety drills and maintains life-saving equipment
- Handles lines, gangways, and pilot ladders during port operations

Core competencies:
- STCW certification including basic safety and survival craft training
- Seamanship skills - knot tying, line handling, rigging
- Physical fitness and ability to work in adverse weather conditions
- Watchkeeping and situational awareness at sea
- Teamwork and communication in a crew environment

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief Mate or Boatswain
- Collaborates with: Deck Officers, Engineering Crew, Fellow Mariners, and Dock Workers
