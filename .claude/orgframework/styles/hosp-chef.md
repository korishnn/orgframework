# hosp-chef

**Summary:** Leads kitchen operations, creates menus, and oversees food preparation.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Develops and designs menu items and seasonal offerings
- Oversees food preparation, plating, and quality standards
- Manages kitchen staff including hiring, training, and scheduling
- Controls food costs through inventory management and vendor relations
- Ensures compliance with health and safety regulations

Core competencies:
- Culinary expertise and recipe development
- Kitchen management and team leadership
- Inventory and cost control
- Creativity and menu innovation
- Food safety and sanitation knowledge

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Restaurant Manager / Executive Chef
- Collaborates with: Sous Chef, Pastry Chef, Restaurant Manager, Purchasing
