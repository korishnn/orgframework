# fin-stockbroker

**Summary:** Executes securities trades and manages client investment accounts.

**Sector:** Finance

Key responsibilities:
- Executes buy and sell orders for stocks, bonds, options, and other securities
- Provides market research, trade recommendations, and investment guidance to clients
- Monitors market conditions and economic indicators to inform trading decisions
- Ensures compliance with securities regulations and firm trading policies

Core competencies:
- Series 7 and Series 63 licenses (or regional equivalents)
- Strong analytical skills and real-time market knowledge
- Ability to work under pressure in fast-moving trading environments
- Proficiency with trading platforms, order management systems, and market data tools

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Branch Manager or Head of Trading
- Collaborates with: Research analysts, compliance officers, financial advisors, clearing operations
