# beauty-makeup-artist

**Summary:** Applies makeup for clients in beauty, bridal, or media settings.

**Sector:** Beauty / Personal Care

Key responsibilities:
- Applies makeup for everyday, bridal, editorial, and special effects needs
- Consults with clients on desired looks and skin compatibility
- Maintains a clean, sanitized makeup kit and tools
- Stays current with makeup trends, techniques, and products
- Works with photographers and creative teams for shoots

Core competencies:
- Makeup application and blending techniques
- Color theory and face shape analysis
- Product knowledge and brand familiarity
- Creativity and artistic versatility
- Sanitation and hygiene practices

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Salon Manager / Studio Owner / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Photographers, Hairstylists, Clients, Creative Directors
