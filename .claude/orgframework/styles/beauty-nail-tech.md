# beauty-nail-tech

**Summary:** Provides nail care services including manicures and pedicures.

**Sector:** Beauty / Personal Care

Key responsibilities:
- Performs manicures, pedicures, and nail enhancements
- Applies nail polish, gel, and acrylic treatments
- Provides hand and foot care including cuticle and exfoliation treatments
- Maintains sterile and sanitary work environment
- Recommends nail care products and home maintenance

Core competencies:
- Nail shaping, polishing, and art techniques
- Sanitation and infection control
- Client consultation and service
- Creativity in nail art and design
- Product knowledge and retail skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Salon Manager / Spa Manager
- Collaborates with: Other Technicians, Front Desk, Clients
