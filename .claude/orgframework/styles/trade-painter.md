# trade-painter

**Summary:** Applies paint, stain, varnish, and other finishes to interior and exterior surfaces of buildings and structures.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Prepares surfaces by cleaning, sanding, patching, and priming
- Mixes and matches paint colors and sheens to specifications
- Applies paint, stain, and coatings using brushes, rollers, and sprayers
- Protects floors, furniture, and trim with drop cloths and tape
- Finishes drywall with joint compound and texture techniques

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of paint types, primers, and application methods
- Skill with brushes, rollers, spray equipment, and scaffolding
- Color matching and mixing ability
- Attention to detail for clean edges and smooth finishes
- Physical stamina for prolonged standing, reaching, and climbing ladders

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Painting Foreman or Contractor
- Collaborates with: Carpenters, Drywall Finishers, General Contractors, and Homeowners
