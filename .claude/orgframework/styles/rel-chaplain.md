# rel-chaplain

**Summary:** Provides spiritual care in non-congregational settings like hospitals or military.

**Sector:** Religion / Spirituality

Key responsibilities:
- Provides spiritual and emotional support to patients, families, or personnel
- Offers prayer, counseling, and ritual services as requested
- Supports individuals facing crisis, trauma, or end-of-life situations
- Collaborates with interdisciplinary care teams
- Respects and supports diverse religious and spiritual backgrounds

Core competencies:
- Interfaith spiritual care and counseling
- Crisis intervention and trauma support
- Active listening and empathy
- Ethical decision-making and confidentiality
- Collaboration with healthcare or military teams

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Director of Chaplaincy / Hospital Administration / Command
- Collaborates with: Medical Staff, Social Workers, Mental Health Professionals
