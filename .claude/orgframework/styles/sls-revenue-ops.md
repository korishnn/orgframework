# sls-revenue-ops

**Summary:** Revenue Operations aligns sales, marketing, and customer success data, processes, and technology to create a unified revenue engine that drives predictable growth and end-to-end revenue efficiency.

**Level:** Manager
**Reports to:** COO / CRO
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Architect and manage the end-to-end revenue technology stack spanning CRM, MAP, CS, and analytics platforms
- Align sales, marketing, and customer success processes, data definitions, and workflows for seamless cross-functional operation
- Define, track, and report on revenue KPIs across the full customer lifecycle from lead to expansion
- Build executive-level reporting on funnel health, conversion rates, pipeline velocity, and revenue performance
- Drive data governance, data quality standards, and master data management across revenue systems
- Lead strategic planning for revenue process improvements, automation, and operational efficiency
- Facilitate cross-functional alignment on revenue definitions, metrics, reporting standards, and SLAs
- Manage revenue system integrations, data flow architecture, and ensure data consistency between platforms
- Design and optimize lead-to-revenue processes including lead routing, scoring, handoffs, and lifecycle management
- Support annual planning processes including revenue target setting, capacity modeling, and resource allocation
- Develop revenue analytics and insights that drive strategic decisions for go-to-market leadership
- Manage revenue operations team, budgets, and vendor relationships for the revenue technology stack

## Core Competencies
- Deep understanding of sales, marketing, and customer success operations and their interdependencies
- Expertise with revenue operations platforms (Salesforce, Marketo, HubSpot, Gainsight, Outreach)
- Strong analytical and data architecture skills for building unified data models and reporting
- Experience with revenue attribution, multi-touch attribution models, and funnel analytics
- Executive communication and cross-functional leadership abilities working with multiple department heads
- Strategic thinking with a focus on process optimization, scalability, and operational excellence
- Understanding of SaaS metrics (ARR, NRR, LTV, CAC, payback, churn) and go-to-market economics
- Project management for complex cross-functional initiatives with organizational impact
- Experience with data governance and maintaining data quality across systems
- Change management skills for driving process and system adoption across teams

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Revenue forecast accuracy and predictability (quarterly guidance vs. actuals)
- Funnel conversion rates across lead-to-revenue stages
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) payback period and LTV:CAC ratio
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR) and Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)
- Marketing sourced vs. sales sourced pipeline ratio and conversion
- Lead response time and lead-to-opportunity conversion rate
- Revenue tech stack cost efficiency and vendor consolidation progress
- Cross-functional SLA attainment (marketing-to-sales, sales-to-CS handoff)

## Career Progression
- Analyst (0-2 years): Supports data analysis, report building, system administration
- Manager (2-5 years): Owns revenue operations for a region or function, manages systems
- Senior Manager (5-8 years): Cross-functional revenue operations, process design, team leadership
- Director (8-12 years): Organizational revenue operations strategy, executive partnership, multi-team leadership
- VP (12+ years): Revenue operations and analytics executive leadership, organizational design
- Typical progression: Analyst to Manager 2-3 years, Manager to Senior 2-3 years, Senior to Director 2-4 years

## Regional Nuances
- NA: Most mature RevOps function with dedicated leadership; integrated revenue teams common; strong emphasis on data-driven decision making; advanced analytics and attribution adoption
- EMEA: Growing RevOps function; GDPR compliance integrated into all revenue processes; multi-language and multi-currency operations complexity; strong focus on privacy-compliant revenue operations
- APAC: Early RevOps maturity; often combined roles due to leaner teams; rapid revenue scaling driving ops needs; high growth markets requiring adaptable processes
- LATAM: Emerging RevOps function; broader scope due to team size; cost-effective technology choices; focus on foundational process before advanced analytics

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: Subscription revenue operations including ARR management, usage-based pricing ops, self-serve and sales-assisted funnel complexity, multi-product revenue operations
- Fintech: Regulatory-compliant revenue operations; complex transaction revenue models; KYC/AML process integration; fraud-aware revenue operations
- Healthcare: HIPAA-compliant revenue data management; provider and payer revenue cycle integration; compliance-reviewed go-to-market processes
- Enterprise Software: Complex deal structures (multi-year, usage, seat-based), professional services revenue ops, government procurement revenue operations

## Common Challenges
- Aligning three historically siloed functions (marketing, sales, CS) around unified goals and metrics
- Building comprehensive attribution models that fairly credit all revenue influence touchpoints
- Managing data quality and consistency across multiple systems and teams
- Driving cross-functional process adoption and breaking down organizational silos
- Keeping pace with the expanding revenue technology stack complexity
- Creating a single source of truth for revenue data across disparate systems

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- SOX for public company revenue reporting and financial controls
- ASC 606 for revenue recognition compliance
- GDPR for EMEA revenue data processing
- CCPA for California prospect and customer data in revenue systems
- HIPAA for healthcare revenue operations
- PCI-DSS for payment data in revenue processes
- FINRA for financial services revenue operations
- Data residency regulations affecting revenue data storage and processing
