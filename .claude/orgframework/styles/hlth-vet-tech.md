# hlth-vet-tech

**Summary:** Assists veterinarians with procedures, tests, and animal care.

**Sector:** Veterinary

Key responsibilities:
- Assist veterinarians during examinations, surgeries, and dental procedures
- Collect and process laboratory samples including blood, urine, and feces
- Administer medications, vaccines, and treatments as directed
- Take and develop radiographs for diagnostic purposes
- Monitor anesthesia during surgical procedures
- Provide nursing care for hospitalized animals
- Educate pet owners on medication administration and home care

Core competencies:
- Animal handling and restraint
- Veterinary laboratory procedures and sample collection
- Surgical assistance and sterile technique
- Anesthesia monitoring and patient vital signs
- Radiography positioning and technique
- Client communication and education

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Lead Veterinary Technician / Practice Manager
- Collaborates with: Veterinarians, veterinary assistants, practice staff, pet owners
