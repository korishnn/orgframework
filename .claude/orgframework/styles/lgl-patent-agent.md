# lgl-patent-agent

**Summary:** Prepares and prosecutes patent applications before patent offices.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Drafts patent applications including claims, specifications, and drawings
- Prosecutes patent applications by responding to office actions and examiner rejections
- Conducts prior art searches and patentability assessments
- Advises clients on patent strategy and intellectual property protection

Core competencies:
- Registration to practice before the USPTO (or equivalent patent office)
- Technical or scientific degree in a relevant engineering or science discipline
- Strong technical writing and patent drafting skills
- Knowledge of patent law, examination procedures, and case law

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Patent Department Head or Lead Counsel
- Collaborates with: Patent attorneys, inventors, R&D teams, patent examiners, IP paralegals
