# media-sports-broadcaster

**Summary:** Provides play-by-play or commentary for sports broadcasts.

**Sector:** Media

Key responsibilities:
- Provides live play-by-play commentary during sporting events
- Offers expert analysis and color commentary before, during, and after games
- Conducts pre-game and post-game interviews with athletes and coaches
- Researches teams, players, statistics, and storylines for broadcast preparation

Core competencies:
- Deep knowledge of multiple sports, rules, and game strategy
- Strong voice, diction, and on-air presence
- Ability to think and speak quickly during live, unpredictable events
- Storytelling skills and engaging personality for audience connection

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Sports Director or Broadcast Producer
- Collaborates with: Producers, camera operators, statisticians, sports journalists
