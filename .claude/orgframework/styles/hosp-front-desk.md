# hosp-front-desk

**Summary:** Handles guest check-in/out, reservations, and inquiries.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Processes guest check-ins and check-outs efficiently
- Manages room reservations and modifications
- Answers guest inquiries and provides information about the property
- Handles payments, billing, and guest folios
- Coordinates with housekeeping and maintenance for guest requests

Core competencies:
- Customer service and hospitality
- Reservation and property management systems
- Communication and phone etiquette
- Multitasking and organization
- Problem resolution

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Front Desk Manager / Hotel Manager
- Collaborates with: Housekeeping, Concierge, Maintenance, Reservations
