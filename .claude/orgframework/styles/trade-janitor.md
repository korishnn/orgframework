# trade-janitor

**Summary:** Cleans and maintains buildings and facilities to ensure sanitary, safe, and orderly environments.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Sweeps, mops, vacuums, and buffs floors and carpets
- Cleans and sanitizes restrooms, break rooms, and common areas
- Empties trash receptacles and disposes of waste properly
- Restocks supplies including soap, paper towels, and toilet paper
- Reports maintenance issues and cleans up spills or hazards

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of cleaning chemicals, proper dilution, and safe handling
- Proficiency with floor care equipment (buffers, extractors, auto-scrubbers)
- Attention to detail and thoroughness in cleaning routines
- Time management for completing assigned areas within schedule
- Dependability and ability to work independently or on a crew

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Custodial Supervisor or Facility Manager
- Collaborates with: Facility Maintenance Staff, Building Tenants, and Office Managers
