# hlth-phlebotomist

**Summary:** Draws blood samples from patients for testing.

**Sector:** Allied Health

Key responsibilities:
- Perform venipuncture and capillary blood collection from patients
- Properly label and handle blood specimens for laboratory processing
- Verify patient identity and confirm test orders before collection
- Maintain aseptic technique and infection prevention during procedures
- Manage patient anxiety and ensure comfort during blood draws
- Process and prepare specimens for transport and analysis
- Maintain phlebotomy supplies and equipment

Core competencies:
- Venipuncture and capillary puncture techniques
- Blood specimen collection and handling protocols
- Patient identification and verification procedures
- Infection control and standard precautions
- Patient communication and bedside manner
- Knowledge of order of draw and tube additives

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Laboratory Manager / Phlebotomy Supervisor
- Collaborates with: Laboratory technicians, nurses, physicians, patients, front desk staff
