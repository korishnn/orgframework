# sport-fitness-trainer

**Summary:** Designs and leads exercise programs for clients.

**Sector:** Sports / Wellness

Key responsibilities:
- Designs personalized fitness programs based on client goals
- Leads one-on-one and group training sessions
- Instructs proper exercise technique and form
- Tracks client progress and adjusts programs accordingly
- Educates clients on nutrition, recovery, and healthy habits

Core competencies:
- Exercise science and program design
- Coaching and motivational skills
- Knowledge of anatomy and biomechanics
- Client assessment and goal setting
- Safety and injury prevention

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Gym Manager / Studio Owner / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Other Trainers, Nutritionists, Physical Therapists
