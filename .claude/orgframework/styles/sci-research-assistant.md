# sci-research-assistant

**Summary:** Supports research projects through experiments and data collection.

**Sector:** Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Conduct experiments and laboratory procedures under supervision
- Collect, record, and organize research data accurately
- Maintain lab notebooks and documentation
- Assist with literature reviews and data analysis
- Prepare samples, reagents, and materials for experiments

Core competencies:
- Laboratory techniques and experimental protocols
- Data recording and attention to detail
- Literature search and basic data analysis
- Time management and task prioritization

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Principal Investigator / Lab Manager / Research Scientist
- Collaborates with: Postdocs, Graduate students, Other research assistants
