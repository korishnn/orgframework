# edu-corporate-trainer

**Summary:** Designs and delivers training programs for employees.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Assess organizational training needs and skill gaps
- Design and develop engaging training programs and materials
- Facilitate workshops, seminars, and e-learning courses
- Evaluate training effectiveness and measure learning outcomes
- Maintain knowledge of industry trends and compliance requirements

Core competencies:
- Instructional design and adult learning theory
- Facilitation and public speaking skills
- Training needs analysis and program evaluation
- Adaptability across subject matter and audience levels

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Training & Development Manager / HR Director
- Collaborates with: Department managers, Subject matter experts, HR Business Partners, External vendors
