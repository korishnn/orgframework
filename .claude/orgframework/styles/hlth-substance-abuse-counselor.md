# hlth-substance-abuse-counselor

**Summary:** Treats addiction and supports recovery from substance use disorders.

**Sector:** Mental Health

Key responsibilities:
- Assess clients for substance use disorders and co-occurring conditions
- Develop individualized treatment and recovery plans
- Facilitate individual and group counseling sessions
- Educate clients on addiction, relapse prevention, and coping skills
- Administer or coordinate urine drug screening and monitoring
- Refer clients to detoxification, rehabilitation, and aftercare programs
- Document treatment progress and maintain confidentiality

Core competencies:
- Addiction counseling and motivational interviewing
- Knowledge of substance use disorders and treatment modalities
- Relapse prevention planning and skill-building
- Group facilitation and peer support models
- Co-occurring disorder recognition and referral
- Ethical practice and confidentiality (42 CFR Part 2)

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Clinical Director / Substance Abuse Program Manager
- Collaborates with: Psychiatrists, social workers, therapists, medical providers, recovery support groups, legal system
