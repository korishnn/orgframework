# cre-videographer

**Summary:** Shoots and edits video content for various media platforms, producing visual narratives for commercial, editorial, and digital use.

**Sector:** Digital Content

Key responsibilities:
- Plan, shoot, and direct video content for various platforms and clients
- Operate cameras, lighting, and audio equipment during production
- Edit raw footage into polished final videos with pacing and narrative flow
- Color grade, mix audio, and add graphics to video projects
- Collaborate with creative teams on concept development and storyboarding
- Manage video production schedules and deliverables

Core competencies:
- Proficiency with video cameras, lighting, and audio recording equipment
- Expertise with video editing software (Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve)
- Understanding of composition, pacing, and visual storytelling
- Knowledge of video formats, codecs, and delivery specifications
- Project management and client communication skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Creative Director / Content Manager
- Collaborates with: Director, Editor, Sound Designer, Graphic Designer, Content Creator, Client
