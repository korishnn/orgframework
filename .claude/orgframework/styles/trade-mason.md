# trade-mason

**Summary:** Builds structures from brick, block, stone, and concrete for walls, foundations, walkways, and architectural features.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Lays brick, block, and stone using mortar to construct walls and structures
- Mixes and places concrete for foundations, slabs, and decorative surfaces
- Reads blueprints to determine dimensions, reinforcement, and material needs
- Cuts and shapes masonry materials to precise specifications
- Repairs and restores existing masonry and concrete structures

Core competencies:
- Skill with trowels, levels, jointers, and masonry saws
- Knowledge of mortar mixing ratios and concrete curing processes
- Blueprint reading and layout measurement
- Physical endurance for heavy lifting, kneeling, and repetitive motion
- Attention to detail for level and plumb finish work

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Masonry Foreman or Construction Supervisor
- Collaborates with: Carpenters, General Laborers, Concrete Finishers, and Architects
