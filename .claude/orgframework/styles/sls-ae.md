# sls-ae

**Summary:** Account Executive owns the complete sales cycle from prospecting through qualification, demonstration, negotiation, and close, driving revenue growth against a quota.

**Level:** IC (quota-carrying)
**Reports to:** Sales Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Manage the complete sales cycle from lead qualification through discovery, demonstration, proposal, negotiation, and close
- Conduct product demonstrations and articulate compelling value propositions tailored to prospect needs and pain points
- Build and maintain a qualified sales pipeline to meet or exceed quarterly and annual revenue quota targets
- Develop strategic account plans and execute territory plans to maximize revenue coverage
- Negotiate pricing, contract terms, and legal agreements within approved deal parameters
- Maintain accurate forecasts, pipeline updates, and sales stage progression in CRM
- Partner with solutions engineers for technical validation, proof of concept, and complex technical deal support
- Leverage customer references and case studies to accelerate deal cycles and de-risk buying decisions
- Manage stakeholder relationships across prospect organizations including executives, line of business, procurement, and legal
- Collaborate with marketing on demand generation programs and campaign-driven pipeline acceleration
- Participate in sales training, enablement sessions, and ongoing skill development
- Provide market feedback to product management on competitive positioning, feature demand, and product requirements
- Maintain deep product knowledge, competitive intelligence, and industry expertise

## Core Competencies
- Proven track record of exceeding sales quotas in a comparable sales environment
- Strong consultative selling skills with ability to uncover business pain and build value-based justification
- Excellent presentation, storytelling, and executive communication abilities
- CRM proficiency (Salesforce, HubSpot) for pipeline management and forecasting
- Understanding of complex B2B sales cycles, procurement processes, and organizational buying dynamics
- Relationship building and stakeholder management across multiple levels and departments
- Negotiation skills for pricing, contract terms, and multi-party deal structures
- Time management and territory planning for effective pipeline coverage
- Resilience and discipline for managing long sales cycles and deal volatility

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Quota attainment percentage (quarterly and annual)
- Average deal size and sales cycle length
- Pipeline coverage ratio (weighted pipeline to quota multiple)
- Win rate (opportunity-to-close conversion percentage)
- Forecast accuracy (commit vs. actual attainment)
- ACV (Annual Contract Value) and TCV (Total Contract Value)
- Customer acquisition cost efficiency relative to deal value
- Tenure and customer retention of closed deals

## Career Progression
- Junior AE (0-2 years in closing role): Handles smaller deals, SMB or mid-market, builds foundational closing skills
- Mid-Market AE (2-4 years): Manages complex mid-market deals, consistent quota attainment
- Enterprise AE (4-7 years): Handles enterprise-scale deals, multi-stakeholder sales, strategic account execution
- Senior/Principal AE (7+ years): Strategic deals, named accounts, C-suite relationships, highest quota expectations
- Manager track: Sales Manager (leads team of AEs) or Director of Sales (multiple teams)
- IC track: Major Account Executive, Strategic Account Executive (largest, most complex accounts)
- Typical progression: Junior to Mid 1-2 years, Mid to Enterprise 2-3 years, Enterprise to Senior 2-4 years

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Morning pipeline review and deal progression assessment for active opportunities
- Prospect discovery calls and initial qualification meetings scheduled with BDR-sourced leads
- Product demonstrations tailored to specific prospect use cases and pain points
- Proposal development and pricing configuration using CPQ tools
- Internal deal reviews with sales management for forecast updates and deal strategy
- Contract negotiation and legal review coordination with procurement teams
- Account research and territory planning for pipeline building activities
- Weekly 1:1 with sales manager for coaching, deal strategy, and performance review
- CRM updates throughout the day for accurate pipeline management

## Regional Nuances
- NA: Highest compensation with uncapped commissions; aggressive quota culture; strong CRM and sales tool adoption; high deal velocity; sophisticated procurement processes
- EMEA: Stronger employment protections; works council involvement in some countries; longer vacation extending sales cycles in August and December; GDPR expertise expected; multi-currency deal management
- APAC: Relationship-driven sales culture; longer trust-building phase; entertainment and face-to-face valued; hierarchical decision-making; varying business practices across countries
- LATAM: Relationship-focused selling; complex import/export considerations for software; currency volatility affects deal pricing; RFPs more common; government procurement in some markets

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: Subscription-based pricing, usage-based models, product-led growth influence on sales, multi-year commitments with discounting, customer success handoff critical
- Fintech: Long regulatory approval cycles, compliance-driven buying, trust and security buying factors, financial justification requirements, procurement with legal oversight
- Healthcare: Clinical and administrative buyer complexity, HIPAA compliance in every deal, long evaluation cycles, FDA-regulated product considerations, provider-specific procurement
- Manufacturing: Engineering-led buying processes, pilot-driven evaluations, integration requirements, plant-level and enterprise-level decisions, multi-site deployment selling

## Common Challenges
- Managing pipeline volatility and unpredictable deal slippage across quarters
- Navigating complex multi-stakeholder buying committees with competing priorities
- Balancing discount pressure with margin preservation and deal profitability
- Differentiating against entrenched competitors with established relationships
- Maintaining consistent performance across economic cycles and market changes
- Forecasting accurately and managing deal qualification rigor

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- SOX for public company revenue recognition and sales practices
- GDPR for EMEA customer data handling in sales processes
- Anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws (FCPA, UK Bribery Act)
- Export control regulations for cross-border sales
- Industry-specific regulations (FDA for health, SEC for fintech)
- Contract law across jurisdictions for international sales
- Data privacy regulations affecting prospect and customer information
