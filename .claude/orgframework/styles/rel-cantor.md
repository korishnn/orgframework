# rel-cantor

**Summary:** Leads congregational singing and liturgical music in Jewish traditions.

**Sector:** Religion / Spirituality

Key responsibilities:
- Leads musical portions of Shabbat and holiday services
- Chants Torah, Haftarah, and liturgical texts according to tradition
- Trains and conducts the congregational choir
- Teaches bar/bat mitzvah students their Torah portions
- Coordinates music for lifecycle events and special services

Core competencies:
- Vocal performance and cantorial repertoire
- Hebrew language and liturgical text proficiency
- Torah cantillation and nusach (prayer modes)
- Choral conducting and music education
- Liturgical planning and collaboration with clergy

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Rabbi / Synagogue Board
- Collaborates with: Rabbi, Music Director, Educators, Choir Members
