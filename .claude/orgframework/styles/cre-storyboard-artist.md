# cre-storyboard-artist

**Summary:** Draws visual storyboards to plan film, animation, or commercial sequences, translating scripts into sequential visual panels for pre-production.

**Sector:** Film

Key responsibilities:
- Translate scripts and briefs into sequential visual storyboard panels
- Sketch camera angles, compositions, and character blocking
- Convey action sequences, scene transitions, and narrative flow
- Work with directors to visualize scenes before production
- Create animatics by timing storyboard panels to audio or dialogue
- Revise boards based on directorial and creative feedback

Core competencies:
- Strong drawing and visual communication skills
- Understanding of cinematic composition, camera angles, and editing
- Ability to quickly visualize and iterate on story concepts
- Knowledge of film language, pacing, and sequence construction
- Collaboration with directors and creative teams

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Director / Creative Director
- Collaborates with: Director, Animator, Cinematographer, Production Designer, Art Director
