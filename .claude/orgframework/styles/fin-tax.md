# fin-tax

**Summary:** Manages all tax compliance, reporting, planning, and controversy matters including income tax, indirect tax, international tax, and transfer pricing to ensure compliance while optimizing the organization's global tax position.

**Level:** IC
**Reports to:** CFO / Tax Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Prepare and file federal, state, and local income tax returns (Form 1120, state returns) accurately and on time, including all supporting schedules and elections.
- Manage quarterly estimated tax payment calculations and filings to avoid underpayment penalties across all applicable jurisdictions.
- Compute the quarterly and annual tax provision (ASC 740) including current and deferred tax expense, valuation allowances, and uncertain tax positions.
- Monitor federal, state, and international tax legislation changes and assess the impact on the organization's tax position and effective tax rate.
- Develop and implement tax planning strategies to optimize the organization's global effective tax rate while maintaining compliance.
- Manage transfer pricing documentation and compliance including master file, local file, and country-by-country (CbC) reporting per OECD BEPS requirements.
- Support federal and state income tax audits, responding to information document requests (IDRs) and negotiating with tax authorities.
- Manage indirect tax compliance including sales and use tax, VAT/GST, excise tax, and property tax filings across all operating jurisdictions.
- Provide tax guidance on business transactions including M&A, equity financing, debt restructuring, intellectual property migrations, and entity rationalization.
- Manage employee compensation tax including equity compensation (RSU, stock option, ESPP) withholding and reporting.
- Research and document tax positions on complex or uncertain tax matters including memoranda for ASC 740-10 (FIN 48) reserves.
- Coordinate with external tax advisors on specialized tax matters, international compliance, and tax controversy support.
- Manage tax attribute tracking including NOL carryforwards, tax credit carryforwards (R&D credits, foreign tax credits), and tax basis.
- Prepare and maintain tax fixed asset schedules including depreciation and amortization for tax purposes (MACRS, Section 179, bonus depreciation).

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Tax return filing timeliness: percentage of all tax returns filed by the extended due date
- Effective tax rate: GAAP effective tax rate vs. strategic target
- Tax audit adjustment rate: percentage of tax return positions adjusted during audit
- Cash tax rate: actual cash taxes paid as percentage of pretax income
- Transfer pricing compliance: percentage of required documentation completed on time
- Provision accuracy: variance between estimated annual ETR and final audited ETR
- R&D credit per dollar of qualified spend: efficiency of R&D credit claims
- Tax controversy cost: professional fees and assessments per audit cycle

## Career Progression
- Entry level: Tax Analyst or Staff Tax Accountant preparing tax returns, gathering data, and maintaining tax workpapers.
- Mid level: Tax Accountant or Senior Tax Analyst managing assigned tax areas (compliance, provision, state taxes) and supporting tax planning.
- Senior level: Tax Manager overseeing a tax function area (compliance, international, provision), managing audits, and supervising staff.
- Lead level: Senior Tax Manager or Associate Director overseeing multiple tax functions, leading planning initiatives, and managing the external advisor relationship.
- Director level: Director of Tax owning the entire tax function, tax strategy, board reporting, and tax risk management.
- VP level: VP of Tax or Chief Tax Officer responsible for global tax strategy, policy advocacy, and executive leadership on tax matters.
- Timeline: 1-2 years as staff analyst, 2-3 years as senior analyst, 2-3 years as manager, then director track.

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- During close (days 1-5): Compute tax provision, prepare journal entries, review deferred tax balances, update FIN 48 reserves, provide tax rate inputs to fin-fpa.
- After close (days 5-15): Quarterly estimated tax payment calculations and payments, extension payment calculations, tax return preparation progress, audit response work.
- Ongoing: Research tax issues, respond to business queries on tax implications, review contracts for tax language, monitor legislative developments.
- Weekly: Review tax inbox and respond to IRS/DOR notices, check payment status, monitor pending legislation, coordinate with external advisors on active projects.
- Monthly: Estimated tax payment monitoring, sales and use tax filing preparation, property tax return preparation, tax provision data gathering.
- Quarterly: Estimated tax filings and payments, tax provision close, transfer pricing intercompany transactions documentation, BEAT/GILTI/FDII calculations.
- Annual peak (Jan-Sept): Tax return preparation and review. Returns due March 15 (entities) and April 15 (individual) with extensions to September 15 and October 15. Heavy intensity period requiring significant overtime.
- Rhythms: Quarterly estimated tax and provision cycles create regular deliverables. Annual return season creates a 6-9 month intense period. Audit periods are unpredictable and reactive.

## Regional Nuances
- NA: Complex multi-state income tax with apportionment and combined reporting. Federal rate 21% post-TCJA. GILTI, FDII, and BEAT rules are critical for multinationals. State rates range from 0-9.99%. Sales tax economic nexus post-Wayfair. Strong IRS audit presence for large corporates.
- EMEA: VAT rates range from 17-27% across EU countries. VAT compliance is a major administrative burden. German trade tax adds 7-17% on top of corporate income tax (15%+5.5% solidarity surcharge). Patent box regimes in UK, Netherlands, Ireland, France. Country-by-country reporting is mandatory for groups over EUR 750M.
- APAC: China's standard CIT rate is 25% with incentives for high-tech enterprises (15%). Australia's rate is 25% (30% for large entities). Japan's effective rate is approximately 30%. India's rate reduced to 22% for domestic companies (plus surcharge). Transfer pricing scrutiny is intense across APAC, particularly India and China.
- LATAM: Brazil's complex tax system has the highest compliance burden in the region (federal, state, and municipal taxes). Effective rates can exceed 34% in Brazil. Argentina has high rates and frequent changes. Mexico's rate is 30%. Transfer pricing rules are well-developed in Brazil, Mexico, and Argentina. Informality is a challenge in some markets.

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: R&D tax credits are significant (federal and state). ASC 606 implementation has tax implications. Stock-based compensation deduction timing (Section 83). Global digital services taxes (DST) in UK, France, Italy, etc. Multi-state SaaS sourcing for sales tax (method of taxation varies by state).
- Fintech: Specific tax rules for lending, payments, and investment activities. Regulatory fee deductions. Bad debt deduction rules specific to lending. State tax treatment of fintech services varies widely. Customer withholding tax considerations for payments businesses.
- Healthcare: Medicare and Medicaid reimbursement tax treatment. Provider tax and assessment deductibility. 340B program tax implications. Non-profit tax exemption for many healthcare organizations. IRC Section 501(r) requirements for non-profit hospitals.
- Manufacturing: LIFO inventory tax accounting methods. Section 199A qualified business income deduction (pass-through entities). Section 179 and bonus depreciation for equipment. Domestic production activities deduction (repealed but still has legacy implications). Export tax benefits.
- E-commerce: State sales tax economic nexus (Wayfair decision) creates compliance complexity for 12,000+ tax jurisdictions. Marketplace facilitator laws shifting responsibility to platforms. International cross-border tax compliance. Section 199A for qualified trade or business income. Digital services tax in multiple countries.

## Common Challenges
- Managing multi-state income tax compliance with 44 states filing corporate income tax and dozens of local returns.
- Keeping up with rapidly evolving international tax landscape (OECD BEPS Pillar 1 and 2, GloBE rules, minimum tax).
- Navigating the complexity of sales tax economic nexus post-Wayfair with 12,000+ taxing jurisdictions.
- Defending transfer pricing positions against increasingly aggressive tax authority audits worldwide.
- Accelerating digitization of tax authorities (IRS Digitalization, tax authority data analytics) requiring faster, more data-rich responses.
- Balancing tax planning objectives with reputational risk in an era of increased tax transparency scrutiny.

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- US: Internal Revenue Code (IRC), Treasury Regulations, IRS Revenue Procedures and Revenue Rulings, state-specific tax codes, ASC 740 (FIN 48) uncertain tax positions, SEC tax disclosure requirements, OECD BEPS Action 13 country-by-country reporting, FATCA for foreign accounts, Section 901/903 foreign tax credits, Section 482 transfer pricing, Section 59A BEAT, Section 250 FDII/GILTI, Section 174 R&D capitalization, Section 163(j) interest limitation.
- OECD: BEPS 15 Action Plan (including Pillar 1/Pillar 2 GloBE minimum tax of 15%), Country-by-Country Reporting (Action 13), Transfer Pricing Guidelines, Multilateral Instrument (MLI), Model Tax Convention, CRS (Common Reporting Standard) for automatic exchange of information.
- UK: Corporation Tax Act 2010, Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992, Value Added Tax Act 1994, HMRC transfer pricing and diverts profits tax code, UK DST (Digital Services Tax 2%), Annual Tax on Enveloped Dwellings (ATED).
- EU: EU Anti-Tax Avoidance Directive (ATAD I and II), EU VAT Directive, DAC6 mandatory disclosure rules, EU Code of Conduct Group business taxation, EU List of Non-Cooperative Jurisdictions, State Aid rules for tax rulings.
- Brazil: CTN (Codigo Tributario Nacional), RIR/2018 (Regulamento do Imposto de Renda), ICMS (state-level VAT), ISS (municipal service tax), PIS/COFINS (social contributions), IPI (federal excise tax), SPED fiscal digital filing obligations.
