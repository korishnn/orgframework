# rel-imam

**Summary:** Leads Islamic prayer and provides religious guidance to the Muslim community.

**Sector:** Religion / Spirituality

Key responsibilities:
- Leads the five daily prayers and Friday congregational prayers
- Delivers khutbah (sermon) at Friday services
- Provides religious education and Quranic instruction
- Offers marriage, family, and spiritual counseling
- Advises on Islamic law and ethical matters

Core competencies:
- Quran memorization and recitation (tajweed)
- Islamic jurisprudence and theology
- Arabic language proficiency
- Public speaking and teaching
- Community leadership and interfaith engagement

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Mosque Board / Islamic Center Administration
- Collaborates with: Islamic Educators, Community Leaders, Interfaith Partners
