# sci-geologist

**Summary:** Studies Earth's structure, materials, and geological processes.

**Sector:** Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Study rock formations, minerals, and Earth's geological history
- Conduct field surveys and collect geological samples
- Analyze geological data using maps, GIS, and laboratory techniques
- Assess natural resources such as minerals, oil, and groundwater
- Evaluate geological hazards such as earthquakes and landslides

Core competencies:
- Field geology and mapping skills
- Mineralogy, petrology, and geochemistry knowledge
- Geophysical survey and remote sensing techniques
- Hazard assessment and resource evaluation

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Research Director / Project Geologist
- Collaborates with: Geophysicists, Engineers, Mining companies, Environmental consultants, Government agencies
