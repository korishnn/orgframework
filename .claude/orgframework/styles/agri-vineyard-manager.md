# agri-vineyard-manager

**Summary:** Manages grape cultivation and vineyard operations for wine production.

**Sector:** Agriculture

Key responsibilities:
- Oversees all vineyard operations from planting to harvest
- Manages vine health, irrigation, and canopy management
- Implements pest and disease control programs
- Coordinates harvest timing and grape quality assessment
- Manages vineyard staff and seasonal labor

Core competencies:
- Viticulture and grape growing knowledge
- Pest and disease management in vineyards
- Irrigation and soil management
- Harvest planning and quality assessment
- Staff management and operational planning

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Winery Owner or Director of Viticulture
- Collaborates with: Winemakers, Enologists, Agricultural Consultants, Labor Crews
