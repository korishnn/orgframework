# cre-content-creator

**Summary:** Produces digital content across multiple platforms, developing engaging material for social media, video, audio, and written formats.

**Sector:** Digital Content

Key responsibilities:
- Create and publish content across social media, video, and digital platforms
- Develop content strategies aligned with audience growth and engagement goals
- Produce multimedia content including video, graphics, and written posts
- Analyze content performance metrics to refine strategy
- Engage with followers and build community around content
- Collaborate with brands on sponsored content partnerships

Core competencies:
- Proficiency across content formats (video, photo, writing, audio)
- Understanding of social media algorithms and platform best practices
- Content planning and editorial calendar management
- Basic video editing, graphic design, and photography skills
- Audience analysis and data-driven content optimization

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Content Manager / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Videographer, Graphic Designer, Social Media Manager, Brand Partners, Editor
