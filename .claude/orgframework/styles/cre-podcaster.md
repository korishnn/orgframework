# cre-podcaster

**Summary:** Creates and hosts audio podcast content, managing production, editing, and distribution while building an engaged listening audience.

**Sector:** Digital Content

Key responsibilities:
- Research, script, and prepare podcast episode content and topics
- Host and record podcast episodes with guests or solo commentary
- Edit audio for clarity, pacing, and production quality
- Publish and distribute episodes across podcast platforms
- Promote episodes through social media and audience engagement
- Manage show branding, artwork, and episode show notes

Core competencies:
- Strong verbal communication and interviewing skills
- Audio recording and editing proficiency (Audacity, GarageBand, Logic Pro)
- Content planning and editorial calendar management
- Audience development and community engagement abilities
- Understanding of podcast distribution and RSS feed management

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Content Director / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Sound Engineer, Content Creator, Guest Booking Coordinator, Marketing Team, Editor
