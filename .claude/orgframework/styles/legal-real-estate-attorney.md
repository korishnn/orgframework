# legal-real-estate-attorney

**Summary:** Handles real estate transactions, property disputes, and land use.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Drafts and reviews purchase agreements, leases, deeds, and title documents
- Conducts title searches and resolves title defects or encumbrances
- Manages property closings including escrow coordination and fund disbursement
- Advises on zoning, land use regulations, and property tax matters

Core competencies:
- Juris Doctor degree and state bar admission
- Knowledge of real property law, title insurance, and closing procedures
- Familiarity with zoning ordinances, land use regulations, and environmental laws
- Strong negotiation and document drafting skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Real Estate Practice Group Lead or Partner
- Collaborates with: Real estate agents, title companies, lenders, appraisers, surveyors
