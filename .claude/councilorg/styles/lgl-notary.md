# lgl-notary

**Summary:** Witnesses document signings and verifies identities for legal documents.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Verifies the identity of document signers through government-issued identification
- Witnesses signatures and administers oaths and affirmations
- Maintains a notary journal of all notarial acts performed
- Completes notarial certificates on deeds, affidavits, powers of attorney, and contracts

Core competencies:
- State-commissioned notary public credential
- Attention to detail and strict adherence to notarial laws and procedures
- Integrity and impartiality in performing notarial acts
- Knowledge of acceptable identification forms and record-keeping requirements

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Varies; often operates within a law firm, bank, or government office
- Collaborates with: Attorneys, title companies, real estate agents, lenders, general public
