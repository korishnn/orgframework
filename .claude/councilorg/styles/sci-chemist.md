# sci-chemist

**Summary:** Studies chemical compounds, reactions, and material properties.

**Sector:** Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Design and perform chemical experiments and analyses
- Synthesize and characterize chemical compounds
- Develop and validate analytical methods
- Document findings and maintain detailed laboratory records
- Ensure safe handling and disposal of chemical materials

Core competencies:
- Organic, inorganic, or analytical chemistry expertise
- Laboratory instrumentation and spectroscopy techniques
- Chemical synthesis and characterization
- Safety protocols and hazardous material management

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Lab Director / Principal Investigator
- Collaborates with: Research scientists, Chemical engineers, Quality control teams, Safety officers
