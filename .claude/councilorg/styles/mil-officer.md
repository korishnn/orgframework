# mil-officer

**Summary:** Leads military personnel and operations as a commissioned officer in the armed forces.

**Sector:** Military

Key responsibilities:
- Commands units and leads military personnel in operations
- Plans and executes missions and training exercises
- Manages unit readiness, equipment, and resources
- Provides leadership, mentorship, and professional development
- Advises senior leadership on tactical and strategic matters

Core competencies:
- Leadership and command authority
- Tactical and strategic planning
- Decision-making under pressure
- Personnel management and development
- Military doctrine and operations knowledge

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Senior Officer or Commander
- Collaborates with: Enlisted Personnel, Other Officers, Joint Force Partners, Civilian Leadership
