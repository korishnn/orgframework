# hosp-hotel-manager

**Summary:** Oversees hotel operations, guest services, and staff management.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Manages all hotel departments including front desk, housekeeping, and maintenance
- Ensures high standards of guest service and satisfaction
- Oversees budgets, revenue management, and financial reporting
- Implements property policies, procedures, and safety standards
- Drives occupancy and revenue through sales and marketing strategies

Core competencies:
- Hotel operations and multi-department management
- Financial acumen and revenue management
- Guest service and problem resolution
- Leadership and team building
- Regulatory compliance and safety

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Regional Manager / Owner / Corporate Leadership
- Collaborates with: Front Desk Manager, Housekeeping Manager, Sales, Maintenance
