# cre-vlogger

**Summary:** Creates video content for platforms like YouTube and TikTok, producing engaging visual stories that build audience and community.

**Sector:** Digital Content

Key responsibilities:
- Plan, script, and film video content for digital platforms
- Edit videos with pacing, graphics, music, and transitions
- Optimize video titles, thumbnails, and descriptions for discoverability
- Engage with viewers through comments and community features
- Maintain consistent posting schedules to grow audience
- Collaborate with brands and other creators on video projects

Core competencies:
- Video production skills including shooting and editing
- On-camera presence and natural communication abilities
- Understanding of platform algorithms (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram Reels)
- Video editing expertise (Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve)
- Audience growth and content strategy knowledge

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Content Manager / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Videographer, Editor, Content Creator, Graphic Designer, Brand Partners, Social Media Manager
