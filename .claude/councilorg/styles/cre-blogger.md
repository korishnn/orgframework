# cre-blogger

**Summary:** Writes and manages a blog on specific topics or niches, creating regular written content to inform, entertain, and engage readers.

**Sector:** Digital Content

Key responsibilities:
- Research and write blog posts on assigned or personal topics
- Develop editorial calendars and content publishing schedules
- Optimize blog content for SEO and reader engagement
- Manage blog platform, comments, and reader community
- Incorporate multimedia elements like images, videos, and infographics
- Monetize content through ads, sponsorships, or affiliate marketing

Core competencies:
- Strong writing and editorial skills for digital formats
- Understanding of SEO principles and keyword research
- Familiarity with blogging platforms (WordPress, Medium, Substack)
- Content planning and consistent publishing discipline
- Audience engagement and community management abilities

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Content Director / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Editor, Content Creator, Social Media Manager, SEO Specialist, Graphic Designer
