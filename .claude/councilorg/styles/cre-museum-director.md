# cre-museum-director

**Summary:** Leads museum strategy, operations, and programming, guiding institutional vision, financial sustainability, and public engagement.

**Sector:** Museums & Galleries

Key responsibilities:
- Set strategic direction and institutional vision for the museum
- Oversee financial management including budgets, fundraising, and endowments
- Lead curatorial, educational, and operational departments
- Develop exhibition programs and public engagement initiatives
- Build relationships with donors, board members, and community partners
- Represent the museum to media, government, and cultural organizations

Core competencies:
- Leadership and organizational management expertise
- Understanding of museum operations, collections, and curation
- Fundraising, development, and donor relations skills
- Strategic planning and financial acumen
- Public speaking and institutional representation abilities

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Board of Trustees
- Collaborates with: Curator, Conservator, Development Director, Exhibition Designer, Educator, Board Members
