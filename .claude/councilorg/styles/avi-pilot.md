# avi-pilot

**Summary:** Commands and operates aircraft for commercial or private aviation, responsible for flight safety and passenger transport.

**Sector:** Aviation

Key responsibilities:
- Commands aircraft as pilot-in-command, responsible for safe flight operations
- Conducts pre-flight planning including weather, route, and fuel calculations
- Operates aircraft controls during takeoff, cruise, approach, and landing
- Communicates with air traffic control and crew during all flight phases
- Manages in-flight emergencies and abnormal situations decisively

Core competencies:
- Airline Transport Pilot License (ATPL) or Commercial Pilot License
- Multi-engine and instrument rating qualifications
- Extensive flight hours in applicable aircraft type
- Leadership, crew resource management, and decision-making under pressure
- Regulatory knowledge of aviation laws and airspace procedures

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief Pilot or Director of Operations (Part 121/135)
- Collaborates with: First Officers, Flight Attendants, Dispatchers, Air Traffic Control, and Maintenance
