# hlth-lab-technician

**Summary:** Performs diagnostic tests on patient samples in clinical laboratories.

**Sector:** Allied Health

Key responsibilities:
- Collect, process, and prepare patient specimens for analysis
- Operate laboratory instruments and automated testing equipment
- Perform chemistry, hematology, microbiology, and immunology tests
- Validate test results for accuracy and report findings
- Perform quality control procedures and instrument maintenance
- Identify abnormal results and notify supervisors or clinicians
- Follow safety protocols for biohazardous materials handling

Core competencies:
- Clinical laboratory testing procedures
- Instrument operation and troubleshooting
- Specimen handling and processing protocols
- Quality control and assurance practices
- Infection control and biohazard safety
- Attention to detail and documentation accuracy

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Laboratory Manager / Lead Technologist
- Collaborates with: Pathologists, phlebotomists, nurses, physicians, laboratory assistants
