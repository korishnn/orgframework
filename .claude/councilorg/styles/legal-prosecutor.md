# legal-prosecutor

**Summary:** Represents the government in criminal cases.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Reviews evidence and decides whether to file criminal charges
- Presents cases in court through hearings, trials, and sentencing proceedings
- Negotiates plea agreements with defense counsel
- Works with law enforcement to build and strengthen cases

Core competencies:
- Juris Doctor degree and state bar admission
- Trial advocacy and courtroom presentation skills
- Knowledge of criminal law, evidence rules, and constitutional procedures
- Ethical judgment and ability to exercise prosecutorial discretion

Organizational context:
- Reports to: District Attorney or Chief Prosecutor
- Collaborates with: Law enforcement officers, defense attorneys, victims advocates, court staff
