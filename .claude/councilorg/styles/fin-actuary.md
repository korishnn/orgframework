# fin-actuary

**Summary:** Analyzes financial risk using statistical models for insurance and pensions.

**Sector:** Finance

Key responsibilities:
- Develops and maintains mathematical models to quantify financial risk and uncertainty
- Calculates premiums, reserves, and pension funding requirements
- Analyzes mortality, morbidity, and other statistical data to inform pricing
- Ensures regulatory compliance with actuarial standards and solvency requirements

Core competencies:
- Fellowship or associate credential from a recognized actuarial body (SOA, CAS)
- Advanced proficiency in statistical modeling and data analysis
- Strong mathematical, probability, and statistical theory background
- Knowledge of insurance products, pension plans, and risk management frameworks

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief Actuary or Actuarial Department Head
- Collaborates with: Underwriters, financial planners, data scientists, regulatory compliance teams
