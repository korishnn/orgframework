# acad-historian

**Summary:** Researches and interprets historical events and records.

**Sector:** Academia

Key responsibilities:
- Conduct archival research using primary and secondary sources
- Analyze historical documents, artifacts, and records
- Interpret and contextualize historical events and trends
- Write books, articles, and scholarly papers on historical topics
- Teach history courses and mentor student researchers

Core competencies:
- Archival research and source evaluation
- Historiography and historical methodology
- Analytical writing and narrative construction
- Foreign languages and paleography as needed

Organizational context:
- Reports to: University Department Chair / Museum Director
- Collaborates with: Archivists, Librarians, Other historians, Museum curators, Publishing editors
