# govt-inspector

**Summary:** Inspects facilities, operations, and products for compliance with government regulations and standards.

**Sector:** Government

Key responsibilities:
- Conducts on-site inspections of facilities and operations
- Reviews records and documentation for regulatory compliance
- Identifies violations and issues citations or corrective orders
- Provides guidance on compliance requirements
- Prepares detailed inspection reports and findings

Core competencies:
- Thorough knowledge of applicable regulations and codes
- Attention to detail and observation skills
- Investigative and analytical abilities
- Report writing and documentation
- Professional judgment and impartiality

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Inspection Supervisor or Agency Division Head
- Collaborates with: Agency Staff, Business Operators, Compliance Officers, Legal Counsel
