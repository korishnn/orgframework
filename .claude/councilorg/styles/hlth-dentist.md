# hlth-dentist

**Summary:** Diagnoses and treats oral health conditions and diseases.

**Sector:** Healthcare

Key responsibilities:
- Perform comprehensive oral examinations and diagnostic evaluations
- Diagnose dental caries, periodontal disease, oral pathology, and malocclusion
- Perform restorative procedures including fillings, crowns, bridges, and implants
- Conduct extractions, root canals, and minor oral surgeries
- Develop treatment plans and educate patients on oral hygiene and prevention
- Prescribe medications for dental conditions and infections
- Take and interpret dental radiographs

Core competencies:
- Dental diagnosis and treatment planning
- Restorative and surgical dental procedures
- Radiographic interpretation
- Infection control and sterilization protocols
- Patient communication and anxiety management
- Knowledge of oral-systemic health connections

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Dental Director / Practice Owner
- Collaborates with: Dental hygienists, dental assistants, dental laboratory technicians, oral surgeons, orthodontists
