# hlth-ophthalmologist

**Summary:** Diagnoses and treats eye diseases and performs eye surgery.

**Sector:** Alternative Medicine

Key responsibilities:
- Diagnose and manage medical and surgical conditions of the eye
- Perform eye surgeries including cataract, glaucoma, retinal, and refractive procedures
- Prescribe medications for ocular diseases and conditions
- Manage complex eye conditions such as diabetic retinopathy and macular degeneration
- Conduct comprehensive eye examinations using specialized diagnostic equipment
- Provide emergency eye care for trauma and acute vision loss
- Coordinate care with optometrists and primary care physicians

Core competencies:
- Ophthalmic surgery and microsurgical technique
- Diagnosis and management of ocular diseases
- Ocular pharmacology and therapeutics
- Interpretation of ophthalmic imaging and diagnostics
- Knowledge of ocular anatomy and pathology
- Preoperative and postoperative patient management

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Ophthalmology Department Chair / Practice Owner
- Collaborates with: Optometrists, ophthalmic technicians, ophthalmic nurses, primary care physicians, retinal specialists
