# fun-mortician

**Summary:** Prepares deceased bodies for burial or cremation.

**Sector:** Funeral Services

Key responsibilities:
- Prepares deceased bodies for viewing including embalming, dressing, and cosmetology
- Restores facial features and body presentation for open-casket services
- Assists funeral directors with service logistics and body transportation
- Maintains preparation facilities, equipment, and inventory supplies

Core competencies:
- Mortuary science education and state licensure
- Embalming, restorative art, and cosmetology skills
- Knowledge of sanitation, infection control, and health regulations
- Compassionate professionalism when handling deceased individuals and family interactions

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Funeral Director or Funeral Home Manager
- Collaborates with: Funeral directors, embalmers, crematory operators, family members
