# hlth-patient-coordinator

**Summary:** Coordinates patient care across providers and services.

**Sector:** Healthcare

Key responsibilities:
- Schedule patient appointments, procedures, and follow-up visits
- Facilitate communication between patients, providers, and healthcare facilities
- Coordinate referrals to specialists and ancillary services
- Verify insurance coverage and obtain prior authorizations
- Prepare patients for appointments with relevant medical records and information
- Follow up on test results and ensure timely communication to patients
- Assist patients with navigating the healthcare system and accessing resources

Core competencies:
- Healthcare scheduling and referral coordination
- Patient communication and customer service
- Insurance verification and authorization processes
- Electronic health record navigation
- Care coordination across multidisciplinary teams
- Problem-solving and conflict resolution

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Practice Manager / Care Coordination Director
- Collaborates with: Physicians, nurses, front desk staff, insurance companies, specialists offices, patients and families
