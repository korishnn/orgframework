# intel-signals-analyst

**Summary:** Intercepts and analyzes communications and electronic signals for intelligence purposes.

**Sector:** Intelligence

Key responsibilities:
- Intercepts and collects communications and electronic signals
- Analyzes signal characteristics and message content
- Identifies targets and metadata from signal traffic
- Maintains technical libraries and signal databases
- Develops collection requirements and tasking

Core competencies:
- Signals analysis and waveform identification
- Cryptologic and communications theory
- Technical proficiency with collection systems
- Foreign language capabilities
- Pattern analysis and traffic correlation

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Signals Intelligence Branch Chief
- Collaborates with: Intelligence Analysts, Cryptanalysts, Collection Managers, IT Security
