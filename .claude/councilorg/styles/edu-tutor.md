# edu-tutor

**Summary:** Provides individualized academic instruction and support to students.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Assess individual student learning needs and knowledge gaps
- Develop personalized tutoring plans and learning strategies
- Provide one-on-one or small group instruction in specific subjects
- Track student progress and adjust approaches as needed
- Build student confidence and independent learning skills

Core competencies:
- Deep knowledge in specific academic subjects
- Patience and ability to explain concepts in multiple ways
- Active listening and diagnostic assessment
- Motivational coaching and study skills guidance

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Tutoring Center Director / Lead Teacher (or self-employed)
- Collaborates with: Classroom Teachers, Parents, Students
