# avi-copilot

**Summary:** Assists the captain with aircraft operation, navigation, communication, and monitoring during all phases of flight.

**Sector:** Aviation

Key responsibilities:
- Assists captain with pre-flight checks, taxi, takeoff, and landing procedures
- Monitors flight instruments, aircraft systems, and navigation
- Communicates with air traffic control as directed by the captain
- Performs calculations for fuel, weight and balance, and performance
- Cross-checks captain's actions and handles duties during assigned flight legs

Core competencies:
- Commercial Pilot License with multi-engine and instrument ratings
- Strong technical knowledge of aircraft systems and performance
- Crew resource management and communication skills
- Situational awareness and ability to monitor multiple parameters
- Professionalism and support for captain's command authority

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Captain (pilot-in-command) and Chief Pilot
- Collaborates with: Captain, Flight Attendants, Dispatchers, and Ground Crew
