# hosp-event-coordinator

**Summary:** Plans and executes events from conception to completion.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Plans event logistics including venue, catering, and setup
- Coordinates with vendors, clients, and internal teams
- Manages event budgets and timelines
- Oversees on-site event execution and troubleshooting
- Conducts post-event evaluation and follow-up

Core competencies:
- Event planning and project management
- Vendor negotiation and relationship management
- Budget management and cost control
- Attention to detail and multitasking
- Client communication and customer service

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Events Manager / Director of Events
- Collaborates with: Catering, Venue Staff, Vendors, Clients
