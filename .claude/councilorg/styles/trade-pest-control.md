# trade-pest-control

**Summary:** Eliminates and prevents pest infestations in buildings, properties, and outdoor areas through inspection and treatment.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Inspects properties for signs of pests and identifies infestation sources
- Applies pesticides, baits, and traps according to label and regulations
- Seals entry points and recommends exclusion measures
- Develops integrated pest management plans for ongoing prevention
- Documents treatments and maintains compliance with pesticide regulations

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of pest identification, biology, and behavior patterns
- Understanding of pesticide chemistry, application methods, and safety
- State pesticide applicator licensing and continuing education
- Customer service and clear communication of treatment plans
- Attention to detail for thorough inspections and documentation

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Pest Control Service Manager or Business Owner
- Collaborates with: Property Managers, Homeowners, Exterminator Crews, and Regulatory Agencies
