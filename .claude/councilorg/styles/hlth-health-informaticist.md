# hlth-health-informaticist

**Summary:** Manages health IT systems and clinical data analytics.

**Sector:** Healthcare

Key responsibilities:
- Implement and optimize electronic health record systems and clinical applications
- Analyze clinical data to identify trends, outcomes, and quality improvement opportunities
- Design and maintain clinical decision support tools and alerts
- Ensure health data interoperability and compliance with data standards
- Train clinical staff on health information technology systems
- Manage health IT projects including system upgrades and migrations
- Protect patient data privacy and security in accordance with regulations

Core competencies:
- Health information systems and EHR implementation
- Clinical data analysis and visualization
- Health data standards (HL7, FHIR, ICD-10, SNOMED)
- Clinical workflow analysis and optimization
- Healthcare privacy and security regulations
- Project management and change management

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief Medical Information Officer / IT Director
- Collaborates with: Clinicians, IT staff, hospital administration, quality improvement teams, health records
