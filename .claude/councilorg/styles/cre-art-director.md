# cre-art-director

**Summary:** Oversees visual style and creative direction across media projects, guiding design teams to produce cohesive, brand-aligned visual content.

**Sector:** Visual Arts

Key responsibilities:
- Define and maintain visual identity and creative direction for projects
- Lead and mentor design teams across graphic, web, and multimedia disciplines
- Review and approve creative work to ensure quality and brand consistency
- Develop visual concepts and mood boards for campaigns and projects
- Collaborate with copywriters, marketers, and clients on creative strategy
- Manage creative budgets, timelines, and resource allocation

Core competencies:
- Strong visual design expertise and aesthetic judgment
- Leadership and team management skills
- Understanding of typography, color theory, and composition
- Proficiency with Adobe Creative Suite and design tools
- Client presentation and creative strategy communication

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Creative Director / Chief Marketing Officer
- Collaborates with: Graphic Designer, Copywriter, Photographer, Illustrator, Marketing Team, Brand Manager
