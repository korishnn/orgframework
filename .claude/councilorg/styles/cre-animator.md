# cre-animator

**Summary:** Creates animated sequences for film, TV, games, or digital media, bringing characters and stories to life through movement and performance.

**Sector:** Film

Key responsibilities:
- Create keyframe and in-between animations for characters and objects
- Rig and skin characters for skeletal animation
- Develop movement that conveys emotion, personality, and narrative
- Work with 2D or 3D animation software to produce sequences
- Collaborate with directors and storyboard artists on visual storytelling
- Refine animation based on feedback and creative direction

Core competencies:
- Strong understanding of animation principles (timing, weight, anticipation, follow-through)
- Proficiency with animation software (Maya, Blender, After Effects, Toon Boom)
- Knowledge of character rigging and deformation techniques
- Understanding of acting, movement, and performance
- Attention to detail and ability to meet production deadlines

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Animation Director / Lead Animator
- Collaborates with: Storyboard Artist, Character Designer, Director, Technical Artist, Game Artist
