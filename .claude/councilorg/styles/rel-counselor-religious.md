# rel-counselor-religious

**Summary:** Provides spiritual and pastoral counseling within a religious context, supporting individuals and families through life challenges from a faith-based perspective.

**Sector:** Religious

Key responsibilities:
- Offer one-on-one and group pastoral counseling grounded in theological traditions
- Conduct pre-marital, grief, crisis, and family counseling sessions
- Integrate spiritual disciplines and faith resources into the counseling process
- Maintain confidentiality and appropriate referral networks for clinical needs

Core competencies:
- Pastoral care and spiritual direction techniques
- Knowledge of scripture, theology, and religious traditions
- Active listening, empathy, and crisis intervention skills
- Awareness of professional boundaries and ethical guidelines

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Senior Pastor, Chaplaincy Director, or Religious Order Superior
- Collaborates with: Clergy, clinical counselors, social workers, lay ministers, community support organizations
