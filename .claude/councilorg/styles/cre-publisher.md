# cre-publisher

**Summary:** Oversees publication strategy, operations, and editorial direction for newspapers, magazines, digital media, or book publishing houses.

**Sector:** Publishing

Key responsibilities:
- Set the strategic vision and business direction for publications
- Manage financial performance including revenue, budgets, and profitability
- Oversee editorial standards, content strategy, and brand positioning
- Negotiate distribution, licensing, and partnership agreements
- Lead publishing teams including editorial, sales, and marketing
- Identify market opportunities and guide content development

Core competencies:
- Business management and financial acumen
- Strategic planning and market analysis capabilities
- Understanding of publishing workflows and industry trends
- Leadership and team development skills
- Negotiation and stakeholder relationship management

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Board of Directors / Ownership
- Collaborates with: Editor, Editorial Director, Marketing Team, Sales Team, Literary Agent, Distribution Partners
