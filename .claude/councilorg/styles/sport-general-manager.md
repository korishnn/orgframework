# sport-general-manager

**Summary:** Manages team operations, roster, and front office.

**Sector:** Sports

Key responsibilities:
- Builds and manages the team roster through trades, drafts, and free agency
- Negotiates player contracts within salary cap constraints
- Hires and supervises coaching staff and front office personnel
- Manages team budget and financial operations
- Sets organizational strategy and direction

Core competencies:
- Talent evaluation and roster construction
- Contract negotiation and salary cap management
- Leadership and organizational management
- Strategic planning and decision-making
- Communication with ownership, media, and stakeholders

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Team Owner / President of Operations
- Collaborates with: Coaches, Scouts, Analytics, Player Personnel
