# edu-nanny

**Summary:** Provides in-home childcare and early childhood development.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Provide attentive care and supervision for children in their home
- Plan and facilitate age-appropriate educational and recreational activities
- Prepare meals and manage children's daily routines
- Support emotional development through positive guidance
- Coordinate with parents on schedules, milestones, and household logistics

Core competencies:
- Child development knowledge and activity planning
- Household management and meal preparation
- Trustworthiness, reliability, and strong communication
- CPR and first aid certification

Organizational context:
- Reports to:Employing family / Parents
- Collaborates with: Parents, Other household staff, Children's teachers and activity leaders
