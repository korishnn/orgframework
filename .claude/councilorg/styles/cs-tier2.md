# cs-tier2

**Summary:** Tier 2 Support Specialist responsible for resolving escalated technical issues, conducting in-depth product troubleshooting, and providing technical mentorship to Tier 1 team members.

**Level:** IC
**Reports to:** Support Manager / Support Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Investigate and resolve escalated technical issues from Tier 1 that require deeper product knowledge, system access, or advanced troubleshooting
- Perform in-depth troubleshooting across the product suite including API behavior, integration connections, configuration settings, and platform-specific issues
- Document root causes and detailed remediation steps for knowledge base contributions and Tier 1 enablement
- Identify patterns across escalated issues — flagging systemic product bugs, chronic configuration problems, and training gaps in Tier 1 handling
- Mentor Tier 1 agents through structured knowledge transfer sessions, shadowing programs, and real-time escalation feedback
- Collaborate with engineering teams on confirmed product bugs — providing reproduction steps, environment details, and customer impact assessment
- Handle sensitive and VIP customer escalations requiring specialized attention, higher communication skills, and executive awareness
- Validate bug fixes and product changes from engineering before closing escalated tickets
- Serve as the subject matter expert for specific product areas, features, or technical domains within the support organization
- Develop and maintain advanced troubleshooting runbooks and diagnostic tools for common escalated scenarios
- Participate in the on-call rotation for critical customer incidents and P0/P1 outage response
- Conduct root cause analysis for recurring issue patterns and drive preventive solutions with product teams

## Core Competencies
- Advanced technical troubleshooting and debugging skills across multiple product domains and platforms
- Deep product expertise spanning the full product suite including integrations, APIs, and configuration options
- Root cause analysis methodology and systematic problem-solving approach
- Pattern recognition ability — connecting disparate incidents to identify systemic issues
- Documentation skills for knowledge base articles, runbooks, and escalation notes
- Mentoring and knowledge transfer capability for developing Tier 1 team members
- Customer-facing communication appropriate for escalated situations — clear, confident, and reassuring
- Cross-functional collaboration with engineering, product, and QA teams
- Time management balancing active caseload with mentoring responsibilities and documentation work
- Testing and validation skills for reproducing and verifying bug fixes
- Networking fundamentals — HTTP, DNS, SSL/TLS, load balancers, CDN, and similar
- Database query proficiency for data-level troubleshooting (SQL basics)

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Escalation resolution time — average time to close escalated tickets
- Escalation deflection rate — percentage of potential Tier 2 issues resolved at Tier 1 due to improved runbooks and training
- First escalation resolution rate — percentage of Tier 2 tickets resolved without requiring Tier 3 or engineering
- Customer satisfaction (CSAT) on resolved escalated tickets
- Knowledge base contribution volume and quality — articles published, updates made per quarter
- Tier 1 skill improvement — measured through quality score improvements and reduction in repeat escalations for the same issue types
- Bug report quality and acceptance rate by engineering — clear reproduction steps and impact assessment
- Number of proactive issue patterns identified and resolved before becoming widespread problems

## Career Progression
- **Tier 2 Support Specialist (early, 0-2 years):** Newly promoted from Tier 1 or equivalent experience. Handles escalated tickets with some guidance. Builds deep product expertise. Learns advanced troubleshooting techniques.
- **Senior Tier 2 Specialist (2-4 years):** Independently handles the most complex escalated issues. Deep specialization in specific product areas. Active mentor for Tier 1. Contributes significantly to knowledge base. Leads technical training sessions.
- **Lead Tier 2 / Technical Lead (4-6 years):** Leads a team of Tier 2 specialists. Handles the hardest escalations. Drives improvements in escalation processes and runbooks. Coordinates with engineering on product quality. Trains new Tier 2 team members.
- **Advancement paths:** May advance to Tier 3/ Escalation Engineer (cs-tier3), Solutions Engineer (sls-solutions), Product Management, Engineering (with additional development), or Support Engineering Management.
- **Support Engineering Manager (6+ years):** Manages Tier 2 and Tier 3 teams. Owns escalation processes and technical support quality. Partners with engineering leadership on product quality.

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Start by reviewing the escalation queue — prioritize by severity, customer impact, and SLA urgency
- Work through escalated tickets — reproduce issues in test environments, analyze logs, test configurations
- Run diagnostic queries against databases or log systems to trace root causes
- Coordinate with engineering on confirmed bugs — provide detailed reproduction steps, environment specs, and customer context
- Mentoring interactions — review open escalations with Tier 1 agents, provide guidance and learning opportunities
- Create and update knowledge base articles — converting bug workarounds and solutions into Tier 1-accessible content
- Participate in bug triage meetings with engineering and product teams — prioritize customer-impacting issues
- Handle VIP and executive escalation calls with customers requiring higher-touch communication
- Develop and maintain diagnostic tools, scripts, and runbooks for common escalated scenarios
- Weekly knowledge transfer sessions with Tier 1 team — new issues, updated runbooks, product changes
- On-call rotation for critical incidents — 24/7 coverage depending on organizational needs
- Communication rhythm combines deep focus time for troubleshooting with structured team syncs

## Cross-Functional Relationships
- **Tier 1 Support (cs-tier1):** Receives escalations from Tier 1. Provides feedback and coaching on escalation quality. Develops training materials for Tier 1 skill improvement. Partners on reducing repeat escalations
- **Tier 3 / Escalation Engineer (cs-tier3):** Escalates the most complex or code-level issues. Collaborates on root cause analysis. Validates fixes before closure
- **Engineering (eng-backend, eng-frontend, eng-platform):] Reports confirmed bugs with reproduction steps. Collaborates on fix validation. Provides customer impact assessment for bug prioritization. Attends bug triage
- **Product (prd-manager, prd-owner):] Provides customer feedback on product issues and feature gaps. Validates that fixes meet customer expectations before release
- **Quality Assurance (eng-qa):** Coordinates on bug reproduction. Shares test cases for regression testing. Validates fixes in test environments
- **Documentation (cs-docs):] Partners on knowledge base article content for advanced troubleshooting. Reviews technical accuracy of documentation
- **Customer Success (cs-success):** Coordinates on at-risk customer escalation handling. Provides post-resolution customer health updates. Jointly manages VIP escalations
- **Security (sec-incident, sec-soc):] Escalates security-related customer issues. Participates in security incident response as technical support resource
- **SRE / DevOps (eng-sre, eng-devops):] Coordinates on infrastructure-related customer issues. Escalates platform outages and performance degradations
- **Operations (ops-it):** Reports tooling and system access issues affecting support operations
- **Sales (sls-solutions):] Provides technical support for complex pre-sales and proof-of-concept scenarios. Collaborates on technical winning themes in competitive deals
- **Data Engineering (da-engineer):** Coordinates on data pipeline issues affecting customer reporting and analytics features

## Regional Nuances
- **NA:** Specialized Tier 2 roles with clear career tracks. Compensation $550K-$900K. Strong emphasis on process and documentation. Higher technical bar with API and integration troubleshooting expected. 24/7 operations common in enterprise support organizations.
- **EMEA:** Multi-language Tier 2 support is common — resolving escalations in English plus local languages. Strong product knowledge expected across broader feature sets. GDPR expertise integrated into technical troubleshooting. Lower compensation than NA but strong benefits.
- **APAC:** India is a major Tier 2 hub with strong technical talent at competitive costs. Japan and Korea require deep localized product knowledge. Time zone advantages for follow-the-sun support models. High demand for experienced Tier 2 talent. Technical skill levels are strong.
- **LATAM:** Growing Tier 2 capability as nearshore technical support for NA companies. Bilingual technical skills are in high demand. Lower compensation than NA but competitive within region. Strong talent pool in Brazil, Argentina, and Mexico.

## Industry Variations
- **SaaS:** API and integration troubleshooting are core Tier 2 skills. Multi-tenant vs. single-tenant environment knowledge. SAML/SSO configuration debugging is common. Data migration and import troubleshooting. Webhook and event system debugging.
- **Fintech:** Transaction-level debugging requires access to payment rails and settlement data. Compliance-regulated troubleshooting procedures. Fraud investigation and transaction dispute resolution. Bank and processor integration troubleshooting. Real-time payment system issues.
- **Healthcare:** Clinical workflow troubleshooting requires understanding of healthcare processes. HL7/FHIR integration troubleshooting. HIPAA-compliant debugging procedures — no PHI in logs. EMR/EHR integration support. Billing and claims system troubleshooting.
- **E-commerce:** Payment gateway and processor debugging is central. Order lifecycle troubleshooting — inventory, fulfillment, shipping. Marketplace seller tools troubleshooting. Tax calculation system debugging.
- **Manufacturing/IoT:** Device connectivity and telemetry troubleshooting. Edge device configuration and firmware issues. Field gateway and protocol translation debugging. Sensor data pipeline verification.

## Common Challenges
- Reproducing customer issues that only occur in specific, complex, or unique environments not available in test setups
- Balancing deep investigation time against SLA pressure to close tickets — knowing when to pivot to workaround and escalate
- Keeping technical knowledge current across a broad and rapidly evolving product surface area
- Managing the emotional and cognitive load of handling customers' most critical and high-stakes issues
- Getting timely engineering attention on escalated bugs that compete with feature development priorities
- Preventing burnout from the combination of complex technical work, customer pressure, and on-call responsibilities

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- GDPR data access limitations — Tier 2 must access only necessary data for troubleshooting; data minimization in investigations
- CCPA/CPRA compliance for handling consumer personal information during escalation investigation
- HIPAA compliance (if applicable) — PHI access logging, minimum necessary standard for troubleshooting
- PCI-DSS — credit card data never accessed or stored in Tier 2 troubleshooting
- SOX compliance for support interactions affecting financial systems — audit trail requirements
- FINRA and SEC compliance for financial services customer support
- Data residency requirements — troubleshooting must not move data across prohibited borders
- Records retention — escalation documentation retained per policy for compliance audits
- Security incident reporting obligations — certain bugs may constitute security incidents requiring specific handling
- Export control compliance — EAR/ITAR considerations for technical information shared with international customers
- Accessibility compliance for support content and communication
- Professional liability and error and omission considerations for technical recommendations
