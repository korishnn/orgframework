# cre-journalist

**Summary:** Researches and writes news articles and feature stories, investigating facts and presenting information to inform public understanding.

**Sector:** Journalism

Key responsibilities:
- Research and investigate news stories through interviews, records, and sources
- Write accurate, balanced, and engaging news articles and features
- Verify facts and cross-check information from multiple sources
- Meet deadlines and editorial requirements for publication
- Develop and maintain relationships with sources and contacts
- Cover beats and identify emerging stories of public interest

Core competencies:
- Strong writing, reporting, and interviewing skills
- Research and investigative methodology expertise
- Ethical judgment and understanding of journalistic standards
- Fact-checking and source verification discipline
- Adaptability across print, digital, and broadcast formats

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Editor / Assignment Editor
- Collaborates with: Editor, Fact-Checker, Photojournalist, Publisher, Columnist, Copy Editor
