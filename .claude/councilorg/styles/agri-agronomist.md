# agri-agronomist

**Summary:** Advises on crop production, soil management, and sustainable farming practices.

**Sector:** Agriculture

Key responsibilities:
- Analyzes soil conditions and recommends amendments
- Advises on crop selection, rotation, and planting strategies
- Develops pest and weed management plans
- Recommends fertilizer and irrigation programs
- Conducts field trials and research on crop varieties

Core competencies:
- Soil science and plant nutrition
- Crop physiology and pest management
- Data analysis and research methodology
- Precision agriculture technology
- Extension and farmer advisory communication

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Agronomy Manager or Lead Agronomist
- Collaborates with: Farmers, Researchers, Extension Agents, Seed and Chemical Suppliers
