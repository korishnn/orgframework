# legal-entertainment-attorney

**Summary:** Advises entertainment industry clients on contracts and rights.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Negotiates and drafts contracts for talent, production, distribution, and licensing
- Advises on intellectual property rights including copyrights and royalties
- Handles clearance of music, footage, and other creative content
- Resolves disputes involving breach of contract, rights ownership, and defamation

Core competencies:
- Juris Doctor degree and state bar admission
- Knowledge of entertainment law, copyright, and contract law
- Familiarity with industry practices in music, film, television, or digital media
- Strong negotiation and relationship management skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Entertainment Practice Group Lead or Partner
- Collaborates with: Agents, managers, producers, studios, talent, unions, guilds
