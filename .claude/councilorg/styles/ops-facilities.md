# ops-facilities

**Summary:** Manages office spaces, workplace services, physical security, and facilities operations to create safe, productive, and engaging work environments that support organizational goals and employee experience.

**Level:** Manager
**Reports to:** COO / Ops Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Oversee all office operations including space planning, maintenance, janitorial services, and daily facility management across all locations.
- Manage workplace experience programs including catering, breakroom services, event support, ergonomics, and employee amenity programs.
- Coordinate physical security systems including access control, CCTV surveillance, alarm systems, visitor management, and emergency response procedures.
- Negotiate and manage commercial leases, property agreements, and vendor contracts for office, warehouse, and retail facility locations.
- Plan and execute office moves, expansions, contractions, and renovations including construction management and furniture procurement.
- Manage the facilities budget including operating expenses, capital expenditures, utility costs, and cost allocation to business units.
- Oversee building maintenance programs including HVAC, electrical, plumbing, fire safety systems, and preventive maintenance schedules.
- Ensure compliance with health, safety, and accessibility regulations including OSHA, ADA, fire codes, and local building codes.
- Manage parking programs, employee transportation, bike storage, and commuter benefit administration.
- Develop and implement sustainability programs including energy efficiency, waste reduction, recycling, water conservation, and green building certifications.
- Manage emergency preparedness including evacuation plans, first aid programs, business continuity for facilities, and severe weather protocols.
- Track and report on facilities metrics including cost per square foot, utilization rates, work order resolution time, and energy consumption.
- Manage mailroom operations including incoming/outgoing mail, package handling, courier services, and shipping supplies inventory.
- Develop and maintain space utilization data to inform hybrid work policies, desk-sharing ratios, and long-term real estate strategy.

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Cost per square foot: total facilities operating cost divided by occupied square footage
- Space utilization rate: percentage of available workspace actually used on average
- Work order resolution time: average time from maintenance request to completion
- Employee satisfaction with workplace: annual survey score on workplace environment
- Energy consumption: kWh per square foot year-over-year reduction
- Lease renewal rate: percentage of leases renewed vs. relocated
- Safety incident rate: number of workplace safety incidents per facility per year
- Sustainability progress: waste diversion rate, energy reduction, water conservation metrics

## Career Progression
- Entry level: Facilities Coordinator managing work orders, vendor scheduling, access card administration, and daily facility support tickets.
- Mid level: Facilities Manager overseeing a single location or small portfolio of sites, managing vendors, and leading renovation projects.
- Senior level: Senior Facilities Manager managing a regional portfolio of facilities, leading the facilities budget, and developing workplace strategy.
- Lead level: Director of Facilities overseeing the entire corporate real estate portfolio, facilities team, and workplace strategy.
- VP level: VP of Real Estate and Facilities or Chief Workplace Officer responsible for global real estate strategy, workplace experience, and sustainability.
- Timeline: 1-2 years as coordinator, 2-3 years as manager, 2-4 years as senior manager, then director/VP track.

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Morning: Walk-through of office common areas to check cleanliness and maintenance, review work order queue, address urgent facility issues (HVAC, plumbing, power), greet new employees with workspace orientations.
- Midday: Vendor coordination for ongoing maintenance, meeting with contractors for renovation projects, workplace event setup support, respond to employee facility requests.
- Afternoon: Budget tracking, lease administration, security system monitoring, space planning updates, sustainability data tracking, vendor invoice approval.
- Weekly: Facilities team standup, vendor service quality reviews, work order backlog review, space utilization data review, safety system testing.
- Monthly: Facilities budget tracking and reporting, utility consumption analysis, fire safety equipment inspection, vendor performance reviews, facilities KPI reporting.
- Quarterly: Lease and property review, space planning updates for headcount changes, preventive maintenance schedule review, emergency drill coordination.
- Annual: Budget planning for next fiscal year, lease renewal negotiations, major maintenance planning, sustainability goal review, office space strategy review.
- Rhythms: Daily reactive work (maintenance requests, facility issues) mixed with proactive project work (renovations, moves, improvements). Weekends often used for construction or major maintenance. Seasonal peaks for HVAC changeovers (spring/fall) and holiday office events.

## Regional Nuances
- NA: Suburban office-to-residential conversion trend. Hybrid work drives reduced office footprints. Aggressive lease renegotiations in post-pandemic market. PropTech (smart buildings, IoT) is widely adopted. Focus on wellness certifications (WELL). Compliance with ADA requirements is critical.
- EMEA: Strong tenant protection laws in many European countries (Germany's KSchG). Energy efficiency requirements are strict (EU Energy Performance of Buildings Directive). Historical building restrictions complicate renovations. Works council involvement in workspace decisions in Germany, Netherlands. Lower average square footage per employee than NA.
- APAC: Japan has strict earthquake building code requirements. Singapore and Hong Kong have the highest commercial real estate costs globally. China's building standards and permitting processes vary significantly by city. India's infrastructure quality varies, requiring more on-site management. Open-plan offices dominate.
- LATAM: Security concerns in some markets require enhanced physical security measures. Building codes and permit processes vary significantly. Brazil's labor requirements for facility maintenance staff (CLT). Higher utility costs relative to revenue. Leasing laws generally favor tenants in Brazil but less so in other countries.

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: Open, collaborative space design with focus on employee amenities. Less square footage per employee due to remote/hybrid work. Lounge and collaboration spaces prioritized over private offices. Startup-friendly short-term leases are common. Flex office providers (WeWork, Regus) are frequently used.
- Fintech: Enhanced physical security requirements for regulated entities. Secure data center facilities or colocation management. Client meeting spaces are high-priority. Compliance records storage requirements. Trading floor environments for investment firms.
- Healthcare: Patient-facing facility requirements (ADA, infection control). 24/7 facility operations need round-the-clock maintenance coverage. Medical gas, HVAC with specialized filtration, backup power requirements. Sterile environment protocols. Regulatory surveys require facility documentation.
- Manufacturing: Large industrial facilities with specialized infrastructure (heavy power, ventilation, waste handling). OSHA compliance is the highest priority. Shift operations require 24/7 facilities coverage. Environmental permits and reporting. Storage, loading dock, and material handling infrastructure.
- E-commerce: Warehouse and fulfillment center management is the primary facility focus. Automation and conveyor system maintenance. Energy-intensive operations. Safety systems for high-traffic material handling. Expansion and contraction flexibility for seasonal peaks. Multi-site network across regions.

## Common Challenges
- Managing reduced office utilization and changing space needs in a hybrid work environment.
- Balancing cost reduction pressure with maintaining a high-quality workplace experience.
- Navigating lease exit costs and sublease challenges when reducing office footprint post-pandemic.
- Coordinating facility operations across multiple geographically dispersed locations with different vendors.
- Implementing sustainability initiatives that require capital investment with long payback periods.
- Adapting facilities for changing workplace models (activity-based working, hot-desking, hybrid) with employee resistance.

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- US: OSHA General Duty Clause and specific standards (fire safety, means of egress, electrical), ADA accessibility (Title III for public accommodations), local building codes and fire codes, ANSI/BOMA office space measurement standards, EPA energy and water regulations, ASHRAE ventilation standards, state and local indoor air quality requirements.
- UK: Health and Safety at Work Act 1974, Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005, Equality Act 2010 accessibility requirements, Energy Performance of Buildings Regulations, Workplace (Health, Safety and Welfare) Regulations 1992, Landlord and Tenant Act 1954.
- EU: EU Energy Performance of Buildings Directive (EPBD), EU Construction Products Regulation, member state building codes (German Bauordnung, French Code de la construction), EU Accessibility Act, Working Conditions Directive (workplace requirements), Waste Framework Directive facility waste management.
- Canada: Canada Occupational Health and Safety Regulations, provincial building codes (National Building Code adopted by provinces), provincial fire codes, AODA accessibility standard for Ontario facilities, provincial environmental protection acts.
- Brazil: NR-18 (construction safety and conditions), NR-24 (occupational hygiene and comfort conditions), municipal building codes and fire department (CBM) requirements, CLT workplace condition requirements, CONAMA environmental regulations, PMOC (Plano de Manutencao, Operacao e Controle) for HVAC.
- Australia: WHS Act 2011 (Work Health and Safety), National Construction Code (NCC), state building and fire safety regulations, Disability Discrimination Act 1992 accessibility requirements, state environmental protection authorities, National Australian Built Environment Rating System (NABERS).
