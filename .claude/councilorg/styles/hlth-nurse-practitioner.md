# hlth-nurse-practitioner

**Summary:** Advanced practice nurse who diagnoses, prescribes, and treats independently.

**Sector:** Healthcare

Key responsibilities:
- Conduct comprehensive health assessments and diagnose acute and chronic conditions
- Prescribe medications, treatments, and therapeutic interventions autonomously
- Order and interpret diagnostic tests and laboratory results
- Develop and manage patient treatment plans across the care continuum
- Provide health promotion, disease prevention, and patient education
- Refer patients to specialists when advanced care is needed
- Collaborate with physicians and interdisciplinary care teams

Core competencies:
- Advanced clinical assessment and diagnostic reasoning
- Pharmacotherapeutics and prescriptive authority
- Chronic disease management and preventive care
- Patient-centered communication and counseling
- Evidence-based practice and research utilization
- Autonomous clinical decision-making within scope

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Medical Director / Lead Physician
- Collaborates with: Physicians, registered nurses, specialists, allied health professionals, administrative staff
