# sci-forensic-scientist

**Summary:** Analyzes physical evidence for criminal investigations.

**Sector:** Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Collect, preserve, and document physical evidence from crime scenes
- Conduct laboratory analyses on biological, chemical, and trace evidence
- Interpret test results and prepare detailed forensic reports
- Provide expert testimony in court proceedings
- Maintain chain of custody and rigorous quality control

Core competencies:
- Laboratory analysis techniques (DNA, toxicology, ballistics, etc.)
- Evidence handling and chain of custody procedures
- Report writing and expert witness testimony
- Knowledge of criminal law and evidentiary standards

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Crime Lab Director / Forensic Science Supervisor
- Collaborates with: Law enforcement, Detectives, Prosecutors, Defense attorneys, Other forensic specialists
