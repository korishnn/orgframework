# hlth-medical-records

**Summary:** Maintains and protects patient health records and information systems.

**Sector:** Healthcare

Key responsibilities:
- Organize and manage patient health records in electronic and paper formats
- Ensure accuracy, completeness, and confidentiality of medical documentation
- Process release of information requests in compliance with privacy regulations
- Implement and maintain health information management systems
- Code diagnoses and procedures using ICD-10 and CPT classification systems
- Audit records for compliance with legal and accreditation standards
- Train staff on documentation standards and health record procedures

Core competencies:
- Health information management and records organization
- Medical terminology and clinical documentation knowledge
- ICD-10 and CPT coding systems
- HIPAA privacy and security regulations
- Electronic health record system administration
- Data quality auditing and compliance monitoring

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Health Information Management Director / HIM Manager
- Collaborates with: Physicians, nurses, billing staff, compliance officers, IT department, legal counsel
