# tran-courier

**Summary:** Transports documents, parcels, and sensitive materials on local or regional timelines, often with expedited service.

**Sector:** Transportation

Key responsibilities:
- Picks up and delivers documents, packages, and time-sensitive materials
- Plans efficient routes for prompt delivery within time windows
- Obtains signatures and proof of delivery for each transaction
- Handles confidential or valuable items with proper chain of custody
- Tracks deliveries and provides status updates to dispatch and customers

Core competencies:
- Valid driver's license and clean driving record
- Dependability and punctuality for time-critical deliveries
- Customer service and professional communication skills
- Knowledge of local geography and navigation
- Organization and tracking of multiple deliveries

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Courier Service Dispatcher or Operations Manager
- Collaborates with: Dispatch Coordinators, Office Managers, and Recipients
