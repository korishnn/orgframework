# fin-wealth-manager

**Summary:** Manages high-net-worth client portfolios and comprehensive financial planning.

**Sector:** Finance

Key responsibilities:
- Constructs and oversees customized investment portfolios for high-net-worth individuals
- Coordinates tax, estate, and philanthropic planning strategies with specialists
- Conducts regular portfolio reviews and performance reporting for clients
- Identifies alternative investment opportunities including private equity and hedge funds

Core competencies:
- Advanced knowledge of asset allocation, risk management, and estate planning
- Series 65 or equivalent investment advisor credentials
- Relationship management and high-touch client service skills
- Deep understanding of tax-efficient investing and wealth transfer strategies

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Wealth Management Director or Partner
- Collaborates with: Financial advisors, tax attorneys, estate planners, trust officers, family office services
