# mkt-social

**Summary:** Social Media Manager develops and executes social media strategy, manages brand presence across platforms, and drives community engagement to build brand awareness and customer relationships.

**Level:** IC
**Reports to:** Marketing Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Develop and execute comprehensive social media strategies aligned with marketing goals and brand positioning
- Create, curate, and schedule engaging content across platforms (LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube)
- Monitor social channels daily and engage with the community in authentic brand voice
- Track social media metrics and report on performance, engagement, and ROI to stakeholders
- Manage social listening programs to capture brand sentiment, trends, and competitive intelligence
- Coordinate with paid media teams on social advertising campaigns and boosted content
- Handle crisis communication and brand reputation management across social platforms
- Identify and engage with influencers, brand advocates, and community leaders for partnership opportunities
- Develop platform-specific content strategies that optimize for each platform's algorithm and audience behavior
- Manage social content calendar and coordinate with content marketing for cross-channel distribution
- Stay current with social media platform changes, algorithm updates, and emerging trends
- Manage social media budget for paid promotions and influencer partnerships
- Develop and maintain social media brand guidelines, voice documentation, and community management playbooks

## Core Competencies
- Deep knowledge of major social media platforms, their algorithms, and best practices for each
- Strong copywriting and visual storytelling abilities adapted to different platforms and audiences
- Experience with social media management and scheduling tools (Hootsuite, Sprout Social, Later, Buffer)
- Understanding of social analytics, reporting, and ROI measurement approaches
- Community management skills including customer engagement, moderation, and crisis response
- Ability to react quickly to trends, cultural moments, and real-time marketing opportunities
- Knowledge of social media advertising fundamentals and paid promotion strategies
- Visual content sensibility including photography, video, and graphic design basics
- Strategic thinking for long-term brand building through social channels
- Crisis communication and issues management experience on social platforms

## Tools & Technology
- Management: Hootsuite, Sprout Social, Buffer, Later, HubSpot Social
- Analytics: Native platform analytics, Brandwatch, Sprout Social Analytics, Talkwalker
- Design: Canva, Adobe Creative Suite, Figma, CapCut, Premiere Pro
- Listening: Brand24, Mention, Meltwater, Sprout Social Listening
- Scheduling: Later, Buffer, Hootsuite, Planoly
- Platforms: LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, Pinterest, Reddit
- Influencer: Upfluence, Aspire, CreatorIQ, Grin
- AI: Jasper AI, ChatGPT for copy ideation, Opus Clip for video repurposing

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Follower growth rate and audience demographics across platforms
- Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares, clicks per post and per follower)
- Reach and impressions (organic and paid)
- Social referral traffic to website and conversion rate
- Brand sentiment score from social listening
- Community response time and customer satisfaction on social
- Social media attributed pipeline and revenue (where trackable)
- Video view count and completion rate per platform

## Career Progression
- Junior/Coordinator (0-2 years): Schedules posts, monitors comments, compiles basic reports
- Specialist (2-4 years): Owns content calendar, creates content, manages community engagement
- Senior/Manager (4-7 years): Develops social strategy, manages campaigns, leads influencer programs
- Director (7-10 years): Sets multi-platform strategy, manages team, drives social innovation
- VP (10+ years): Executive social strategy, organizational reputation management, thought leadership programs
- Typical progression: Junior to Specialist 1-2 years, Specialist to Senior 2-3 years, Senior to Director 2-4 years

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Morning social listening check for brand mentions, comments, and emerging issues
- Content creation, curation, and scheduling across platforms following the content calendar
- Community engagement through comments, replies, DMs, and mentions throughout the day
- Real-time engagement for trending topics and cultural moments as they arise
- Weekly team check-in on campaign performance and upcoming content needs
- Monthly performance reporting with analysis of what worked and optimization recommendations
- Regular platform algorithm update reviews and strategy adjustments
- Influencer outreach and relationship management as part of ongoing programs

## Regional Nuances
- NA: LinkedIn and Instagram dominant for B2B and B2C respectively; TikTok growing for all segments; high content velocity expected; strong creator economy
- EMEA: Platform preferences vary by country (VK in Russia, WeChat for Chinese audiences in Europe, WhatsApp popular in Spain and Italy); multi-language social management required; GDPR limits tracking
- APAC: WeChat, LINE, KakaoTalk, and WhatsApp dominate in different markets; Instagram and TikTok very popular; local platform expertise required for each market; higher mobile-first social consumption
- LATAM: Strong Instagram and WhatsApp usage; Facebook still relevant; TikTok growing rapidly; Spanish and Portuguese content required; high social media engagement rates

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: LinkedIn-focused for B2B lead generation; thought leadership content; community building in product-specific groups; employee advocacy programs
- Fintech: Trust-building social content; educational financial literacy; regulatory compliance in social copy; LinkedIn for B2B; Instagram for consumer fintech
- E-commerce: Visual product content on Instagram and Pinterest; shoppable social posts; user-generated content campaigns; influencer and affiliate programs
- Healthcare: Educational health content; provider-focused LinkedIn strategy; compliance-reviewed social copy; patient privacy protection in social engagement
- Media/Entertainment: Platform-native content creation; high video volume; real-time engagement; platform partnership programs

## Common Challenges
- Keeping up with constantly changing platform algorithms and feature updates
- Managing brand reputation during social media crises with limited response time
- Measuring social media ROI and connecting engagement to business outcomes
- Creating platform-specific content at volume without sufficient design resources
- Navigating platform policy changes that affect organic reach or content restrictions
- Balancing authentic community engagement with promotional content goals

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- FTC endorsement guidelines for influencer partnerships
- GDPR for social media tracking and data collection in EMEA
- CCPA for California user data from social campaigns
- CAN-SPAM for social media direct messaging practices
- Platform-specific advertising policies and community guidelines
- Industry-specific regulations (FINRA for fintech, FDA for healthcare) on social content
- Copyright and intellectual property law for user-generated content sharing
