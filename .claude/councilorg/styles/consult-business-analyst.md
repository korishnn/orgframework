# consult-business-analyst

**Summary:** Analyzes business processes, systems, and data to recommend improvements for organizations.

**Sector:** Consulting

Key responsibilities:
- Analyzes current business processes and identifies inefficiencies
- Gathers requirements through stakeholder interviews and workshops
- Develops process maps, use cases, and functional specifications
- Performs data analysis to support business cases
- Recommends process improvements and technology solutions

Core competencies:
- Business process modeling and analysis
- Requirements gathering and documentation
- Data analysis and visualization
- Stakeholder interviewing and facilitation
- Solution design and recommendation

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Engagement Manager or Business Analysis Lead
- Collaborates with: Client Stakeholders, Project Managers, Technical Teams, Subject Matter Experts
