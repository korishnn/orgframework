# trade-tailor

**Summary:** Alters, repairs, and creates custom garments for individual clients and retail businesses.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Takes client measurements and interprets fit requirements
- Alters garments including hemming, taking in, letting out, and adjusting lengths
- Constructs custom garments from patterns and fabric selections
- Repairs torn seams, zippers, buttons, and damaged fabric
- Operates and maintains industrial sewing machines and pressing equipment

Core competencies:
- Proficiency with industrial sewing machines, sergers, and hand stitching
- Pattern reading, drafting, and garment construction knowledge
- Understanding of fabrics, drape, and garment structure
- Precision measuring and mathematical calculation
- Customer consultation for fit, style, and fabric choices

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Tailor Shop Manager or Business Owner
- Collaborates with: Seamstresses, Dry Cleaners, Retail Staff, and Clients
