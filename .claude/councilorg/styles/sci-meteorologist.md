# sci-meteorologist

**Summary:** Studies weather patterns and forecasts atmospheric conditions.

**Sector:** Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Analyze atmospheric data to produce weather forecasts
- Monitor severe weather events and issue warnings
- Research climate patterns and long-term climate trends
- Develop and improve atmospheric computer models
- Communicate weather information to the public and stakeholders

Core competencies:
- Atmospheric science and meteorology knowledge
- Weather modeling and data analysis
- Remote sensing and radar interpretation
- Risk communication and public messaging

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Weather Service Director / Research Lead
- Collaborates with: Climatologists, Emergency management, Aviation and maritime agencies, Media
