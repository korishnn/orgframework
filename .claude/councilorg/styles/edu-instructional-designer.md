# edu-instructional-designer

**Summary:** Designs learning experiences and educational materials.

**Sector:** Education / Academia

Key responsibilities:
- Analyze learning needs and develop instructional objectives
- Design course content, assessments, and learning activities
- Develop digital and multimedia learning materials
- Collaborate with subject matter experts to ensure accuracy
- Evaluate learning outcomes and iterate on instructional approaches

Core competencies:
- Learning theory and instructional design models (e.g., ADDIE, UDL)
- Multimedia content development and authoring tools
- Assessment design and learning analytics
- Project management and cross-functional collaboration

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Director of Instructional Design / Dean / Program Manager
- Collaborates with: Faculty, Subject matter experts, Media producers, IT staff
