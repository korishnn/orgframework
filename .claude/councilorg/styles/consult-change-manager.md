# consult-change-manager

**Summary:** Manages organizational change, adoption, and transformation programs for clients.

**Sector:** Consulting

Key responsibilities:
- Develops change management strategies and plans
- Assesses organizational readiness and change impact
- Designs stakeholder engagement and communication plans
- Delivers training and capability-building programs
- Measures adoption rates and sustains behavioral change

Core competencies:
- Change management methodologies and frameworks
- Stakeholder analysis and engagement
- Communication planning and execution
- Training design and delivery
- Organizational design and culture transformation

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Engagement Manager or Transformation Lead
- Collaborates with: Client Leadership, HR, Communications Teams, Project Managers, Implementation Leads
