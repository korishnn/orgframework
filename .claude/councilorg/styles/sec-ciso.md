# sec-ciso

**Summary:** Leads enterprise cybersecurity strategy, policy, and team as the Chief Information Security Officer, protecting information assets and managing cyber risk.

**Sector:** Security

Key responsibilities:
- Develop and execute the organization's cybersecurity strategy and roadmap
- Oversee security operations, incident response, and threat intelligence programs
- Ensure regulatory compliance and manage security risk across the enterprise
- Report on security posture to executive leadership and the board of directors

Core competencies:
- Strategic leadership and executive communication
- Deep knowledge of security frameworks (NIST, ISO 27001) and regulatory landscape
- Risk management, budget planning, and vendor security assessment
- Crisis management and incident command

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief Information Officer, Chief Risk Officer, or CEO
- Collaborates with: IT leadership, legal and compliance, audit, engineering, HR, board of directors
