# govt-tax-examiner

**Summary:** Reviews tax returns and conducts tax compliance examinations for government.

**Sector:** Government

Key responsibilities:
- Examine individual and business tax returns for accuracy, completeness, and legal compliance
- Identify discrepancies, unreported income, or disallowed deductions through document analysis
- Conduct correspondence audits and field examinations to verify tax liability
- Prepare adjustment reports, deficiency notices, and penalty recommendations

Core competencies:
- Thorough knowledge of tax law, regulations, and administrative rulings
- Analytical skills for reviewing financial records, bank statements, and business filings
- Ability to communicate audit findings clearly to taxpayers and their representatives
- Integrity and impartiality in applying tax law consistently across cases

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Audit group manager or examination section chief
- Collaborates with: Taxpayer representatives, collection specialists, legal counsel, and revenue agents
