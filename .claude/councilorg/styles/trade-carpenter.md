# trade-carpenter

**Summary:** Constructs, installs, and repairs wooden structures and frameworks in residential and commercial construction.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Reads blueprints and building plans to determine layout and dimensions
- Measures, cuts, and shapes lumber, plywood, and other materials
- Erects frameworks, floor systems, walls, roofs, and staircases
- Installs doors, windows, cabinets, and trim work
- Repairs and replaces damaged wooden structures and fittings

Core competencies:
- Proficiency with hand tools (saws, hammers, chisels, planes)
- Skill with power tools (circular saws, nail guns, routers)
- Blueprint reading and mathematical calculation
- Knowledge of building codes and construction methods
- Physical stamina, balance, and manual dexterity

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Construction Foreman or General Contractor
- Collaborates with: Electricians, Plumbers, Masons, Painters, and other trades on job sites
