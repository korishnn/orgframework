# dsg-design-ops

**Summary:** Builds design tools, processes, asset libraries, and cross-team workflows to increase design team efficiency, quality, and impact at scale.

**Level:** IC or Manager
**Reports to:** Design Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Design and maintain design tools, templates, workflows, and operational systems for the design organization.
- Build and manage design asset libraries, component systems, and shared resources for team-wide access.
- Establish and optimize design processes for efficiency, quality, and consistency across design teams.
- Improve designer-developer handoff processes and cross-functional collaboration workflows.
- Track design team metrics, capacity planning, project throughput, and resource allocation.
- Onboard new designers and document design team best practices, standards, and ways of working.
- Manage design tool administration including Figma organization, plugin management, and licensing.
- Facilitate design critique sessions, design reviews, and team ceremonies.
- Drive design system governance, contribution models, and version management strategies.
- Measure and report on design operations effectiveness, identifying bottlenecks and improvement opportunities.
- Coordinate design team hiring processes including portfolio reviews and interview scheduling.
- Manage agency and vendor relationships for design production overflow and specialized expertise.

## Core Competencies
- Design operations and process optimization for scaling design organizations efficiently.
- Design systems and tooling expertise covering component libraries, design tokens, and tool administration.
- Cross-functional workflow design for improving collaboration between design, product, and engineering.
- Project management and operational thinking for resource planning, capacity management, and workflow tracking.
- Communication and change management for driving adoption of new tools and processes.
- Technical proficiency in design and collaboration tools across the design technology stack.
- Vendor management and procurement for tool evaluation, licensing negotiation, and vendor relationships.
- Data analysis and reporting for measuring design team health, productivity, and impact.
- Facilitation and training for onboarding, skill-building, and process education.
- Strategic planning for designing design organization structure, team topology, and operating model.

## Tools & Technology
- Design tools administration (Figma Admin, Sketch, Adobe Creative Cloud Enterprise)
- Design system platforms (Design System Manager, ZeroHeight, Supernova, Knapsack)
- Design token and theming tools (Style Dictionary, Theo, Design Tokens Format)
- Project management platforms (Asana, Monday.com, Jira, Notion)
- Digital asset management (Bynder, Brandfolder, Frontify, Canto)
- Version control and collaboration (GitHub, Abstract, Plant, Design Version Control)
- Collaboration and communication (Slack, Microsoft Teams, Confluence, Notion)
- Tool procurement and license management (SaaS management platforms, G2 for evaluation)
- Metrics and analytics (Tableau, Google Analytics, custom dashboards for design KPIs)
- Survey and feedback tools (Culture Amp, Lattice, Polly for team health measurement)

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Design team velocity — throughput measured by design tasks completed per sprint or quarter.
- Design-to-development handoff cycle time and reduction trends.
- Design system adoption rate across teams and products.
- Designer satisfaction score with tools, processes, and workflow efficiency.
- Onboarding ramp time for new designers joining the team.
- Tool utilization and license efficiency (cost per active user, unused licenses).
- Design debt reduction through process improvements and system maturity.

## Career Progression
DesignOps is a relatively newer specialization in the design field. Practitioners often transition from UX design, project management, or design management roles. Career progression typically starts at DesignOps specialist (0-3 years), senior DesignOps (3-6 years), DesignOps lead/manager (6-9 years), and head of DesignOps or Director (9+ years). Growth involves expanding from tactical operations to strategic organizational design. Senior DesignOps leaders shape design organization structure, operating models, and cross-functional partnership frameworks. Some DesignOps professionals come from operational backgrounds outside design (business operations, HR operations, program management). The field has professional networks (DesignOps Assembly) and conferences (DesignOps Summit). Advancement depends on measurable operational improvements and strategic impact on design team effectiveness.

## Day-to-Day Workflow
DesignOps roles blend operational execution, strategic planning, and tool administration. Mornings often involve triaging design team requests, tool issues, and process questions. Tool administration includes managing Figma organization settings, plugin updates, and license allocation. Process improvement work involves documenting workflows, creating templates, and refining handoff procedures. Weekly DesignOps team meetings coordinate on system improvements, onboarding status, and tool roadmap. Monthly design operations reviews present metrics on team health, throughput, and system adoption. Vendor management includes tool evaluations, license renewals, and relationship management. Onboarding new designers involves scheduling orientation sessions, setting up tool access, and mentoring on team processes. Cross-functional meetings with engineering and product operations align on shared workflows and tool integrations. Agency and freelance resource coordination includes scoping, contracts, and project assignment.

## Cross-Functional Relationships
- **Design Director** — Reports to; receives strategic direction on design organization priorities and improvements.
- **Design Team Members** — Primary stakeholders for tools, processes, and workflow improvements.
- **Engineering Ops / Platform Team** — Partners on design-engineering handoff tooling and integration.
- **Product Operations** — Coordinates on shared roadmapping, prioritization, and cross-functional workflow alignment.
- **IT / IT Ops** — Collaborates on tool procurement, licensing, security compliance, and device management.
- **People Ops / HR** — Partners on design hiring, onboarding, career frameworks, and team culture.
- **Procurement** - Coordinates tool vendor contracting, procurement, and budget management.
- **Marketing Operations** - Aligns on brand asset management and marketing-design workflows.
- **Finance** - Partners on design tool budget, headcount planning, and resource cost tracking.
- **Agency Partners** - Manages agency onboarding, briefs, asset delivery, and quality standards.

## Regional Nuances
**NA:** DesignOps is most established in NA with dedicated roles at major tech companies. Strong emphasis on tooling and technology-driven efficiency. Higher budgets for design tools and operations staff. Remote DesignOps managing distributed teams is standard.
**EMEA:** DesignOps is growing but less established than NA. Focus on cross-cultural and multi-language design operations challenges. Works Council engagement for tool adoption affecting data privacy. GDPR considerations in design tool selection and data processing. Leaner DesignOps teams with broader responsibilities.
**APAC:** DesignOps is emerging as a recognized role, primarily in larger tech companies. Language and localization operations are significant DesignOps responsibilities. Tool access and licensing challenges in some markets (China-specific tool ecosystem). Remote design team coordination across time zones is complex.
**LATAM:** DesignOps is rarely a dedicated role — operational responsibilities are distributed among design managers. Focus on basic operational foundations rather than advanced DesignOps practices. Tool budget constraints require creative solutions and open-source alternatives. Growing awareness of DesignOps as a discipline through professional networks.

## Industry Variations
**SaaS:** DesignOps focuses heavily on design system operations, tool integration with development workflows, and scaling design for multi-product portfolios. Figma administration and plugin ecosystem management are critical. DesignOps often sits between design and engineering operations.
**Fintech:** Compliance and security requirements affect design tool selection, approval workflows, and asset management. Design review and approval processes have regulatory implications. Audit trail requirements for design decisions affecting customer-facing compliance information. More formalized design governance processes.
**Healthcare:** HIPAA-compliant design tools and workflows are required. DesignOps must manage access controls for patient-facing design assets. Regulatory review cycles add complexity to design workflows. Accessibility compliance monitoring is a DesignOps responsibility.
**E-commerce:** High velocity design operations for fast campaign and feature turnaround. DesignOps manages seasonal peaks through resource planning and agency coordination. Global-local design operations for multi-market content adaptation. Performance optimization of design assets is an operational concern.

## Common Challenges
- Quantifying and communicating the ROI of DesignOps investments to leadership focused on product outcomes.
- Keeping pace with design tool evolution and migration (tool switching, version changes, new entrants).
- Balancing standardization for efficiency with design team autonomy and creative flexibility.
- Getting engineering and product teams to adopt improved handoff processes and collaboration tools.
- Managing design operations across multiple time zones, locations, and cultural contexts.
- Scaling design operations from a small team to large organization without losing effectiveness.
- Navigating the tension between design team needs and organizational compliance and procurement requirements.
- Building DesignOps capability when there is no established playbook and best practices are still emerging.

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- Software licensing compliance for design tools across the organization.
- GDPR and data privacy — design tool data processing compliance (Figma, Adobe cloud services).
- Accessibility requirements (WCAG, Section 508) embedded in design system governance processes.
- Security compliance for design tool access, asset sharing, and collaboration platforms.
- Intellectual property rights management for design assets created internally and by external partners.
- Procurement compliance for design tool and service vendor selection and contracting.
- Record retention policies for design artifacts, research data, and project documentation.
- SOC 2 compliance for design tools used in handling customer data through design artifacts.
