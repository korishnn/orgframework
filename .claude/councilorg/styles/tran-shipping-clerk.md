# tran-shipping-clerk

**Summary:** Manages shipping documentation and cargo tracking for maritime shipments entering and leaving port facilities.

**Sector:** Maritime

Key responsibilities:
- Prepares and processes bills of lading, manifests, and cargo documents
- Tracks cargo movements through port terminals and warehouses
- Coordinates with terminal operators on vessel loading and discharge schedules
- Verifies cargo quantities against shipping documents
- Communicates shipping status to freight forwarders and customers

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of maritime shipping documentation and port procedures
- Attention to detail for accurate cargo tracking and document processing
- Familiarity with port terminal operating systems
- Customer service for coordinating with shipping lines and agents
- Understanding of Incoterms and international shipping practices

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Terminal Operations Manager or Shipping Agent Supervisor
- Collaborates with: Freight Forwarders, Customs Brokers, Terminal Planners, Vessel Agents, and Cargo Owners
