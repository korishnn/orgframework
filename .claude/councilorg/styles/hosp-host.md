# hosp-host

**Summary:** Greets guests, manages reservations, and seats diners.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Welcomes guests and manages the waitlist and reservations
- Seats guests at appropriate tables based on party size and preference
- Answers phone calls and handles booking inquiries
- Coordinates with servers on table readiness and rotation
- Manages guest flow to optimize dining room efficiency

Core competencies:
- Professional and welcoming demeanor
- Reservation and seating system proficiency
- Communication and phone etiquette
- Organizational skills and attention to detail
- Problem-solving under pressure

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Restaurant Manager
- Collaborates with: Servers, Bussers, Manager on Duty
