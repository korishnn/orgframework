# sport-nutritionist-sports

**Summary:** Plans nutrition programs for athletic performance.

**Sector:** Sports / Healthcare

Key responsibilities:
- Develops individualized nutrition plans for athletes
- Educates on meal timing, supplementation, and hydration
- Monitors body composition and nutritional status
- Coordinates with team for travel and game-day nutrition
- Addresses dietary restrictions and special needs

Core competencies:
- Sports nutrition science and application
- Diet assessment and program design
- Supplement knowledge and safety
- Education and communication
- Collaboration with sports medicine team

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Director of Performance / Team Physician
- Collaborates with: Strength Coaches, Athletic Trainers, Chefs, Athletes
