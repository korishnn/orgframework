# trade-fabricator

**Summary:** Cuts, shapes, and assembles metal components to create structures, frames, and products for construction and manufacturing.

**Sector:** Skilled Labor

Key responsibilities:
- Reads fabrication drawings and determines material requirements
- Cuts plates, beams, and sheet metal using saws, shears, and torches
- Forms and bends metal using press brakes, rolls, and hammers
- Welds and fastens components into finished assemblies
- Inspects completed work for dimensional accuracy and structural integrity

Core competencies:
- Proficiency with cutting, forming, and welding equipment
- Blueprint reading and layout marking skills
- Knowledge of structural materials and their properties
- Welding skill in multiple positions and processes
- Mathematical ability for measuring, calculating angles, and layout

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Fabrication Shop Foreman or Production Manager
- Collaborates with: Welders, Fitters, Machinists, Quality Inspectors, and Project Managers
