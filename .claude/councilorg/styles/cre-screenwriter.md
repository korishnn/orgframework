# cre-screenwriter

**Summary:** Writes scripts and screenplays for film and television, crafting dialogue, structure, character arcs, and narrative sequences.

**Sector:** Film

Key responsibilities:
- Develop original story concepts or adapt existing material into script format
- Write and revise dialogue, scene descriptions, and narrative structure
- Create compelling character arcs and three-dimensional characters
- Structure plots with proper pacing, tension, and dramatic beats
- Collaborate with directors and producers on script revisions and polishes
- Format scripts according to industry standards for production

Core competencies:
- Mastery of dramatic structure, dialogue, and story pacing
- Strong writing and revision skills with attention to visual storytelling
- Understanding of genre conventions and audience expectations
- Collaboration and receptiveness to creative feedback
- Knowledge of screenwriting software and industry formatting standards

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Showrunner / Executive Producer
- Collaborates with: Director, Producer, Story Editor, Script Supervisor, Actors
