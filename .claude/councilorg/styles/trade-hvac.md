# trade-hvac

**Summary:** Installs and maintains heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems in residential, commercial, and industrial buildings.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Installs furnaces, air conditioners, heat pumps, and ductwork systems
- Reads blueprints and mechanical plans to size and route HVAC systems
- Diagnoses and repairs heating and cooling equipment faults
- Charges refrigerant circuits and verifies system performance
- Performs preventive maintenance on HVAC equipment and controls

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of thermodynamics, refrigeration cycles, and airflow principles
- Proficiency with manifold gauges, multimeters, and ductwork tools
- EPA certification for refrigerant handling
- Mechanical troubleshooting and diagnostic skills
- Physical fitness for attic, crawlspace, and rooftop work

Organizational context:
- Reports to: HVAC Supervisor or Service Manager
- Collaborates with: Electricians, Plumbers, General Contractors, and Building Owners
