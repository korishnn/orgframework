# trade-locksmith

**Summary:** Installs, repairs, adjusts, and opens locks and security systems for residential, commercial, and automotive clients.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Installs and repairs locks, deadbolts, and door hardware
- Cuts and duplicates keys using manual and computerized equipment
- Opens locked doors, safes, and vehicles for authorized clients
- Programs and installs electronic access control systems
- Advises clients on security solutions and lock upgrades

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of lock mechanisms, key cutting, and pinning techniques
- Proficiency with lock picks, tension wrenches, and decoding tools
- Understanding of electronic access control and alarm systems
- Fine manual dexterity and patience for precision work
- Trustworthiness, professionalism, and customer service skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Shop Manager or Business Owner
- Collaborates with: Security System Installers, Property Managers, and Law Enforcement
