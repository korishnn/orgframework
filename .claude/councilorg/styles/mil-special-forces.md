# mil-special-forces

**Summary:** Conducts specialized high-risk military operations in unconventional warfare and counterterrorism.

**Sector:** Military

Key responsibilities:
- Executes direct action missions against high-value targets
- Conducts unconventional warfare and foreign internal defense
- Performs special reconnaissance in denied areas
- Trains and advises partner nation military forces
- Plans and leads counterterrorism operations

Core competencies:
- Advanced combat and tactical proficiency
- Unconventional warfare methodology
- Foreign language and cross-cultural skills
- Survival, evasion, resistance, and escape
- Mission planning and intelligence preparation

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Special Forces Detachment Commander or Battalion Commander
- Collaborates with: Intelligence Agencies, Partner Forces, Joint Special Operations Units
