# legal-attorney-general

**Summary:** Serves as the chief legal officer of a government jurisdiction.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Provide legal opinions and advice to the executive branch and government agencies
- Oversee criminal prosecutions and civil litigation on behalf of the jurisdiction
- Represent the government in appeals before appellate and supreme courts
- Supervise the attorney general's office staff, including deputies, solicitors, and support personnel

Core competencies:
- Extensive litigation experience and deep knowledge of constitutional, administrative, and criminal law
- Leadership and management of large legal organizations with diverse practice areas
- Political acumen and ability to advise elected officials on legal risk and policy implications
- Highest standards of professional ethics and public trust

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Governor, prime minister, or directly elected by the electorate
- Collaborates with: Agency general counsels, law enforcement leaders, legislative bodies, and other state attorneys general
