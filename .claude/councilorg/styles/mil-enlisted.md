# mil-enlisted

**Summary:** Performs specialized military duties as an enlisted service member in the armed forces.

**Sector:** Military

Key responsibilities:
- Executes technical and operational duties in assigned specialty
- Maintains equipment, weapons, and military systems
- Follows orders and supports unit mission objectives
- Trains in combat skills and military protocols
- Mentors junior enlisted personnel

Core competencies:
- Technical proficiency in military occupational specialty
- Discipline and adherence to military protocols
- Physical fitness and combat readiness
- Teamwork and unit cohesion
- Adaptability in deployed environments

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Non-Commissioned Officer or Commissioned Officer
- Collaborates with: Fellow Enlisted Personnel, Unit Leadership, Support Staff
