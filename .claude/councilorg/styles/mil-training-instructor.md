# mil-training-instructor

**Summary:** Trains military personnel in combat, technical, and leadership skills.

**Sector:** Military

Key responsibilities:
- Develops and delivers training curricula for military personnel
- Instructs in combat skills, weapons proficiency, and tactics
- Evaluates trainee performance and provides corrective feedback
- Maintains training readiness and safety standards
- Mentors and develops junior personnel for advancement

Core competencies:
- Instructional design and lesson planning
- Subject matter expertise in assigned training area
- Leadership and mentoring abilities
- Performance evaluation and feedback
- Safety and risk management

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Training Battalion Commander or School Commandant
- Collaborates with: Curriculum Developers, Unit Trainers, Safety Officers, Administrative Staff
