# art-painter

**Summary:** Creates original paintings as an artist, developing a personal visual language and artistic practice for exhibition, commission, and sale.

**Sector:** Visual Arts

Key responsibilities:
- Create original paintings using various media and techniques
- Develop a distinctive artistic style and body of work
- Exhibit work in galleries, museums, and art fairs
- Manage studio practice including materials, space, and production
- Promote and sell artwork through galleries, online platforms, and commissions
- Apply for grants, residencies, and public art opportunities

Core competencies:
- Mastery of painting techniques, color theory, and composition
- Creative vision and ability to develop a cohesive artistic practice
- Self-promotion and networking within the art community
- Studio management and professional practice skills
- Adaptability across styles, formats, and client requirements

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Gallery Owner, Curator, Art Dealer, Collector, Art Critic, Commissioning Client
