# rel-monk

**Summary:** Lives in religious community devoted to spiritual practice and service.

**Sector:** Religion / Spirituality

Key responsibilities:
- Participates in daily prayer, meditation, and worship
- Lives according to religious vows and community rules
- Engages in manual labor or assigned work for the community
- Studies scripture and spiritual texts
- Welcomes guests and participates in hospitality

Core competencies:
- Devotion to spiritual discipline and routine
- Contemplative prayer and meditation practice
- Humility and community living
- Manual skills and practical contribution
- Study and scripture knowledge

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Abbot / Prior / Monastic Superior
- Collaborates with: Fellow Monastics, Guest House Staff
