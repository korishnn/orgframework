# mkt-ppc

**Summary:** Paid Media Manager plans, executes, and optimizes paid advertising campaigns across search, social, and display platforms to drive acquisition, revenue, and marketing ROI.

**Level:** Manager or IC
**Reports to:** Marketing Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Plan, execute, and optimize paid search campaigns (Google Ads, Bing Ads) and paid social campaigns (Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok)
- Manage advertising budgets, bid strategies, and spend allocation across channels for maximum ROI
- Conduct A/B testing on ad copy, creative assets, landing pages, audience targeting, and bidding approaches
- Perform keyword research, negative keyword management, and search query analysis for paid search
- Monitor and optimize campaign performance metrics including CPA, ROAS, CTR, conversion rate, and impression share
- Build and maintain audience targeting strategies including retargeting, lookalike audiences, and custom intent segments
- Produce regular campaign performance reporting with actionable insights for marketing stakeholders
- Stay current with ad platform changes, policy updates, new ad formats, and competitive landscape
- Manage attribution modeling approach and ensure proper conversion tracking across channels
- Collaborate with creative teams on ad copywriting and visual asset development for paid channels
- Implement landing page optimization strategies in partnership with web development and CRO teams
- Forecast campaign performance and build media plans for new launches and seasonal campaigns

## Core Competencies
- Expertise with paid advertising platforms (Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, LinkedIn Ads, TikTok Ads, Microsoft Ads)
- Strong analytical and quantitative skills for budget optimization, performance analysis, and ROI calculation
- Experience with conversion tracking implementation, pixel management, and attribution setup
- Understanding of landing page optimization and conversion rate optimization (CRO) principles
- Knowledge of audience targeting, retargeting, lookalike modeling, and first-party data strategies
- Proficiency with analytics and reporting tools (GA4, Looker Studio, Supermetrics, campaign management platforms)
- Experience with A/B testing methodology and statistical significance in advertising experiments
- Understanding of bid management strategies (manual, automated, portfolio bidding, Smart Bidding)
- Knowledge of ad creative best practices for different platforms and formats
- Budget management and forecasting skills for multi-channel media planning

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) and Cost per Acquisition (CPA)
- Click-through rate (CTR) and Quality Score for search ads
- Conversion rate and cost per lead by campaign and channel
- Impression share and average position for search campaigns
- Budget utilization and spend efficiency across channels
- Incrementality and lift measurement from advertising experiments
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) by channel and campaign type

## Career Progression
- Junior/Coordinator (0-2 years): Manages campaign execution, keyword builds, basic optimization, reporting
- Specialist (2-4 years): Owns campaigns across channels, manages budgets, runs tests, analyzes performance
- Senior/Manager (4-7 years): Develops paid media strategy, manages multi-channel programs, leads team members
- Director (7-10 years): Sets paid media vision, manages team and agency relationships, drives channel innovation
- VP (10+ years): Executive marketing leadership, oversees entire demand generation function
- Typical progression: Junior to Specialist 1-2 years, Specialist to Senior 2-3 years, Senior to Director 2-4 years

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Morning campaign performance review checking spend, CPA, and ROAS by campaign
- Bid adjustments and budget pacing to stay on track with goals
- Ad copy and creative rotation based on fatigue and performance data
- Search query analysis for negative keyword additions and new keyword opportunities
- Weekly campaign optimization and testing iteration based on performance data
- Creative brief development and review cycles with design team
- Monthly budget reconciliation and reallocation across channels
- Platform policy changes review and compliance checks
- Weekly alignment with marketing team on campaign priorities and landing page needs

## Regional Nuances
- NA: Most competitive and expensive digital advertising market; Google and Meta dominant; sophisticated attribution and measurement; privacy changes (iOS ATT, third-party cookie deprecation) reshaping strategies
- EMEA: GDPR impacts targeting and tracking significantly; consent management platforms required; Google and Meta dominant but LinkedIn strong for B2B; varying competitiveness by country
- APAC: Lower CPCs in many markets; platform mix varies (Baidu, Naver, Line, WeChat in different countries); mobile-first strategy critical; high social ad engagement in Southeast Asia
- LATAM: Growing digital ad market; cost-effective CPCs; Facebook and Instagram dominant; mobile-first approach required; Spanish and Portuguese creative localization essential

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: Long sales cycle requires multi-touch attribution; LinkedIn for B2B demand gen; free trial and demo conversion optimization; lead quality focus over volume
- E-commerce: High-volume seasonal campaigns; dynamic product ads; shopping feed optimization; ROAS-driven strategy; retargeting critical for conversion
- Fintech: Strict advertising compliance regulations; limited targeting options for financial products; trust-building creative; regulated claims in ad copy
- Healthcare: HIPAA-compliant remarketing limitations; provider-focused LinkedIn targeting; condition-specific landing pages; compliant claims management

## Common Challenges
- Managing rising CPCs and competition across key search terms and audiences
- Adapting to privacy-driven tracking limitations (iOS ATT, third-party cookie deprecation, GA4 transition)
- Proving incrementality and avoiding overspending on organic-converting channels
- Balancing brand awareness investment with direct-response performance targets
- Keeping up with rapidly changing ad platform policies, algorithms, and ad formats
- Managing cross-channel attribution complexity in multi-touch customer journeys

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- CAN-SPAM Act for paid email advertising
- GDPR for EMEA ad targeting and tracking consent
- CCPA for California advertising data practices
- FTC truth-in-advertising guidelines
- Platform-specific advertising policies (Google Ads policies, Meta ad policies, LinkedIn ad policies)
- Industry-specific regulations (SEC for fintech, FDA for healthcare, gambling regulations)
- DSA and DMA for EU digital services and markets compliance
