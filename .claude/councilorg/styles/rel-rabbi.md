# rel-rabbi

**Summary:** Leads Jewish congregation through teaching, counseling, and ritual leadership.

**Sector:** Religion / Spirituality

Key responsibilities:
- Leads Shabbat and holiday worship services
- Teaches Torah, Talmud, and Jewish traditions
- Provides pastoral counseling to congregants
- Officiates lifecycle events including weddings and bar/bat mitzvahs
- Engages in interfaith and community outreach

Core competencies:
- Torah and rabbinic literature knowledge
- Liturgical leadership and cantorial skill
- Teaching and education
- Pastoral counseling and empathy
- Community leadership and organizational management

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Synagogue Board / Congregation
- Collaborates with: Cantor, Educators, Lay Leaders, Community Organizations
