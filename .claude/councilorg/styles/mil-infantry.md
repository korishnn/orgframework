# mil-infantry

**Summary:** Engages in ground combat operations as an infantry soldier in the armed forces.

**Sector:** Military

Key responsibilities:
- Conducts ground combat patrols and offensive operations
- Operates and maintains infantry weapons and equipment
- Establishes defensive positions and security perimeters
- Performs reconnaissance and surveillance missions
- Executes urban and close-quarters combat operations

Core competencies:
- Small arms proficiency and weapons handling
- Tactical movement and field craft
- Physical fitness and combat endurance
- Team coordination in hostile environments
- Navigation and land orientation skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Infantry Squad Leader or Platoon Sergeant
- Collaborates with: Fire Support Teams, Armored Units, Aviation Support, Logistics
