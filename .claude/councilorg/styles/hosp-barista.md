# hosp-barista

**Summary:** Prepares and serves coffee and espresso beverages.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Prepares espresso-based and specialty coffee beverages
- Operates and maintains espresso machines and brewing equipment
- Provides friendly customer service and order processing
- Maintains inventory of coffee beans, milk, and supplies
- Keeps the workspace clean and organized

Core competencies:
- Espresso preparation and latte art
- Coffee knowledge including origins and roast profiles
- Customer service and efficiency during rush periods
- Equipment maintenance and troubleshooting
- Cash handling and POS operation

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Café Manager / Restaurant Manager
- Collaborates with: Servers, Kitchen Staff, Shift Supervisor
