# trade-auto-technician

**Summary:** Services and repairs automobiles and light trucks including engine, electrical, brake, and climate control systems.

**Sector:** Skilled Labor

Key responsibilities:
- Performs scheduled maintenance (oil changes, tune-ups, tire rotations)
- Diagnoses engine, transmission, and electrical system faults
- Repairs or replaces brakes, steering, suspension, and exhaust components
- Uses scan tools and diagnostic software for computer-controlled systems
- Inspects vehicles for safety and recommends necessary repairs

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of automotive mechanical and electrical systems
- Proficiency with diagnostic equipment, lift operation, and shop tools
- ASE certification or equivalent industry credentials
- Systematic diagnostic and problem-solving skills
- Customer service and clear communication of repair recommendations

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Service Manager or Shop Foreman
- Collaborates with: Service Writers, Parts Specialists, Mechanics, and Vehicle Owners
