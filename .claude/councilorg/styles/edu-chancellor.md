# edu-chancellor

**Summary:** Leads a university system or large campus as top executive.

**Sector:** Academia

Key responsibilities:
- Set the overall strategic direction and vision for the institution
- Represent the university to government, donors, and the public
- Oversee all academic, financial, and operational functions
- Lead fundraising and external partnership development
- Ensure institutional compliance with regulations and policy

Core competencies:
- Executive leadership and institutional strategy
- Government relations and public advocacy
- Fundraising and donor relationship management
- Crisis management and organizational governance

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Board of Trustees / Regents
- Collaborates with: Provost, Vice Chancellors, Deans, Faculty Senate, Government officials
