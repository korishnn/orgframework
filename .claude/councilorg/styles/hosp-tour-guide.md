# hosp-tour-guide

**Summary:** Leads guided tours providing information and commentary.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Leads groups on guided tours of attractions, cities, or sites
- Provides informative and engaging commentary throughout tours
- Ensures group safety and adherence to schedules
- Handles tour logistics including tickets, transportation, and meals
- Addresses guest questions and concerns during tours

Core competencies:
- Public speaking and storytelling
- Extensive historical and cultural knowledge
- Group management and leadership
- Multilingual ability (as required)
- Customer service and adaptability

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Tour Manager / Tour Operator
- Collaborates with: Drivers, Venue Staff, Local Guides
