# avi-ground-crew

**Summary:** Handles aircraft ground operations including marshalling, towing, loading, and servicing between flights.

**Sector:** Aviation

Key responsibilities:
- Marshals aircraft into and out of gates using hand signals
- Operates tugs, belt loaders, GPU units, and air start equipment
- Connects ground power, air conditioning, and potable water services
- Loads and unloads baggage, cargo, and mail from aircraft holds
- Places chocks, safety cones, and ground support equipment around aircraft

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of aircraft marshalling signals and ramp safety procedures
- Operation of ground support equipment with proper training
- Situational awareness for working around active taxiways and jet blast
- Physical fitness for lifting, bending, and working on ramps in all weather
- Team coordination during time-sensitive turnaround operations

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Ramp Supervisor or Ground Operations Manager
- Collaborates with: Ramp Agents, Gate Agents, Baggage Handlers, and airline Flight Crews
