# tran-warehouse-manager

**Summary:** Oversees warehouse operations including inventory management, staff supervision, safety, and facility maintenance.

**Sector:** Transportation

Key responsibilities:
- Manages receiving, storage, picking, packing, and shipping operations
- Supervises warehouse staff and assigns daily work tasks
- Maintains accurate inventory records and conducts cycle counts
- Ensures compliance with safety regulations and sanitation standards
- Optimizes warehouse layout and storage utilization

Core competencies:
- Warehouse management systems (WMS) proficiency
- Inventory control and cycle counting methods
- OSHA safety standards and material handling best practices
- Leadership, training, and team management skills
- Budget management and operational KPI analysis

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Distribution Center Manager or Director of Operations
- Collaborates with: Logistics Coordinators, Shipping Clerks, Procurement, and Safety Officers
