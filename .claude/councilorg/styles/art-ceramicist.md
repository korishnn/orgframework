# art-ceramicist

**Summary:** Creates ceramic artworks and functional pottery, shaping clay through hand-building, wheel-throwing, and glazing techniques.

**Sector:** Visual Arts

Key responsibilities:
- Design and create ceramic pieces using wheel-throwing or hand-building techniques
- Develop and apply glaze formulations for desired surface effects
- Fire kilns to specified temperatures and atmospheric conditions
- Experiment with clay bodies, forms, and finishing techniques
- Produce functional pottery and sculptural ceramic works
- Manage studio operations including kiln maintenance and material inventory

Core competencies:
- Mastery of clay preparation, forming, and finishing techniques
- Knowledge of glaze chemistry, kiln firing, and ceramic science
- Understanding of form, function, and design in ceramic practice
- Studio management and production planning skills
- Physical stamina for working with clay and managing kiln operations

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Gallery Owner, Art Fair Organizer, Commissioning Client, Studio Technician, Supplier
