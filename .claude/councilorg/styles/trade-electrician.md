# trade-electrician

**Summary:** Installs, maintains, and repairs electrical systems, wiring, and equipment in residential, commercial, and industrial settings.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Installs and connects wiring, outlets, switches, and fixtures
- Reads electrical blueprints and schematics to plan layouts
- Troubleshoots and repairs electrical faults and malfunctions
- Tests circuits and ensures code-compliant installations
- Upgrades electrical panels and systems for capacity or safety

Core competencies:
- Deep knowledge of electrical theory, circuits, and safety practices
- Proficiency with testing meters, conduit benders, and hand tools
- Ability to interpret the National Electrical Code and local amendments
- Analytical troubleshooting and problem-solving skills
- Physical fitness for work in confined spaces and at heights

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Electrical Foreman or Project Manager
- Collaborates with: Carpenters, Plumbers, HVAC technicians, and General Contractors
