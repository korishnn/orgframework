# edu-driving-instructor

**Summary:** Teaches driving skills and road safety.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Provide behind-the-wheel instruction to student drivers
- Teach traffic laws, defensive driving techniques, and road safety
- Evaluate student readiness for driver licensing examinations
- Maintain a dual-controlled vehicle in safe operating condition
- Keep records of student progress and lesson completion

Core competencies:
- Advanced driving skills and defensive driving knowledge
- Patience and clear communication under pressure
- Knowledge of traffic laws and licensing requirements
- Risk assessment and safety awareness

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Driving School Owner / Program Coordinator
- Collaborates with: Other driving instructors, Licensing authorities, Students' parents
