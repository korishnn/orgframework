# np-community-organizer

**Summary:** Mobilizes communities around social issues and campaigns for systemic change.

**Sector:** Nonprofit

Key responsibilities:
- Identifies and recruits community leaders and members
- Builds coalitions with allied organizations and stakeholders
- Conducts public meetings and grassroots campaigns
- Develops community leadership and capacity
- Advocates for policy changes at local and state levels

Core competencies:
- Grassroots organizing and coalition building
- Community engagement and facilitation
- Campaign planning and strategy
- Public speaking and persuasive communication
- Understanding of political and legislative processes

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Advocacy Director or Campaign Manager
- Collaborates with: Coalition Partners, Community Leaders, Policy Staff, Media
