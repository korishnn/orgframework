# sport-dance-instructor

**Summary:** Teaches dance techniques and choreography.

**Sector:** Sports / Performing Arts

Key responsibilities:
- Teaches dance classes in specific styles and techniques
- Creates and teaches choreography for performances
- Provides feedback on technique, timing, and expression
- Plans lesson progressions for different skill levels
- Ensures a safe and encouraging learning environment

Core competencies:
- Dance technique and performance expertise
- Choreography and creative direction
- Communication and demonstration skills
- Class management and student development
- Musicality and rhythm

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Studio Director / Artistic Director / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Other Instructors, Choreographers, Students, Parents
