# art-printmaker

**Summary:** Creates prints and editions using various printmaking techniques including etching, lithography, screen printing, and relief printing.

**Sector:** Visual Arts

Key responsibilities:
- Create original print works using chosen printmaking techniques
- Prepare plates, screens, or blocks for edition printing
- Pull and edition prints with consistent quality
- Experiment with techniques, inks, papers, and registration methods
- Sign, number, and document limited edition prints
- Maintain print studio equipment and materials inventory

Core competencies:
- Technical mastery of one or more printmaking methods
- Understanding of ink, paper, and printing press operation
- Knowledge of editioning procedures and artist proof conventions
- Design and compositional skills for print media
- Problem-solving for technical challenges in the printing process

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Gallery Owner, Master Printer, Curator, Papermaker, Publisher, Collector
