# sls-ops

**Summary:** Sales Operations manages CRM systems, forecasting, territory planning, sales analytics, and process optimization to enable the sales organization to operate efficiently and drive predictable revenue.

**Level:** IC
**Reports to:** Sales Director / Revenue Operations
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Administer and maintain the CRM system (Salesforce, HubSpot) ensuring data integrity, user adoption, and system optimization
- Build and manage sales forecasting models, reporting dashboards, and pipeline analytics for sales leadership
- Design territory assignments, account segmentation, and quota allocation frameworks for balanced coverage
- Analyze sales performance data to identify trends, predict outcomes, and surface actionable insights
- Develop, document, and maintain sales processes, workflows, automation rules, and approval workflows
- Support compensation plan design, commission calculations, SPIFF administration, and payout reconciliations
- Partner with finance on revenue reporting, pipeline analysis, and annual planning processes
- Evaluate, select, and integrate sales technology tools into the overall sales tech stack
- Manage sales data governance including data quality standards, deduplication, and hygiene processes
- Support annual planning cycles including territory carving, quota setting, and headcount planning
- Build and maintain sales playbooks for processes, systems, and operational best practices
- Provide ad-hoc analysis, reporting, and operational support to sales leadership and teams

## Core Competencies
- Strong analytical, quantitative, and data modeling skills for sales performance analysis
- Expertise in CRM administration (Salesforce, HubSpot) with deep configuration and reporting knowledge
- Proficiency with analytics and visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI, Looker, Domo)
- Understanding of sales process design, pipeline management, and funnel metrics
- Knowledge of forecasting methodologies (commit, upside, best case, scenario planning)
- Familiarity with territory planning and quota setting approaches
- Experience with compensation plan design and sales incentive mechanics
- Attention to detail and ability to manage multiple stakeholder needs simultaneously
- Understanding of sales methodologies and how operations support them
- Project management skills for leading cross-functional operational initiatives

## Tools & Technology

- Analytics: Tableau, Power BI, Looker, Domo, Sigma Computing

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Forecast accuracy (commit vs. actual attainment variance)
- CRM data quality scores and user adoption rates
- Sales cycle length trends and win rate analysis accuracy
- Territory balance metrics (opportunity coverage, attainment distribution)
- Lead-to-cash process efficiency and automation coverage
- Reporting dashboard usage and stakeholder satisfaction
- Commission and payment accuracy and timeliness

## Career Progression
- Analyst (0-2 years): Maintains reports, manages data hygiene, supports basic analysis
- Specialist/Manager (2-4 years): Owns forecasting, territory modeling, compensation support
- Senior Manager (4-7 years): Leads operational programs, designs processes, manages tools
- Director (7-10 years): Drives sales ops strategy, manages team, partners with sales leadership
- VP (10+ years): Executive operational leadership, organizational revenue operations strategy
- Typical progression: Analyst to Manager 2-3 years, Manager to Senior 2-3 years, Senior to Director 2-4 years

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Morning pipeline and forecast review with sales leadership for deal progression insights
- CRM administration and support including user setup, permissions, and data quality management
- Ad-hoc reporting requests from sales teams and leadership for performance analysis
- Territory and account assignment changes as sales team evolves
- Weekly forecast calls and pipeline review preparation
- Commission calculation support and SPIFF tracking administration
- Sales process documentation and improvement project work
- Monthly performance reporting for sales leadership meetings
- Regular CRM training sessions for new hires and refresher training

## Regional Nuances
- NA: Most mature sales ops function; deep Salesforce admin expertise common; sophisticated forecasting and analytics; sales ops career path well-established; higher compensation with RSUs common
- EMEA: GDPR compliance integrated into sales ops processes; multi-currency and multi-language CRM complexity; works council involvement for territory changes; stronger data protection requirements
- APAC: Growing sales ops maturity; often combined with broader revenue ops roles; high growth requiring scalable processes; India has strong sales ops talent pool for analytics
- LATAM: Emerging sales ops function; often part of general management; cost-effective tooling; focus on basic CRM hygiene and reporting before advanced analytics

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: Subscription metrics operationalized (ARR, NRR, churn, LTV); usage-based pricing ops; self-serve and sales-assisted operations; complex entitlement management
- Fintech: Regulatory compliance in commission structures; audit trail requirements; revenue recognition complexity; channel compliance tracking
- Healthcare: CRM compliance for HIPAA; complex buying group territory management; grant and contract revenue operations
- Enterprise Software: Multi-year contract operations; professional services revenue ops; usage and seat management; complex deal desk operations

## Common Challenges
- Maintaining CRM data quality and user adoption across a busy, quota-driven sales team
- Balancing strategic analysis work with day-to-day operational support requests
- Aligning territories and quotas fairly across a dynamic and evolving sales organization
- Forecasting accurately in the face of deal slippage and pipeline volatility
- Keeping up with sales tech stack complexity and integration management
- Getting sales team adoption of new processes and tools

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- SOX for revenue reporting controls and commission accuracy
- GDPR for EMEA CRM data management
- CCPA for California prospect and customer data
- ASC 606 for revenue recognition compliance in reporting
- FINRA regulations for financial services sales operations
- HIPAA for healthcare CRM data handling
- Internal audit requirements for sales compensation and revenue processes
