# sci-environmental-scientist

**Summary:** Studies environmental systems and develops solutions to ecological problems.

**Sector:** Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Conduct field and laboratory research on environmental systems
- Monitor air, water, and soil quality for pollutants
- Assess environmental impact of human activities and development
- Develop remediation strategies and conservation plans
- Prepare environmental reports and regulatory compliance documentation

Core competencies:
- Environmental monitoring and sampling techniques
- Data analysis and Geographic Information Systems (GIS)
- Knowledge of environmental regulations and policy
- Ecological assessment and remediation planning

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Environmental Research Director / Project Manager
- Collaborates with: Government agencies, Conservation organizations, Industry, Community groups
