# np-executive-director

**Summary:** Leads nonprofit organization strategy, fundraising, and operations to advance the mission.

**Sector:** Nonprofit

Key responsibilities:
- Sets organizational vision, strategy, and direction
- Leads fundraising and resource development efforts
- Oversees program delivery and impact measurement
- Manages budget, finance, and organizational operations
- Builds relationships with board, donors, and community partners

Core competencies:
- Nonprofit leadership and management
- Fundraising and donor relations
- Strategic planning and board governance
- Financial management and budgeting
- Mission-driven communication and advocacy

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Board of Directors
- Collaborates with: Program Managers, Development Director, Board Members, Community Partners
