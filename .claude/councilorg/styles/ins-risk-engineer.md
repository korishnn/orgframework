# ins-risk-engineer

**Summary:** Assesses physical and operational risks for insurance underwriting.

**Sector:** Insurance

Key responsibilities:
- Conducts on-site inspections of facilities, operations, and safety protocols
- Identifies and evaluates physical hazards, fire risks, and safety deficiencies
- Provides risk improvement recommendations to policyholders
- Prepares detailed risk assessment reports for underwriters

Core competencies:
- Engineering or technical degree in safety, fire protection, or related field
- Knowledge of loss prevention, safety standards, and building codes
- Strong observation and technical report writing skills
- Familiarity with industry-specific risk exposures across various sectors

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Risk Engineering Manager or Chief Engineer
- Collaborates with: Underwriters, loss control teams, policyholders, safety managers
