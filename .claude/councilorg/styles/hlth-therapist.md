# hlth-therapist

**Summary:** Provides talk therapy for individuals, couples, and groups.

**Sector:** Mental Health

Key responsibilities:
- Conduct therapeutic sessions using evidence-based modalities
- Assess client mental health status and develop treatment plans
- Diagnose mental health conditions using DSM-5 criteria
- Guide clients through cognitive, emotional, and behavioral changes
- Monitor client progress and adjust therapeutic approaches as needed
- Maintain confidential client records and treatment documentation
- Collaborate with other providers on coordinated care plans

Core competencies:
- Psychotherapy modalities (CBT, DBT, psychodynamic, humanistic)
- Clinical assessment and diagnosis
- Therapeutic alliance building and rapport
- Crisis intervention and risk assessment
- Cultural competency and trauma-informed care
- Ethical practice and confidentiality standards

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Clinical Director / Therapy Practice Owner
- Collaborates with: Psychiatrists, social workers, psychologists, primary care providers, case managers
