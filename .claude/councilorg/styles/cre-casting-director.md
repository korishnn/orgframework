# cre-casting-director

**Summary:** Casts actors for film, TV, and theater productions by identifying, auditioning, and selecting talent that aligns with creative and character requirements.

**Sector:** Film

Key responsibilities:
- Read scripts and determine casting needs for each role
- Source and recruit actors through agencies, showcases, and talent databases
- Organize and conduct auditions, callbacks, and screen tests
- Evaluate actor performances and suitability for specific roles
- Negotiate contracts and compensation with talent representatives
- Maintain relationships with agents, managers, and talent agencies

Core competencies:
- Deep knowledge of acting techniques and performance evaluation
- Extensive network within the talent and entertainment industry
- Strong interpersonal and negotiation skills
- Organizational ability to manage large audition processes
- Understanding of character requirements and creative vision alignment

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Producer / Director
- Collaborates with: Director, Producer, Talent Agents, Actors, Production Manager
