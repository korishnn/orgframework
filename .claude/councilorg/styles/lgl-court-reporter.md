# lgl-court-reporter

**Summary:** Creates verbatim transcripts of court proceedings and depositions.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Records sworn testimony in courtrooms, depositions, and other legal proceedings
- Operates stenography equipment or digital recording systems to capture verbatim accounts
- Transcribes and formats certified transcripts for legal review and record
- Reads back portions of testimony upon request during proceedings

Core competencies:
- Certified court reporter credential (RPR, CSR) or regional equivalent
- Expert-level stenography skills with high accuracy and speed
- Proficiency with computer-aided transcription (CAT) software
- Knowledge of legal terminology, grammar, and transcript formatting standards

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Court Administrator or Freelance Agency Coordinator
- Collaborates with: Judges, attorneys, litigants, videographers, transcription services
