# fin-underwriter

**Summary:** Assesses risk for insurance policies or securities offerings.

**Sector:** Finance

Key responsibilities:
- Evaluates applications and determines appropriate coverage terms and premiums
- Analyzes risk factors using actuarial data, financial history, and industry benchmarks
- Sets policy conditions, exclusions, and pricing within defined risk appetite
- Reviews and decisions submissions that fall outside delegated authority guidelines

Core competencies:
- Deep understanding of risk assessment methodologies and underwriting guidelines
- Analytical decision-making with attention to detail
- Knowledge of relevant regulations and compliance standards
- Strong negotiation skills for policy terms and conditions

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Underwriting Manager or Chief Underwriter
- Collaborates with: Actuaries, brokers, agents, claims adjusters, risk engineers
