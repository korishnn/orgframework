# hosp-pastry-chef

**Summary:** Creates desserts, baked goods, and pastry menu items.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Develops and prepares dessert and pastry menu items
- Bakes breads, pastries, cakes, and specialty items
- Decorates and presents finished desserts attractively
- Manages pastry inventory and ingredient ordering
- Maintains cleanliness and organization of the pastry kitchen

Core competencies:
- Baking and pastry techniques
- Recipe development and scaling
- Decoration and plating artistry
- Attention to detail and precision
- Time management for multiple projects

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Executive Chef
- Collaborates with: Line Cooks, Sous Chef, Restaurant Manager, Purchasing
