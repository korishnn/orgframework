# avi-airport-manager

**Summary:** Oversees airport operations, security, facilities, and regulatory compliance for an airport or aviation authority.

**Sector:** Aviation

Key responsibilities:
- Manages daily airport operations including terminals, runways, and airfield
- Ensures compliance with FAA, TSA, and environmental regulations
- Oversees airport security programs and emergency response planning
- Manages tenant relationships with airlines, concessionaires, and service providers
- Develops budgets, capital improvement plans, and operational policies

Core competencies:
- Airport management or aviation administration experience
- Knowledge of FAR Part 139 airport certification requirements
- Security management and familiarity with TSA regulatory framework
- Financial management including budgeting, grants, and capital planning
- Leadership, stakeholder communication, and crisis management skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Airport Director or Board of County Commissioners
- Collaborates with: Airline Station Managers, FAA, TSA, Concessionaires, Contractors, and Municipal Agencies
