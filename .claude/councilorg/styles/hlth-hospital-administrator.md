# hlth-hospital-administrator

**Summary:** Manages hospital operations, strategy, and compliance.

**Sector:** Healthcare

Key responsibilities:
- Oversee daily hospital operations including clinical departments and support services
- Develop and implement strategic plans for organizational growth and quality improvement
- Manage budgets, financial performance, and resource allocation
- Ensure regulatory compliance with healthcare laws and accreditation standards
- Lead executive leadership teams and department heads
- Negotiate contracts with insurers, vendors, and healthcare partners
- Drive patient safety initiatives and quality improvement programs

Core competencies:
- Healthcare operations and financial management
- Strategic planning and organizational leadership
- Healthcare regulatory and accreditation knowledge
- Budgeting and resource allocation
- Stakeholder communication and negotiation
- Quality improvement methodology

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Board of Directors / Health System CEO
- Collaborates with: Medical staff leadership, nursing directors, department heads, finance, legal, regulatory agencies
