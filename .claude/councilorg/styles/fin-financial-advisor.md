# fin-financial-advisor

**Summary:** Advises clients on investments, retirement, and financial planning.

**Sector:** Finance

Key responsibilities:
- Develops personalized financial plans based on client goals and risk tolerance
- Recommends investment products including stocks, bonds, mutual funds, and insurance
- Monitors and rebalances client portfolios to align with changing objectives
- Educates clients on tax implications, retirement strategies, and estate planning

Core competencies:
- Certified Financial Planner (CFP) or equivalent credential
- Strong understanding of financial markets and investment vehicles
- Excellent interpersonal and trust-building communication skills
- Proficiency with financial planning software and portfolio analytics

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Financial Planning Director or Branch Manager
- Collaborates with: Wealth managers, tax professionals, estate attorneys, client relationship teams
