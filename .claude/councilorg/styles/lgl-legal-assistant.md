# lgl-legal-assistant

**Summary:** Provides administrative support to legal professionals.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Manages attorney calendars, schedules meetings, and coordinates court appearances
- Prepares and formats legal correspondence, filings, and client communications
- Handles billing, time entries, and expense reporting for legal matters
- Maintains filing systems and organizes legal documents and records

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of legal terminology and court procedures
- Strong organizational and time management abilities
- Proficiency with office productivity software and legal practice management tools
- Discretion and professionalism when handling confidential information

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Office Manager or Supervising Attorney
- Collaborates with: Attorneys, paralegals, clients, court personnel, vendors
