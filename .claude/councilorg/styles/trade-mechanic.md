# trade-mechanic

**Summary:** Diagnoses, repairs, and maintains mechanical equipment and vehicles including engines, transmissions, and hydraulic systems.

**Sector:** Skilled Labor

Key responsibilities:
- Diagnoses mechanical problems using diagnostic tools and test equipment
- Repairs or replaces engines, transmissions, brakes, and suspension components
- Performs preventive maintenance services including fluid changes and inspections
- Reads service manuals and technical diagrams to guide repairs
- Tests repaired systems to verify proper operation

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of engine, powertrain, hydraulic, and electrical systems
- Proficiency with diagnostic scanners, multimeters, and hand tools
- Mechanical reasoning and systematic troubleshooting ability
- Physical strength and dexterity for heavy components and tight spaces
- Customer communication for explaining repair needs

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Shop Foreman or Service Manager
- Collaborates with: Auto Technicians, Parts Specialists, Service Writers, and Customers
