# hlth-speech-therapist

**Summary:** Treats communication and swallowing disorders across all ages.

**Sector:** Allied Health

Key responsibilities:
- Assess speech, language, cognitive-communication, and swallowing function
- Diagnose communication disorders including articulation, fluency, voice, and aphasia
- Provide therapeutic intervention for speech sound production and language comprehension
- Treat swallowing disorders through compensatory strategies and exercises
- Implement augmentative and alternative communication systems as needed
- Educate patients, families, and caregivers on communication strategies
- Document evaluation findings, treatment plans, and progress reports

Core competencies:
- Speech and language assessment and intervention
- Dysphagia evaluation and management
- Cognitive-communication rehabilitation
- Augmentative and alternative communication technology
- Knowledge of anatomy and physiology of speech and swallowing
- Patient and family counseling and education

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Rehabilitation Services Director / Clinical Manager
- Collaborates with: Physicians, occupational therapists, physical therapists, educators, audiologists, psychologists
