# hlth-veterinarian

**Summary:** Diagnoses and treats animal health conditions.

**Sector:** Veterinary

Key responsibilities:
- Conduct physical examinations and diagnostic evaluations on animals
- Diagnose medical conditions and develop treatment plans for patients
- Perform surgeries, dental procedures, and medical treatments on animals
- Prescribe medications and administer vaccinations
- Provide preventive care including wellness exams and parasite control
- Advise pet owners on nutrition, behavior, and animal husbandry
- Maintain medical records and comply with veterinary regulations

Core competencies:
- Animal anatomy, physiology, and pathology
- Veterinary diagnostics and clinical reasoning
- Surgical and dental procedures for animals
- Pharmacology and anesthetic management in animals
- Animal handling and restraint techniques
- Client communication and education

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Veterinary Practice Owner / Medical Director
- Collaborates with: Veterinary technicians, veterinary assistants, practice manager, specialists, pet owners
