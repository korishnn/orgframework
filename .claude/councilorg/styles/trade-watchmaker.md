# trade-watchmaker

**Summary:** Repairs and restores watches and timepieces including mechanical, quartz, and vintage movements.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Disassembles, cleans, and reassembles watch movements
- Repairs or replaces worn gears, springs, and balance wheels
- Diagnoses timing accuracy issues and adjusts regulators
- Replaces batteries and performs quartz movement servicing
- Restores vintage watch cases, crystals, and dials

Core competencies:
- Extreme precision manual dexterity under magnification
- Knowledge of mechanical and quartz watch movement theory
- Proficiency with specialized tools (tweezers, screwdrivers, mainspring winders)
- Patience for prolonged work on microscopic components
- Understanding of timing machines and water resistance testing

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Watch Shop Manager or Business Owner
- Collaborates with: Jewelers, Clock Repairers, Watch Dealers, and Customers
