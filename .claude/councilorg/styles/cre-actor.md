# cre-actor

**Summary:** Performs roles in theater, film, or television productions, embodying characters through voice, movement, and emotional expression to bring scripts to life.

**Sector:** Theater

Key responsibilities:
- Study scripts and develop character interpretations through research and rehearsal
- Perform角色的 live on stage or in front of cameras for productions
- Collaborate with directors on character development and performance choices
- Memorize lines, blocking, and stage or camera directions
- Maintain vocal and physical technique through ongoing training
- Attend auditions and casting calls for new roles and projects

Core competencies:
- Strong vocal projection, diction, and accent abilities
- Physical expressiveness and movement control
- Emotional range and character immersion capability
- Script analysis and character development skills
- Resilience and adaptability in performance environments

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Director
- Collaborates with: Director, Casting Director, Fellow Actors, Stage Manager, Voice Coach, Movement Coach
