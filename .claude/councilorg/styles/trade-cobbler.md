# trade-cobbler

**Summary:** Repairs and restores shoes, boots, and leather goods for retail and individual customers.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Replaces soles and heels on shoes and boots
- Repairs torn seams, loose stitching, and damaged uppers
- Stretches shoes for improved fit and comfort
- Conditions and polishes leather to restore appearance
- Repairs belts, handbags, luggage, and other leather accessories

Core competencies:
- Skill with stitching machines, lasts, and shoe repair tools
- Knowledge of leather types, treatments, and adhesives
- Understanding of shoe construction and anatomy
- Fine hand-eye coordination for precise repair work
- Customer service for repair estimates and turnaround

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Shop Manager or Business Owner
- Collaborates with: Retail Customers, Shoe Retailers, and Leather Goods Manufacturers
