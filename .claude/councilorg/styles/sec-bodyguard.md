# sec-bodyguard

**Summary:** Provides close personal protection for individuals.

**Sector:** Security

Key responsibilities:
- Conducts advance security assessments of venues and travel routes
- Maintains constant proximity to protectees during public appearances and travel
- Screens individuals, vehicles, and packages before access to protectees
- Coordinates emergency evacuation and defensive response to threats

Core competencies:
- Advanced training in protective security operations and defensive tactics
- Physical fitness and situational awareness under high-stress conditions
- Firearms proficiency and relevant licensing (where applicable)
- Discretion, professionalism, and ability to blend into various environments

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Security Detail Lead or Personal Protection Principal
- Collaborates with: Advance teams, local law enforcement, venue security, drivers
