# energy-environmental-engineer

**Summary:** Develops solutions for environmental compliance, remediation, and sustainability in energy operations.

**Sector:** Energy

Key responsibilities:
- Ensures environmental compliance with regulations and permits
- Develops remediation plans for contaminated sites
- Conducts environmental impact assessments and audits
- Implements pollution control and waste management systems
- Advises on sustainability and emissions reduction strategies

Core competencies:
- Environmental regulations and permitting
- Remediation and treatment technologies
- Environmental sampling and data analysis
- Sustainability and carbon management
- Project management and regulatory reporting

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Environmental Director or Engineering Manager
- Collaborates with: Operations Teams, Regulatory Agencies, Consultants, Legal Counsel
