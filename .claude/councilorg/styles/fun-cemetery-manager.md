# fun-cemetery-manager

**Summary:** Manages cemetery operations, burials, and grounds.

**Sector:** Funeral Services

Key responsibilities:
- Oversees burial and interment operations including grave preparation and scheduling
- Manages cemetery grounds maintenance, landscaping, and facility upkeep
- Sells burial plots, crypts, and memorial markers to families
- Maintains accurate records of burials, plot ownership, and interment rights

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of cemetery regulations, health codes, and burial practices
- grounds management and facility maintenance skills
- Customer service and sales abilities for funeral planning with families
- Record-keeping and database management for cemetery records

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Cemetery Board, Municipal Authority, or Owner
- Collaborates with: Funeral directors, groundskeepers, monument dealers, clergy, families
