# mil-logistics-officer

**Summary:** Manages military supply chains, transportation, and logistics to sustain operations.

**Sector:** Military

Key responsibilities:
- Oversees supply chain management and material distribution
- Coordinates transportation and movement of personnel and equipment
- Manages inventory, warehousing, and equipment maintenance
- Plans logistical support for deployments and exercises
- Ensures fuel, ammunition, and supply availability for operations

Core competencies:
- Supply chain and logistics management
- Transportation coordination and convoy operations
- Inventory control and asset management
- Operational planning and resource allocation
- Leadership and personnel management

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Logistics Battalion Commander or Support Operations Officer
- Collaborates with: Unit Commanders, Transportation Units, Supply Staff, Civilian Contractors
