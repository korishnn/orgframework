# re-appraiser

**Summary:** Determines property values for mortgages, sales, and tax purposes.

**Sector:** Real Estate

Key responsibilities:
- Conducts property inspections to assess condition, features, and improvements
- Analyzes comparable sales, market data, and valuation trends
- Prepares detailed appraisal reports using standard methodologies
- Supports valuations for mortgage lending, tax assessment, and estate planning

Core competencies:
- State certified or licensed appraiser credential
- Knowledge of appraisal methods (sales comparison, cost, income approaches)
- Strong analytical and mathematical skills
- Familiarity with Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice (USPAP)

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Appraisal Department Manager or operates independently
- Collaborates with: Mortgage lenders, real estate agents, property owners, tax assessors
