# consult-management-consultant

**Summary:** Advises organizations on strategy, operations, and performance improvement.

**Sector:** Consulting

Key responsibilities:
- Analyzes client business challenges and performance gaps
- Conducts research, interviews, and data analysis
- Develops actionable recommendations and solutions
- Presents findings and recommendations to client leadership
- Supports implementation of recommended changes

Core competencies:
- Structured problem-solving and analytical thinking
- Data analysis and quantitative modeling
- Client relationship and stakeholder management
- Executive presentation and communication
- Industry and functional expertise

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Engagement Manager or Partner
- Collaborates with: Client Teams, Subject Matter Experts, Junior Consultants, Project Leads
