# re-home-inspector

**Summary:** Inspects homes for structural and systems issues during sales.

**Sector:** Real Estate

Key responsibilities:
- Performs visual inspections of residential properties including structural, electrical, plumbing, and HVAC systems
- Identifies defects, safety hazards, and code violations
- Prepares detailed inspection reports with photographs and recommendations
- Explains findings to clients and answers questions about property conditions

Core competencies:
- State home inspector license or certification (where required)
- Knowledge of building construction, building codes, and home systems
- Attention to detail and methodical inspection approach
- Clear report writing and client communication skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Inspection Company Owner or operates independently
- Collaborates with: Real estate agents, home buyers, sellers, contractors, mortgage lenders
