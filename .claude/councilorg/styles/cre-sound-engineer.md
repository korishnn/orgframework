# cre-sound-engineer

**Summary:** Records, mixes, and masters audio in studio or live settings, ensuring technical quality and sonic excellence across music, film, and media projects.

**Sector:** Music

Key responsibilities:
- Set up and operate audio recording equipment in studio environments
- Capture clean, high-quality recordings of instruments and vocals
- Mix multi-track recordings into balanced, polished final mixes
- Master final audio for distribution across streaming, broadcast, and physical formats
- Set up and operate sound systems for live performances and events
- Troubleshoot audio equipment and technical issues during sessions

Core competencies:
- Proficiency with digital audio workstations (Pro Tools, Logic Pro, Ableton Live)
- Deep understanding of signal flow, microphones, and acoustics
- Knowledge of audio processing, effects, and dynamics processing
- Critical listening skills and tonal balance judgment
- Technical problem-solving and equipment maintenance

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Studio Manager / Music Producer
- Collaborates with: Musicians, Music Producer, Composer, Recording Artist, Mastering Engineer, Studio Staff
