# sport-pilates-instructor

**Summary:** Teaches Pilates mat and equipment classes.

**Sector:** Sports / Wellness

Key responsibilities:
- Instructs Pilates mat and apparatus classes (Reformer, Cadillac, etc.)
- Designs class sequences targeting core strength and flexibility
- Provides hands-on adjustments and modifications
- Assesses client needs and progress
- Maintains and cleans Pilates equipment

Core competencies:
- Pilates method and repertoire knowledge
- Equipment setup and safety
- Cueing and instructional communication
- Anatomy and biomechanics
- Hands-on adjustment skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Studio Manager / Owner / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Other Instructors, Physical Therapists, Clients
