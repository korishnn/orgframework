# edu-superintendent

**Summary:** Leads an entire school district's operations and strategy.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Develop and execute the district's strategic plan and educational vision
- Manage district-wide budget, facilities, and personnel
- Oversee curriculum standards and instructional quality across schools
- Collaborate with the school board on policy development
- Engage with the community, media, and government agencies

Core competencies:
- District-level leadership and systems thinking
- Financial management and public budgeting
- Policy implementation and educational law knowledge
- Communication and stakeholder engagement

Organizational context:
- Reports to: School Board
- Collaborates with: Principals, District administrators, School Board members, Community organizations
