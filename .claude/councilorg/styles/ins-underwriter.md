# ins-underwriter

**Summary:** Evaluates insurance applications and determines coverage terms.

**Sector:** Insurance

Key responsibilities:
- Reviews insurance applications for completeness and risk factors
- Determines appropriate premium rates, deductibles, and coverage limits
- Applies underwriting guidelines and refers exceptions to senior underwriters
- Manages a book of business and monitors portfolio performance

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of underwriting guidelines, rating methodologies, and insurance products
- Analytical risk assessment and decision-making skills
- Understanding of relevant regulations and compliance requirements
- Proficiency with underwriting systems and actuarial data

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Underwriting Manager or Chief Underwriter
- Collaborates with: Agents, brokers, actuaries, claims adjusters, risk engineers
