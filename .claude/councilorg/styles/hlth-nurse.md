# hlth-nurse

**Summary:** Provides direct patient care, administers treatments, and coordinates care plans.

**Sector:** Healthcare

Key responsibilities:
- Assess patient health status through physical examination and health history review
- Administer medications, treatments, and interventions as prescribed
- Monitor vital signs, symptoms, and response to treatments
- Develop and implement nursing care plans in collaboration with the healthcare team
- Educate patients and families on health conditions, treatments, and self-care
- Maintain accurate patient records and document care delivered
- Advocate for patient needs and preferences within the care team

Core competencies:
- Clinical assessment and vital sign monitoring
- Medication administration and dosage calculation
- Patient education and health promotion
- Wound care and basic life support
- Care coordination and discharge planning
- Empathy, communication, and patient advocacy

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Nurse Manager / Director of Nursing
- Collaborates with: Physicians, nurse practitioners, allied health professionals, patients and families
