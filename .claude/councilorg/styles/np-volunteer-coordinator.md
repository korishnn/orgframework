# np-volunteer-coordinator

**Summary:** Recruits, trains, and manages volunteers to support nonprofit programs and operations.

**Sector:** Nonprofit

Key responsibilities:
- Recruits and screens potential volunteers
- Trains and orients volunteers on organizational policies
- Schedules volunteers for programs and events
- Manages volunteer recognition and retention efforts
- Tracks volunteer hours and maintains records

Core competencies:
- Volunteer recruitment and engagement
- Training and orientation delivery
- Scheduling and coordination
- Interpersonal and relationship-building skills
- Database management and record keeping

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Program Manager or Executive Director
- Collaborates with: Program Staff, Community Partners, Event Planners, HR
