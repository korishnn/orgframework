# cre-art-critic

**Summary:** Analyzes and evaluates art for publications and media, providing informed commentary on artistic merit, cultural significance, and market trends.

**Sector:** Museums & Galleries

Key responsibilities:
- Attend exhibitions, gallery openings, and art events for review
- Write critical analysis and reviews of artworks and exhibitions
- Research art historical context and contemporary discourse
- Develop informed perspectives on artistic movements and trends
- Interview artists, curators, and industry figures
- Publish criticism across print, digital, and broadcast media

Core competencies:
- Deep knowledge of art history, theory, and contemporary practice
- Strong analytical and critical thinking abilities
- Excellent writing skills with distinctive critical voice
- Ability to contextualize work within broader cultural conversations
- Ethical judgment and independence in critical evaluation

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Editor / Editorial Director
- Collaborates with: Curator, Journalist, Editor, Artist, Gallery Director, Publisher
