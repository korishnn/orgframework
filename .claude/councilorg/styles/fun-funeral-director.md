# fun-funeral-director

**Summary:** Manages funeral arrangements and supports grieving families.

**Sector:** Funeral Services

Key responsibilities:
- Coordinates all aspects of funeral, memorial, and burial services
- Counsels grieving families on options and helps plan personalized services
- Manages logistics including transportation, permits, death certificates, and obituaries
- Supervises funeral home staff and ensures regulatory compliance

Core competencies:
- State funeral director license (where required)
- Empathy, compassion, and strong interpersonal skills with bereaved families
- Organizational ability to coordinate multiple concurrent service details
- Knowledge of funeral service regulations, health codes, and industry practices

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Funeral Home Owner or Regional Manager
- Collaborates with: Morticians, cemeteries, clergy, florists, grief counselors, transport services
