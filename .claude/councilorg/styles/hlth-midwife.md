# hlth-midwife

**Summary:** Provides prenatal, birth, and postpartum care for women.

**Sector:** Alternative Medicine

Key responsibilities:
- Provide comprehensive prenatal care including examinations and screenings
- Attend labor and delivery, managing natural childbirth processes
- Monitor maternal and fetal wellbeing during pregnancy and birth
- Provide postpartum care for mother and newborn
- Offer breastfeeding support and newborn care education
- Counsel women on reproductive health, family planning, and wellness
- Recognize complications and coordinate transfer to obstetric care when needed

Core competencies:
- Prenatal assessment and pregnancy monitoring
- Labor support and natural childbirth management
- Newborn assessment and immediate postpartum care
- Breastfeeding counseling and lactation support
- Women's health and reproductive education
- Emergency recognition and obstetric triage

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Midwifery Practice Director / Lead Midwife
- Collaborates with: Obstetricians, nurses, doulas, lactation consultants, pediatricians, prenatal educators
