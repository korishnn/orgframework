# legal-bankruptcy-attorney

**Summary:** Represents debtors or creditors in bankruptcy proceedings.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Advise clients on bankruptcy options including Chapter 7, 11, and 13 filings
- Prepare and file petitions, schedules, statements of affairs, and reorganization plans
- Represent clients at meetings of creditors, confirmation hearings, and adversary proceedings
- Negotiate with trustees, creditors, and counterparties on repayment terms and asset disposition

Core competencies:
- Comprehensive knowledge of the Bankruptcy Code, Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure, and local rules
- Financial analysis skills to assess debt structures, asset valuations, and feasibility of repayment plans
- Negotiation and settlement abilities in high-stakes, multi-party insolvency matters
- Attention to detail in voluminous document preparation and court filing deadlines

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Partner, solo practitioner, or bankruptcy department head
- Collaborates with: Bankruptcy trustees, court clerks, financial advisors, turnaround consultants, and creditor committees
