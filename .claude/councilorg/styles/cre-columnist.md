# cre-columnist

**Summary:** Writes regular opinion pieces or specialized columns expressing perspectives, analysis, and insights on specific topics or beats.

**Sector:** Journalism

Key responsibilities:
- Write regular columns expressing informed opinions and analysis
- Research topics thoroughly to provide credible, well-supported arguments
- Develop a distinctive voice and perspective on assigned topics
- Engage readers and stimulate public discourse on key issues
- Stay current on developments within their area of expertise
- Respond to current events with timely commentary

Core competencies:
- Strong persuasive writing and argumentation skills
- Deep subject matter expertise in their beat or specialty
- Ability to develop and maintain a distinctive authorial voice
- Research and analytical thinking capabilities
- Public engagement and audience building skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Editorial Page Editor / Content Director
- Collaborates with: Editor, Journalist, Fact-Checker, Publisher, Audience Engagement Team
