# re-developer

**Summary:** Manages real estate development projects from concept to completion.

**Sector:** Real Estate

Key responsibilities:
- Identifies and evaluates land acquisition and development opportunities
- Secures financing, entitlements, and regulatory approvals for projects
- Manages project budgets, timelines, and contractor relationships
- Oversees construction, marketing, and sale or lease of completed properties

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of real estate finance, feasibility analysis, and project management
- Understanding of zoning, land use, and environmental regulations
- Negotiation skills for land acquisition, financing, and contractor agreements
- Risk management and problem-solving across complex project lifecycles

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Development Director or company ownership
- Collaborates with: Architects, engineers, contractors, lenders, municipal planners, attorneys
