# hosp-dishwasher

**Summary:** Cleans dishes, utensils, and kitchen equipment.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Washes dishes, glassware, utensils, and kitchen equipment
- Maintains dishwashing station cleanliness and chemical levels
- Assists with basic kitchen prep and restocking
- Properly stores clean items in designated locations
- Breaks down cardboard and disposes of kitchen waste

Core competencies:
- Efficiency and speed in cleaning tasks
- Knowledge of cleaning chemicals and safety
- Physical stamina for standing and repetitive tasks
- Attention to sanitation standards
- Reliability and punctuality

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Sous Chef / Kitchen Manager
- Collaborates with: Line Cooks, Kitchen Staff, Prep Team
