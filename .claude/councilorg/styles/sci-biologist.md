# sci-biologist

**Summary:** Studies living organisms and their ecosystems.

**Sector:** Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Conduct field and laboratory research on organisms and ecosystems
- Collect and analyze biological samples and data
- Study organism behavior, genetics, physiology, and evolution
- Document findings in research papers and technical reports
- Apply findings to conservation, medicine, or agriculture

Core competencies:
- Biological field and laboratory research methods
- Data analysis and statistical modeling
- Scientific writing and documentation
- Knowledge of taxonomy, ecology, and molecular biology

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Principal Investigator / Research Director
- Collaborates with: Ecologists, Conservationists, Research teams, Government agencies
