# consult-organization-design

**Summary:** Designs organizational structures, roles, and operating models for clients.

**Sector:** Consulting

Key responsibilities:
- Analyze client strategy, workflows, and pain points to identify structural misalignments
- Design organization charts, role definitions, reporting lines, and decision rights frameworks
- Develop operating model options with clear trade-offs in cost, speed, and accountability
- Facilitate leadership alignment sessions and support change management during restructuring

Core competencies:
- Deep understanding of organizational theory, spans of control, and governance models
- Stakeholder facilitation and conflict navigation across senior leadership teams
- Analytical rigor in diagnosing inefficiencies and modeling structural alternatives
- Change management expertise to guide adoption of new structures and processes

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Engagement manager, partner, or practice lead
- Collaborates with: Client executives, HR business partners, internal communications teams, and other consulting workstreams
