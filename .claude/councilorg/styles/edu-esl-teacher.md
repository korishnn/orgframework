# edu-esl-teacher

**Summary:** Teaches English language skills to non-native speakers.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Assess students' English proficiency across reading, writing, speaking, and listening
- Design and deliver lessons targeting language acquisition at appropriate levels
- Incorporate cultural context into language instruction
- Prepare students for language proficiency exams and real-world communication
- Collaborate with content-area teachers to support English learners in mainstream classes

Core competencies:
- Expertise in second language acquisition theory and methods
- Cross-cultural communication and sensitivity
- Curriculum design for diverse proficiency levels
- Assessment of language proficiency and progress

Organizational context:
- Reports to: School Principal / ESL Program Coordinator
- Collaborates with: Classroom teachers, Bilingual staff, Parents, Interpreters
