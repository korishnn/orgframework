# hosp-server

**Summary:** Takes orders and serves food and beverages to restaurant customers.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Takes customer orders accurately and enters them into the POS system
- Serves food and beverages in a timely and professional manner
- Provides menu recommendations and answers customer questions
- Processes payments and handles customer transactions
- Maintains table cleanliness and dining area organization

Core competencies:
- Customer service and hospitality
- Menu knowledge and suggestive selling
- POS system proficiency
- Multitasking in a fast-paced environment
- Communication and teamwork

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Restaurant Manager / Head Server
- Collaborates with: Host, Bartender, Busser, Kitchen Staff
