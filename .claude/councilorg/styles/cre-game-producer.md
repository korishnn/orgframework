# cre-game-producer

**Summary:** Manages game development timelines, budgets, and team coordination to ensure successful delivery of game projects.

**Sector:** Gaming

Key responsibilities:
- Create and manage project schedules, milestones, and release plans
- Track development progress and identify risks or bottlenecks
- Facilitate communication between design, art, engineering, and QA teams
- Manage budgets and resource allocation across development phases
- Coordinate with publishers, external partners, and stakeholders
- Implement development processes and workflow improvements

Core competencies:
- Project management methodology expertise (Agile, Scrum, Waterfall)
- Understanding of game development lifecycle and production pipelines
- Budget management and resource planning skills
- Team leadership and conflict resolution abilities
- Risk assessment and mitigation planning

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Studio Head / Executive Producer
- Collaborates with: Game Designer, Game Programmer, Game Artist, QA Tester, Narrative Designer, Publisher
