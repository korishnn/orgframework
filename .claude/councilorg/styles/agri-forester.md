# agri-forester

**Summary:** Manages forestlands for timber production, conservation, and sustainable resource use.

**Sector:** Agriculture

Key responsibilities:
- Develops and implements forest management plans
- Oversees timber harvesting and reforestation operations
- Conducts forest inventories and resource assessments
- Manages wildlife habitat and conservation programs
- Prevents and controls forest fires, pests, and diseases

Core competencies:
- Silviculture and forest ecology knowledge
- Timber valuation and harvesting methods
- GIS and forest mapping technologies
- Conservation and natural resource management
- Regulatory compliance and permitting

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Forest Manager or Conservation Director
- Collaborates with: Logging Contractors, Wildlife Biologists, Landowners, Government Agencies
