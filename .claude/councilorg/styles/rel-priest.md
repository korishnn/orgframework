# rel-priest

**Summary:** Performs sacraments and leads worship in Catholic and Orthodox traditions.

**Sector:** Religion / Spirituality

Key responsibilities:
- Celebrates the Eucharist and other sacraments
- Leads liturgical worship according to tradition
- Provides spiritual direction and confession ministry
- Visits the sick and administers last rites
- Guides parish administration and faith formation

Core competencies:
- Liturgical and sacramental theology
- Scripture and church tradition knowledge
- Spiritual counseling and direction
- Homiletics and preaching
- Pastoral leadership and administration

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Bishop / Religious Superior
- Collaborates with: Deacons, Religious Sisters/Brothers, Parish Staff
