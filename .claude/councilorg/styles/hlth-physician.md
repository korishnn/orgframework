# hlth-physician

**Summary:** Diagnoses and treats medical conditions, manages patient care across settings.

**Sector:** Healthcare

Key responsibilities:
- Conduct comprehensive patient evaluations including history, physical examination, and diagnostic interpretation
- Diagnose acute and chronic medical conditions and develop evidence-based treatment plans
- Prescribe medications, therapies, and interventions in accordance with clinical guidelines
- Perform medical procedures within scope of practice and specialty
- Monitor patient progress and adjust treatment plans based on clinical response
- Coordinate care with specialists, nurses, and allied health professionals
- Maintain accurate and timely medical documentation in electronic health records

Core competencies:
- Clinical diagnostic reasoning and differential diagnosis
- Evidence-based medicine and treatment planning
- Patient communication and shared decision-making
- Procedural skills relevant to specialty
- Interprofessional collaboration and care coordination
- Medical informatics and health records management

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief of Medical Staff / Department Chair
- Collaborates with: Nursing staff, specialists, pharmacists, allied health professionals, hospital administration
