# fun-embalmer

**Summary:** Preserves deceased bodies through embalming techniques.

**Sector:** Funeral Services

Key responsibilities:
- Performs arterial embalming and cavity treatment for body preservation
- Restores natural appearance through cosmetic application and restorative techniques
- Prepares bodies for viewing, shipping, or identification purposes
- Maintains embalming equipment, chemicals, and sanitary conditions in the preparation room

Core competencies:
- State embalmer license and mortuary science education
- Knowledge of anatomy, chemistry of embalming fluids, and preservation techniques
- Manual dexterity and attention to detail in restorative work
- Understanding of OSHA standards and infection control protocols

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Funeral Director or Mortuary Manager
- Collaborates with: Morticians, funeral directors, crematory operators, medical examiners
