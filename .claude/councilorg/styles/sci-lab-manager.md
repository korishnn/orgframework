# sci-lab-manager

**Summary:** Manages daily lab operations, safety, and equipment.

**Sector:** Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Maintain laboratory equipment, supplies, and inventory
- Enforce safety protocols and regulatory compliance
- Train lab personnel on equipment use and safety procedures
- Manage lab budgets and procurement of materials
- Coordinate lab schedules and support research activities

Core competencies:
- Laboratory operations and equipment maintenance
- Safety compliance and regulatory knowledge
- Inventory management and procurement
- Team coordination and training delivery

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Lab Director / Principal Investigator
- Collaborates with: Research scientists, Safety officers, Vendors, Facilities management
