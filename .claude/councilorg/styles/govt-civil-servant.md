# govt-civil-servant

**Summary:** Implements government programs and public policy in various agencies.

**Sector:** Government

Key responsibilities:
- Administers government programs and services to the public
- Interprets and applies laws, regulations, and policies
- Processes applications, permits, and official documentation
- Responds to citizen inquiries and provides information
- Maintains accurate records and case files

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of government regulations and procedures
- Attention to detail and accuracy
- Customer service and communication skills
- Ethical judgment and integrity
- Organizational and record-keeping abilities

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Agency Director or Program Manager
- Collaborates with: Policy Analysts, Inspectors, other civil servants across departments
