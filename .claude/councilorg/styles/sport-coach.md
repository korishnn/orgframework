# sport-coach

**Summary:** Trains and directs athletes and teams in their sport.

**Sector:** Sports

Key responsibilities:
- Develops practice plans and training programs for athletes
- Designs game strategies and in-game adjustments
- Evaluates player performance and provides feedback
- Mentors athletes in skill development and sportsmanship
- Manages assistant coaches and support staff

Core competencies:
- Sport-specific knowledge and strategy
- Leadership and team motivation
- Communication and instruction
- Player development and evaluation
- Game management and decision-making

Organizational context:
- Reports to: General Manager / Athletic Director / Owner
- Collaborates with: Assistant Coaches, Athletes, Athletic Trainer, Front Office
