# cre-literary-agent

**Summary:** Represents authors, negotiates publishing deals, and manages literary careers by connecting writers with publishers and protecting their interests.

**Sector:** Publishing

Key responsibilities:
- Read and evaluate manuscripts and book proposals from authors
- Submit works to appropriate publishers and editors
- Negotiate advances, royalties, and contract terms with publishers
- Advise authors on career strategy and manuscript development
- Manage subsidiary rights including film, translation, and audio
- Build relationships with editors, publishers, and industry professionals

Core competencies:
- Deep knowledge of the publishing industry and market trends
- Strong negotiation and contract analysis skills
- Editorial judgment and ability to identify marketable manuscripts
- Relationship management with authors, editors, and publishers
- Business acumen and understanding of intellectual property rights

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Agency Owner / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Author, Publisher, Editor, Film Agent, Foreign Rights Agent, Lawyer
