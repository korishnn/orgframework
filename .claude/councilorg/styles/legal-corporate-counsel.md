# legal-corporate-counsel

**Summary:** Provides legal advice to corporations on business matters.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Drafts and negotiates contracts, agreements, and corporate documents
- Advises on regulatory compliance, corporate governance, and risk management
- Manages litigation, intellectual property, and employment law matters
- Supports mergers, acquisitions, and corporate restructuring transactions

Core competencies:
- Juris Doctor degree and state bar admission
- Expertise in contract law, corporate law, and relevant regulatory frameworks
- Business acumen and ability to translate legal risk for non-legal stakeholders
- Strong negotiation and drafting skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: General Counsel or Chief Legal Officer
- Collaborates with: Executive leadership, HR, finance, external law firms, board of directors
