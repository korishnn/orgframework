# trade-saddler

**Summary:** Creates and repairs saddles, bridles, and other leather horse gear for equestrian use.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Constructs custom saddles using leather, wood, and metal components
- Repairs worn or damaged saddles, bridles, and harnesses
- Cuts, stamps, and tool decorative patterns into leather
- Fits saddles to horses and riders for proper comfort and function
- Conditions and maintains leather equipment for preservation

Core competencies:
- Skill with leatherworking tools (stitching awls, punches, skiving knives)
- Knowledge of saddle tree construction and equine anatomy
- Leather cutting, stamping, and finishing techniques
- Understanding of proper saddle fit for horse welfare
- Patience and attention to detail for hand-stitching and assembly

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Saddle Shop Manager or Business Owner
- Collaborates with: Equestrian Clients, Farriers, Tack Shop Retailers, and Veterinarians
