# sls-partner

**Summary:** Partner Manager develops, manages, and grows strategic channel partnerships, reseller relationships, and technology alliances to expand market reach, drive joint revenue, and build ecosystem value.

**Level:** Manager
**Reports to:** Sales Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Identify, recruit, onboard, and enable channel partners, resellers, and strategic alliance partners
- Develop and execute partner programs, enablement plans, and joint business plans (JBPs) for key partners
- Manage partner relationships as the primary point of contact, ensuring partner satisfaction and engagement
- Drive co-selling and co-marketing initiatives with partners including joint pipeline generation and campaigns
- Track partner performance, pipeline contributions, sourced and influenced revenue, and goal attainment
- Negotiate partnership agreements, commercial terms, and manage contract compliance and renewals
- Align internal teams including sales, marketing, product, and legal around partner strategy and execution
- Build and maintain partner training and certification programs for technical and sales readiness
- Develop partner ROI models and business cases for partnership investment decisions
- Manage partner conflict resolution and escalation management for partner-related issues
- Represent the company at partner events, industry conferences, and ecosystem meetings
- Report on partner ecosystem performance, trends, and strategic recommendations to leadership

## Core Competencies
- Strong relationship management, negotiation, and influence skills without direct authority
- Understanding of channel sales models, partner economics, and indirect revenue mechanics
- Experience with partner relationship management (PRM) tools and channel management platforms
- Ability to develop and execute joint business plans with measurable outcomes
- Cross-functional collaboration and stakeholder management across internal teams
- Business development and strategic thinking for identifying and cultivating partnership opportunities
- Understanding of co-selling, co-marketing, and channel program design
- Data-driven approach to partner performance management and ecosystem ROI analysis
- Knowledge of partnership contract terms, agreements, and legal considerations
- Executive presence for partner executive relationship management

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Partner-sourced and partner-influenced revenue (bookings and pipeline)
- Number of active, productive partners and partner recruitment velocity
- Partner pipeline contribution (influence on sourced pipeline)
- Partner enablement completion rates and certification attainment
- Partner NPS and satisfaction survey scores
- Co-sell motion adoption and co-sell deal win rates
- Partner program ROI and cost per dollar of partner-influenced revenue

## Career Progression
- Partner Coordinator (0-2 years): Supports partner operations, manages partner data, coordinates enablement
- Partner Manager (2-4 years): Manages partner portfolio, executes JBPs, drives joint pipeline
- Senior Partner Manager (4-7 years): Strategic partner relationships, complex alliances, program design
- Director (7-10 years): Partner ecosystem strategy, team management, organizational partner vision
- VP (10+ years): Executive ecosystem leadership, corporate development, M&A partnership strategy
- Typical progression: Coordinator to Manager 2-3 years, Manager to Senior 2-3 years, Senior to Director 2-4 years

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Partner check-ins and status calls with key partners in the portfolio
- Joint pipeline reviews with partner sales teams and internal sales counterparts
- Partner enablement session delivery for new product releases and program updates
- Co-marketing campaign coordination and joint content development with partners
- Internal alignment meetings with sales, marketing, and legal on partner initiatives
- Partner pipeline tracking and performance reporting for management visibility
- Deal registration management and partner opportunity tracking in PRM system
- New partner sourcing, evaluation, and onboarding process management
- Partner conflict resolution and deal registration dispute management

## Regional Nuances
- NA: Mature partner ecosystem with well-established channel programs; large VAR and SI partner networks; technology alliance partnerships common; partner program standardization
- EMEA: Complex multi-country partner management; different partner types by market (VARs, SIs, distributors); language and cultural considerations; longer partner development cycles
- APAC: Partner-heavy sales motion in many countries (Japan, Korea, China); relationship-driven partnerships; distributor model common; government relationship partners in regulated industries
- LATAM: Partner ecosystem essential for market coverage; distributor models common; relationship-intensive partner management; smaller partner landscape with long-standing relationships

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: ISV and marketplace partnerships; referral and affiliate programs; technology alliance partnerships; cloud marketplace (AWS, GCP, Azure) partnerships; SI implementation partnerships
- Fintech: Banking and financial institution partnerships; fintech ecosystem alliances; regulatory partnership considerations; embedded finance partnership models; white-label partnership opportunities
- Healthcare: EMR integration partnerships (Epic, Cerner); provider alliance partnerships; distribution partnerships for medical products; health system GPO partnerships
- Enterprise Software: Global SI partnerships (Accenture, Deloitte); regional VAR networks; OEM and embedded partnerships; hardware-software bundling partnerships

## Common Challenges
- Keeping partners engaged and productive after initial onboarding enthusiasm
- Measuring partner attribution accurately across indirect and influenced revenue
- Balancing partner channel conflicts with direct sales team interests
- Ensuring partners maintain adequate technical and sales readiness
- Managing partner performance across diverse partner types and sizes
- Getting internal cross-functional support for partner initiatives competing with direct priorities

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- Channel conflict and anti-trust regulations
- SOX for partner revenue reporting and compliance
- GDPR for EMEA partner data management
- Anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws (FCPA, UK Bribery Act) for international partnerships
- Export control regulations for cross-border partner relationships
- Partner agreement compliance and contract law
- Reseller agreement regulatory requirements by jurisdiction
