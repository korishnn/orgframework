# cre-author

**Summary:** Writes books, novels, or long-form literary works, developing original content for publication in fiction or nonfiction genres.

**Sector:** Publishing

Key responsibilities:
- Research, outline, and write book-length manuscripts
- Develop characters, plots, themes, and narrative structure for fiction
- Research and organize source material for nonfiction works
- Revise and edit drafts based on feedback from editors and beta readers
- Collaborate with literary agents and publishers on manuscript development
- Participate in book promotions, readings, and author events

Core competencies:
- Strong writing craft including voice, pacing, and narrative technique
- Long-form project management and self-discipline
- Research methodology for fiction or nonfiction works
- Receptiveness to editorial feedback and revision
- Understanding of publishing markets and reader expectations

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Literary Agent / Publisher (self-employed or contracted)
- Collaborates with: Literary Agent, Editor, Publisher, Book Designer, Publicist, Proofreader
