# np-development-director

**Summary:** Leads nonprofit development and major gift fundraising strategy to sustain organizational growth.

**Sector:** Nonprofit

Key responsibilities:
- Develops and executes comprehensive fundraising strategy
- Cultivates and solicits major gifts from high-net-worth donors
- Manages development team and fundraising operations
- Builds relationships with foundations and corporate partners
- Oversees planned giving and capital campaign programs

Core competencies:
- Major gift solicitation and stewardship
- Strategic fundraising planning
- Team leadership and development
- Donor relationship management
- Campaign strategy and execution

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Executive Director or CEO
- Collaborates with: Board of Directors, Grant Writers, Program Directors, Communications Team
