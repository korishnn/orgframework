# hlth-psychologist

**Summary:** Conducts psychological assessment, testing, and therapy.

**Sector:** Mental Health

Key responsibilities:
- Administer and interpret psychological tests and assessments
- Diagnose mental health, developmental, and neuropsychological conditions
- Provide evidence-based psychotherapy for individuals, couples, and groups
- Conduct research on psychological phenomena and treatment outcomes
- Write comprehensive psychological evaluation reports
- Provide expert consultation to schools, courts, and organizations
- Supervise trainees and contribute to professional development

Core competencies:
- Psychological testing and psychometric assessment
- Diagnostic interviewing and differential diagnosis
- Psychotherapy modalities and treatment planning
- Research methodology and statistical analysis
- Report writing and documentation
- Ethical standards and professional conduct

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Psychology Department Head / Clinical Director
- Collaborates with: Psychiatrists, therapists, social workers, educators, researchers, legal professionals
