# tran-ship-captain

**Summary:** Commands vessels, responsible for navigation, crew safety, cargo operations, and regulatory compliance at sea.

**Sector:** Maritime

Key responsibilities:
- Commands bridge operations and navigates vessels safely
- Ensures compliance with maritime laws, COLREGS, and environmental regulations
- Manages crew assignments, training, and watch schedules
- Oversees cargo loading, stowage, and discharge operations
- Responds to emergencies including man-overboard, fire, and abandon-ship situations

Core competencies:
- Master Mariner license or equivalent certification
- Advanced navigation including electronic chart and radar proficiency
- Leadership and crisis management under isolation at sea
- Knowledge of ship stability, cargo operations, and maritime safety
- Communication via GMDSS and vessel traffic services

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Fleet Operations Manager or Shipping Company Management
- Collaborates with: Chief Mate, Marine Engineers, Port Agents, Pilots, and Tug Operators
