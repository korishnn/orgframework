# legal-family-attorney

**Summary:** Handles family law matters including divorce, custody, and adoption.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Draft and file petitions for divorce, custody, child support, spousal support, and adoption
- Negotiate settlement agreements on property division, parenting plans, and financial support
- Represent clients in family court hearings, mediation sessions, and settlement conferences
- Advise clients on prenuptial agreements, postnuptial agreements, and cohabitation contracts

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of family law statutes, child welfare guidelines, and domestic violence protections
- Negotiation and mediation skills for high-emotion, often adversarial family disputes
- Sensitivity to the psychological and financial stresses clients experience during family transitions
- Drafting precision for complex settlement agreements and court orders

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Partner, supervising attorney, or solo practitioner
- Collaborates with: Mediators, child custody evaluators, guardian ad litem, financial planners, and mental health professionals
