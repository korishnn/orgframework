# edu-flight-instructor

**Summary:** Trains student pilots for certification.

**Sector:** Education

Key responsibilities:
- Conduct ground school instruction on aeronautics, regulations, and navigation
- Provide in-flight training on aircraft operation and maneuvers
- Prepare students for written, oral, and practical FAA examinations
- Ensure safety compliance during all flight training activities
- Track student progress and maintain training records

Core competencies:
- Certified flight instructor credentials and advanced piloting skills
- In-depth knowledge of aviation regulations and safety procedures
- Ability to remain calm and instructional in high-stress situations
- Precision in evaluating and correcting student technique

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Chief Flight Instructor / Flight School Director
- Collaborates with: Other flight instructors, Maintenance staff, FAA representatives
