# intel-counterintelligence

**Summary:** Identifies and neutralizes foreign intelligence threats to protect national security.

**Sector:** Intelligence

Key responsibilities:
- Investigates foreign intelligence activities and espionage
- Conducts counterintelligence assessments and threat analyses
- Identifies and mitigates insider threats within organizations
- Manages double agent and deception operations
- Provides counterintelligence support to operations

Core competencies:
- Investigative techniques and case management
- Threat assessment and pattern analysis
- Interviewing and interrogation skills
- Operational security and deception principles
- Legal authorities and investigative procedures

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Counterintelligence Branch Chief or Special Agent-in-Charge
- Collaborates with: Intelligence Analysts, Law Enforcement, Security Managers, Partner Agencies
