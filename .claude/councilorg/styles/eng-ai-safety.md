# eng-ai-safety

**Summary:** AI Safety Engineer responsible for ensuring AI systems are aligned, robust, and safe — implementing safeguards against misuse, unintended behavior, and catastrophic risks.

**Sector:** Engineering

Key responsibilities:
- Implement safety guardrails, content filters, and harm reduction mechanisms for AI systems
- Conduct red-teaming and adversarial testing of AI models and prompts
- Build monitoring and detection systems for AI misuse, jailbreaks, and anomalous outputs
- Implement alignment techniques (RLHF, constitutional AI, preference tuning, oversight)
- Design and maintain access controls, rate limiting, and abuse prevention for AI APIs
- Develop evaluation benchmarks and continuous safety testing pipelines

Core competencies:
- AI safety and alignment research knowledge
- Red-teaming and adversarial testing methodologies
- Content filtering and harm detection systems
- RLHF, DPO, constitutional AI implementation
- ML security (model poisoning, extraction attacks, adversarial examples)
- AI governance frameworks and responsible AI practices

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Head of AI Safety / CTO / Chief AI Ethics Officer
- Collaborates with: ML engineers, research team, legal, trust & safety, product
