# energy-pipeline-operator

**Summary:** Monitors and controls pipeline operations for oil, gas, or liquid transport.

**Sector:** Energy

Key responsibilities:
- Remotely monitor pipeline pressure, flow rates, and product composition from a control center
- Operate valves, pumps, compressors, and metering stations to maintain safe throughput
- Dispatch line breaks, pressure excursions, or leak detection alarms per emergency procedures
- Coordinate batch sequencing, product deliveries, and line fill with shippers and terminals

Core competencies:
- Proficiency with SCADA systems, telemetry, and pipeline control room technology
- Understanding of hydraulic principles, pipeline hydraulics, and product behavior under pressure
- Methodical decision-making during abnormal operations or emergency shutdown scenarios
- Knowledge of PHMSA or equivalent regulatory standards for pipeline integrity and safety

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Pipeline control center supervisor or operations shift manager
- Collaborates with: Field technicians, maintenance planners, integrity engineers, environmental compliance staff, and product shippers
