# hlth-acupuncturist

**Summary:** Applies acupuncture and Traditional Chinese Medicine techniques.

**Sector:** Alternative Medicine

Key responsibilities:
- Assess patients using Traditional Chinese Medicine diagnostic methods including pulse and tongue
- Insert and manipulate acupuncture needles at specific points on the body
- Apply adjunctive therapies such as cupping, moxibustion, and electroacupuncture
- Prescribe Chinese herbal formulas based on individual patterns
- Develop treatment plans for pain, stress, and various health conditions
- Educate patients on lifestyle modifications and self-care practices
- Maintain treatment records and comply with regulatory standards

Core competencies:
- Acupuncture point location and needling technique
- Traditional Chinese Medicine theory and differential diagnosis
- Chinese herbal medicine knowledge
- Cupping, moxibustion, and TCM adjunctive therapies
- Understanding of meridian and channel systems
- Patient assessment and treatment planning

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Clinic Director / Practice Owner
- Collaborates with: Medical doctors, chiropractors, massage therapists, naturopaths, physical therapists
