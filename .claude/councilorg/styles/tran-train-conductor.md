# tran-train-conductor

**Summary:** Coordinates train crew operations, passenger safety, and adherence to schedules in freight or passenger rail service.

**Sector:** Transportation

Key responsibilities:
- Coordinates train movements and crew activities during trips
- Ensures compliance with railroad rules, signals, and safety procedures
- Communicates with engineers, dispatchers, and yard personnel
- Assists passengers with boarding, ticketing, and onboard needs
- Inspects train consists, air brakes, and safety equipment

Core competencies:
- Railroad operating rules and signal system knowledge
- Leadership and crew management skills
- Communication and radio protocol proficiency
- Safety-first mindset and situational awareness
- Customer service orientation for passenger rail roles

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Trainmaster or Terminal Superintendent
- Collaborates with: Train Engineers, Dispatchers, Yard Masters, and Maintenance Crews
