# tran-customs-broker

**Summary:** Facilitates customs clearance for international shipments by preparing documentation and ensuring regulatory compliance.

**Sector:** Transportation

Key responsibilities:
- Prepares and files customs entry documents for imported and exported goods
- Classifies products using harmonized tariff schedule codes
- Calculates and arranges payment of duties, taxes, and fees
- Communicates with customs officials to resolve clearance issues
- Advises clients on trade regulations, free trade agreements, and compliance

Core competencies:
- Customs broker license and in-depth knowledge of customs regulations
- Harmonized system (HS) tariff classification expertise
- Understanding of import/export compliance and trade agreements
- Attention to detail for accurate documentation and classifications
- Client advisory and regulatory interpretation skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Customs Brokerage Manager or Trade Compliance Director
- Collaborates with: Importers/Exporters, Freight Forwarders, Customs Officials, and Logistics Coordinators
