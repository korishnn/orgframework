# trade-waste-management

**Summary:** Collects and processes waste and recycling materials from residential, commercial, and industrial locations.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Operates refuse collection vehicles on scheduled routes
- Loads waste and recycling bins into trucks using manual or automated systems
- Sort materials at transfer stations or processing facilities
- Maintains collection equipment and reports vehicle issues
- Follows safety procedures for hazardous waste handling

Core competencies:
- CDL license for operating heavy collection vehicles
- Knowledge of waste sorting, recycling streams, and disposal regulations
- Physical stamina for lifting and repetitive loading
- Route navigation and time management skills
- Safety awareness for traffic, equipment, and material hazards

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Operations Supervisor or Route Manager
- Collaborates with: Dispatchers, Landfill/Transfer Station Staff, Recycling Sorters, and Municipal Clients
