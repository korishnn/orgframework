# np-social-entrepreneur

**Summary:** Builds ventures that address social problems through market-based approaches and innovation.

**Sector:** Nonprofit

Key responsibilities:
- Identifies social problems and develops venture solutions
- Builds sustainable business models for social impact
- Secures funding from impact investors and donors
- Scales programs and ventures for broader reach
- Measures and communicates social and financial returns

Core competencies:
- Entrepreneurial mindset and business development
- Social impact measurement and reporting
- Fundraising and impact investing knowledge
- Innovation and creative problem-solving
- Leadership and team building

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Board of Directors or Founder
- Collaborates with: Impact Investors, Program Staff, Community Partners, Business Advisors
