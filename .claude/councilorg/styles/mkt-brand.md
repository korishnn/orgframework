# mkt-brand

**Summary:** Brand Manager defines, evolves, and protects the company's brand identity, messaging, and positioning to differentiate the organization in the market and build long-term brand equity.

**Level:** Manager
**Reports to:** CMO
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Develop and evolve comprehensive brand strategy, positioning, messaging framework, and brand architecture
- Create, maintain, and enforce brand guidelines ensuring consistent brand expression across all touchpoints
- Lead brand campaigns and go-to-market branding initiatives for product launches and company milestones
- Conduct brand audits, market research, and competitive analysis to inform brand strategy decisions
- Manage brand architecture including sub-brands, product naming, and portfolio branding strategy
- Partner with design teams on visual identity evolution, brand asset creation, and design system integration
- Educate the organization on brand standards, governance, and proper brand usage through training and documentation
- Measure brand health through awareness, consideration, perception, and net promoter score tracking
- Manage brand budget and external agency relationships for brand projects and campaigns
- Ensure legal compliance for trademark usage, brand protection, and intellectual property
- Lead brand positioning and messaging for new market entry or product category expansion
- Develop and present brand strategy to executive leadership with business rationale and ROI

## Core Competencies
- Strategic brand management expertise including positioning, architecture, and portfolio strategy
- Strong creative judgment, visual literacy, and storytelling ability across media
- Understanding of market research methodologies including brand tracking, perception studies, and segmentation
- Experience developing and maintaining brand guidelines and design systems at scale
- Excellent cross-functional collaboration, stakeholder management, and executive presentation skills
- Knowledge of trademark law, brand protection, and intellectual property fundamentals
- Understanding of brand measurement frameworks (brand equity, awareness, consideration, preference)
- Experience managing external creative agencies and production partners
- Ability to balance creative ambition with business strategy and measurable outcomes
- Understanding of digital brand experience including web, social, and product brand expression

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Brand awareness (aided and unaided) and brand consideration metrics
- Brand net promoter score (NPS) and brand perception tracking
- Share of voice in relevant market categories
- Brand guideline compliance and consistency scores across touchpoints
- Customer acquisition cost trends relative to brand investment
- Brand-driven traffic and engagement metrics
- Employee brand engagement and internal brand understanding
- Trademark portfolio health and brand protection metrics

## Career Progression
- Associate (0-2 years): Supports brand projects, manages asset libraries, coordinates with agencies
- Brand Manager (2-5 years): Owns brand initiatives, manages campaigns, maintains guidelines
- Senior (5-8 years): Leads brand strategy for major initiatives, manages team members, agency relationships
- Director (8-12 years): Defines brand direction, manages brand team, drives organizational brand maturity
- VP Brand (12+ years): Executive brand leadership, organizational identity, market position strategy
- Typical progression: Associate to Manager 2-3 years, Manager to Senior 2-3 years, Senior to Director 3-5 years

## Regional Nuances
- NA: Highly competitive brand landscape; strong brand differentiation imperative; sophisticated brand measurement; emotion-driven brand marketing dominant
- EMEA: Multi-market brand complexity requiring flexible but consistent positioning; local cultural adaptation critical; stronger regulatory environment for brand claims; heritage and trust-focused brand values
- APAC: Brand localization critical for each market; different brand perceptions and values in each country; emerging middle class brand engagement; digital-first brand building in mobile-heavy markets
- LATAM: Brand loyalty and emotional connection valued; local cultural relevance essential; growing digital brand engagement; strong community-oriented brand positioning

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: Product-led brand positioning; technical audience credibility; thought leadership brand building; category creation brand strategy; community-driven brand loyalty
- Fintech: Trust and security-focused brand positioning; regulatory-sensitive brand messaging; heritage vs. innovation brand tension; transparency as brand value
- Healthcare: Empathy and expertise brand positioning; strict regulatory claims environment; patient trust as brand equity; accessible and inclusive brand expression
- E-commerce: Brand as curation and selection; customer experience as brand differentiator; loyalty program brand integration; price and value brand positioning
- Consumer Goods: Shelf-impact brand design; multi-channel brand experience; emotional brand connection; packaging brand expression

## Common Challenges
- Maintaining brand consistency across rapid product expansion and multiple teams
- Measuring brand equity ROI and connecting brand investment to business outcomes
- Balancing global brand consistency with local market relevance and cultural adaptation
- Protecting brand integrity while enabling speed and autonomy in go-to-market execution
- Navigating brand perception challenges during company pivots or acquisitions
- Getting organizational buy-in for brand governance and consistent standards enforcement

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- Trademark and intellectual property law for brand protection
- FTC truth-in-advertising guidelines for brand claims
- GDPR for brand data collection in market research
- Industry-specific regulations for brand claims (FDA for health, SEC for finance)
- EU Unfair Commercial Practices Directive for European brand claims
- Social media platform brand guidelines for branded content
- Copyright law for brand creative assets and brand content
