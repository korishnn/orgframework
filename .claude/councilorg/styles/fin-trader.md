# fin-trader

**Summary:** Buys and sells financial instruments for firms or clients.

**Sector:** Finance

Key responsibilities:
- Executes trades in equities, fixed income, currencies, commodities, or derivatives
- Manages inventory risk and positions within established risk limits
- Analyzes market trends, order flow, and economic data to identify trading opportunities
- Maintains relationships with brokers, exchanges, and liquidity providers

Core competencies:
- In-depth knowledge of financial markets and trading instruments
- Ability to make rapid decisions under pressure with incomplete information
- Proficiency with electronic trading platforms and algorithmic execution tools
- Strong risk management discipline and regulatory compliance awareness

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Head of Trading or Portfolio Manager
- Collaborates with: Research analysts, sales traders, risk managers, compliance officers, operations
