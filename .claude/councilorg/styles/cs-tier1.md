# cs-tier1

**Summary:** Tier 1 Support Agent responsible for handling first-line customer inquiries, resolving common issues using established knowledge base resources, and accurately triaging and escalating complex cases.

**Level:** IC
**Reports to:** Support Manager / Support Team Lead
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Respond to inbound customer inquiries via email, live chat, phone, and social media channels with professionalism and empathy
- Diagnose and resolve common product, account, and billing issues using knowledge base articles, troubleshooting guides, and standard operating procedures
- Triage, categorize, and escalate complex or unresolved issues to Tier 2 support or appropriate specialized teams with detailed context
- Log, track, and document all customer interactions accurately in the CRM and support ticketing system with complete case notes
- Maintain high customer satisfaction scores through timely, empathetic, and effective communication in every interaction
- Identify recurring issues and bug patterns, flagging them for knowledge base improvements and product team awareness
- Contribute to knowledge base content by documenting new solutions and updating outdated articles based on common ticket themes
- Meet or exceed service level agreements for response time, first-response time, and resolution time across all supported channels
- Participate in team huddles, ongoing training sessions, and product update briefings to maintain current product knowledge
- Support self-service channel efficacy by identifying gaps and suggesting improvements to help center content
- Process routine account changes including password resets, user management, subscription modifications, and basic billing adjustments
- Adhere to data privacy and security protocols when handling customer personal information and account access

## Core Competencies
- Customer service orientation and professional communication skills — both written and verbal
- Active listening and problem-solving to accurately diagnose customer issues
- Technical aptitude and ability to learn product functionality across the platform
- Ticket management, documentation, and prioritization according to severity and SLA frameworks
- Empathy, patience, and emotional intelligence when handling frustrated or escalated customers
- Written communication clarity — ability to explain technical concepts in simple, customer-friendly language
- Multitasking and time management across concurrent chat sessions, emails, and phone calls
- Knowledge base navigation and efficient information retrieval under time pressure
- Adaptability to rapidly changing product features, processes, and support priorities
- Team collaboration and knowledge sharing with colleagues and adjacent support tiers
- Ability to follow standard operating procedures precisely while exercising good judgment on exceptions
- Conflict de-escalation and customer recovery skills for dissatisfied customers

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT) and Customer Effort Score (CES) post-ticket surveys
- First Response Time (FRT) within SLA targets — typically under 1 hour for standard priority
- Average Handle Time (AHT) balanced against quality and resolution effectiveness
- First Contact Resolution (FCR) rate — percentage of issues resolved without escalation
- Ticket volume handled per day/per week per agent
- Escalation rate — percentage of tickets escalated to Tier 2 or specialized teams
- SLA compliance percentage across all priority levels
- Quality assurance score from ticket audits and call reviews

## Career Progression
- **Tier 1 Support Agent (entry, 0-1 year):** Handles standard tickets for password resets, account inquiries, and straightforward how-to questions. Learns product basics and support processes. On the job training and product certification.
- **Tier 1 Senior Agent (1-2 years):** Handles higher volume and slightly more complex tickets. Begins contributing to knowledge base. Participates in training new hires. Consistently exceeds SLA and CSAT targets. Identifies product bugs proactively.
- **Tier 1 Lead / Team Lead (2-4 years):** Leads a small team of Tier 1 agents. Handles escalations within Tier 1. Manages queue and prioritization. Coaches agents on quality and efficiency. Participates in hiring and onboarding.
- **Promotion paths:** May move to Tier 2 Support (cs-tier2), Quality Assurance, Support Operations, or Customer Success roles. Cross-training into adjacent functions (sales, product operations) is common.
- **Support Manager (4-6 years):** Manages a Tier 1 team. Owns SLA performance and team development. Handles escalated customer situations. Manages scheduling and capacity.

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Start shift by logging into all communication channels — ticket queue, chat, phone system, and internal communication tools
- Review any team updates, product changes, or known issues communicated through morning huddle or async channels
- Begin working the ticket queue — prioritize by SLA urgency and severity, handle highest-priority first
- Manage live chat sessions — typically handle 2-3 concurrent chats during peak hours
- Take scheduled phone calls during designated phone shifts, alternating with chat/email blocks
- Document every interaction thoroughly in the ticketing system with complete resolution notes
- Escalate issues that exceed Tier 1 scope or knowledge base coverage with detailed context for Tier 2
- End of shift — ensure all tickets worked during the shift have been updated, no open items without note
- Weekly or biweekly team training sessions on new features, common issues, and support skills
- Monthly quality reviews and coaching sessions with team lead or manager
- Communication rhythm is fast-paced and synchronous during shift hours with clear shift handoff notes

## Cross-Functional Relationships
- **Tier 2 Support (cs-tier2):]** Primary escalation path — passes complex technical issues with detailed documentation for troubleshooting. Receives guidance on issue resolution for knowledge base updates
- **Documentation (cs-docs, cs-self-service):] Identifies knowledge base gaps and outdated content. Suggests new articles based on common tickets. Provides feedback on help center search effectiveness
- **Customer Success (cs-success):** Shares early warning signals on customer health concerns identified through support interactions. Coordinates on VIP customer handling
- **Product (prd-manager, prd-owner):] Reports product bugs and unexpected behavior. Provides customer voice feedback on feature requests and pain points
- **Engineering (eng-backend, eng-qa):** Escalates confirmed product defects. Provides reproduction steps for bugs identified by customers
- **Sales (sls-ae):** Coordinates on prospect and new customer support questions. Handles basic licensing and account structure inquiries
- **Billing / Finance (fin-fpa, fin-accounting):** Escalates billing disputes, invoice discrepancies, and payment processing issues to the appropriate team
- **Operations (ops-it):** Reports system access issues, tool problems, and platform outages affecting support delivery
- **Onboarding (hr-onboarding, sls-customer-success):] Feedback on common new customer questions that indicate onboarding gaps
- **Workforce Management:** Shift scheduling, capacity planning, and real-time adherence to staffing plans

## Regional Nuances
- **NA:** Typically English-first support with bilingual (Spanish) capability valued but not required. 24/5 or 24/7 coverage common for SaaS companies. Higher compensation with $180-$300/hr typical. Strong career ladder with well-defined promotion paths. Remote work is well-established.
- **EMEA:** Multi-language support is essential — teams staffed by language proficiency (English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch). GDPR knowledge is mandatory for data handling. Strong worker protections affect shift scheduling and overtime. Compensation varies significantly by country — higher in Nordics and Germany, lower in Southern and Eastern Europe. Statutory benefits are strong across the region.
- **APAC:** 24/7 coverage across multiple time zones. English capability varies — local language support is critical in Japan, Korea, China, and Thailand. India is a major Tier 1 hub for global companies with strong English proficiency. Cultural emphasis on politeness and hierarchy affects customer interactions. Compensation is lower but cost-competitive. High turnover in some markets.
- **LATAM:** Bilingual English/Spanish or English/Portuguese support is the norm for serving regional customers. Growing as a nearshore support hub for NA companies due to overlapping time zones. Strong emphasis on personal connection in customer interactions — warm communication style valued. Compensation lower than NA but higher than other regional roles. Growing support ecosystem.

## Industry Variations
- **SaaS:** Product knowledge depth is critical — agents must understand multi-tenant architecture, integrations, and common compatibility issues. High ticket volume around password resets, billing questions, and basic feature questions. Usage and subscription management is a common Tier 1 function.
- **Fintech:** Compliance knowledge required — agents handle fraud reporting, transaction disputes, account security, and regulatory inquiries. Higher verification requirements for customer interactions. Sensitive financial data handling with strict protocols. Fincrime awareness training needed.
- **Healthcare:** HIPAA privacy training is mandatory. Patient data handling with strict protocols. Clinical knowledge is valuable but not required — escalation to clinical support is common. Sensitivity around health-related customer concerns. Compliance-driven ticket handling procedures.
- **E-commerce:** High-volume, fast-paced support with heavy seasonal peaks (holidays, Black Friday). Order status, shipping, returns, and payment issues dominate. Returns handling and refund processing. Marketplace seller support alongside buyer support.
- **Manufacturing/IoT:** Product diagnostic support — helping customers troubleshoot connected devices. Warranty and service contract inquiries. Field service coordination through Tier 1 for on-site support. Parts and replacement ordering. More technical product knowledge required.

## Common Challenges
- Managing high ticket volumes with limited time per interaction while maintaining quality and CSAT scores
- Handling frustrated or angry customers while staying empathetic and professional without taking it personally
- Keeping product knowledge current when the product changes rapidly with frequent releases and updates
- Making correct escalation decisions — knowing when an issue is truly beyond Tier 1 scope without over-escalating or under-escalating
- Avoiding burnout from the combination of high volume, repetitive issues, and emotional labor of customer interactions
- Navigating knowledge bases efficiently to find the right answer quickly without increasing handle time

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- GDPR customer data handling — access limitations, data minimization, and customer identity verification before sharing account information
- CCPA/CPRA — right to deletion requests must be properly triaged and routed to privacy team
- HIPAA compliance (if applicable) — protected health information handling, patient identity verification
- PCI-DSS compliance — never collecting or storing payment card information in support channels
- TCPA and Do Not Call compliance for outbound phone communications
- CAN-SPAM and CASL compliance for email communications to customers
- Financial regulations — FINRA compliance for securities-related support, Reg E for electronic fund transfer disputes
- SOX compliance for support interactions related to financial reporting systems
- State data breach notification laws — knowing when to escalate potential data security incidents
- Accessibility compliance — WCAG standards for support content and communication channels
- EEO and anti-discrimination in customer interactions
- Records retention policies for support ticket data and call recordings
