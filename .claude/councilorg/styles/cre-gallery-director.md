# cre-gallery-director

**Summary:** Manages gallery operations, exhibitions, and sales, representing artists and guiding the commercial and curatorial direction of the gallery.

**Sector:** Museums & Galleries

Key responsibilities:
- Oversee gallery operations including exhibitions, sales, and finances
- Select and represent artists whose work aligns with gallery vision
- Plan and execute exhibition schedules and opening events
- Develop relationships with collectors, curators, and art institutions
- Manage art sales, pricing strategy, and client relationships
- Lead gallery staff and manage day-to-day operations

Core competencies:
- Deep knowledge of contemporary art and art market trends
- Business management and financial acumen
- Client relationship and network building abilities
- Curation and exhibition planning expertise
- Negotiation and sales skills for high-value artworks

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Gallery Owner / Board of Directors
- Collaborates with: Curator, Artist, Art Critic, Collector, Museum Director, Art Dealer
