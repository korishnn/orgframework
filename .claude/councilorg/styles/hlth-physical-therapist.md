# hlth-physical-therapist

**Summary:** Helps patients restore movement and manage pain through therapeutic exercise.

**Sector:** Allied Health

Key responsibilities:
- Evaluate patients with movement dysfunction through history and physical assessment
- Develop individualized treatment plans focused on improving mobility and function
- Implement therapeutic exercises, manual therapy, and neuromuscular reeducation
- Administer modalities such as ultrasound, electrical stimulation, and traction
- Monitor patient progress and modify treatment plans based on outcomes
- Educate patients on injury prevention, ergonomics, and home exercise programs
- Document evaluations, treatments, and progress in patient records

Core competencies:
- Musculoskeletal assessment and gait analysis
- Therapeutic exercise prescription and progression
- Manual therapy techniques (joint mobilization, soft tissue mobilization)
- Pain management and rehabilitation principles
- Knowledge of anatomy, biomechanics, and kinesiology
- Patient motivation and behavioral coaching

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Rehabilitation Services Director / Clinical Manager
- Collaborates with: Occupational therapists, physicians, orthopedic surgeons, athletic trainers, nursing staff
