# cre-photographer

**Summary:** Captures images for commercial, editorial, or artistic purposes, using technical expertise and creative vision to produce compelling photographs.

**Sector:** Visual Arts

Key responsibilities:
- Plan and execute photoshoots for clients, publications, or personal projects
- Select and set up lighting, camera equipment, and backdrops
- Compose and capture images according to creative direction
- Edit and retouch photographs using professional software
- Manage digital asset libraries and client deliverables
- Maintain and care for camera and lighting equipment

Core competencies:
- Advanced photography technique including exposure, composition, and lighting
- Proficiency with photo editing software (Lightroom, Photoshop, Capture One)
- Understanding of color management and image output for various media
- Client relationship management and business development skills
- Creative vision and visual storytelling ability

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Creative Director / Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Art Director, Photo Editor, Graphic Designer, Client, Stylist, Retoucher
