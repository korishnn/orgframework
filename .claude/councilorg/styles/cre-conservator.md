# cre-conservator

**Summary:** Preserves and restores artworks and cultural artifacts, using scientific analysis and specialized techniques to maintain cultural heritage.

**Sector:** Museums & Galleries

Key responsibilities:
- Assess the condition of artworks and artifacts for conservation needs
- Perform cleaning, stabilization, and restoration treatments
- Conduct scientific analysis of materials and degradation processes
- Document conservation treatments with written and photographic records
- Advise on proper storage, handling, and display conditions
- Research conservation techniques and materials for best practices

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of art history, materials science, and chemistry
- Manual dexterity and precision for delicate conservation work
- Understanding of ethical standards in conservation practice
- Documentation and analytical documentation skills
- Problem-solving for complex restoration challenges

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Head Conservator / Museum Director
- Collaborates with: Curator, Exhibition Designer, Registrar, Scientist, Collection Manager
