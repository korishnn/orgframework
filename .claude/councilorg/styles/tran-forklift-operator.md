# tran-forklift-operator

**Summary:** Operates forklifts to move, stack, and organize materials in warehouses, distribution centers, and storage yards.

**Sector:** Transportation

Key responsibilities:
- Operates sit-down and stand-up forklifts to move pallets and materials
- Loads and unloads trucks, containers, and storage racks
- Stacks products in designated locations according to warehouse layout
- Performs pre-operational safety inspections on forklift equipment
- Maintains inventory accuracy through proper put-away and retrieval

Core competencies:
- Forklift operator certification and safe operating practices
- Spatial awareness for maneuvering in tight aisles and loading docks
- Knowledge of load capacity, balance, and stacking principles
- Attention to safety procedures and pedestrian awareness
- Basic inventory system scanning and data entry

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Warehouse Supervisor or Shift Lead
- Collaborates with: Warehouse Workers, Shipping Clerks, Receiving Staff, and Truck Drivers
