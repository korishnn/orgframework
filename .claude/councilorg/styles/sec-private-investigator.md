# sec-private-investigator

**Summary:** Conducts investigations for private clients.

**Sector:** Security

Key responsibilities:
- Conducts surveillance, background checks, and factual investigations
- Gathers evidence through interviews, records research, and field observation
- Prepares detailed investigative reports and case documentation
- Testifies in court or legal proceedings when required

Core competencies:
- State private investigator license (where required)
- Strong research, surveillance, and evidence-gathering skills
- Knowledge of legal procedures and evidence handling requirements
- Discretion, integrity, and ability to maintain client confidentiality

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Investigation Agency Owner or operates independently
- Collaborates with: Attorneys, insurance adjusters, law enforcement, corporate security
