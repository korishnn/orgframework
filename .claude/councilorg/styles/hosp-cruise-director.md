# hosp-cruise-director

**Summary:** Manages onboard entertainment and guest activities on cruises.

**Sector:** Hospitality / Food Service

Key responsibilities:
- Plans and manages onboard entertainment and activity schedules
- Leads the cruise entertainment and events team
- Hosts major events, shows, and announcements
- Ensures guest satisfaction with recreational offerings
- Handles guest feedback and special requests during voyages

Core competencies:
- Event planning and programming
- Public speaking and stage presence
- Team leadership and coordination
- Guest relations and hospitality
- Adaptability to dynamic onboard environment

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Hotel Director / Cruise Manager
- Collaborates with: Entertainment Staff, Activities Team, Guest Services
