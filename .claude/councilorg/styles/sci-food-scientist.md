# sci-food-scientist

**Summary:** Develops food products and improves food processing methods.

**Sector:** Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Research and develop new food products and recipes
- Improve food processing, preservation, and packaging methods
- Ensure products meet safety, quality, and nutritional standards
- Analyze food composition and conduct shelf-life studies
- Collaborate with marketing and production teams on product launches

Core competencies:
- Food chemistry and microbiology knowledge
- Sensory evaluation and product formulation
- Understanding of food safety regulations (FDA, USDA)
- Process optimization and scale-up experience

Organizational context:
- Reports to: R&D Director / Food Science Manager
- Collaborates with: Production teams, Quality assurance, Marketing, Regulatory affairs, Suppliers
