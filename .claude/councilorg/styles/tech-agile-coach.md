# tech-agile-coach

**Summary:** Coaches teams and organizations in Agile methodologies and practices to improve delivery velocity, quality, and culture.

**Sector:** Technology

Key responsibilities:
- Coach cross-functional teams in Scrum, Kanban, and other Agile frameworks
- Facilitate ceremonies: sprint planning, retrospectives, daily stand-ups, and demos
- Identify organizational impediments and work with leadership to remove them
- Mentor product owners and Scrum Masters on Agile best practices

Core competencies:
- Deep expertise in multiple Agile frameworks and scaling models (SAFe, LeSS)
- Facilitation, conflict resolution, and group dynamics
- Metrics-driven improvement using velocity, flow, and cycle-time data
- Change management and organizational transformation skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Director of Agile Practice, VP of Engineering, or CIO
- Collaborates with: Engineering teams, product management, program managers, DevOps, HR
