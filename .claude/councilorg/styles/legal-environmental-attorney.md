# legal-environmental-attorney

**Summary:** Handles environmental regulations, compliance, and litigation.

**Sector:** Legal

Key responsibilities:
- Advises clients on environmental regulatory compliance and permitting
- Represents clients in enforcement actions, citizen suits, and environmental litigation
- Conducts environmental due diligence for real estate and corporate transactions
- Navigates air, water, waste, and hazardous substance regulatory frameworks

Core competencies:
- Juris Doctor degree and state bar admission
- Knowledge of environmental statutes (Clean Air Act, Clean Water Act, CERCLA, RCRA)
- Understanding of scientific and technical environmental concepts
- Experience with regulatory agencies and administrative proceedings

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Environmental Practice Group Lead or Partner
- Collaborates with: Environmental consultants, regulatory agencies, corporate counsel, engineers
