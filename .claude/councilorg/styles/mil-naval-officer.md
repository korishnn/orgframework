# mil-naval-officer

**Summary:** Commands naval vessels and leads maritime operations at sea and ashore.

**Sector:** Military

Key responsibilities:
- Commands naval vessels and leads shipboard personnel
- Plans and executes maritime operations and missions
- Manages navigation, engineering, and weapons systems
- Ensures shipboard readiness, safety, and discipline
- Conducts port visits and international naval engagements

Core competencies:
- Maritime operations and naval tactics
- Ship handling and navigation
- Leadership and command at sea
- Naval engineering and systems knowledge
- International maritime law and protocols

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Senior Naval Officer or Task Force Commander
- Collaborates with: Ship Crew, Naval Aviation, Submarine Forces, Allied Navies
