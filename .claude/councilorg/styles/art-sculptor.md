# art-sculptor

**Summary:** Creates three-dimensional artworks in various media including stone, metal, wood, clay, and mixed materials for exhibition and commission.

**Sector:** Visual Arts

Key responsibilities:
- Create sculptural works using carving, modeling, casting, or assembly techniques
- Develop concepts and maquettes for proposed sculptures
- Select and prepare materials appropriate for each project
- Maintain a studio practice with proper tools, equipment, and safety protocols
- Install sculptures in galleries, public spaces, or site-specific locations
- Manage commissions including client consultation, proposals, and delivery

Core competencies:
- Mastery of sculptural techniques appropriate to chosen media
- Understanding of three-dimensional form, space, and material properties
- Knowledge of tools, equipment, and studio safety practices
- Ability to work with architects, designers, and commissioning clients
- Physical stamina and manual dexterity for working with heavy materials

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Self-employed
- Collaborates with: Gallery Owner, Curator, Architect, Commissioning Client, Fabricator, Installer
