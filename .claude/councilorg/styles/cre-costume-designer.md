# cre-costume-designer

**Summary:** Creates costumes and wardrobe for theatrical, film, and television productions, designing garments that define characters and support the visual narrative.

**Sector:** Theater

Key responsibilities:
- Read scripts and research period, style, and character wardrobe needs
- Design and sketch costume concepts for each character
- Select fabrics, colors, and textures that align with production design
- Oversee costume construction, fitting, and alterations
- Manage dressing and costume changes during performances
- Maintain costume inventory and budgets

Core competencies:
- Strong illustration and design visualization skills
- Knowledge of garment construction, pattern-making, and textiles
- Understanding of historical fashion and costume history
- Collaboration with directors, set designers, and actors
- Budget management and organizational skills for wardrobe departments

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Director / Production Designer
- Collaborates with: Set Designer, Director, Actors, Hair and Makeup Designer, Wardrobe Supervisor, Textile Artist
