# trade-assembly-worker

**Summary:** Assembles products and components on manufacturing production lines according to specifications and quality standards.

**Sector:** Skilled Labor

Key responsibilities:
- Assembles parts and components using hand tools, power tools, and fixtures
- Reads work orders, schematics, and assembly instructions
- Installs wiring, fasteners, seals, and sub-assemblies
- Tests finished assemblies for proper function and fit
- Maintains work area organization and production records

Core competencies:
- Manual dexterity and hand-eye coordination for repetitive tasks
- Ability to follow detailed assembly procedures and diagrams
- Familiarity with hand tools, torque wrenches, and assembly fixtures
- Attention to detail and quality consciousness
- Efficiency at maintaining production pace and deadlines

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Production Supervisor or Assembly Line Lead
- Collaborates with: Quality Inspectors, Material Handlers, and fellow Assembly Workers on the line
