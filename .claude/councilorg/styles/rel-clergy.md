# rel-clergy

**Summary:** Leads religious congregations and performs spiritual duties.

**Sector:** Religion / Spirituality

Key responsibilities:
- Leads worship services and religious ceremonies
- Provides pastoral care, counseling, and spiritual guidance
- Prepares and delivers sermons or teachings
- Administers sacraments or rituals according to tradition
- Oversees congregational administration and community outreach

Core competencies:
- Scripture and theological knowledge
- Preaching and public speaking
- Pastoral counseling and empathy
- Leadership and community building
- Ritual and liturgical expertise

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Denominational Leadership / Board of Trustees
- Collaborates with: Religious Educators, Lay Leaders, Community Organizations
