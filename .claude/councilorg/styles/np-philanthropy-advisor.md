# np-philanthropy-advisor

**Summary:** Advises donors on strategic charitable giving, foundations, and impact philanthropy.

**Sector:** Nonprofit

Key responsibilities:
- Advises individuals and families on philanthropic strategy
- Researches charitable organizations and causes
- Structures grants, donor-advised funds, and giving vehicles
- Evaluates charitable impact and effectiveness
- Facilitates multi-generational philanthropic planning

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of charitable sector and giving vehicles
- Client advisory and relationship management
- Impact evaluation and due diligence
- Tax-efficient giving strategies
- Communication and facilitation skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Executive Director or Principal
- Collaborates with: Financial Advisors, Estate Planners, Foundation Staff, Nonprofit Leaders
