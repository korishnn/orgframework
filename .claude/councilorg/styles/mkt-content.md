# mkt-content

**Summary:** Content Marketer researches, creates, and distributes compelling content across formats and channels to drive brand awareness, engagement, and demand generation.

**Level:** IC
**Reports to:** Marketing Director
**Region variants:** NA, EMEA, APAC, LATAM

## Responsibilities
- Research, write, and edit high-quality content across formats including blog posts, whitepapers, ebooks, case studies, and video scripts
- Develop and maintain a content calendar aligned with marketing campaigns, product launches, and seasonal opportunities
- Produce thought leadership content that establishes brand authority and differentiates the company in competitive markets
- Optimize all content for SEO, readability, audience engagement, and conversion goals
- Collaborate with subject matter experts across product, sales, and engineering for technically accurate content
- Measure content performance through analytics and iterate on content strategy based on data insights
- Maintain consistent brand voice, messaging, and editorial standards across all content touchpoints
- Manage content distribution across owned channels (blog, email, social) and external publications
- Repurpose long-form content into multiple formats (infographics, social posts, videos, podcasts)
- Build and manage relationships with external contributors, guest authors, and freelance writers
- Conduct content competitor analysis to identify gaps and opportunities in the content landscape
- Develop content briefs, editorial guidelines, and style guides for the content team
- Manage content operations including publishing workflows, content calendars, and stakeholder reviews

## Core Competencies
- Exceptional writing, editing, and narrative storytelling skills across multiple content formats
- Strong understanding of content marketing strategy, funnel alignment, and buyer journey mapping
- Deep knowledge of SEO best practices, keyword research, and content optimization for search
- Proficiency with CMS platforms (WordPress, Contentful, HubSpot, Webflow) and content analytics tools
- Ability to translate complex technical topics into clear, accessible content for target audiences
- Project management skills for managing multi-channel content programs and editorial calendars
- Understanding of content distribution and amplification strategies across owned and earned channels
- Familiarity with content design principles and visual storytelling
- Analytical mindset for measuring content performance and making data-driven decisions
- Research skills for developing credible, well-sourced content with subject matter depth

## KPIs & Success Metrics
- Organic traffic to content (blog, landing pages) and search rank improvements
- Content engagement metrics (time on page, scroll depth, social shares, comments)
- Lead generation from content (form fills, gated content downloads, demo requests)
- Content-assisted conversion rate and influence on pipeline
- Backlinks and domain authority growth from content marketing
- Email subscriber growth and newsletter engagement rates
- Content production velocity and publishing consistency

## Career Progression
- Junior/Associate (0-2 years): Writes blog posts under editorial guidance, manages publishing, assists with research
- Content Marketer (2-4 years): Independently owns content pieces, manages content calendar, optimizes for performance
- Senior (4-7 years): Develops content strategy, manages complex campaigns, mentors junior writers
- Content Lead/Editor (7-10 years): Owns content direction, manages content team, drives editorial strategy
- Director (10+ years): Sets overall content strategy, manages team, influences marketing direction
- Typical progression: Junior to Mid 1-2 years, Mid to Senior 2-3 years, Senior to Lead 2-4 years

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Morning review of content performance metrics and editorial calendar priorities
- Writing blocks dedicated to drafting, editing, and revising content pieces
- Coordination with subject matter experts for interviews, reviews, and technical input
- Content publishing and distribution across channels following editorial schedule
- Weekly editorial team meeting for content planning, pipeline review, and alignment
- SEO optimization work including keyword gap analysis and content refresh initiatives
- Stakeholder reviews and feedback incorporation on draft content
- Analytics review for published content performance and strategy iteration

## Regional Nuances
- NA: High content volume expected; emphasis on data-driven content marketing; strong blog and LinkedIn presence; content specialization by funnel stage common
- EMEA: Multi-language content requirements critical; stronger B2B content expectations in DACH region; GDPR compliance in content tracking; UK and Germany lead content marketing maturity
- APAC: Mobile-first content consumption; local language content critical for market penetration; higher video content adoption; India has strong English-language content talent pool
- LATAM: Growing content marketing maturity; Spanish and Portuguese content required for regional reach; adaptation of global content to local context important; cost-effective content production

## Industry Variations
- SaaS: Technical blog content, product-led content, comparison pages, integration guides, documentation-driven content marketing
- Fintech: Educational financial content, compliance-aware content, trust-building thought leadership, regulatory change content
- Healthcare: Evidence-based medical content, patient education, provider resources, regulatory-reviewed content workflows
- E-commerce: Product descriptions, buying guides, comparison content, user-generated content curation, seasonal content calendars

## Common Challenges
- Producing consistent high-quality content volume while maintaining editorial standards
- Balancing SEO-optimized content with authentic, engaging storytelling
- Getting meaningful access to subject matter experts who are already time-constrained
- Measuring content ROI and connecting content activities to revenue outcomes
- Keeping content fresh and accurate as products, markets, and strategies evolve
- Standing out in competitive content landscapes with high content saturation

## Key Regulations & Compliance
- GDPR for content tracking cookies and data collection in EMEA
- CCPA for California content marketing data
- CAN-SPAM Act for email content compliance
- FTC endorsement guidelines for sponsored content and influencer partnerships
- FDA regulations for healthcare/medical content claims
- SEC regulations for financial services content (fair disclosure, advertising rules)
- ADA accessibility requirements for web content (WCAG compliance)
