# beauty-esthetician

**Summary:** Provides skincare treatments including facials and waxing.

**Sector:** Beauty / Personal Care

Key responsibilities:
- Performs facial treatments, chemical peels, and exfoliation
- Provides hair removal services including waxing and threading
- Conducts skin analysis and recommends skincare routines
- Applies makeup for special occasions as needed
- Educates clients on home skincare and product use

Core competencies:
- Skincare treatment techniques and protocols
- Skin analysis and condition identification
- Sanitation and hygiene standards
- Client consultation and education
- Product knowledge and retail sales

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Spa Manager / Salon Manager
- Collaborates with: Massage Therapists, Front Desk, Clients
