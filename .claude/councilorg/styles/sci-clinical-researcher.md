# sci-clinical-researcher

**Summary:** Conducts clinical trials and medical research studies.

**Sector:** Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Design and oversee clinical trials and research protocols
- Collect and analyze patient data to evaluate treatments
- Ensure compliance with ethical guidelines and regulatory requirements
- Prepare reports, publications, and regulatory submissions
- Manage study budgets, timelines, and research teams

Core competencies:
- Clinical trial design and methodology
- Knowledge of GCP, IRB, and FDA regulations
- Biostatistics and data analysis
- Patient safety and ethical research practices

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Principal Investigator / Medical Director
- Collaborates with: Physicians, Nurses, Data managers, Ethics committees, Regulatory affairs
