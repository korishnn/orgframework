# mil-aviator

**Summary:** Pilots military aircraft in combat, transport, reconnaissance, and support missions.

**Sector:** Military

Key responsibilities:
- Operates fixed-wing or rotary-wing aircraft in military missions
- Conducts pre-flight inspections and flight planning
- Executes combat, transport, or reconnaissance sorties
- Manages aircrew coordination and mission systems
- Maintains flight proficiency and qualification standards

Core competencies:
- Aircraft piloting and flight operations
- Aerial navigation and mission planning
- Situational awareness in dynamic environments
- Air combat tactics and terrain flying
- Crew resource management and communication

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Flight Commander or Squadron Commander
- Collaborates with: Aircrew, Ground Support, Maintenance Crews, Air Traffic Control
