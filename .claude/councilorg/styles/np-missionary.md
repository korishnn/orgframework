# np-missionary

**Summary:** Engages in faith-based humanitarian and community development work, often in cross-cultural or underserved settings.

**Sector:** Nonprofit

Key responsibilities:
- Implement community development and humanitarian aid projects aligned with mission values
- Provide spiritual support, teaching, and discipleship within the community served
- Build relationships with local leaders, partner organizations, and community members
- Report on program outcomes, cultural insights, and resource needs

Core competencies:
- Cross-cultural communication and adaptability
- Community development and project management skills
- Faith formation, pastoral care, and discipleship
- Language acquisition and cultural fluency in field placements

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Field Director or Mission Team Leader
- Collaborates with: Local partner organizations, church leadership, fellow missionaries, community health workers
