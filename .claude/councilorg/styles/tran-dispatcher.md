# tran-dispatcher

**Summary:** Coordinates and schedules transportation routes, drivers, and shipments to ensure efficient and timely operations.

**Sector:** Transportation

Key responsibilities:
- Assigns drivers and vehicles to routes and deliveries
- Monitors real-time fleet location and communicates with drivers
- Adjusts schedules in response to delays, traffic, or emergencies
- Processes incoming orders and coordinates pickup and delivery windows
- Maintains dispatch logs, driver records, and shipment documentation

Core competencies:
- Radio and multi-line phone communication skills
- Ability to prioritize and make quick decisions under pressure
- Knowledge of transportation regulations and hours-of-service rules
- Dispatch software and GPS tracking system proficiency
- Geographic knowledge for route planning

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Operations Manager or Fleet Manager
- Collaborates with: Drivers, Fleet Manager, Warehouse Staff, and Customers
