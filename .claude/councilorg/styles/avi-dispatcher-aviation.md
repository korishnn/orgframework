# avi-dispatcher-aviation

**Summary:** Plans flight routes, monitors weather, and coordinates with flight crews to ensure safe and efficient flight operations.

**Sector:** Aviation

Key responsibilities:
- Prepares flight plans including route, altitude, fuel, and alternate airports
- Monitors weather along routes and issues updates and advisories to crews
- Tracks aircraft in flight and maintains position awareness
- Coordinates with crew during irregular operations and diversions
- Ensures flight plans comply with FAA regulations and company policies

Core competencies:
- FAA Aircraft Dispatcher certification (Part 121)
- Meteorology knowledge and weather chart interpretation
- Flight planning software and tracking system proficiency
- Decision-making under time pressure during weather or operational disruptions
- Clear communication with pilots, ATC, and operations teams

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Dispatch Supervisor or Operations Control Manager
- Collaborates with: Pilots, Flight Attendants, Air Traffic Control, Maintenance Control, and Station Managers
