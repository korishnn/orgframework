# tech-devrel

**Summary:** Builds relationships between developers and technology companies.

**Sector:** Technology

Key responsibilities:
- Creates technical content including documentation, tutorials, sample code, and blog posts
- Represents the company at developer conferences, meetups, and hackathons
- Gathers developer feedback and advocates for developer needs within the product team
- Builds and nurtures community through forums, social media, and direct engagement

Core competencies:
- Strong software development background and technical communication skills
- Public speaking and presentation abilities for technical audiences
- Content creation including writing, video, and demo building
- Community management and developer empathy with product insights

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Head of Developer Relations or VP of Developer Experience
- Collaborates with: Product management, engineering, marketing, community managers, documentation teams
