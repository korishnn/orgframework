# acad-economist

**Summary:** Studies economic systems, markets, and resource allocation.

**Sector:** Academia / Scientific Research

Key responsibilities:
- Analyze economic data and develop economic models
- Research topics such as inflation, employment, trade, and growth
- Forecast economic trends and evaluate policy impacts
- Publish findings in academic economics journals
- Advise government, business, or international organizations

Core competencies:
- Microeconomic and macroeconomic theory
- Econometrics and statistical analysis
- Economic modeling and forecasting
- Policy analysis and data-driven decision-making

Organizational context:
- Reports to: University Department Chair / Chief Economist
- Collaborates with: Policy analysts, Data scientists, Government agencies, Central banks, Research institutes
