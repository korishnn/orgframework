# trade-jeweler

**Summary:** Designs, fabricates, repairs, and sells jewelry and precious stone pieces for retail and custom clients.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Designs and fabricates custom jewelry pieces from precious metals
- Sets stones including diamonds, gemstones, and custom cuts
- Repairs broken chains, rings, clasps, and settings
- Sizes rings and adjusts jewelry for proper fit
- Appraises and assesses jewelry quality and value

Core competencies:
- Skill with jeweler's tools (torches, pliers, files, micro-saws)
- Knowledge of precious metals, gemstones, and their properties
- Stone setting and faceting techniques
- Fine manual dexterity and eyesight for detail work
- Customer service and custom design consultation skills

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Shop Manager or Business Owner
- Collaborates with: Gemologists, Sales Associates, Goldsmiths, and Customers
