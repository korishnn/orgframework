# trade-welder

**Summary:** Joins metal parts using welding techniques in construction, manufacturing, and industrial fabrication settings.

**Sector:** Trades

Key responsibilities:
- Welds metal components using MIG, TIG, stick, and flux-cored processes
- Reads welding symbols, blueprints, and work orders
- Prepares surfaces by cleaning, grinding, and fitting parts
- Inspects welds for quality, strength, and code compliance
- Repairs cracked or damaged metal structures and equipment

Core competencies:
- Proficiency in multiple welding processes and positions
- Knowledge of metallurgy and filler material selection
- Ability to read technical drawings and weld symbols
- Steady hand, good hand-eye coordination, and attention to detail
- Understanding of welding safety protocols and PPE requirements

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Welding Supervisor or Fabrication Shop Manager
- Collaborates with: Fitters, Fabricators, Machinists, and Quality Inspectors
