# govt-urban-planner

**Summary:** Develops plans for community growth, infrastructure, and revitalization in urban areas.

**Sector:** Government

Key responsibilities:
- Creates long-range plans for urban development and land use
- Analyzes demographic, economic, and transportation data
- Develops zoning ordinances and development codes
- Leads community visioning and stakeholder engagement
- Evaluates environmental impact of development proposals

Core competencies:
- Urban design and planning theory
- Spatial analysis and GIS proficiency
- Community engagement and facilitation
- Data analysis and forecasting
- Knowledge of urban economics and sustainability

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Planning Director or City Development Officer
- Collaborates with: City Planners, Architects, Transportation Officials, Developers, Community Groups
