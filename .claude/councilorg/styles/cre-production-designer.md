# cre-production-designer

**Summary:** Designs the overall visual look of a production including sets, props, color palettes, and environments to support the narrative and creative vision.

**Sector:** Film

Key responsibilities:
- Develop the visual concept and design direction in collaboration with the director and cinematographer
- Create design sketches, mood boards, and renderings for sets and environments
- Oversee set construction, decoration, and dressing
- Select color palettes, textures, and materials that support the production's aesthetic
- Coordinate with costume, lighting, and art departments for visual consistency
- Manage the art department budget and production schedule

Core competencies:
- Strong visual design skills including drawing, rendering, and spatial planning
- Knowledge of architectural styles, period design, and visual history
- Leadership for managing art department teams
- Understanding of construction methods, materials, and budget management
- Collaboration with cinematographers on color and lighting integration

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Director
- Collaborates with: Director, Cinematographer, Art Director, Set Decorator, Costume Designer, Construction Manager
