# cre-composer

**Summary:** Writes and arranges original music for performances, media, recordings, and releases across genres and formats.

**Sector:** Music

Key responsibilities:
- Create original musical compositions for film, television, games, or concert settings
- Arrange existing musical themes and melodies for different ensembles or contexts
- Orchestrate music for various instrument combinations and ensembles
- Collaborate with directors, producers, and musicians on musical direction
- Produce demo recordings and mockups of compositions
- Adapt and revise compositions based on creative feedback

Core competencies:
- Advanced music theory knowledge including harmony, counterpoint, and orchestration
- Proficiency with composition and notation software (Sibelius, Finale, Dorico)
- Understanding of digital audio workstations and virtual instruments
- Creative storytelling through music and thematic development
- Collaboration with creative directors and performers

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Music Director / Producer
- Collaborates with: Director, Music Producer, Sound Engineer, Musicians, Orchestrator, Music Supervisor
