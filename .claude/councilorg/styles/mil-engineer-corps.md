# mil-engineer-corps

**Summary:** Builds infrastructure and conducts demolition in military operations to support forces.

**Sector:** Military

Key responsibilities:
- Constructs roads, bridges, and forward operating bases
- Conducts demolition and obstacle breaching operations
- Detects and neutralizes explosive hazards and mines
- Provides construction engineering in deployed environments
- Supports disaster response with engineering capabilities

Core competencies:
- Combat engineering and construction techniques
- Explosives handling and demolition procedures
- Heavy equipment operation and maintenance
- Route clearance and counter-IED procedures
- Blueprint reading and engineering design

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Engineer Platoon Leader or Company Commander
- Collaborates with: Combat Units, Construction Teams, EOD Personnel, Civil Affairs
