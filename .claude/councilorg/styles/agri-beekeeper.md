# agri-beekeeper

**Summary:** Manages beehives for honey production and crop pollination services.

**Sector:** Agriculture

Key responsibilities:
- Inspect and maintain beehives to ensure colony health and productivity
- Harvest, extract, and process honey and other hive products
- Provide pollination services to agricultural crops through managed hive placement
- Monitor and treat hives for pests, diseases, and environmental stressors

Core competencies:
- Knowledge of bee biology, colony behavior, and seasonal management cycles
- Physical stamina for lifting heavy hive components and working in outdoor conditions
- Proficiency in hive inspection techniques and integrated pest management
- Business acumen for marketing honey products and contracting pollination services

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Farm owner, apiary manager, or cooperative board
- Collaborates with: Crop farmers, agricultural extension agents, equipment suppliers, and food safety inspectors
