# consult-digital-transformation

**Summary:** Guides organizations through digital technology and process transformation to modernize operations.

**Sector:** Consulting

Key responsibilities:
- Assesses digital maturity and identifies transformation opportunities
- Develops digital strategy and technology roadmaps
- Leads adoption of cloud, AI, and automation technologies
- Redesigns processes for digital-first operations
- Supports organizational change around digital adoption

Core competencies:
- Digital strategy and technology landscape knowledge
- Cloud, AI, and automation expertise
- Process digitization and optimization
- Change management and adoption
- Vendor evaluation and technology selection

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Engagement Manager or Digital Practice Lead
- Collaborates with: IT Leaders, Business Stakeholders, Technology Vendors, Implementation Teams
