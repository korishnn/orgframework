# hlth-medical-transcriber

**Summary:** Transcribes medical dictation into patient records.

**Sector:** Healthcare

Key responsibilities:
- Transcribe physician dictation into accurate written medical reports
- Edit and proofread transcribed documents for grammar and clarity
- Interpret medical terminology, abbreviations, and acronyms correctly
- Format reports according to healthcare facility standards and templates
- Review and correct speech recognition-generated drafts
- Maintain patient confidentiality and comply with privacy regulations
- Meet productivity and quality benchmarks for transcription accuracy

Core competencies:
- Medical terminology, anatomy, and pharmacology knowledge
- Typing speed and accuracy
- Listening comprehension and auditory processing
- Grammar, punctuation, and editing skills
- Electronic health record documentation standards
- Understanding of medical report formats (H&P, operative, discharge summaries)

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Transcription Services Manager / HIM Director
- Collaborates with: Physicians, nurse practitioners, medical records staff, health information management
