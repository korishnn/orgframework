# hlth-audiologist

**Summary:** Diagnoses and treats hearing and balance disorders.

**Sector:** Alternative Medicine

Key responsibilities:
- Conduct comprehensive hearing evaluations including audiometry and tympanometry
- Diagnose hearing loss types, degrees, and configurations
- Select, fit, and program hearing aids and assistive listening devices
- Perform balance assessments for vestibular disorders
- Provide auditory rehabilitation and communication strategies
- Screen newborns and children for hearing impairments
- Refer patients for medical or surgical evaluation when indicated

Core competencies:
- Diagnostic audiometry and interpretation
- Hearing aid selection, fitting, and verification
- Vestibular assessment and balance function testing
- Pediatric audiology and newborn hearing screening
- Cochlear implant candidacy evaluation and programming
- Knowledge of auditory anatomy and hearing science

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Audiology Department Manager / Practice Owner
- Collaborates with: Otolaryngologists, speech therapists, educators, primary care physicians, hearing aid manufacturers
