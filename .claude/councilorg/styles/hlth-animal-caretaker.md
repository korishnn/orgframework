# hlth-animal-caretaker

**Summary:** Provides daily care, feeding, and enrichment for animals.

**Sector:** Veterinary

Key responsibilities:
- Feed and provide fresh water to animals according to dietary schedules
- Clean and maintain animal living spaces, enclosures, and kennels
- Observe animals for signs of illness, injury, or behavioral changes
- Administer basic medications and treatments under supervision
- Provide exercise, socialization, and environmental enrichment
- Maintain hygiene and sanitation standards in animal areas
- Assist with animal handling during veterinary procedures

Core competencies:
- Animal behavior recognition and monitoring
- Animal feeding and dietary management
- Sanitation and infection control in animal environments
- Safe animal handling and restraint
- Basic first aid and health observation
- Record keeping for daily care activities

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Animal Care Supervisor / Kennel Manager
- Collaborates with: Veterinarians, veterinary technicians, shelter staff, pet owners
