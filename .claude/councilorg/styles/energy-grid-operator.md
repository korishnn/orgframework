# energy-grid-operator

**Summary:** Manages electrical grid operations and load balancing to ensure reliable power delivery.

**Sector:** Energy

Key responsibilities:
- Monitors and controls electrical grid operations in real time
- Balances electricity supply and demand across the network
- Coordinates power plant dispatch and transmission scheduling
- Responds to grid disturbances and outage events
- Ensures grid reliability and regulatory compliance

Core competencies:
- Power system operations and engineering principles
- Energy management system and SCADA proficiency
- Decision-making under high-pressure conditions
- Understanding of energy markets and dispatch economics
- Communication and coordination with multiple stakeholders

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Grid Operations Manager or Reliability Coordinator
- Collaborates with: Power Plant Operators, Utility Dispatchers, Transmission Planners, Regulators
