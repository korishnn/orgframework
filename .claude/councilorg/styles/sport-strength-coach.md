# sport-strength-coach

**Summary:** Designs and implements strength and conditioning programs.

**Sector:** Sports

Key responsibilities:
- Designs sport-specific strength and conditioning programs
- Leads team and individual training sessions
- Monitors athlete progress and adjusts programming
- Educates on proper lifting technique and injury prevention
- Coordinates with athletic trainers on rehabilitation transitions

Core competencies:
- Strength training and program design
- Exercise physiology and biomechanics
- Coaching and motivation
- Injury prevention and movement screening
- Sport-specific conditioning knowledge

Organizational context:
- Reports to: Head Coach / Director of Performance
- Collaborates with: Athletic Trainers, Coaches, Nutritionists, Athletes
